# NATURAL PHENOMENA

Natural phenomena are event-driven systems.

Examples:

- rainfall
- storms
- lightning
- floods
- landslides
- erosion
- strong winds
- dust events
- natural fire

Each phenomenon should define:

1. Preconditions
2. Onset
3. Peak/intensity
4. Interaction with surroundings
5. Dissipation
6. Aftermath
7. Recovery where applicable

The phenomenon must not appear or disappear arbitrarily between
adjacent shots.
