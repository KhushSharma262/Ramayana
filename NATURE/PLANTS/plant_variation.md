# PLANT VARIATION

Plant populations must contain controlled variation.

Variation may include:

- height
- age
- branch structure
- leaf density
- orientation
- growth direction
- flowering
- fruiting
- dryness
- damage

Variation must remain consistent with:

- species/type
- habitat
- season
- soil
- water
- climate

Do not clone one plant repeatedly.

Do not randomly introduce incompatible species.
