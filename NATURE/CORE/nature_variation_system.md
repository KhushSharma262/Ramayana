# NATURE VARIATION SYSTEM

Natural variation prevents artificial repetition.

Variation operates at several levels.

## MICRO VARIATION

Small differences between nearby natural elements.

Examples:
- leaf orientation
- branch shape
- grass height
- stone shape

## LOCAL VARIATION

Differences within one environment.

Examples:
- vegetation density
- soil moisture
- rock distribution
- cloud density

## TEMPORAL VARIATION

Changes with time.

Examples:
- sunrise
- drying
- cloud movement
- flowering
- water level

## SEASONAL VARIATION

Changes associated with the established season.

## EVENT-DRIVEN VARIATION

Changes caused by a documented event.

Examples:
- rainfall
- fire
- flood
- divine event
- major disturbance

## Continuity rule

Variation must never become identity drift.

A generated forest can vary while remaining the same forest.

A mountain can change in atmospheric visibility without changing
its physical silhouette.

A river can change its water state without changing its identity
or established course unless the story establishes a real change.
