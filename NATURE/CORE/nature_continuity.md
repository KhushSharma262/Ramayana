# NATURE CONTINUITY

Natural elements must remain consistent across:

- scenes
- shots
- locations
- seasons
- time of day
- weather states
- story events

Track changes caused by:

- time
- season
- weather
- human interaction
- animal interaction
- fire
- water
- divine events
- destruction
- recovery

Do not allow AI generation to randomly change species, vegetation,
mountain silhouettes, river direction or other established features.
