# NATURE SYSTEM

NATURE is the natural-world truth layer.

It defines:

- what natural elements are
- their identity
- their physical and biological characteristics
- their normal operating ranges
- their possible natural states
- their ecological relationships
- their variation
- their continuity requirements
- their source evidence

NATURE does NOT define one fixed appearance for every shot.

## Fundamental model

NATURAL IDENTITY
    +
NATURAL RANGE
    +
CURRENT STATE
    +
TIME
    +
SEASON
    +
LOCATION
    +
WEATHER
    +
STORY EVENT
    =
CURRENT NATURAL APPEARANCE

Therefore:

IDENTITY ≠ APPEARANCE

A tree remains the same type of tree while its:

- leaf density
- wetness
- dryness
- flowering
- movement
- damage
- lighting response
- seasonal condition

may change.

The same principle applies to:

- atmosphere
- clouds
- rivers
- vegetation
- ground
- mountains
- sky
- water
- fire
- natural phenomena

## Domain boundary

NATURE
=
natural-world identity, properties, ranges and possible states

ENVIRONMENT
=
current dynamic world state and interactions

ANIMALS
=
animal identity and behavior

LOCATIONS
=
spatial existence and placement

MATERIALS
=
material identity and physical material properties

CINEMATOGRAPHY
=
camera interpretation

VFX
=
technical implementation of effects

COMPOSITING
=
integration of visual elements

## Core rule

NATURE must provide enough truth for ENVIRONMENT to generate
different believable states without changing the underlying identity.
