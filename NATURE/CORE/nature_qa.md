# NATURE QA

Check:

- ecological plausibility
- visual consistency
- species consistency
- scale
- seasonal consistency
- geographic consistency
- lighting response
- environmental interaction
- continuity
- source classification
- AI-generated anomalies

Reject:

- impossible plant morphology
- floating vegetation
- inconsistent mountain scale
- duplicated natural formations
- random species changes
- impossible water behavior
- inconsistent seasonal states
- unnatural repetition
