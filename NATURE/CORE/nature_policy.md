# NATURE POLICY

Nature must feel alive rather than procedurally frozen.

Natural variation is REQUIRED where appropriate.

Variation may occur across:

- time
- location
- season
- weather
- age
- ecological conditions
- natural events
- story events
- viewpoint
- distance

However, variation must remain within the established identity and
physical/ecological constraints.

## Never lock a natural element to one visual appearance

For example:

A forest must not always have:
- identical fog
- identical cloud density
- identical leaf movement
- identical lighting
- identical vegetation density

A river must not always have:
- identical water level
- identical turbulence
- identical clarity
- identical reflections

Atmosphere must not always have:
- identical haze
- identical humidity
- identical visibility
- identical density

## Variation is controlled, not random

Allowed:
natural variation within established ranges.

Forbidden:
random AI variation that changes identity or violates continuity.

## Source hierarchy

Where a natural feature is explicitly described by the selected
canonical source, that information has priority.

Where the source is silent:
- traditional evidence may be used
- historical/regional evidence may be used
- scientific/ecological evidence may be used
- production reconstruction may be used

The classification must remain documented.
