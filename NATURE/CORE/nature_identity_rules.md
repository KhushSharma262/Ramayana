# NATURE IDENTITY RULES

Natural identity is the stable part of a natural element.

Identity may include:

- type/species
- morphology
- geological class
- ecological role
- structural characteristics
- characteristic scale
- characteristic growth form
- characteristic physical behavior

Identity does NOT mean fixed:

- color under every lighting condition
- wetness
- dryness
- density
- movement
- atmospheric appearance
- seasonal condition
- damage
- weather response

## Example

TREE IDENTITY:
- species/type
- morphology
- trunk structure
- branching pattern
- leaf form

TREE STATE:
- flowering
- dry
- wet
- wind-affected
- damaged
- seasonal

The state can change without changing the identity.

## Continuity

A recurring natural landmark may have controlled changes over time,
but those changes must be explainable by the story timeline or
natural processes.
