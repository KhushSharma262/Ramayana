# ATMOSPHERE

Atmosphere is NOT a fixed visual preset.

NATURE defines the physical/natural characteristics and possible
ranges of atmospheric conditions.

Possible variables include:

- humidity
- haze
- aerosol concentration
- visibility
- atmospheric depth
- temperature-related effects
- moisture
- dust
- mist potential

## Current atmosphere belongs to ENVIRONMENT

ENVIRONMENT determines the current state using:

- location
- altitude
- time
- season
- weather
- water proximity
- terrain
- wind
- story events
- established continuity

Therefore the same location may have different atmospheric states.

Example:

SAME LOCATION

Morning:
high humidity + light mist

Afternoon:
clearer visibility + stronger solar heating

Evening:
increasing haze + lower light

Rain:
high moisture + reduced visibility

Storm:
dense cloud + disturbed atmosphere

Night:
different visibility and atmospheric lighting

These are different STATES of the same natural system.

## Continuity rule

Atmosphere may change between shots only when:

- the change is physically plausible
- the timeline permits it
- the environmental state permits it
- the change is not an accidental AI inconsistency
