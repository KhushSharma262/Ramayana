# ECOLOGY

Nature must function as an interconnected system.

Relationships include:

plant ↔ soil
plant ↔ water
plant ↔ sunlight
plant ↔ season
plant ↔ animal
river ↔ vegetation
mountain ↔ water
weather ↔ vegetation
fire ↔ vegetation
soil ↔ vegetation

Avoid treating natural elements as isolated decoration.
