# RIVER SYSTEM

River identity includes:

- established course
- major bends
- banks
- bed
- surrounding geography
- ecological character
- characteristic flow behavior

River STATE may vary through:

- seasonal flow
- rainfall
- drought
- sediment
- turbulence
- water level
- clarity
- surface conditions

A river may therefore look different at different times while
remaining the same river.

Major geographic changes require an explicit story/time event.
