# RAMAYANA — CAMERA CONTINUITY CONTRACT

## MASTER PRINCIPLE

The CAMERA is the observer of the established world.

CAMERA does not silently redefine:

- character identity
- character state
- animal identity
- object identity
- location geometry
- environmental truth
- event truth
- physical truth
- transformation truth
- canonical truth

WORLD STATE
→ SPATIAL STATE
→ CHARACTER / OBJECT / ENVIRONMENT STATE
→ CAMERA INTENT
→ CAMERA IDENTITY
→ CAMERA POSITION
→ CAMERA ORIENTATION
→ LENS / SENSOR
→ FRAMING
→ COMPOSITION
→ FOCUS
→ EXPOSURE
→ MOVEMENT
→ SHOT
→ EDIT
→ NEXT CAMERA STATE

## CAMERA CONTINUITY MODEL

IDENTITY
→ BASELINE
→ PREVIOUS CAMERA STATE
→ CAMERA EVENT
→ STATE DELTA
→ CURRENT CAMERA STATE
→ CONSEQUENCE
→ PERSISTENCE
→ NEXT CAMERA STATE

## AUTHORITY

CAMERA owns:

- camera identity
- camera configuration
- lens configuration
- camera position
- camera orientation
- framing
- composition
- focus
- exposure
- camera movement
- optical behavior
- camera-specific visual behavior
- camera-specific continuity

Other domains remain authoritative for their own truth.

## NO SILENT RESET

AI generation must not silently change:

- lens
- focal length
- field of view
- camera height
- camera distance
- camera position
- camera orientation
- camera side of axis
- screen direction
- framing
- composition
- focus
- depth of field
- exposure
- shutter behavior
- camera movement
- visual language

unless explicitly authorized.

## PHYSICAL CAMERA RULE

Camera movement must be physically plausible unless explicitly classified as:

- divine
- supernatural
- dream
- memory
- subjective
- magical
- otherwise intentionally impossible

## SPATIAL RULE

The camera must remain consistent with the authoritative SPATIAL system.

A camera may observe a space differently.

It may not silently change the space.

## CHARACTER RULE

Character position and identity come from CHARACTER/SPATIAL/PERFORMANCE.

Camera records and observes those states.

Camera must preserve:

- eyeline
- screen position
- gaze direction
- relative distance
- relative orientation
- movement direction
- interaction geometry

unless an intentional transition changes them.

## 180-DEGREE RULE

The camera side of the established axis is persistent.

Crossing the axis requires:

- explicit event
- intentional visual decision
- transition
- validation

## LENS RULE

Focal length is not cosmetic.

It changes:

- field of view
- perspective
- spatial compression
- subject/background relationship
- depth of field
- visual scale

Therefore lens changes require continuity tracking.

## FOCUS RULE

Focus is a camera state.

Focus changes require:

- previous focus
- target
- transition
- duration
- next focus state

## EXPOSURE RULE

Exposure must remain compatible with:

- LIGHTING
- SENSOR
- LENS
- TIME
- ENVIRONMENT

## MOVEMENT RULE

Camera movement must preserve:

- start position
- direction
- velocity
- acceleration
- duration
- end position
- movement phase

The camera cannot teleport.

## SHOT CONTINUITY

Every shot should know:

- previous shot
- current shot
- next shot
- camera identity
- camera position
- orientation
- lens
- framing
- focus
- exposure
- movement
- subject
- spatial state
- eyeline
- axis
- screen direction

## AI RULE

AI_GENERATION implements camera requirements.

AI does not decide camera continuity.

AI does not become the source of truth.

Previous AI output is a reference, not authority.

Regeneration is repair, not reset.

## NATURAL VARIATION

Allowed:

- controlled handheld movement
- operator micro-movement
- natural vibration
- minor focus variation
- environmental camera response
- physically plausible exposure adaptation

Not allowed:

- unexplained camera teleportation
- random lens changes
- random reframing
- random axis changes
- random perspective changes
- random camera height changes
- random visual-language changes

## FINAL RULE

The camera must behave as though a real cinematographer and physical camera continuously observed the same persistent world.

It may change intentionally.

It may move naturally.

It may vary within controlled limits.

It must never silently reset the visual reality of the world.
