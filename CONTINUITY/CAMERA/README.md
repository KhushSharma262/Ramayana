# CAMERA CONTINUITY

Global camera continuity system for the Ramayana production.

## CORE

The camera is an observer of the persistent world.

## CAMERA OWNS

- camera identity
- camera configuration
- lens
- optics
- sensor behavior
- position
- orientation
- framing
- composition
- focus
- exposure
- movement
- camera physics
- visual camera language
- shot camera state

## CAMERA DOES NOT OWN

- character truth
- animal truth
- world state
- event truth
- spatial geometry
- physics truth
- transformation truth
- canonical truth

## CONTINUITY

FRAME
→ SHOT
→ SCENE
→ SEQUENCE
→ EPISODE
→ SERIES

## AI

AI renders established camera requirements.

AI cannot silently change camera continuity.

## REGENERATION

Regeneration preserves approved camera state.

## NATURAL VARIATION

Natural camera imperfection is allowed within defined limits.

## QA

Every camera state can be validated before:

- generation
- shot lock
- scene lock
- sequence lock
- episode lock
- series lock
