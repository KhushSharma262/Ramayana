# RETROACTIVE LIGHTING CORRECTION

If a lighting error is discovered:

1. Preserve the original output/history.
2. Identify the incorrect lighting attribute.
3. Determine affected shots.
4. Determine affected dependent domains.
5. Correct only the affected dependency chain.
6. Revalidate material and identity.
7. Revalidate shadows and environment.
8. Revalidate AI output.
9. Approve the corrected state.
10. Propagate only validated corrections.

Do not regenerate unrelated animal states.
