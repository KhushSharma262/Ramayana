# LIGHTING AUTHORITY

Authority hierarchy:

1. GLOBAL LIGHTING
2. ANIMAL/LIGHTING
3. ANIMAL/ANATOMY
4. ANIMAL/STATE
5. ENVIRONMENT
6. CAMERA
7. AI GENERATION

Where two systems conflict, the authoritative domain wins.

ANIMAL/LIGHTING cannot override:
- canonical animal anatomy
- physical environment
- global lighting state
- established animal identity

It defines the animal-specific consequences of those systems.
