# ANIMAL LIGHTING MASTER MODEL

The Animal Lighting model connects:

ANIMAL IDENTITY
ANIMAL ANATOMY
ANIMAL MATERIAL
ANIMAL STATE
ENVIRONMENT
GLOBAL LIGHTING
CAMERA
PHYSICS
AI GENERATION

Lighting is a contextual representation of a stable physical animal.

The same animal must remain recognizable under:

sunlight
moonlight
firelight
shade
cloud cover
indoor light
reflected light
water-reflected light
atmospheric scattering
partial occlusion
backlight
rim light

unless the animal itself changes.
