# ANIMAL / LIGHTING

## Purpose

Defines how lighting interacts with animals while preserving
identity, material, anatomy, state and continuity.

## Boundary

GLOBAL LIGHTING
= actual scene illumination

ANIMAL/LIGHTING
= animal-specific illumination response

## Master Model

LIGHT SOURCE
? ILLUMINATION
? MATERIAL RESPONSE
? ANATOMICAL VISIBILITY
? SURFACE APPEARANCE
? PERCEPTION / PERFORMANCE
? CONTINUITY

## Critical Rule

Lighting changes appearance.

Lighting does not automatically change the animal.

## Stable Across Lighting Changes

- identity
- anatomy
- body proportions
- markings
- material identity
- eye anatomy
- injury
- scars
- equipment
- residue state
- transformation state

## Allowed Natural Variation

- highlight intensity
- shadow softness
- apparent brightness
- visibility of texture
- catchlight position
- partial occlusion
- apparent contrast

provided they follow actual lighting conditions.

## Divine / Mythological Rule

BIOLOGICAL BASELINE
+
CANONICAL EXCEPTION
+
SUPERNATURAL PROPERTY

Mythological status alone does not justify supernatural illumination.

## AI Rule

AI generation implements lighting.

It does not define lighting truth.

Generated images/videos do not automatically become authoritative.

## Continuity

FRAME
? SHOT
? SCENE
? SEQUENCE
? EPISODE
? SERIES

Lighting must remain causally continuous across all levels.

## Global Boundary

ANIMAL/LIGHTING must interface with GLOBAL/LIGHTING.

It must not duplicate the global lighting engine, camera system,
physics system, timeline, VFX system or AI generation system.
