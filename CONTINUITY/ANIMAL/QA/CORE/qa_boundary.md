# QA DOMAIN BOUNDARY

QA owns:

validation
error detection
severity
impact
repair requirements
revalidation
approval status
audit

QA does NOT own:

identity truth
species truth
physiology truth
behavior truth
canon truth
physics truth
camera truth
lighting truth
audio truth
AI model truth

Those domains remain authoritative.

QA asks:

DOES THE OUTPUT AGREE WITH THE AUTHORITATIVE DOMAIN STATE?
