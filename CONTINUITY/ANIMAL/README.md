# ANIMAL CONTINUITY

The ANIMAL system defines continuity for animals appearing in the Ramayana production.

## Core Principle

ANIMAL defines what an animal is, its biological or canonical state, its identity,
behavior, relationships, movement, interaction, environmental effects and persistence.

Global systems remain authoritative for their respective domains.

## Authority

Canon has priority.

Biological and scientific knowledge may reconstruct unspecified mechanics,
but biological reconstruction must never overwrite explicit canonical information.

## Identity

Species identity and individual animal identity are separate.

An individual animal remains the same entity across shots, scenes and episodes
unless an explicit continuity event establishes death, replacement, transformation,
identity change or another valid exception.

## Detail Levels

### Principal Animal
Full individual continuity.

### Supporting Individual
Persistent individual identity with reduced detail.

### Population / Background
Species, group, population, ecological and collective continuity without
unnecessary individual tracking.

## Human Realism

Animals must behave as animals of their species.

Natural variation is permitted.

Identity drift, anatomy drift, species-inappropriate behavior,
impossible biomechanics and unexplained state resets are not permitted.
