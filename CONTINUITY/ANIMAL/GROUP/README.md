## Group Is Not Population

POPULATION = broader biological population.

GROUP = particular social/behavioral aggregation.

## Group Is Not Individual

INDIVIDUAL = one animal.

GROUP = collective structure containing individuals.

## Group Is Not Collective Consciousness

Group behavior emerges from:

individual perception
individual cognition
social interaction
communication
species-specific behavior
environment
ecology
group structure

Do not invent a shared human-like mind.

## Identity

A group receives a persistent group_id.

Membership may change without changing group identity.

Splitting/merging may create lineage and new group identities.

## Continuity

Groups persist across:

frames
shots
scenes
sequences
episodes

unless a valid event changes the state.

## Natural Variation

Members should not look or move like clones.

Variation is required within controlled limits.

## Canon

Canon overrides reconstruction.

Biology explains unspecified mechanics.

Unsupported group details remain UNKNOWN or UNCERTAIN.

## AI

AI_GENERATION implements group continuity.

AI-generated output does not automatically become group truth.
