# GROUP RETROACTIVE CORRECTION

When a group error is discovered:

1. Preserve original history.
2. Identify the incorrect group attribute.
3. Identify affected members.
4. Identify affected events.
5. Identify downstream dependencies.
6. Correct the smallest affected dependency chain.
7. Revalidate group state.
8. Revalidate individual consistency.
9. Revalidate ecology/environment where affected.
10. Approve.
11. Propagate.

Unaffected group and individual state remains unchanged.
