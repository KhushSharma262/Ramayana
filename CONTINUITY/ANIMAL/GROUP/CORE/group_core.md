# ANIMAL GROUP CORE

## Purpose

ANIMAL/GROUP defines persistent groups of animals and their
collective continuity.

Examples:

herd
flock
pack
troop
pride
family group
colony
swarm
school/shoal
breeding group
migratory group
temporary aggregation

## Boundary

GROUP owns:

- group identity
- group type
- membership
- composition
- hierarchy
- roles
- cohesion
- collective spatial state
- group movement
- group perception interface
- collective behavior
- group communication
- group events
- group history
- group-level consequences

GROUP does not replace:

SPECIES
POPULATION
INDIVIDUAL
COGNITION
BEHAVIOR
ECOLOGY
ENVIRONMENT
EVENT
SPATIAL
PHYSICS
STATE_LEDGER
AI_GENERATION

## Fundamental Rule

A group is a continuity entity, not merely a collection of
animals visually placed together.
