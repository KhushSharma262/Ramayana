# SPATIAL AUDIO

## Purpose

Defines animal sound-source spatial continuity.

---

# 1. SOURCE

Track where relevant:

- source position
- source direction
- source elevation
- source movement
- listener position
- distance

---

# 2. MOVING SOURCE

Example:

animal approaching
? passes listener
? moves behind
? becomes distant

Audio must follow the established trajectory.

---

# 3. OFF-SCREEN ANIMALS

An animal may remain audible while outside the frame.

Its sound source must remain spatially consistent.

---

# 4. OCCLUSION

Objects and environments may alter sound:

- walls
- doors
- vegetation
- terrain
- buildings
- crowds

---

# 5. DISTANCE

Distance affects perceived:

- loudness
- clarity
- direct/reverberant ratio
- localization

---

# 6. CAMERA

Camera movement does not automatically move the actual animal sound source.

The source exists in world space.

---

# 7. GLOBAL AUDIO

Final spatial rendering belongs to the global audio system.
