# ENVIRONMENTAL AUDIO

## Purpose

Defines how animal sound interacts with the environment.

---

# 1. ENVIRONMENT

Consider:

- forest
- open field
- mountain
- cave
- settlement
- palace
- river
- lake
- ocean
- desert
- village
- battlefield

where applicable.

---

# 2. PROPAGATION

Environmental conditions may affect:

- attenuation
- reflection
- absorption
- reverberation
- filtering
- masking

---

# 3. VEGETATION

Vegetation may:

- absorb sound
- obstruct sound
- create incidental movement sounds

---

# 4. WEATHER

Where relevant:

- wind
- rain
- storm
- humidity
- temperature

may affect sound propagation.

---

# 5. WATER

Water changes sound propagation and animal-produced sound.

Aquatic and terrestrial contexts must not be treated identically.

---

# 6. GLOBAL AUDIO

Final environmental acoustics remain governed by the global AUDIO system.
