# MOVEMENT AUDIO

## Purpose

Defines sounds produced by animal movement.

---

# 1. MOVEMENT SOURCES

Where applicable:

- walking
- running
- trotting
- sprinting
- jumping
- landing
- climbing
- crawling
- slithering
- swimming
- flying
- wing movement
- tail movement
- body movement
- scratching
- shaking
- grooming

---

# 2. SPECIES

Movement sound must correspond to actual anatomy.

---

# 3. BODY MASS

Sound may vary according to:

- body mass
- movement speed
- force
- surface
- gait
- fatigue

---

# 4. SURFACE

Movement sound depends on:

- soil
- mud
- grass
- stone
- wood
- sand
- water
- snow
- other established surfaces

---

# 5. CONTINUITY

Footstep and movement rhythm must remain consistent with visible movement.

---

# 6. PHYSICS

Sound must correspond to:

- contact
- force
- weight
- momentum
- acceleration
- deceleration

---

# 7. AI

Do not generate generic repeated footsteps independent of actual movement.
