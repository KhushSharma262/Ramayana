# CONTACT AUDIO

## Purpose

Defines sound caused by physical contact involving animals.

---

# 1. CONTACT TYPES

Examples:

- hoof ? ground
- paw ? ground
- body ? foliage
- animal ? animal
- animal ? human
- claw ? wood
- body ? water
- body ? saddle
- equipment ? animal

---

# 2. FORCE

Sound may depend on:

- mass
- speed
- impact
- pressure
- surface
- contact area

---

# 3. PHYSICAL CONTINUITY

Contact sound must correspond to visible contact.

---

# 4. EQUIPMENT

Animal contact with:

- saddle
- harness
- reins
- bells
- ornaments
- armor
- packs

must correspond to equipment continuity.

---

# 5. IMPOSSIBLE CONTACT

Reject audio implying contact when the visual/physical state
does not establish that contact.
