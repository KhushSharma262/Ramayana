# ANIMAL AUDIO VALIDATION

## Purpose

Validates animal-specific audio before approval.

---

# 1. SPECIES

Check:

- species-appropriate sound
- anatomical sound production
- correct vocalization type

---

# 2. INDIVIDUAL

Check:

- correct animal
- audio identity
- established vocal characteristics

---

# 3. STATE

Check:

- activity
- fear
- pain
- fatigue
- injury
- social state
- environmental state

---

# 4. VISUAL SYNCHRONIZATION

Check:

- breathing
- footsteps
- wing beats
- mouth movement
- contact
- movement
- equipment

---

# 5. SPATIAL

Check:

- position
- distance
- direction
- movement
- occlusion
- environment

---

# 6. CONTINUITY

Compare:

previous shot
? current shot
? next shot

---

# 7. CANON

Check extraordinary sounds against authority.

---

# 8. ARTIFACTS

Check for:

- repeated loops
- inappropriate species sound
- humanized voice
- impossible sound source
- incorrect timing
- incorrect spatial position
- excessive vocalization
- missing required sound
