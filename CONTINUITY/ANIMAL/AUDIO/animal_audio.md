# ANIMAL AUDIO CONTINUITY

## Purpose

Defines the complete animal-specific audio continuity system.

Animal audio includes:

- vocalization
- breathing
- movement sounds
- contact sounds
- feeding sounds
- grooming sounds
- biological sounds
- communication
- group sound
- predator/prey sound
- equipment-related sound
- environmental interaction
- spatial sound behavior

This layer defines animal-specific audio truth.

The global CONTINUITY/AUDIO system remains authoritative for
overall audio production, mixing, mastering, recording,
soundscape architecture and final soundtrack continuity.

---

# 1. AUTHORITY

Animal audio follows:

1. Direct Canon
2. Derived Canon
3. Traditional Interpretation
4. Approved Research
5. Biological Reconstruction
6. Historical Reconstruction
7. Audio Reconstruction
8. Directorial Choice
9. Production Choice
10. Unknown
11. Uncertain

Explicit canon overrides biological assumptions.

---

# 2. CORE MODEL

ANIMAL STATE
? PHYSIOLOGICAL CONDITION
? BEHAVIOR
? COMMUNICATION INTENT
? SOUND PRODUCTION
? ACOUSTIC EVENT
? SPATIAL PROPAGATION
? RECEIVER PERCEPTION
? RESPONSE

Audio must therefore remain causally connected to animal state.

---

# 3. AUDIO IDENTITY

Important individual animals may have persistent audio characteristics.

Identity may include:

- pitch tendency
- timbre
- resonance
- vocal range
- call structure
- rhythm
- breathing characteristics
- movement characteristics
- characteristic sounds

Natural variation is permitted.

Identity drift is not.

---

# 4. SPECIES

Animal sounds must be species-appropriate.

The same sound must not be reused indiscriminately across unrelated species.

Species baseline should determine:

- available vocalizations
- anatomical sound production
- normal frequency characteristics
- movement sounds
- communication patterns

---

# 5. SILENCE

Silence is a valid animal state.

Animals must not vocalize continuously merely because an animal
is present in the scene.

---

# 6. CONTINUITY

Audio must remain consistent across:

- frames
- shots
- scenes
- sequences
- episodes

when the animal remains in the same state.

---

# 7. CANONICAL EXCEPTION

If canon establishes extraordinary animal speech or communication,
canon takes priority over ordinary biological limitations.

---

# 8. UNKNOWN

Unknown audio information must remain UNKNOWN.

AI must not invent a canonical vocalization simply because
a sound is needed.
