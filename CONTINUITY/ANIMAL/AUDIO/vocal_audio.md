# VOCAL AUDIO

## Purpose

Defines animal-produced vocal sounds.

---

# 1. VOCALIZATION TYPES

Where applicable:

- contact call
- alarm
- warning
- distress
- aggression
- submission
- territorial
- mating
- maternal
- juvenile
- feeding
- social
- attention
- contentment
- pain
- fear

---

# 2. SPECIES CONSTRAINT

Every vocalization must be physically possible for the species.

---

# 3. INDIVIDUAL IDENTITY

Important animals may have persistent vocal characteristics.

---

# 4. NATURAL VARIATION

Vocalization may vary in:

- pitch
- duration
- intensity
- rhythm
- timing
- harmonic character

within species and individual limits.

---

# 5. STATE DEPENDENCY

Vocalization may depend on:

- fear
- pain
- fatigue
- excitement
- aggression
- social context
- reproductive state
- hunger
- environmental threat

---

# 6. SILENCE

No vocalization should be generated unless biologically,
canonically or narratively justified.

---

# 7. AI

AI-generated animal voices must not automatically become:

- human-like
- speech-like
- melodically artificial
- emotionally exaggerated
- cinematic monster sounds

unless explicitly directed or canonically required.
