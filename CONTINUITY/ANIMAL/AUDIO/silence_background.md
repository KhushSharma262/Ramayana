# SILENCE AND BACKGROUND ANIMAL AUDIO

## Purpose

Controls realistic animal sound density.

---

# 1. SILENCE

Animals may remain silent.

Do not add vocalizations solely to fill silence.

---

# 2. BACKGROUND DENSITY

Background sound may depend on:

- species
- population
- habitat
- season
- time
- weather
- human disturbance
- predator presence
- breeding season
- activity

---

# 3. NATURAL VARIATION

Background calls should vary in:

- timing
- distance
- direction
- intensity
- individual character

---

# 4. REPETITION

Avoid obvious repeated audio loops.

---

# 5. POPULATION

Background audio density should correspond to established population
and group state.

---

# 6. CONTINUITY

Background sound must not abruptly change without a causal reason.
