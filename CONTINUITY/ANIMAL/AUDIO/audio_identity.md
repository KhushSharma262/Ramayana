# ANIMAL AUDIO IDENTITY

## Purpose

Defines persistent audio identity for important individual animals.

---

# 1. IDENTITY

Potential attributes:

- vocal timbre
- pitch range
- resonance
- vocal texture
- characteristic call
- breathing character
- movement signature

---

# 2. IDENTITY STABILITY

Identity must remain recognizable across:

- shots
- scenes
- episodes
- microphones
- camera distances
- environments
- regeneration
- AI models

---

# 3. VARIATION

Natural variation is permitted.

---

# 4. DRIFT

Audio identity drift includes:

- completely different vocal character
- impossible species voice
- sudden human voice
- unexplained pitch character change
- unexplained call structure change

---

# 5. RELATION TO AI

Actual technical voice/model identity remains governed by
AI_GENERATION.

This file defines the desired animal-specific audio identity.
