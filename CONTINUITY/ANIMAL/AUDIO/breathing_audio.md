# BREATHING AUDIO

## Purpose

Defines animal breathing as an audio continuity component.

---

# 1. STATE

Breathing depends on:

- species
- activity
- rest
- sleep
- exertion
- fear
- stress
- fatigue
- temperature
- illness
- injury

---

# 2. SYNCHRONIZATION

Breathing audio must synchronize with visible physiological movement.

---

# 3. INTENSITY

Breathing may change in:

- rate
- depth
- audible intensity
- rhythm

according to physiological state.

---

# 4. CONTINUITY

A continuously present animal should not have its breathing
randomly reset between shots.

---

# 5. SILENT BREATHING

Normal breathing may be effectively inaudible at distance.

Audio should respect:

- distance
- environment
- microphone perspective
- masking
- scene dynamics

---

# 6. INJURY

Respiratory injury may produce altered breathing where biologically
and canonically appropriate.
