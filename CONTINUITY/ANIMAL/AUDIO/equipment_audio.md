# EQUIPMENT AUDIO

## Purpose

Defines animal-associated equipment sounds.

---

# 1. EQUIPMENT

Examples:

- saddle
- harness
- reins
- bells
- ornaments
- packs
- armor
- ceremonial equipment

---

# 2. MOTION

Equipment sound depends on:

- movement
- attachment
- material
- contact
- speed
- tension

---

# 3. CONTINUITY

Equipment audio must correspond to equipment state.

If equipment is removed, its sound must stop unless another source remains.

---

# 4. MATERIAL

Sound should correspond to material where established:

- leather
- metal
- wood
- cloth
- rope
- other materials

---

# 5. ANIMAL RESPONSE

Equipment may produce animal reactions where:

- weight
- pressure
- sound
- discomfort
- unfamiliarity

would plausibly affect the animal.
