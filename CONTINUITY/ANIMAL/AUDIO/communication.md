# ANIMAL COMMUNICATION

## Purpose

Defines communication through sound and other species-appropriate
signals.

---

# 1. COMMUNICATION MODEL

Stimulus
? perception
? internal state
? communication intent
? vocal/body signal
? receiver perception
? response

---

# 2. CHANNELS

Where biologically applicable:

- vocalization
- posture
- movement
- facial/physical signal
- scent
- touch
- vibration

---

# 3. SPECIES

Communication must follow species-specific capability.

---

# 4. GROUP COMMUNICATION

Communication may affect:

- nearby individuals
- family groups
- herds
- flocks
- colonies
- populations

where appropriate.

---

# 5. HUMAN LANGUAGE

Ordinary animals must not produce human-language speech
unless explicitly established.

---

# 6. DIVINE / MYTHOLOGICAL

Canonical extraordinary communication overrides ordinary biological limits.

Such communication must be classified by authority.

---

# 7. RESPONSE

A receiver should respond only if it could plausibly:

- detect
- interpret
- recognize
- react

to the signal.
