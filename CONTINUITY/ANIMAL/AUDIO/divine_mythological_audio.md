# DIVINE AND MYTHOLOGICAL ANIMAL AUDIO

## Purpose

Defines audio treatment for divine, mythological and supernatural animals.

---

# 1. BASELINE

Where appropriate:

BIOLOGICAL VOCALIZATION
+
CANONICAL EXTRAORDINARY PROPERTY

---

# 2. EXTRAORDINARY COMMUNICATION

Possible categories:

- extraordinary vocalization
- supernatural communication
- human-language speech
- divine sound
- canonical call
- supernatural auditory effect

---

# 3. AUTHORITY

Every extraordinary audio property should be classified:

DIRECT_CANON
DERIVED_CANON
TRADITIONAL_INTERPRETATION
BIOLOGICAL_RECONSTRUCTION
HISTORICAL_RECONSTRUCTION
AUDIO_RECONSTRUCTION
DIRECTORIAL_CHOICE
PRODUCTION_CHOICE
UNKNOWN
UNCERTAIN

---

# 4. NO AUTOMATIC FANTASY

A divine animal does not automatically receive:

- roaring
- thunder
- magical echoes
- glowing sound
- supernatural reverb
- human voice
- exaggerated bass

unless supported or explicitly approved.

---

# 5. SPEECH

If canon establishes human-language speech, ordinary animal vocalization
and speech must remain separate audio states.

---

# 6. TRANSFORMATION

When an animal transforms:

previous_audio_state
? transformation event
? new_audio_state

The historical audio state remains preserved.
