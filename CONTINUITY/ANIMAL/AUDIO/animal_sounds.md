# ANIMAL SOUNDS

## Purpose

Defines non-vocal biological and incidental animal sounds.

---

# 1. SUPPORTED SOUNDS

Where relevant:

- chewing
- biting
- tearing
- swallowing
- drinking
- pecking
- scratching
- grooming
- shaking
- body contact
- wing movement
- claw contact
- hoof contact
- paw contact

---

# 2. FOOD DEPENDENCY

Feeding sounds should correspond to actual food.

---

# 3. ANATOMICAL DEPENDENCY

Sound must correspond to the animal's anatomy.

---

# 4. STATE

Sounds may vary with:

- hunger
- fatigue
- injury
- food type
- activity
- environment

---

# 5. REALISM

Incidental sounds should not be exaggerated merely to announce
that an animal exists.
