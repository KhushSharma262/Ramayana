# GROUP AUDIO

## Purpose

Defines collective animal sound behavior.

---

# 1. GROUPS

Applicable to:

- herds
- flocks
- packs
- troops
- colonies
- swarms
- schools
- other social groups

---

# 2. COLLECTIVE AUDIO

Track where relevant:

- collective movement
- contact calls
- alarm
- social noise
- feeding noise
- territorial noise
- synchronized activity

---

# 3. INDIVIDUAL VARIATION

Group audio must not consist of hundreds of identical repeated calls.

Individuals should retain natural variation.

---

# 4. GROUP SIZE

Audio density may depend on:

- group size
- activity
- distance
- species
- environment
- population density

---

# 5. GROUP CONTINUITY

Group audio must correspond to the established group state.

---

# 6. BACKGROUND POPULATIONS

Distant/background animals may use population-level audio continuity
when individual identification is unnecessary.
