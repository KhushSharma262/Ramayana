# PREDATOR PREY AUDIO

## Purpose

Defines audio continuity during predator-prey interactions.

---

# 1. PREDATOR STATE

Potential stages:

- unaware
- detecting
- stalking
- approaching
- pursuing
- attacking
- feeding
- retreating

---

# 2. PREY STATE

Potential stages:

- unaware
- detecting
- suspecting
- alarmed
- fleeing
- hiding
- defending
- captured
- escaping

---

# 3. AUDIO

Audio should correspond to the actual state.

---

# 4. SILENCE

Predator stalking may require reduced sound rather than dramatic
continuous vocalization.

---

# 5. ENVIRONMENT

Vegetation, terrain, water and distance influence sound.

---

# 6. CONTINUITY

A predator should not sound as though it is attacking while visually
remaining distant unless intentionally established through sound perspective.
