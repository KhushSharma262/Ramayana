# AUTHORITY

Audio authority must be identified for every extraordinary or uncertain property.

---

# DEPENDENCIES

Animal audio may depend on:

- animal identity
- species
- anatomy
- physiology
- behavior
- perception
- locomotion
- interaction
- environment
- equipment
- spatial state
- transformation

---

# GLOBAL BOUNDARY

This registry does not replace the global AUDIO registry.

It records animal-specific audio requirements and references.
