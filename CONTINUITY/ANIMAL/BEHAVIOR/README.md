# ANIMAL BEHAVIOR

## Purpose

Animal Behavior defines what an animal does, why it does it,
how it reacts, learns, remembers and changes over time.

# ARCHITECTURE

Behavior is divided from:

PERCEPTION
    What can the animal detect?

COGNITION
    How does it process, learn and remember?

PHYSIOLOGY
    What is its physical internal condition?

BEHAVIOR
    What does it do and why?

LOCOMOTION
    How does the body physically perform movement?

INTERACTION
    What happens between entities?

HUMAN_INTERACTION
    How does it behave toward humans?

PERFORMANCE
    How is approved behavior represented in a shot?

AI
    How is approved behavior enforced during generation?

---

# DETAIL LEVELS

Principal Animal:
full behavioral continuity.

Supporting Individual:
persistent important behavior.

Population/Background:
collective behavioral rules.

---

# CANON

Explicit canonical behavior overrides biological reconstruction.

---

# BIOLOGY

Biology explains unspecified behavior.

It cannot overwrite explicit canon.

---

# ANTHROPOMORPHISM

Animals may have genuine:

- fear
- attachment
- curiosity
- aggression
- stress
- comfort
- learning
- memory

without automatically possessing human reasoning.

---

# PERSISTENCE

Behavior does not reset between shots.

Use:

previous_behavior
? event
? change
? consequence
? duration
? current_behavior

---

# MEMORY

Important behavioral history persists.

Learned responses, fear, trust, relationships and established habits
must not disappear without cause.

---

# DIVINE / MYTHOLOGICAL

Use:

BIOLOGICAL BASELINE
+
CANONICAL EXCEPTION
+
SUPERNATURAL PROPERTY

Extraordinary behavior requires authority classification.

---

# UNKNOWN

UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN.

AI generation cannot convert an unknown behavioral fact into canon.
