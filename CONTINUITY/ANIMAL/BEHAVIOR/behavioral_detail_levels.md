# BEHAVIORAL DETAIL LEVELS

## PRINCIPAL ANIMAL

Full behavioral tracking:

- individual identity
- temperament
- motivation
- perception
- cognition
- memory
- relationships
- learned behavior
- state
- history
- consequences
- environmental response
- performance

---

## SUPPORTING INDIVIDUAL

Track:

- identity
- species
- major state
- health
- group
- important relationships
- major events
- persistent behavioral characteristics

---

## POPULATION / BACKGROUND

Track:

- species
- population
- group state
- density
- collective behavior
- ecological response

---

## BACKGROUND MICRO-ANIMALS

Use compressed behavioral rules where individual tracking
does not affect continuity.

---

# ESCALATION

A background animal becomes an individual-level entity when
the story makes its identity or behavior significant.
