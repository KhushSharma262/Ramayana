# AGGRESSION AND DEFENSE

## Purpose

Defines aggressive and defensive behavior.

---

# DISTINCTION

Aggression is not identical to defense.

Track:

- territorial defense
- offspring defense
- resource defense
- predator defense
- dominance conflict
- mating conflict
- threat response

---

# ESCALATION

Where appropriate:

warning
? threat display
? escalation
? attack
? retreat / resolution

---

# SPECIES

Behavior must reflect species-specific warning and attack patterns.

---

# CONSEQUENCE

Injury, fear, territorial change or social changes may persist afterward.
