# REST AND SLEEP BEHAVIOR

## Purpose

Defines rest and sleep behavior.

---

# STATE

Track:

- resting
- sleeping
- waking
- alerting
- returning to rest

---

# SPECIES

Sleep architecture and duration must remain species-appropriate.

---

# ENVIRONMENT

Rest may depend on:

- safety
- temperature
- shelter
- social group
- time
- environmental disturbance

---

# INTERRUPTION

A disturbance should create a behavioral transition rather than
instantaneous switching between unrelated states.
