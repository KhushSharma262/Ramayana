# ANIMAL BEHAVIOR CONTINUITY

## Purpose

Defines what an animal does, why it does it, how behavior changes,
and how behavioral consequences persist through the story.

Behavior is not merely animation.

Behavior is the result of:

SPECIES
? INDIVIDUAL
? PERCEPTION
? COGNITION
? MOTIVATION
? CURRENT STATE
? BEHAVIOR
? ACTION
? CONSEQUENCE
? MEMORY
? NEXT STATE

---

# AUTHORITY

Priority:

1. Direct Canon
2. Derived Canon
3. Traditional Interpretation
4. Approved Research
5. Biological Reconstruction
6. Historical Reconstruction
7. Behavioral Reconstruction
8. Directorial Choice
9. Production Choice
10. Unknown
11. Uncertain

Explicit canonical behavior overrides biological assumptions.

Biology explains unspecified mechanics but cannot overwrite explicit canon.

---

# CORE RULE

Behavior must have a cause where reasonably inferable.

AI must not invent arbitrary animal actions merely because a shot
would look visually interesting.

---

# BEHAVIORAL IDENTITY

Important individual animals may possess persistent behavioral identity.

This may include:

- temperament
- activity pattern
- social tendencies
- fear response
- curiosity
- aggression threshold
- attachment
- learned behavior
- preferred movement patterns
- response to specific individuals
- established habits

Individual identity remains distinct from species baseline.

---

# NATURAL VARIATION

Animals of the same species may behave differently.

Variation must remain:

- biologically plausible
- species-appropriate
- state-dependent
- causally explainable where relevant

---

# CONTINUITY

Behavior persists across:

- frames
- shots
- scenes
- sequences
- episodes

unless an event causes a change.

---

# UNKNOWN

Unknown behavior remains UNKNOWN.

No unsupported behavior becomes canon merely because an AI model generated it.
