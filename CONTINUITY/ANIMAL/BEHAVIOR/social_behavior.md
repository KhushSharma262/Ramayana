# SOCIAL BEHAVIOR

## Purpose

Defines social interaction between animals.

---

# RELATIONSHIPS

Where biologically applicable:

- parent
- offspring
- mate
- sibling
- group member
- rival
- dominant
- subordinate
- unfamiliar individual

---

# RECOGNITION

Animals may recognize individuals when species biology,
experience and context support it.

---

# RELATIONSHIP MEMORY

Established relationships persist unless changed by:

- separation
- conflict
- death
- social restructuring
- maturation
- transformation

---

# SOLITARY SPECIES

Solitary species must not be assigned unnecessary human-like
social behavior merely because another animal is nearby.
