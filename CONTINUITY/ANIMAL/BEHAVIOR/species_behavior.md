# SPECIES BEHAVIOR

## Purpose

Defines the behavioral baseline of a species.

---

# SPECIES BASELINE

Record where applicable:

- social structure
- territorial behavior
- feeding behavior
- hunting
- defense
- mating
- parental care
- grooming
- sleep
- migration
- communication
- activity cycle
- threat response
- environmental adaptation

---

# SPECIES RULE

Species-specific behavior overrides generic "animal" behavior.

---

# CONTEXT

Species behavior may vary according to:

- age
- sex
- season
- reproductive state
- environment
- food availability
- population density
- social structure

---

# INDIVIDUALIZATION

Species baseline does not determine every individual action.

Individual state, history, learning and personality may modify behavior.

---
