# INDIVIDUAL BEHAVIOR

## Purpose

Defines persistent behavioral identity of important individual animals.

---

animal_id:
species_id:
age:
sex:
social_role:
temperament:

social_profile:
curiosity_profile:
attachment_profile:
learned_behaviors:
known_associations:
behavioral_history:

---

# PERSONALITY

Personality is species-appropriate individual variation.

It must not automatically become human personality.

---

# STABILITY

Behavioral characteristics persist unless:

- development
- learning
- injury
- illness
- environmental change
- social change
- major event
- transformation

provides a cause for change.

---

# VARIATION

Daily behavior may vary without changing the underlying individual profile.
