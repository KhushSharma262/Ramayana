# ANIMAL MOTIVATION

## Purpose

Defines the immediate biological/behavioral drivers of animal action.

---

# MOTIVATIONS

Where applicable:

- survival
- food
- water
- shelter
- safety
- reproduction
- offspring
- territory
- social bonding
- curiosity
- avoidance
- play
- rest

---

# COMPETING MOTIVATIONS

Multiple motivations may exist simultaneously.

Example:

hunger
+
fear
+
offspring nearby

may produce behavior different from hunger alone.

---

# PRIORITY

Behavior should reflect the animal's current priorities.

---

# HUMANIZATION CONTROL

Motivation must remain species-appropriate.

Do not automatically convert biological motivation into human reasoning.
