# BEHAVIORAL HISTORY

## Purpose

Maintains persistent behavioral history of important animals.

---

# EVENT RECORD

Each significant event may contain:

event_id:
animal_id:
timestamp:
location:
previous_behavior:
trigger:
behavioral_change:
duration:
consequence:
memory_effect:
relationship_effect:
physiological_effect:
current_behavior:
source:
classification:
confidence:

---

# PURPOSE

Behavioral history prevents later scenes from forgetting:

- learned behavior
- fear
- trust
- relationships
- injuries
- established routines
- previous encounters
- environmental consequences
