# ENVIRONMENTAL BEHAVIOR

## Purpose

Defines how environment modifies animal behavior.

---

# VARIABLES

Consider:

- habitat
- terrain
- weather
- temperature
- time of day
- season
- food
- water
- shelter
- predators
- prey
- human disturbance
- population density

---

# ECOLOGICAL CAUSALITY

Environmental change
? animal state change
? behavioral change
? consequence

---

# PERSISTENCE

Behavioral consequences of environmental changes may persist.

---

# BOUNDARY

ENVIRONMENT defines environmental state.

BEHAVIOR defines the animal response to that state.
