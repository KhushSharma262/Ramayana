# FEAR AND STRESS BEHAVIOR

## Purpose

Defines behavioral consequences of fear, stress and perceived threat.

---

# STATES

Possible responses:

- alertness
- freezing
- avoidance
- hiding
- fleeing
- defensive behavior
- aggression
- vocalization
- group alarm
- submission

Species determines which responses are plausible.

---

# INTENSITY

Track:

- trigger
- intensity
- duration
- recovery
- repeated exposure
- learned association

---

# PERSISTENCE

Fear may persist after the original stimulus disappears
where biologically plausible.

---

# RECOVERY

Recovery should occur progressively rather than automatically
resetting between shots.
