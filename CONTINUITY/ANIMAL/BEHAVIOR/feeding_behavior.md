# FEEDING BEHAVIOR

## Purpose

Defines animal feeding behavior.

---

# STATES

Where applicable:

- searching
- locating
- approaching
- evaluating
- feeding
- defending food
- carrying food
- storing food
- stopping
- resting

---

# FOOD

Behavior depends on:

- species
- diet
- food type
- availability
- competition
- hunger
- environment

---

# CONTINUITY

An animal should not repeatedly behave as though it has not eaten
if established story state indicates otherwise.

---

# PHYSICAL STATE

Feeding may influence:

- hunger
- energy
- movement
- alertness
- social behavior
