# HUMAN-ANIMAL BEHAVIOR

## Purpose

Defines behavior toward humans.

---

# RELATIONSHIP

Track where relevant:

- unfamiliar
- familiar
- trained
- domesticated
- bonded
- fearful
- threatened
- handled
- controlled

---

# TRAINING

Training may create persistent learned behavior.

---

# COMMANDS

Human commands must only influence behavior where:

- species capability
- training
- conditioning
- relationship
- canonical knowledge

support it.

---

# CONTACT

Physical interaction may influence future behavior.

---

# MISTREATMENT

Repeated negative treatment may produce persistent avoidance,
fear or defensive behavior where biologically plausible.

---

# DOMESTICATION

Domestic behavior must not automatically be applied to wild animals.
