# BEHAVIORAL CONSEQUENCES

## Purpose

Defines effects that continue after an event.

---

# EXAMPLES

Threat
? fear
? avoidance
? altered route
? altered group relationship

Training
? learned response
? repeated behavior
? persistent association

Injury
? pain
? reduced activity
? altered movement
? social change

---

# PERSISTENCE

Consequences remain active until:

- resolved
- replaced
- naturally decayed
- medically recovered
- socially changed
- canonically reversed

---

# PROPAGATION

Consequences may affect:

- behavior
- cognition
- physiology
- locomotion
- interaction
- human interaction
- audio
- performance
- future scenes
