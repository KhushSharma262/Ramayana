# BEHAVIORAL COGNITIVE BOUNDARY

## Purpose

Prevents accidental anthropomorphism.

---

# NORMAL RULE

Animals may possess:

- perception
- emotion
- learning
- memory
- decision-making
- motivation

without possessing human-style reasoning.

---

# PROHIBITED DEFAULTS

Do not automatically give animals:

- human reasoning
- human language comprehension
- human moral reasoning
- human planning depth
- human conversational turn-taking
- human symbolic thinking

unless canonically or biologically justified.

---

# EXTRAORDINARY INTELLIGENCE

Extraordinary intelligence must be classified as:

DIRECT_CANON
DERIVED_CANON
TRADITIONAL_INTERPRETATION
or another explicitly recorded authority.

---

# BOUNDARY

COGNITION explains processing.

BEHAVIOR records the resulting behavioral state/action.
