# GROUP BEHAVIOR

## Purpose

Defines collective animal behavior.

---

# INDIVIDUALITY

Individuals retain individual behavior inside groups.

---

# EMERGENT BEHAVIOR

Group behavior should emerge from:

- individual perception
- communication
- proximity
- social relationships
- environmental conditions
- threat
- movement

rather than identical synchronized actions.

---

# GROUP STATES

Examples:

- resting
- feeding
- travelling
- migrating
- alarmed
- fleeing
- defending
- dispersing
- regrouping

---

# BACKGROUND

Anonymous background animals may use collective behavioral rules
without full individual tracking.
