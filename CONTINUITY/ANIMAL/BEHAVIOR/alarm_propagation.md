# ALARM PROPAGATION

## Purpose

Defines propagation of threat information through animal groups.

---

# MODEL

THREAT
? INDIVIDUAL DETECTION
? SIGNAL
? NEARBY DETECTION
? GROUP STATE CHANGE
? COLLECTIVE RESPONSE

---

# LIMITS

Propagation depends on:

- distance
- sensory capability
- visibility
- sound
- species communication
- group structure
- reaction latency

---

# CONTINUITY

Animals outside the original camera frame may respond if they
could plausibly receive the signal.
