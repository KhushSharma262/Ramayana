# AGE RELATED BEHAVIOR

## Purpose

Defines behavioral development across an animal's life.

---

# STAGES

Where applicable:

- newborn
- juvenile
- adolescent
- adult
- mature adult
- elderly

---

# DEVELOPMENT

Behavior may change with:

- experience
- physical capability
- learning
- social role
- reproductive maturity

---

# CONTINUITY

Age-related behavioral changes must be gradual and persistent.

---

# NO RESET

A mature animal must not randomly revert to juvenile behavior
without a cause.
