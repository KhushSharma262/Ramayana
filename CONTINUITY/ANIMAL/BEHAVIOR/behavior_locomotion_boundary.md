# BEHAVIOR AND LOCOMOTION BOUNDARY

## BEHAVIOR

Defines:

WHAT the animal chooses/attempts to do
and WHY.

---

## LOCOMOTION

Defines:

HOW the body physically performs movement.

---

# EXAMPLE

Behavior:

"Flee from predator."

Locomotion:

"Species-specific running gait with acceleration,
foot placement, balance and momentum."

---

# TRANSITION

Behavioral change must produce a plausible locomotion transition.

Instantaneous teleportation or impossible pose changes are prohibited.
