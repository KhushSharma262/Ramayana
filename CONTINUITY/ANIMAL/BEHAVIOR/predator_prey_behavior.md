# PREDATOR PREY BEHAVIOR

## Purpose

Defines behavioral continuity during predator-prey interactions.

---

# PREDATOR STATES

SEARCH
? DETECT
? ASSESS
? STALK
? APPROACH
? PURSUE
? ATTACK
? FEED
? REST / RETREAT

Not every interaction uses every state.

---

# PREY STATES

UNAWARE
? DETECT
? ASSESS
? ALERT
? FLEE / HIDE / DEFEND
? ESCAPE / INJURY / CAPTURE

---

# PREDATOR VARIABLES

Behavior may depend on:

- hunger
- energy
- risk
- opportunity
- previous experience
- prey condition
- environment

---

# PREY VARIABLES

Behavior may depend on:

- threat distance
- detection certainty
- escape route
- group protection
- injury
- offspring
- environment

---

# MEMORY

Previous encounters may influence future behavior when plausible.
