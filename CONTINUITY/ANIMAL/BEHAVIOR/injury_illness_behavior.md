# INJURY AND ILLNESS BEHAVIOR

## Purpose

Defines behavioral consequences of physical injury and illness.

---

# EFFECTS

May include:

- reduced activity
- altered gait
- avoidance
- defensive behavior
- reduced feeding
- hiding
- altered social behavior
- increased rest
- distress

only where species-appropriate.

---

# PERSISTENCE

Behavioral effects persist according to injury recovery.

---

# RECOVERY

Recovery should be progressive.

---

# PERMANENT CHANGE

Permanent injury may create permanent behavioral changes.

---

# STATE LINK

PHYSIOLOGY determines physical condition.

BEHAVIOR determines behavioral consequences.
