# EQUIPMENT DETAIL LEVELS

## Principal Equipment

Track in full:

- identity
- dimensions
- material
- structure
- components
- attachment
- fit
- condition
- damage
- repair
- ownership
- history
- movement
- animal effects
- environmental interaction
- audio
- AI constraints

## Supporting Equipment

Track:

- identity
- type
- owner/animal
- attachment
- condition
- major changes
- major damage

## Background Equipment

Track only information required for:

- visual continuity
- physical plausibility
- spatial continuity
- narrative relevance

Detail reduction must never contradict established state.
