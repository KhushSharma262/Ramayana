# EQUIPMENT CORE

## Purpose

Equipment defines physical objects that are worn by, attached to, carried by,
used by, or directly associated with an animal.

Examples:

- saddles
- bridles
- reins
- harnesses
- collars
- ropes
- chains
- packs
- bags
- baskets
- blankets
- coverings
- armor
- protective equipment
- ceremonial equipment
- training equipment
- restraint equipment

Equipment is a continuity domain, not merely a visual decoration system.

## Master Model

EQUIPMENT IDENTITY
→ OWNERSHIP
→ POSSESSION
→ ATTACHMENT
→ FIT
→ USE
→ ANIMAL EFFECT
→ MOVEMENT
→ INTERACTION
→ WEAR / DAMAGE
→ REPAIR / CHANGE
→ NEXT STATE

## Authority

The global CONTINUITY/state_ledger remains the ultimate continuity authority.

Equipment provides equipment-specific truth and interfaces with the global ledger.

## Canon Principle

Canon defines what happened.

Historical, archaeological, technical and visual reconstruction may explain
unspecified equipment characteristics but may not silently become canon.
