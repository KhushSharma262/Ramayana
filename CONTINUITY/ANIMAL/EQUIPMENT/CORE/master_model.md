# EQUIPMENT MASTER MODEL

Every important equipment item should be representable through:

- identity
- type
- owner
- current possessor
- associated animal
- previous users
- lifecycle state
- attachment
- fit
- dimensions
- material
- structure
- components
- condition
- wear
- residue
- biomechanics
- animal effects
- movement
- environmental interaction
- audio characteristics
- damage
- repair history
- storage
- transfer
- loss/recovery
- transformation
- canon/research authority
- references
- AI constraints
- validation
- approval
