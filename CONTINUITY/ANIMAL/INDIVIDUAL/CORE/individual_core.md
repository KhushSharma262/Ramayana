# INDIVIDUAL ANIMAL CORE

## Purpose

Defines the persistent identity and individual continuity of a specific animal.

The Individual domain answers:

WHO IS THIS SPECIFIC ANIMAL?

It does not replace species biology, anatomy, physiology, cognition, behavior,
locomotion, group state, population state, human interaction, equipment,
events, ecology, environment, physics, spatial continuity or AI generation.

## Master Model

SPECIES
→ INDIVIDUAL IDENTITY
→ INDIVIDUAL BASELINE
→ CURRENT STATE
→ EVENT / EXPERIENCE
→ CONSEQUENCE
→ PERSISTENCE
→ NEXT STATE

## Core Principle

An individual animal is a persistent entity, not merely a visual instance.

Identity survives ordinary changes in:
- age
- location
- group
- health
- appearance
- equipment
- behavior
- training
- relationships
- environment

## Authority

Global STATE_LEDGER remains the ultimate continuity authority.
