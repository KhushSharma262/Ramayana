# ANIMAL / INDIVIDUAL

## Purpose

The Individual domain defines the persistent identity of a specific animal.

## Core Question

WHO IS THIS SPECIFIC ANIMAL?

## Master Model

SPECIES
→ INDIVIDUAL IDENTITY
→ INDIVIDUAL BASELINE
→ CURRENT STATE
→ EVENT / EXPERIENCE
→ CONSEQUENCE
→ PERSISTENCE
→ NEXT STATE

## Most Important Rule

An individual animal is not merely a species with a different appearance.

It has:

- persistent identity
- individual anatomy
- individual appearance
- age
- health
- life history
- lineage
- temperament
- experiences
- relationships
- group history
- location history
- equipment history
- cognitive history
- behavioral history
- transformation history

## Identity Rule

Same individual:
same animal_id.

Replacement:
new animal_id.

Transformation:
same animal_id when continuity establishes identity preservation, with a new form/state lineage.

Visual similarity alone never establishes identity.

## Authority

Global STATE_LEDGER remains ultimate continuity authority.

EQUIPMENT = WHAT EQUIPMENT IS ATTACHED/ASSOCIATED

EVENT = WHAT HAPPENED

ECOLOGY = HOW IT RELATES TO THE ECOSYSTEM

ENVIRONMENT = PHYSICAL SURROUNDINGS

## Continuity

No reset without cause.

No identity replacement without an explicit replacement event.

No injury disappearance without healing/correction.

No scar disappearance without cause.

No group change without membership transition.

No unexplained location teleportation.

No transformation treated as ordinary appearance variation.

UNKNOWN ≠ UNCHANGED.

## AI Principle

AI-generated images are representations of the individual.

They do not define the individual's truth.

Approved continuity state must control generation.
