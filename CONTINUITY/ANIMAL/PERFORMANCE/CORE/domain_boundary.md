# PERFORMANCE DOMAIN BOUNDARY

PERFORMANCE:
How the animal's established state is visibly and audibly performed.

INDIVIDUAL:
Who the animal is.

PERCEPTION:
What the animal can detect.

COGNITION:
How the animal processes information.

BEHAVIOR:
What action or behavioral state exists.

PHYSIOLOGY:
Biological condition.

LOCOMOTION:
Movement mechanics.

INTERACTION:
Interaction causality.

GROUP:
Collective behavior.

HUMAN_INTERACTION:
Human-animal relationship.

ENVIRONMENT:
Physical surroundings.

SPATIAL:
Geometry.

PHYSICS:
Physical simulation.

LIGHTING:
Illumination response.

AUDIO:
Final sound.

CAMERA:
Viewpoint and framing.

AI_GENERATION:
Technical generation.

PERFORMANCE must not duplicate these systems.
