# REGISTRY PRINCIPLES

1. Every tracked entity receives a stable ID.
2. IDs must not be silently reused.
3. Replacement entities receive new IDs.
4. Registry entries point to authoritative records.
5. Historical records remain discoverable.
6. Superseded records remain auditable.
7. Deleted-looking entities are not silently erased.
8. UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN.
9. Registry presence does not mean factual approval.
10. A generated AI output does not become truth merely because it is registered.
