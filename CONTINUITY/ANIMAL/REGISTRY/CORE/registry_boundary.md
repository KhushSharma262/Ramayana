# REGISTRY DOMAIN BOUNDARY

REGISTRY owns:

IDs
indexes
cross-references
record locations
relationships between records
version pointers
approval pointers
dependency pointers
search metadata
provenance pointers
audit pointers
status indexes

REGISTRY does NOT own:

canonical truth
animal identity truth
anatomy
physiology
behavior
ecology
environment
physics
AI generation truth
final QA truth

Those remain with their authoritative domains.
