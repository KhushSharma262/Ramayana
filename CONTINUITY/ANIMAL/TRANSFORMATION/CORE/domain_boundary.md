# TRANSFORMATION DOMAIN BOUNDARY

TRANSFORMATION:
What changed the animal from one form/state to another?

STATE:
What is the animal's current condition?

ANATOMY:
What physical structure exists in the current form?

PHYSIOLOGY:
How does the transformed body function?

COGNITION:
How does the transformed animal process information?

BEHAVIOR:
How does it behave?

LOCOMOTION:
How does it move?

IDENTITY:
Who is the entity?

EVENT:
What happened and when?

AI_GENERATION:
How is the transformation technically rendered?

Transformation coordinates the change without duplicating those domains.
