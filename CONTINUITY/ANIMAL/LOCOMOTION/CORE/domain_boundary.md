# LOCOMOTION DOMAIN BOUNDARY

LOCOMOTION:
Animal movement mechanics and movement-state continuity.

ANATOMY:
Physical structures that produce movement.

PHYSIOLOGY:
Current biological capacity, fatigue and bodily state.

BEHAVIOR:
Why / what the animal chooses to do.

COGNITION:
Perception, decision and planning.

SPATIAL:
Coordinates and geometry.

PHYSICS:
Forces, momentum, gravity, collision and simulation.

ENVIRONMENT:
Terrain and physical surroundings.

GROUP:
Collective movement.

EQUIPMENT:
Load, harness and attached equipment.

AI_GENERATION:
Technical generation.

LOCOMOTION must reference these systems rather than duplicate their authoritative truth.
