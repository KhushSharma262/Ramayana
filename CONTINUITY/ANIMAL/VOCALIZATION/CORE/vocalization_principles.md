# VOCALIZATION PRINCIPLES

1. Species determines the baseline vocal repertoire.
2. Individual animals may have stable individual variation.
3. Age can change vocalization.
4. Sex can affect vocalization where biologically supported.
5. Health can alter sound.
6. Injury can alter sound.
7. Stress can alter sound.
8. Context determines call usage.
9. Silence is also a valid state.
10. Non-vocalization is not automatically an error.
11. Human speech is prohibited unless canonically supported.
12. Mythological/divine exceptions require authority.
13. AI-generated sound must not invent unsupported animal communication.
