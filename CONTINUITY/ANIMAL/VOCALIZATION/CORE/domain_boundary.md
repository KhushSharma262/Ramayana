# VOCALIZATION DOMAIN BOUNDARY

VOCALIZATION:
What sound does the animal produce and why?

AUDIO:
How the final sound is recorded, mixed, spatialized and delivered.

PHYSIOLOGY:
Whether the animal's body can physically produce the sound.

PERCEPTION:
Whether the animal can detect a sound.

COGNITION:
How the animal interprets it.

BEHAVIOR:
What the animal does before/during/after vocalization.

PERFORMANCE:
How vocalization is expressed in a particular shot.

AI_GENERATION:
How the sound is technically generated.

Vocalization defines animal vocal truth.
