# ANIMAL / VOCALIZATION

## Purpose

Defines the complete continuity system for animal vocalization.

## Master Model

VOCAL ANATOMY
→ PHYSIOLOGY
→ INTERNAL STATE
→ PERCEPTION
→ MOTIVATION
→ VOCAL DECISION
→ VOCAL PRODUCTION
→ SOUND
→ PROPAGATION
→ RECEIVER PERCEPTION
→ RESPONSE
→ CONSEQUENCE
→ PERSISTENCE

## Critical Boundary

VOCALIZATION defines:

what the animal can vocalize
what it vocalizes
why it vocalizes
when it vocalizes
how its individual vocal identity behaves

AUDIO defines final production and playback.

## Humanization Rule

Animals do NOT automatically:

speak human language
use human sentence structure
laugh like humans
cry like humans
communicate human concepts
produce human emotional performances

unless canonically or scientifically justified.

## Silence

Silence is a valid continuity state.

Do not add animal sounds merely because a shot feels empty.

## Identity

Species baseline + individual vocal identity + current state
determine the sound.

## Continuity

The same animal must remain recognizably vocally consistent across:

frame
shot
scene
sequence
episode
cross-episode

while allowing bounded natural variation.

## Canon

Exact textual claims are distinguished from:

traditional interpretation
scientific reconstruction
historical reconstruction
visual reconstruction
directorial choice
production choice
unknown

## AI

AI_GENERATION renders the established vocal truth.

It does not determine:

species
call meaning
identity
state
canon
biology

## Final Principle

Animal vocalization must sound like the specific animal that exists
in the story, in its current physical, emotional, social, environmental,
temporal and canonical state.
