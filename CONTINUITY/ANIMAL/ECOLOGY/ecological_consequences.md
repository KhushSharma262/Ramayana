# ECOLOGICAL CONSEQUENCES

## Purpose

Defines downstream effects of ecological events.

---

# CORE MODEL

EVENT
? ECOLOGICAL CHANGE
? RESOURCE EFFECT
? POPULATION EFFECT
? SPECIES RELATIONSHIP EFFECT
? INDIVIDUAL EFFECT
? BEHAVIOR / PHYSIOLOGY EFFECT
? FUTURE ECOLOGICAL STATE

---

# EXAMPLES

Drought
? reduced water
? reduced prey
? predator pressure
? migration
? altered population distribution

Fire
? habitat loss
? food reduction
? animal displacement
? competition
? recovery

---

# PERSISTENCE

Consequences remain active until resolved.
