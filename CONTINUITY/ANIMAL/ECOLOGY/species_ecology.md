# SPECIES ECOLOGY

## Purpose

Defines ecological requirements and relationships of a species.

---

# ATTRIBUTES

Where relevant:

- habitat preference
- food requirements
- water requirements
- temperature tolerance
- shelter requirements
- nesting requirements
- breeding conditions
- territory
- migration
- predator relationships
- prey relationships
- competitors
- ecological role

---

# DISTRIBUTION

Species distribution should correspond to ecological requirements
unless canon establishes an exception.

---

# CANONICAL EXCEPTION

Explicit canonical presence overrides ordinary ecological expectations.

The exception must be recorded.

---

# QA

Flag ecological combinations that are biologically incompatible
unless supported by canon or approved reconstruction.
