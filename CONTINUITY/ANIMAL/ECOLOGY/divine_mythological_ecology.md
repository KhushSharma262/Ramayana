# DIVINE AND MYTHOLOGICAL ECOLOGY

## Purpose

Defines ecological treatment of extraordinary ecosystems.

---

# BASE MODEL

BIOLOGICAL ECOLOGY BASELINE
+
CANONICAL ECOLOGICAL EXCEPTION
+
SUPERNATURAL PROPERTY

---

# POSSIBLE EXCEPTIONS

Only where supported:

- supernatural abundance
- extraordinary species presence
- impossible ecological relationship
- divine ecological influence
- supernatural resource
- extraordinary habitat
- canonical ecological transformation

---

# RULE

Divine animals may still participate in ordinary ecological systems.

---

# NO AUTOMATIC FANTASY

Mythological setting does not automatically justify:

- unlimited animals
- impossible food chains
- arbitrary species mixing
- supernatural abundance
- impossible habitats

---

# AUTHORITY

Extraordinary ecological properties require explicit classification.
