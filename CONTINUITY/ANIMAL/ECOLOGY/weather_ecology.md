# WEATHER ECOLOGY

## Purpose

Defines short-term weather effects on ecosystems.

---

# WEATHER

Examples:

- rain
- storm
- wind
- heat
- cold
- drought conditions
- flooding
- extreme weather

---

# EFFECTS

Weather may alter:

- animal activity
- food availability
- water
- movement
- shelter
- population distribution
- migration
- habitat condition

---

# PERSISTENCE

Major weather events may produce longer-term ecological effects.

---

# BOUNDARY

ENVIRONMENT records physical weather.

ECOLOGY records ecological consequences.
