# DISEASE AND PARASITE ECOLOGY

## Purpose

Defines ecological disease and parasite relationships.

---

# DISEASE FACTORS

Where relevant:

- population density
- contact
- species
- water
- environment
- vectors
- season
- mobility

---

# PARASITES

Track where ecologically relevant:

- host species
- parasite species
- transmission
- prevalence
- environmental conditions

---

# OUTBREAK

An outbreak may affect:

- population
- mortality
- reproduction
- migration
- behavior
- physiology

---

# DOMAIN BOUNDARY

PHYSIOLOGY:
Individual disease condition.

ECOLOGY:
Population/environmental disease dynamics.
