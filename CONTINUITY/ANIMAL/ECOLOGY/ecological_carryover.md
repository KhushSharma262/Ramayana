# ECOLOGICAL CARRYOVER

## Purpose

Prevents ecological state from resetting between scenes.

---

# PERSISTENT STATES

Examples:

- drought
- flood
- depleted resource
- altered population
- migration
- habitat damage
- predator decline
- prey increase
- human disturbance
- disease outbreak

---

# RULE

A later scene inherits the approved ecological state of the world.

---

# RESET PROHIBITION

Ecological conditions cannot silently return to baseline.

A reset requires:

- recovery event
- environmental change
- passage of sufficient time
- canonical event
- approved production decision
