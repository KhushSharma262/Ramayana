# ECOLOGICAL RESOURCES

## Purpose

Tracks resources relevant to animal ecology.

- shelter

- breeding sites
- territory
- mineral resources
- other species-dependent resources

# REGENERATION

Resources may recover according to:

- biological regeneration
- rainfall
- season
- environmental conditions
- explicit production event
