# HUMAN ECOLOGICAL IMPACT

## Purpose

Defines ecological consequences of human activity.

---

# ACTIVITIES

Where relevant:

- hunting
- agriculture
- settlement
- domestication
- construction
- roads
- barriers
- livestock
- warfare
- resource extraction
- habitat modification

---

# CONSEQUENCES

May affect:

- habitat
- food
- water
- population
- migration
- predator-prey relationships
- competition
- disease
- animal presence

---

# PERSISTENCE

Major ecological effects remain until reversed or naturally recovered.

---

# HISTORICAL CONTEXT

Historical ecological reconstruction must be clearly classified.
