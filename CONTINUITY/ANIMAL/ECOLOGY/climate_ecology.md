# CLIMATE ECOLOGY

## Purpose

Defines long-term climatic constraints on animal ecology.

---

# CLIMATE

Consider:

- temperature
- rainfall
- humidity
- seasonal pattern
- long-term climatic conditions

---

# CLIMATE VS WEATHER

Climate:
long-term ecological pattern.

Weather:
short-term atmospheric condition.

---

# EFFECTS

Climate may influence:

- species distribution
- habitat suitability
- food
- water
- breeding
- migration
- population

---
