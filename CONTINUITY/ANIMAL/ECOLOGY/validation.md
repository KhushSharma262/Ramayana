### SEASON
Does ecological state match season?

### CLIMATE
Does ecological state match climate?

### MIGRATION
Are movements temporally and spatially consistent?

### HUMAN DISTURBANCE
Are human ecological effects persistent?

### TIMELINE
Does ecological history remain consistent?

### CANON
Does the state contradict explicit canon?
