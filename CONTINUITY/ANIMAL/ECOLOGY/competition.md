# ECOLOGICAL COMPETITION

## Purpose

Defines competition for shared ecological resources.

---

# TYPES

- food
- water
- shelter
- territory
- nesting sites
- breeding sites
- mates

---

# LEVELS

Competition may occur:

- within species
- between species

---

# DISTINCTION

Resource competition is not identical to individual aggression.

---

# CONSEQUENCES

Competition may affect:

- population distribution
- abundance
- migration
- reproduction
- mortality
- territory
- behavior

---

# PERSISTENCE

Major ecological competition changes remain part of ecological history.
