# CARRYING CAPACITY

## Purpose

Defines ecological limits imposed by available resources.

---

# RESOURCES

Potential limiting resources:

- food
- water
- shelter
- nesting sites
- territory
- breeding locations

---

# CAPACITY

Carrying capacity may change with:

- resource availability
- habitat area
- climate
- season
- disturbance
- disease
- predator pressure

---

# CONSEQUENCES

Resource limitation may produce:

- competition
- migration
- reduced reproduction
- mortality
- territorial conflict
- population decline

---

# UNKNOWN

Exact carrying capacity remains UNKNOWN unless supported or
reconstructed with explicit methodology.
