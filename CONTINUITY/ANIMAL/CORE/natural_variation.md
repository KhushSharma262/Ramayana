# ANIMAL NATURAL VARIATION

## PURPOSE

Separates continuity from pixel-perfect repetition.

---

# CONTINUITY DOES NOT MEAN

Every frame must be mathematically identical.

---

# ALLOWED VARIATION

Where state permits:

- posture
- breathing
- blinking
- eye movement
- ear movement
- tail movement
- fur movement
- feather movement
- minor expression
- movement timing
- environmental interaction

---

# TIGHTLY CONTROLLED

- identity
- species
- anatomy
- body proportions
- persistent injury
- established equipment
- major markings
- canonical attributes
- historical state

---

# RULE

Natural variation cannot change established facts.

---

# STATE DEPENDENCY

Variation limits may change according to:

- activity
- emotion
- environment
- fatigue
- injury
- species
- individual
