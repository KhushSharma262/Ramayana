# ANIMAL PHYSICAL PERSISTENCE

## PURPOSE

Coordinates persistent physical consequences without replacing
Anatomy or Physiology.

---

# TRACKABLE CONSEQUENCES

Where relevant:

- injury
- wound
- scar
- blood
- dirt
- dust
- mud
- wetness
- fatigue
- aging
- missing body part
- damaged equipment

---

# RULE

Physical residue persists until:

- removed
- cleaned
- healed
- repaired
- replaced
- naturally changed
- canonically changed

---

# ENVIRONMENT CONTACT

Animal contact may create:

- footprints
- displaced vegetation
- disturbed soil
- transferred dirt
- water disturbance
- biological residue

where relevant.

---

# DOMAIN BOUNDARY

ANATOMY defines body structure.

PHYSIOLOGY defines current physical condition.

CORE coordinates persistence.
