# ANIMAL REPLACEMENT

## PURPOSE

Tracks replacement without identity corruption.

---

# RULE

A replacement animal receives a NEW animal_id.

---

# PREDECESSOR

The original animal remains historically traceable.

---

# RELATIONSHIP

replacement_id:
predecessor_id:
replacement_reason:
replacement_time:
approved_inheritance:
non_inherited_attributes:

---

# INHERITANCE

A replacement may inherit only explicitly approved attributes.

Examples:

- equipment
- location role
- group role
- production role

Identity must not be silently inherited.

---

# HISTORY

Replacement never erases predecessor history.
