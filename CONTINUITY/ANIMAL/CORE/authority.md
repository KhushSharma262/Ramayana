# ANIMAL AUTHORITY

## Purpose

Defines the universal authority hierarchy for Animal information.

---

# PRIORITY

1. DIRECT_CANON
2. DERIVED_CANON
3. TRADITIONAL_INTERPRETATION
4. APPROVED_RESEARCH
5. BIOLOGICAL_RECONSTRUCTION
6. HISTORICAL_RECONSTRUCTION
7. DOMAIN_RECONSTRUCTION
8. DIRECTORIAL_CHOICE
9. PRODUCTION_CHOICE
10. UNKNOWN
11. UNCERTAIN

---

# CORE RULE

CANON DEFINES WHAT HAPPENED.

BIOLOGY / RESEARCH EXPLAINS HOW UNSPECIFIED MECHANICS COULD OCCUR.

---

# CONFLICT

Conflicting sources must be preserved.

CORE must not silently erase conflicting evidence.

---

# ATTRIBUTE-SCOPED AUTHORITY

A source may control one attribute without controlling every
other Animal attribute.

Example:

A source may establish species identity without establishing
exact coloration.

---

# PROMOTION PROHIBITION

Reconstruction must never silently become canon.

Production choice must never silently become factual source material.
