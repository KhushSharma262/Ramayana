# ANIMAL SPATIAL CONTINUITY

## PURPOSE

Coordinates Animal location across world and production levels.

---

# LEVELS

PHYSICAL LOCATION
WORLD POSITION
GROUP POSITION
SCENE POSITION
SHOT POSITION

---

# OFF-SCREEN CONTINUITY

An animal does not cease to exist merely because it is outside
the camera frame.

---

# MOVEMENT

Movement between locations requires:

origin
transition
destination
time
cause

where relevant.

---

# TELEPORTATION

Unexplained instantaneous relocation is prohibited.

Canon or explicit production decision may authorize an exception.

---

# WORLD SPACE

Animal location exists independently of camera position.
