# ANIMAL RETROACTIVE CORRECTION

## PURPOSE

Defines how historical errors are corrected without destroying history.

---

# RULE

A correction does not erase the fact that the previous state existed
in the production history.

---

# PROCESS

ERROR IDENTIFIED
? SOURCE/ROOT CAUSE
? AFFECTED DEPENDENCIES
? IMPACT ANALYSIS
? CORRECTED STATE
? DOWNSTREAM REVALIDATION
? APPROVAL
? PROPAGATION

---

# HISTORY

Original records remain preserved.

---

# PROPAGATION

Only affected future states require correction.

---

# VERSIONING

Corrected outputs receive new versions.

---

# APPROVAL

User/production approval remains authoritative.
