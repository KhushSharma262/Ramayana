# ANIMAL KNOWLEDGE CONTINUITY

## PURPOSE

Tracks what an individual animal has actually experienced,
learned or can reasonably know.

---

# KNOWLEDGE IS LOCAL

Information received by one animal does not automatically
become known by every other animal.

---

# ACQUISITION

Knowledge may arise through:

- perception
- experience
- learning
- social communication
- training
- canonical supernatural means
