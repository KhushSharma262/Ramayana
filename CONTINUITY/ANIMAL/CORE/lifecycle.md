# ANIMAL LIFECYCLE

## MODEL

UNKNOWN
? IDENTIFIED
? INTRODUCED
? ACTIVE
? STATE CHANGES
? TRANSFORMED / INJURED / AGED / MOVED
? DECEASED / REMOVED
? HISTORICAL

---

# IDENTIFIED

Animal identity is established.

---

# INTRODUCED

Animal enters the story/world continuity.

---

# ACTIVE

Animal currently exists in the world state.

---

# DECEASED

Death is normally permanent.

---

# HISTORICAL

Historical record remains accessible after death/removal.

---

# RESURRECTION

Resurrection, reanimation or equivalent reversal is a new
canonical event.

It must not silently erase death history.

---

# ENTRY / EXIT

Entering or leaving a scene is an explicit event/state transition.

---

# HISTORY

Lifecycle history remains preserved.
