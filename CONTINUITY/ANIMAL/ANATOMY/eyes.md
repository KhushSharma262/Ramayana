# EYES

## Purpose

Defines anatomical eye structure.

---

# 1. ANATOMY

Where applicable:

- eye position
- eye size
- eye shape
- iris
- pupil
- sclera
- eyelids
- surrounding structures
- cornea
- species-specific structures

---

# 2. SPECIES DIFFERENCES

Eye anatomy varies by species.

Do not default to human eyes.

---

# 3. IDENTITY

Distinctive eye structure may be an individual identity anchor.

---

# 4. ASYMMETRY

Natural asymmetry may be preserved.

---

# 5. SEPARATION

Eye anatomy is distinct from:

- eye movement
- visual perception
- attention
- stimulus response

Those are governed by PERCEPTION and BEHAVIOR.

---

# 6. AI

AI must not randomly alter:

- eye placement
- eye count
- eye shape
- pupil structure
- species-specific anatomy

---

# 7. CANON

Canonical extraordinary eye properties override ordinary biological assumptions
when explicitly established.
