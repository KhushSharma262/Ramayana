# SEXUAL DIMORPHISM

## Purpose

Defines anatomical differences between sexes where biologically or
canonically relevant.

---

# 1. APPLICABILITY

Use only when supported by:

- species biology
- canonical description
- historical evidence
- approved reconstruction

---

# 2. POSSIBLE DIFFERENCES

May include:

- size
- body proportions
- horns
- antlers
- coloration
- musculature
- specialized structures
- reproductive anatomy

---

# 3. IDENTITY

Sex-related anatomical characteristics may contribute to individual identity.

---

# 4. CAUTION

Do not infer sex from appearance when the source does not support it.

Record UNKNOWN where necessary.

---

# 5. AI

AI must not randomly introduce sexual dimorphism inconsistent with
the established animal state.
