# TAIL ANATOMY

## Purpose

Defines tail structure.

---

# 1. STRUCTURE

Where applicable:

- length
- thickness
- vertebral structure
- musculature
- covering
- shape
- attachment
- specialized structures

---

# 2. MOVEMENT

Anatomy establishes possible tail movement.

Actual movement belongs to behavior/locomotion/performance.

---

# 3. IDENTITY

Distinctive tail characteristics may become identity anchors.

---

# 4. DAMAGE

Tail injury, loss or structural change must persist.

---

# 5. AI

AI must not:

- duplicate tails
- remove tails
- change tail length
- change tail structure
- attach tail incorrectly

without an authorized continuity event.
