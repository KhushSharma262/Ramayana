# SKELETAL ANATOMY

## Purpose

Defines the structural skeletal framework of animals.

---

# 1. SKELETAL IDENTITY

Where relevant record:

- skull
- vertebral column
- ribs
- sternum
- pelvis
- forelimbs
- hindlimbs
- digits
- tail vertebrae
- wing skeleton
- fin support structures

---

# 2. SPECIES-SPECIFIC STRUCTURE

Skeleton must follow species body plan.

---

# 3. JOINTS

Record where relevant:

- joint type
- position
- range of motion
- structural limitation
- load-bearing function

---

# 4. INDIVIDUAL VARIATION

Minor natural differences may occur.

Major skeletal differences require evidence or an explicit state event.

---

# 5. GROWTH

Juvenile skeletal anatomy may differ from adult anatomy.

Growth must be progressive.

---

# 6. INJURY

Fracture, dislocation or structural damage must persist through
appropriate continuity states.

---

# 7. TRANSFORMATION

Supernatural or canonical skeletal transformation must be represented
as a transformation event.

---

# 8. AI RULE

AI cannot generate impossible skeletal configurations simply because
they produce a visually interesting pose.
