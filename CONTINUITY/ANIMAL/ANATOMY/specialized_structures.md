# SPECIALIZED STRUCTURES

## Purpose

Defines specialized anatomical structures.

---

# 1. SUPPORTED STRUCTURES

Examples:

- horns
- antlers
- tusks
- claws
- fangs
- quills
- trunk
- whiskers
- barbels
- spines
- crests
- pouches
- scent glands
- specialized beaks
- specialized feet

---

# 2. IDENTITY

Distinctive specialized structures may be major identity anchors.

---

# 3. GROWTH

Where biologically applicable, track:

- growth
- maturation
- wear
- shedding
- breakage
- regrowth

---

# 4. DAMAGE

Damage must persist.

---

# 5. CANONICAL STRUCTURES

If a canonical animal possesses an extraordinary structure,
record its textual/traditional authority.

---

# 6. SUPERNATURAL STRUCTURES

Extraordinary anatomy must not be invented merely because the animal
is divine or mythological.

---

# 7. AI

Specialized structures receive high anatomical validation priority.
