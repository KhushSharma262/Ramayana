# BODY PROPORTIONS

## Purpose

Defines proportional relationships within an animal's body.

---

# 1. PROPORTION CATEGORIES

Track where relevant:

- total body length
- body height
- shoulder/hip height
- head-to-body ratio
- neck length
- torso length
- limb length
- limb segment ratios
- paw/foot/hoof size
- tail length
- tail thickness
- wing span
- wing-to-body ratio
- chest depth
- torso width
- pelvic width
- head width
- muzzle/beak proportions

---

# 2. SPECIES PROPORTIONS

Species proportions establish the normal biological range.

They are not automatically identical between individuals.

---

# 3. INDIVIDUAL PROPORTIONS

Important individual animals may have persistent proportional characteristics.

Examples:

- unusually long neck
- broad chest
- narrow face
- large ears
- distinctive tail
- unusual but plausible body size

These become identity characteristics when sufficiently distinctive.

---

# 4. SCALE

Actual physical scale must remain consistent with:

- humans
- animals
- buildings
- vehicles
- props
- terrain
- equipment

Camera perspective may alter apparent size but not actual anatomical scale.

---

# 5. AGE

Body proportions may change through:

- growth
- maturation
- aging

Such changes must occur progressively.

---

# 6. AI PROHIBITIONS

AI must not randomly alter:

- limb ratios
- head-to-body ratio
- torso length
- animal height
- wing span
- tail length
- body thickness

between continuous shots.

---
