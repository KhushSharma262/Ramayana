# AGE-RELATED ANATOMY

## Purpose

Defines progressive anatomical change through animal development and aging.

---

# 1. DEVELOPMENTAL STATES

Where applicable:

- newborn
- infant
- juvenile
- adolescent
- adult
- mature
- elderly

Species-specific terminology may be used.

---

# 2. CHANGES

Age may affect:

- body proportions
- skeletal maturity
- musculature
- teeth
- horns
- antlers
- feathers
- fur
- coloration
- body condition
- facial structure

---

# 3. PROGRESSION

Age-related anatomical changes must be progressive.

No unexplained jumps between adjacent shots.

---

# 4. CONTINUITY

Age anatomy must agree with:

ANIMAL/INDIVIDUAL
ANIMAL/PHYSIOLOGY
ANIMAL/STATE
ANIMAL/BEHAVIOR

---

# 5. AI

AI must not randomly make an established animal younger or older.
