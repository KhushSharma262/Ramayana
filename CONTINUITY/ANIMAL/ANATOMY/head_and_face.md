# HEAD AND FACE

## Purpose

Defines animal head and facial anatomy.

---

# 1. STRUCTURES

Where applicable:

- skull
- forehead
- brow region
- cheeks
- muzzle
- nose
- nostrils
- jaw
- mouth
- lips
- chin
- ears
- eyes
- eyelids
- facial muscles
- whiskers
- beak
- facial markings

---

# 2. INDIVIDUAL IDENTITY

For important animals, facial anatomy is a major identity anchor.

Track persistent characteristics.

---

# 3. ASYMMETRY

Natural asymmetry is permitted and should be preserved where established.

---

# 4. SPECIES

Head morphology must remain species-specific.

---

# 5. EXPRESSION

Facial movement belongs partly to behavior/performance.

Anatomy defines the physical structures capable of producing that movement.

---

# 6. AI RULE

Do not humanize animal facial anatomy merely to increase apparent emotion.
