# MUSCULATURE

## Purpose

Defines major visible and functional musculature relevant to animal anatomy.

---

# 1. SPECIES

Musculature follows species-specific body mechanics.

Do not apply human muscular anatomy to animals.

---

# 2. MAJOR REGIONS

Where applicable:

- neck
- shoulders
- chest
- back
- abdomen
- pelvis
- forelimbs
- hindlimbs
- jaw
- tail
- wings

---

# 3. BODY CONDITION

Visible musculature may vary according to:

- age
- nutrition
- activity
- fitness
- illness
- fatigue
- body condition

---

# 4. MOVEMENT CONNECTION

Musculature must correspond to:

- gait
- jumping
- climbing
- flying
- swimming
- running
- carrying loads

---

# 5. PHYSIOLOGY CONNECTION

Changes in physiological state may alter visible muscular behavior.

---

# 6. AI RULE

AI must not exaggerate musculature beyond species anatomy without explicit
production direction.
