# WINGS AND FLIGHT ANATOMY

## Purpose

Defines anatomical flight structures.

---

# 1. APPLICABILITY

Used for:

- birds
- bats
- insects
- other biological flyers
- mythological flyers

where applicable.

---

# 2. STRUCTURE

Track where relevant:

- wing attachment
- wing bones
- joints
- primary feathers
- secondary feathers
- membrane
- span
- wing area
- tail flight structures

---

# 3. FOLDING

Wing folding must remain anatomically plausible.

---

# 4. INDIVIDUAL

Distinctive wing structure may identify an individual.

---

# 5. DIVINE / MYTHOLOGICAL

Divine flying animals may have canonical structures beyond ordinary
biological expectations.

Such features must be explicitly classified.

---

# 6. AI PROHIBITIONS

Prevent:

- extra wings
- missing wings
- impossible attachment
- impossible joints
- impossible folding
- inconsistent span
- random feather duplication
- malformed flight structures

---

# 7. MOVEMENT

Flight movement belongs to LOCOMOTION.

Wing anatomy establishes its structural constraints.
