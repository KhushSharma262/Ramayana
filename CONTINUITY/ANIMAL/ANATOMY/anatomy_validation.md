# ANATOMY VALIDATION

## Purpose

Systematic validation of animal anatomy.

---

# 1. VALIDATION CHAIN

SPECIES
? BODY PLAN
? SKELETON
? MUSCULATURE
? HEAD
? EYES
? EARS
? MOUTH
? SURFACE COVERING
? LIMBS
? TAIL
? SPECIALIZED STRUCTURES
? PROPORTIONS
? INDIVIDUAL FEATURES
? CURRENT ANATOMICAL STATE

---

# 2. SPECIES CHECK

Confirm:

- correct species
- correct body plan
- correct anatomical class
- correct specialized structures

---

# 3. STRUCTURAL CHECK

Confirm:

- correct limb count
- correct appendages
- correct joints
- correct body regions
- correct skeletal structure
- correct major musculature

---

# 4. INDIVIDUAL CHECK

Confirm:

- facial identity
- body proportions
- markings
- scars
- specialized structures
- distinctive features

---

# 5. TEMPORAL CHECK

Confirm anatomical state matches:

- age
- previous events
- injury
- healing
- transformation
- reproductive state where relevant

---

# 6. AI ARTIFACT CHECK

Reject or repair:

- extra limbs
- missing limbs
- fused limbs
- duplicate body parts
- impossible joints
- impossible eyes
- incorrect ears
- incorrect mouth
- incorrect horns
- incorrect antlers
- incorrect wings
- impossible anatomy

---

# 7. SCALE CHECK

Verify animal scale against the established world.

---

# 8. APPROVAL

Possible results:

PASS
CONDITIONALLY_APPROVED
CHANGES_REQUIRED
REJECTED
UNKNOWN
UNRESOLVED

---

# 9. FINAL RULE

An anatomically attractive image is not automatically anatomically correct.

Correctness requires consistency with the authoritative animal state.
