# EARS

## Purpose

Defines external and relevant internal ear anatomy.

---

# 1. STRUCTURE

Track where applicable:

- ear position
- size
- shape
- cartilage
- external opening
- internal structures
- orientation
- asymmetry

---

# 2. SPECIES

Ear anatomy must follow species.

Animals without external ears must not be given mammalian-style ears.

---

# 3. MOVEMENT LIMITS

For mobile ears, define anatomical limits.

Behavior may move the ear only within physically possible ranges.

---

# 4. IDENTITY

Distinctive ears may function as identity anchors.

---

# 5. PERCEPTION

Ear anatomy supports hearing but does not itself define hearing behavior.

Hearing belongs to PERCEPTION.
