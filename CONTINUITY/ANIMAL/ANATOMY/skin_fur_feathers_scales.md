# SKIN, FUR, FEATHERS AND SCALES

## Purpose

Defines the anatomical external covering of animals.

---

# 1. COVERING TYPES

Support:

- skin
- fur
- hair
- feathers
- scales
- shell
- plates
- spines
- hide
- specialized coverings

---

# 2. ANATOMY VS APPEARANCE

Structural covering is anatomical.

Color, temporary arrangement, wetness, dirt and lighting response
are appearance/state characteristics.

---

# 3. FUR

Where applicable track:

- density
- length
- direction
- distribution
- undercoat
- species-specific structure

---

# 4. FEATHERS

Where applicable track:

- feather regions
- feather structure
- primary feathers
- secondary feathers
- body feathers
- tail feathers
- molt state

---

# 5. SCALES

Where applicable track:

- scale type
- distribution
- size
- arrangement
- specialized structures

---

# 6. MARKINGS

Persistent markings may include:

- spots
- stripes
- patches
- scars
- pigmentation
- distinctive patterns

These may function as identity anchors.

---

# 7. TEMPORARY CONDITIONS

Temporary conditions include:

- wetness
- mud
- dust
- blood
- debris
- ruffled fur
- displaced feathers

These belong to current state and must not become permanent anatomy.

---

# 8. SEASONAL CHANGE

Seasonal shedding, coat change or feather change must be treated
as biological state rather than unexplained identity change.
