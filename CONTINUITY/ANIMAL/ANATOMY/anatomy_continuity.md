# ANATOMY CONTINUITY

## Purpose

Defines the persistent anatomical structure of animals throughout the
Ramayana production.

Anatomy describes what an animal physically is.

It is the structural foundation for physiology, locomotion, behavior,
performance, interaction and AI generation.

---

# 1. AUTHORITY

Anatomical information follows:

1. Explicit canonical description
2. Approved canonical interpretation
3. Approved research
4. Species biological knowledge
5. Historical reconstruction
6. Biological reconstruction
7. Visual reconstruction
8. Production decision
9. Unknown / Uncertain

Canon overrides biological expectation when explicit.

Biology fills gaps only where canon is silent.

---

# 2. ANATOMY VS STATE

Stable anatomy:

- skeletal structure
- body plan
- species morphology
- normal body proportions
- permanent markings
- characteristic structures
- normal limb arrangement
- normal head structure

State-dependent anatomy:

- swelling
- injury
- wounds
- temporary deformation
- pregnancy-related changes
- severe weight change
- disease-related physical changes
- healing
- age progression

State-dependent changes must not silently redefine baseline anatomy.

---

# 3. IDENTITY

Important individual animals have persistent anatomical identity.

Individual anatomy may include:

- body proportions
- facial structure
- skull characteristics
- eye structure
- ear structure
- muzzle/beak
- markings
- coat/feather/scale pattern
- limb proportions
- tail
- horns
- antlers
- tusks
- scars
- distinctive structures

---

# 4. SPECIES BASELINE

Every animal must first be interpreted through its species body plan.

Species baseline establishes:

- normal body structure
- normal number of limbs
- normal appendages
- normal proportions
- normal skeletal arrangement
- normal surface covering
- normal specialized structures

Individual anatomy is derived from this baseline unless an explicit
difference is established.

---

# 5. NATURAL VARIATION

Natural individual variation is allowed.

Examples:

- size within normal range
- facial differences
- body condition
- coat variation
- feather variation
- minor asymmetry
- muscle development
- horn/antler variation

Variation must remain biologically and canonically permissible.

---

# 6. AI CONTINUITY

AI must not silently alter:

- species
- body plan
- limb count
- skeletal structure
- facial architecture
- permanent markings
- specialized structures
- age-related anatomy
- established individual anatomy

unless an actual continuity event permits it.

---
