# INJURY ANATOMY

## Purpose

Defines anatomical changes caused by injury.

---

# 1. STATE MODEL

NORMAL ANATOMY
? INJURY EVENT
? ANATOMICAL DAMAGE
? ACUTE STATE
? TREATMENT
? HEALING
? RECOVERY
? PERMANENT CHANGE WHERE APPLICABLE

---

# 2. DAMAGE TYPES

May include:

- wound
- fracture
- dislocation
- swelling
- bruising
- tissue damage
- burns
- bite damage
- puncture
- laceration
- missing structure
- scar

---

# 3. PERSISTENCE

An injury does not disappear between shots without a causal event.

---

# 4. HEALING

Healing must be progressive and biologically/canonically appropriate.

---

# 5. PERMANENT CHANGE

Permanent anatomical consequences become part of the individual's
subsequent state.

---

# 6. AI

AI must preserve established injury anatomy during regeneration.

A new generation seed does not erase an injury.
