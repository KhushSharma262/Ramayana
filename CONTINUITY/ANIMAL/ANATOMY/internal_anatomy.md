# INTERNAL ANATOMY

## Purpose

Defines internal anatomy only to the level necessary for continuity,
storytelling, injury, transformation or production.

---

# 1. SYSTEMS

Where relevant:

- skeletal
- muscular
- nervous
- cardiovascular
- respiratory
- digestive
- reproductive
- endocrine
- sensory

---

# 2. DETAIL LEVEL

Internal anatomy should scale with production need.

Normal exterior shot:
minimal internal representation.

Medical/injury/transformation scene:
detailed internal representation where required.

---

# 3. EXTERNAL CONSEQUENCES

Internal state may produce visible consequences:

- swelling
- bleeding
- altered breathing
- altered posture
- altered movement
- weakness
- pain response

---

# 4. AUTHORITY

Internal anatomy follows:

canon where specified
+
scientific reconstruction where unspecified.

---

# 5. AI

AI must not invent visible internal structures that contradict
the established anatomy.

---

# 6. BOUNDARY

Detailed medical anatomy must not automatically be added to every
animal record when it has no production relevance.
