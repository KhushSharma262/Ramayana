# RELATIONSHIP STATE

Tracks the evolving human–animal relationship.

Possible dimensions:

familiarity
recognition
trust
avoidance
fear
stress_association
attachment
dependency
cooperation
training_relationship
care_relationship
working_relationship
rider_relationship
keeper_relationship
history_strength

Relationship dimensions are independent.

A familiar animal is not automatically trusting.

A trained animal is not automatically affectionate.

A frightened animal is not automatically aggressive.
