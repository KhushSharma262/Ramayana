# INTERACTION IDENTITY

Every narratively or continuity-relevant interaction receives:

interaction_id
interaction_series_id
interaction_type
classification
participants
initiator
recipient
observers
start_state
current_state
end_state
cause
consequences
history
authority
confidence
status

Repeated interactions are separate interaction instances.

Recurring patterns may share an interaction_series_id.

Interaction identity survives:
- camera cuts
- scene cuts
- shot changes
- temporary interruption
- episode boundaries

unless continuity establishes that the interaction ended.
