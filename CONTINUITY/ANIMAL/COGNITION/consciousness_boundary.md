# CONSCIOUSNESS BOUNDARY

## Purpose

Prevents unsupported philosophical claims about animal consciousness.

---

# OPERATIONAL TERMS

The system may operationally track:

- awareness
- attention
- recognition
- memory
- learning
- decision-making

without asserting a particular philosophical theory of consciousness.

---

# UNKNOWN

Questions about subjective experience that cannot be established
remain UNKNOWN.

---

# NO ASSUMPTION

Do not convert observable behavior into certainty about
subjective conscious experience.
