# HABITUATION AND SENSITIZATION

## HABITUATION

Repeated harmless stimulus
? reduced response

where biologically appropriate.

---

## SENSITIZATION

Repeated threatening or significant stimulus
? increased response

where biologically appropriate.

---

# PERSISTENCE

Changes may persist and influence future behavior.

---

# FEEDBACK

Habituation/sensitization may modify:

- attention
- recognition
- fear
- decision-making
- behavior

---

# SPECIES

Mechanism and degree remain species-specific.
