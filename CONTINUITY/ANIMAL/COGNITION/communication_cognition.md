# COMMUNICATION COGNITION

## Purpose

Defines cognitive interpretation of animal communication.

---

# MODEL

SIGNAL
? PERCEPTION
? INTERPRETATION
? INTERNAL STATE
? RESPONSE

---

# SIGNAL TYPES

Where applicable:

- alarm
- contact
- maternal
- territorial
- aggression
- submission
- mating
- social
- warning

---

# INTERPRETATION

Depends on:

- species
- context
- familiarity
- signal clarity
- receiver state

---

# FAILURE

Animals may misunderstand or fail to detect ambiguous signals.

---

# EXTRAORDINARY COMMUNICATION

Supernatural communication must be separated from ordinary
species communication.
