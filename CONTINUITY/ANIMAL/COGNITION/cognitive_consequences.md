# COGNITIVE CONSEQUENCES

## Purpose

Defines downstream effects of cognitive changes.

---

# EXAMPLES

Training
? learned association
? expectation
? behavioral response

Predator encounter
? recognition
? fear memory
? avoidance

Repeated harmless stimulus
? habituation
? reduced attention
? reduced behavioral response

Environmental discovery
? spatial memory
? route preference
? future navigation

---

# PROPAGATION

Cognitive consequences may affect:

- attention
- recognition
- memory
- learning
- motivation
- decision
- behavior
- interaction
- locomotion
- performance
- audio

---

# PERSISTENCE

Consequences persist until legitimately changed.
