# CURIOSITY AND EXPLORATION

## Purpose

Defines investigation of novel stimuli.

---

# VARIABLES

Curiosity may depend on:

- species
- age
- temperament
- novelty
- risk
- hunger
- previous experience
- environmental conditions

---

# EXPLORATION

Exploration may produce:

- new spatial memory
- new associations
- habituation
- avoidance
- learned behavior

---

# NOVELTY

Repeated exposure may reduce novelty.

---

# NO HUMANIZATION

Curiosity does not imply human-style abstract interest.
