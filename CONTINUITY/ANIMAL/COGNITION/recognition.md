# RECOGNITION

## Purpose

Defines recognition of entities, locations and recurring stimuli.

---

# POSSIBLE RECOGNITION

Where biologically supported:

- humans
- handlers
- offspring
- parents
- mates
- group members
- rivals
- predators
- prey
- familiar locations

---

# MODALITY

Recognition may depend on:

- sight
- sound
- smell
- touch
- movement
- other species-specific senses

---

# RELIABILITY

Recognition is not necessarily perfect.

Factors include:

- distance
- lighting
- occlusion
- sensory conditions
- disguise
- environmental change
- familiarity

---

# PERSISTENCE

Established recognition history persists.

---

# RECOGNITION ? HUMAN IDENTIFICATION

Recognition should not automatically imply human-like conceptual understanding.
