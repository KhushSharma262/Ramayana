# ATTENTION SHIFT

## Purpose

Tracks transitions between attended stimuli.

---

# MODEL

CURRENT_FOCUS
? NEW_STIMULUS
? SALIENCE_EVALUATION
? ATTENTION_SHIFT
? NEW_FOCUS

---

# CAUSES

Potential causes:

- sudden movement
- sound
- smell
- predator
- offspring
- food
- human
- group signal
- environmental change

---

# LATENCY

Attention shifts should respect species-specific reaction time.

---

# CONTINUITY

Attention should not arbitrarily jump between unrelated stimuli
without a plausible cause.
