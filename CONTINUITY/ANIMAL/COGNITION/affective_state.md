# AFFECTIVE COGNITIVE STATE

## Purpose

Represents operational internal affective states without
claiming unsupported philosophical theories of consciousness.

---

# STATES

Where biologically supported:

- fear
- stress
- aggression
- attachment
- curiosity
- excitement
- distress
- calm
- comfort

---

# EFFECTS

Affective state may influence:

- attention
- memory
- learning
- recognition
- decision-making
- behavior

---

# PERSISTENCE

States may persist after the original stimulus.

---

# RECOVERY

Recovery should be progressive where biologically appropriate.

---

# HUMANIZATION CONTROL

Do not automatically translate animal affect into human
emotional narratives or thoughts.
