# COGNITIVE DETAIL LEVELS

## PRINCIPAL ANIMAL

Full cognitive tracking:

- attention
- perception dependencies
- recognition
- memory
- learning
- motivation
- decision-making
- social cognition
- spatial cognition
- communication interpretation
- history
- persistent associations

---

## SUPPORTING INDIVIDUAL

Track:

- species cognitive baseline
- important recognition
- major learned behavior
- major memories
- relevant social knowledge
- important cognitive events

---

## POPULATION / BACKGROUND

Use:

- species-level cognition
- group-level recognition
- collective learning where appropriate
- population-level behavioral assumptions

---

## MICRO-ANIMALS / LARGE BACKGROUND POPULATIONS

Use compressed cognitive rules unless an individual becomes narratively important.

---

# ESCALATION

A background animal becomes a detailed cognitive entity when
its identity or cognition becomes relevant to continuity.
