# SOCIAL COGNITION

## Purpose

Defines how animals process social information.

---

# CAPABILITIES

Where supported:

- individual recognition
- group recognition
- hierarchy recognition
- parent-offspring recognition
- mate recognition
- rival recognition
- social learning
- group threat assessment

---

# SPECIES

Social cognition must correspond to species social structure.

---

# RELATIONSHIPS

Social cognition can influence:

- attention
- memory
- motivation
- decision-making
- behavior

---

# HUMANIZATION

Social cognition must not automatically become human social reasoning.
