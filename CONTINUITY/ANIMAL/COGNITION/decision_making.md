# DECISION MAKING

## Purpose

Defines species-appropriate selection between possible actions.

---

# INPUTS

Potential inputs:

- threat
- food
- offspring
- social group
- territory
- fatigue
- injury
- learned experience
- environment
- previous outcomes

---

# COMPETING FACTORS

Multiple factors may compete.

---

# OUTPUT

Cognitive decision
? behavioral intention
? behavior

---

# PROBABILITY

Some decisions may be probabilistic rather than deterministic.

---

# MISTAKES

Biologically plausible mistakes are allowed.

An animal does not need perfect decision-making.

---

# HUMANIZATION

Decision-making does not imply human abstract reasoning.
