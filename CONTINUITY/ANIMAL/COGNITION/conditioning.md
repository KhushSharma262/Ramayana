# CONDITIONING

## Purpose

Defines learned associations between stimuli and responses.

---

# MODEL

STIMULUS
? ASSOCIATION
? EXPECTATION
? RESPONSE

---

# TYPES

Where supported:

- classical conditioning
- operant conditioning
- reward association
- avoidance learning
- handler/training association

---

# TRAINING

Training must be species-appropriate.

---

# STRENGTH

Associations may strengthen or weaken through repetition
and reinforcement.

---

# CONTINUITY

A trained animal should retain established training unless
there is a valid reason for loss or modification.
