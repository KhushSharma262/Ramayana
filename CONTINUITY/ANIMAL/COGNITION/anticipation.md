# ANTICIPATION AND EXPECTATION

## Purpose

Defines future-oriented cognitive behavior without automatically
assigning human-level planning.

---

# LEVELS

1. Immediate reaction
2. Learned expectation
3. Short-term anticipation
4. Species-supported planning
5. Extraordinary planning

---

# BASIS

Expectation should derive from:

- previous experience
- repeated patterns
- learned associations
- environmental cues

---

# EXTRAORDINARY PLANNING

Complex long-term planning requires explicit authority.

---

# CONTINUITY

Established expectations may influence future behavior.
