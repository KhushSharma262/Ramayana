# ATTENTION

## Purpose

Defines which available stimuli receive cognitive processing.

---

# LIMITED ATTENTION

Animals do not process every environmental stimulus equally.

Attention depends on:

- species
- motivation
- threat
- novelty
- familiarity
- hunger
- social context
- current task
- physiological state

---

# ATTENTION SHIFT

An animal may shift attention between stimuli.

---

# PRIORITY

Potential high-priority stimuli include:

- predator
- offspring
- food
- mate
- handler
- territorial threat
- sudden movement
- unusual sound

Species determines actual priority.

---

# CONTINUITY

Persistent stimuli may retain attention across cuts.

---

# DIVIDED ATTENTION

Permitted only where species capability supports it.
