# SPECIES COGNITION

## Purpose

Defines the cognitive baseline of a species.

---

# CAPABILITIES

Where biologically supported:

- sensory processing
- attention
- recognition
- memory
- learning
- spatial cognition
- social cognition
- communication interpretation
- problem-solving
- anticipation
- exploration

---

# SPECIES CONSTRAINT

Not every species possesses every cognitive capability.

---

# SCALE

Cognitive capability may vary according to:

- age
- sex
- social role
- environment
- season
- health
- experience
