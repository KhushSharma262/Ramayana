# SPATIAL COGNITION

## Purpose

Defines animal-specific understanding and memory of space.

---

# POSSIBLE SPATIAL KNOWLEDGE

Where applicable:

- home range
- territory
- nest
- den
- water source
- food source
- route
- shelter
- escape route
- group location

---

# MEMORY

Spatial knowledge may persist across scenes.

---

# ENVIRONMENTAL CHANGE

When the environment changes, spatial confidence may change.

---

# UNFAMILIAR SPACE

Unfamiliar environments may produce:

- exploration
- caution
- hesitation
- altered route selection
- reduced confidence

where species-appropriate.

---

# BOUNDARY

LOCATION/SPATIAL defines world geometry.

COGNITION defines the animal's knowledge of that geometry.
