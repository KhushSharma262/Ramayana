# PHYSIOLOGY DOMAIN BOUNDARY

PHYSIOLOGY:
Current biological functioning and condition.

ANATOMY:
What structures the animal physically possesses.

PERCEPTION:
Sensory access.

COGNITION:
Processing and decision.

BEHAVIOR:
Observable actions.

LOCOMOTION:
Movement mechanics.

PERFORMANCE:
Visible expression of state.

EVENT:
Lifecycle of an event.

ENVIRONMENT:
External physical conditions.

ECOLOGY:
Organism-environment relationships.

PHYSICS:
External physical mechanics.

AI_GENERATION:
Technical implementation.

Physiology must not duplicate these domains.
