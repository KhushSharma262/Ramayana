# ANIMAL AI RULES

## Purpose

Defines the rules governing how AI systems may generate, continue, regenerate,
animate, edit, enhance, or otherwise represent animals in the Ramayana production.

This file defines animal-specific generation rules.

It does not replace the global AI_GENERATION architecture.

---

# 1. AUTHORITY

Animal AI output must follow this hierarchy:

1. Explicit canonical fact
2. Approved research finding
3. Approved animal continuity state
4. Approved species baseline
5. Approved individual animal identity
6. Approved group/population state
7. Biological reconstruction
8. Production-approved visual reconstruction
9. AI generation implementation

AI output is never authoritative merely because it looks plausible.

---

# 2. CORE PRINCIPLE

AI must generate:

AUTHORITATIVE ANIMAL STATE
+
AUTHORIZED NATURAL VARIATION

It must never generate:

AUTHORITATIVE ANIMAL STATE
+
UNAUTHORIZED STATE CHANGE

---

# 3. ANIMAL IDENTITY

AI must preserve the identity of an individual animal across:

- frames
- shots
- scenes
- sequences
- episodes
- regeneration
- model changes
- camera changes
- lighting changes
- environmental changes
- distance
- occlusion

Identity must remain stable even when appearance naturally varies.

---

# 4. SPECIES ACCURACY

AI must respect:

- species
- subspecies where established
- anatomy
- morphology
- locomotion
- sensory capabilities
- vocalization
- feeding behavior
- social behavior
- habitat
- species-specific reactions

AI must not substitute generic animal behavior for species-specific behavior.

---

# 5. INDIVIDUAL VARIATION

Animals of the same species must not automatically become visually identical.

Permitted differences may include:

- facial variation
- body variation within biological limits
- coat/feather/scale variation
- markings
- posture
- temperament
- movement style
- age
- condition
- natural asymmetry

Individual identity remains stable.

---

# 6. HUMAN REALISM

AI-generated animals must display believable:

- breathing
- blinking where biologically applicable
- eye movement
- ear movement
- head movement
- muscle tension
- weight shifting
- balance corrections
- locomotion
- contact
- inertia
- fatigue
- reaction timing
- environmental response

Animals must not appear frozen, mechanically posed, or continuously expressive like humans.

---

# 7. ANTHROPOMORPHISM CONTROL

AI must not introduce human psychology, facial expressions, gestures,
postures, or emotional performance unless supported by canon or production direction.

Animal emotion must be expressed through species-appropriate behavior.

---

# 8. PHYSICS

Generated animals must obey:

- gravity
- mass
- momentum
- inertia
- balance
- ground contact
- water interaction
- air resistance where relevant
- terrain interaction
- body mechanics
- load effects

---

# 9. PERSISTENT STATE

AI must preserve established:

- injury
- dirt
- blood
- wetness
- fatigue
- equipment
- markings
- missing/altered physical features
- environmental residue
- ownership
- group membership
- location state

No unexplained reset is permitted.

---

# 10. CANONICAL EXCEPTIONS

If canon establishes an extraordinary animal ability or event,
AI must preserve it.

Biological realism cannot override explicit canon.

If canon is silent, biological reconstruction may be used only when
identified as reconstruction.

---

# 11. UNKNOWN INFORMATION

Unknown information must remain unknown.

AI must not silently invent:

- species identity
- markings
- age
- sex
- abilities
- ancestry
- behavior
- relationships
- canonical events

unless production authority explicitly resolves the unknown.

---

# 12. GENERATION BOUNDARY

This file defines WHAT the animal generation must preserve.

The global AI_GENERATION system defines HOW the generation is technically executed.

---

# 13. FAILURE CONDITIONS

Animal AI generation fails if it produces:

- identity drift
- species drift
- anatomy errors
- impossible joints
- impossible locomotion
- extra/missing limbs
- duplicate body parts
- unexplained markings
- unexplained age changes
- state reset
- incorrect animal behavior
- humanized behavior
- incorrect scale
- physics violations
- persistent-state loss
- environmental contradiction

---

# 14. APPROVAL

No animal output becomes a continuity reference merely because it was generated.

It must pass validation and receive appropriate production approval.
