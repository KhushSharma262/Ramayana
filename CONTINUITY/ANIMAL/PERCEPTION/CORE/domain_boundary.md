# PERCEPTION DOMAIN BOUNDARY

PERCEPTION:
What information reaches the animal's sensory systems.

COGNITION:
How the animal processes, recognizes and interprets that information.

BEHAVIOR:
What the animal does.

PHYSIOLOGY:
Biological sensory condition.

ANATOMY:
Sensory organs and structures.

ENVIRONMENT:
Physical conditions affecting perception.

LIGHTING:
Actual illumination.

AUDIO:
Actual sound.

SPATIAL:
Geometry and distance.

LOCOMOTION:
Animal movement.

INTERACTION:
Interaction causality.

AI_GENERATION:
Technical visual/audio generation.

Perception must not duplicate these systems.
