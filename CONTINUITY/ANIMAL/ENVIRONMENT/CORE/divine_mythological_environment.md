# DIVINE / MYTHOLOGICAL ENVIRONMENT

Model:

PHYSICAL ENVIRONMENT BASELINE
+
CANONICAL ENVIRONMENTAL EXCEPTION
+
SUPERNATURAL PROPERTY

Extraordinary environmental behavior requires authority.

Examples may include:

- supernatural transformation
- celestial environmental properties
- canonically impossible geography
- divine environmental phenomena
- supernatural weather
- extraordinary environmental illumination where canonically required

Mythological setting alone does not justify arbitrary environmental impossibility.

Once established, supernatural environmental behavior must still maintain continuity.
