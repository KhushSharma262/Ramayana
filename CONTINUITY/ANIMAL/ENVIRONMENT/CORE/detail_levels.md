# ENVIRONMENT DETAIL LEVELS

## Principal Environment

Full:

- geometry
- terrain
- materials
- vegetation
- water
- atmosphere
- weather
- objects
- damage
- residue
- history
- transformation
- spatial state
- physical interaction

## Supporting Environment

Persistent major characteristics and important objects.

## Background Environment

Only attributes required for:

- visual continuity
- spatial plausibility
- ecological plausibility
- physics
- camera
- audio
- narrative requirements

Detail reduction must never contradict established truth.
