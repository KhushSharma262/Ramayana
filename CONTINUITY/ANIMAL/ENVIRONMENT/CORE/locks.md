# ENVIRONMENT LOCKS

## Absolute / Strong Locks

Important:

- terrain geometry
- major landforms
- important trees
- rivers
- mountains
- buildings
- bridges
- roads
- major environmental objects
- established damage
- established environmental transformations

## Controlled Variation

May vary naturally:

- small leaf movement
- minor atmospheric variation
- tiny debris movement
- subtle moisture distribution
- natural vegetation movement

Variation must remain causally plausible.

## No Silent Reset

Regeneration does not authorize environmental reset.
