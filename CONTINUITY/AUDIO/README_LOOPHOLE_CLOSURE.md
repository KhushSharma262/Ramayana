## IMPORTANT

This architecture does NOT create a second global source of truth.

Existing authoritative domains remain authoritative.

AUDIO is responsible for:

1. Translating established world state into audio requirements.
2. Maintaining audio identity and audio state.
3. Maintaining acoustic and audio-specific continuity.
4. Maintaining audio asset lineage.
5. Maintaining audio production state.
6. Validating audio consequences.
7. Propagating audio changes through dependencies.

AUDIO is NOT allowed to silently redefine:

- character identity
- animal identity
- location identity
- world state
- event truth
- physics
- spatial geometry
- transformation truth
- canonical facts

## REGENERATION RULE

Regeneration is repair.

It is not:

- reset
- new identity
- new voice
- new environment
- new acoustic state
- new continuity state

unless explicitly authorized.

## NATURAL VARIATION

Allowed:

- breathing variation
- pause variation
- emotional variation
- natural speech variation
- volume variation
- small performance variation
- environmental variation
- crowd variation
- procedural ambience variation

Not allowed:

- identity drift
- unexplained voice change
- unexplained pronunciation change
- unexplained acoustic environment change
- unexplained source change
- unexplained spatial change
- unexplained music identity change
- unexplained world-state contradiction
