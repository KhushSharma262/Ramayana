# AUDIO DOMAIN BOUNDARY

AUDIO owns the continuity of audible representation.

CHARACTER owns character identity and state.

PERFORMANCE owns physical performance.

PHYSIOLOGY owns physical conditions affecting voice/breath.

ENVIRONMENT owns physical environmental conditions.

SPATIAL owns geometry and positions.

PHYSICS owns physical/acoustic mechanics.

CAMERA owns viewpoint.

LIGHTING owns illumination.

VFX owns visual effects.

MUSIC owns musical composition rules where applicable.

AUDIO coordinates these domains without replacing them.

GLOBAL AUDIO is authoritative for final audio continuity.
