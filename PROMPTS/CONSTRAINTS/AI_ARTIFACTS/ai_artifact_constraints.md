# AI ARTIFACT CONSTRAINTS

Reject:

- extra fingers
- missing fingers
- malformed hands
- duplicated limbs
- distorted faces
- identity drift
- impossible joints
- floating objects
- warped architecture
- duplicated animals
- impossible cloth
- plastic skin
- artificial symmetry
- temporal flicker
- inconsistent backgrounds
- sudden costume changes

AI artifact prevention must never override an intentional
characteristic of the source design.
