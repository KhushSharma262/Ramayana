## 2. Required checks before material approval

For each candidate material identity, verify:
1. A stable material ID and unique identity.
2. Evidence classification and traceable source reference.
3. Whether the claim is directly sourced, traditionally sourced, historically researched, technically reconstructed, or a directorial choice.
4. Composition, construction, physical properties, and surface behavior only to the extent supported by evidence.
5. Explicit uncertainty and alternatives where identity is not established.
6. Registry entry and dependent asset links before production approval.

## 3. Automated checks are not approvals

A missing heading or ID detected by pattern matching does not prove the information is absent from the document. Likewise, a detected heading, ID, URL, or approval word does not prove correctness.
