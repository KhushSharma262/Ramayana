# MEDIUM STRUCTURE

## Scope

Intermediate structures such as grains, pores, fibers, weave, scales, pits,
inclusions, veins, layers, and clusters that may be visible at common shot
distances.

## Structure is material-dependent

Select feature classes from the actual material and its formation or processing
history. Do not apply one universal procedural noise pattern to stone, metal,
wood, cloth, skin, ash, water deposits, and other unrelated materials.

Where relevant, record:
- Feature shape and size distribution.
- Orientation and anisotropy.
- Density, spacing, packing, and connectivity.
- Clustering and spatial correlation.
- Layer order, thickness, and boundary shape.
- Relationship to macro features.
- Relationship to fine roughness and optical response.
- Relationship to wear, moisture, or damage.

## Spatial statistics

Random placement is not automatically natural. Determine whether the observed
structure is periodic, clustered, aligned, correlated, graded, fractal-like,
or otherwise constrained. Use only the distribution supported by evidence or
a clearly identified reconstruction assumption.

Do not repeat a small source texture in a way that produces visible tiling.
Do not independently randomize adjacent scales when their structures should
be correlated.

## Scale and representation

Choose geometry, displacement, normal maps, texture-driven shading, or a
hybrid based on feature dimensions, camera distance, sampling, parallax, and
light interaction.

Features that affect the silhouette, contact, or significant parallax may
require geometry. Texture-based representations must not be claimed to
reproduce actual geometric occlusion or all light transport.

Avoid inconsistent feature size between maps and LODs. Record filtering,
aliasing, and transition behavior.

## Validation

Use representative material samples and lighting. Inspect orientation changes,
grazing illumination, close views, distant views, motion, and transitions
between representation levels. Compare against source measurements or
references when available.
