# MACRO STRUCTURE

Covers large-scale surface and internal features visible at object/shot scale:
- stone layers, bedding, foliation, veins, inclusions and large fractures
- timber growth direction, knots, checks, splits and large grain
- cloth panel/seam/fold geometry (garment design belongs to COSTUMES)
- ceramic thickness, rims, joins and major firing defects
- forging/casting/hammering features and major corrosion
- plaster/paint/coating layers, gilding and delamination
- large wear, chips, impact damage and repairs

Record size, orientation, cause, material state, spatial relation and evidence. Macro structure changes silhouettes, self-shadowing, occlusion, reflection paths and interreflection. Geometry must be represented at a scale appropriate to the camera and illumination. Do not invent unsupported historical construction features.
