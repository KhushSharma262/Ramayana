# SUB-MICRO STRUCTURE

Covers finer structures that may affect optical, mechanical, thermal, chemical or transport behavior: crystal grains/boundaries, mineral/metal phases, fibre bundles, sub-resolution pores, pigment particle sizes, thin films, microvoids, inclusions and cellular structure where relevant.

Do not claim atomistic detail unless the phenomenon requires it and evidence supports it. Use quantum/electronic models only when they materially affect the modeled phenomenon.

Sub-microstructure can determine anisotropy, scattering, absorption, strength, diffusion and effective properties. Link to characterization data or the relevant physics model. Do not invent grain size, porosity or particle distributions.
