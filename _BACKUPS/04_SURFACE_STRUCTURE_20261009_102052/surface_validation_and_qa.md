# SURFACE STRUCTURE VALIDATION AND QA

Check:
- correct material and variant
- evidence/provenance for claimed structure
- dimensions and units
- plausible formation cause
- anisotropy/directionality and spatial correlation
- no tiling or repeated stamps
- no contradictory layers or double-counted geometry/shading
- physically coherent reflection/transmission
- multiple-bounce lighting not baked into albedo
- no impossible energy gain
- no texture crawling/flicker
- wet/dry/dirty/worn states are consistent
- camera scale and filtering are appropriate
- uncertainty/reconstruction are labeled
- source-to-material-to-asset-to-shot traceability exists

CRITICAL failure: visible contradiction of known material structure, causal history or light transport.
