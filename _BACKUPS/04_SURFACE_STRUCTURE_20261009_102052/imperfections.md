# IMPERFECTIONS

An imperfection is a physical feature with an origin, not a realism decoration.

Possible causes: biological growth, mineral formation, forming/forging/casting/weaving/firing, tool marks, handling, abrasion, impact, fracture, corrosion, weathering, erosion, biological activity, manufacturing defect or repair.

For each important imperfection record type, scale, location/orientation, cause, affected surface/volume, classification (defect, wear, damage, weathering, contamination or repair), evidence/confidence and physical/optical consequences.

Avoid random cracks, floating dirt, repeated scratches, impossible damage and features that contradict material history.
