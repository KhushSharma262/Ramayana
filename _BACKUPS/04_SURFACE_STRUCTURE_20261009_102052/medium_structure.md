# MEDIUM STRUCTURE

Covers features between macro form and microtexture:
- wood grain bands, pores, vessels, rays and knots
- stone grains, veins, pits and inclusions
- metal tool/forge/cast marks and corrosion morphology
- textile yarns, weave and fibre bundles
- leather grain, pores, creases and scars
- ceramic temper particles, pores, slip and glaze variation
- plaster aggregate, trowel marks, cracks and layers
- plant fibres, bark ridges and leaf veins
- scratches, abrasion, tool marks and weathering patterns

Features must be tied to the material and a plausible formation process. Preserve directional statistics, dimensions, clustering and correlation. Avoid tiling, repeated stamps, scale-inappropriate detail and uniform noise. Medium structure affects rough reflection, masking, shadowing, contact, dirt retention and wetting.
