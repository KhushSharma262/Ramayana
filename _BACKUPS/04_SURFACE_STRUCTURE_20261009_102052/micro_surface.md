# MICRO SURFACE

Covers fine morphology: asperities, scratches, grooves, small pits/pores, exposed fibres/fibrils, microcracks, fine corrosion, particles and coating irregularities.

Distinguish physical topography from optical roughness parameters. Renderer roughness is not automatically equivalent to measured RMS roughness or correlation length.

Where measured, record roughness metric/standard, sampling length or area, instrument, spatial-frequency band, correlation length, slope distribution, directionality, contamination/state and uncertainty.

Never invent precise micrometre values. If unknown, use a clearly labeled qualitative reconstruction rather than claiming measurement.
