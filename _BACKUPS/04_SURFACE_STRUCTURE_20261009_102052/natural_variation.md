# NATURAL AND PROCESS VARIATION

Variation can arise from growth, mineral deposition, impurities/inclusions, manufacturing, temperature/moisture history, exposure, use and repair.

Use measured distributions when available. Otherwise label variation as reconstruction and do not claim statistical certainty. Preserve spatial correlation when the process produces correlation; independent random noise is not a universal natural-variation model.

Variation may differ between specimens but must not flicker or randomly change on the same object over time. Keep specimen-level variation separate from frame-level noise.
