# SURFACE EVIDENCE SOURCE REGISTER

Purpose: establish a traceable method for researching surface structure.
These references support methods and rendering concepts; they do not establish
material-specific values for any Ramayana asset.

## SRC-SURF-001 — NIST surface roughness calibration
URL: https://www.nist.gov/publications/nist-surface-roughness-and-step-height-calibrations-measurement-conditions-and-sources
Use: roughness parameters, measurement conditions, uncertainty, profile spacing.
Limit: calibration methodology is not a substitute for measurements of the
actual stone, wood, metal, textile, or other production material.

## SRC-SURF-002 — NIST surface texture and forensic topography
URL: https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/surface-texture-and-forensic-topography
Use: surface texture metrology, feature characterization, uncertainty and
traceability principles.
Limit: manufacturing and forensic measurements must not be transferred
directly to an unrelated historical material without justification.

## SRC-SURF-003 — Blender Principled BSDF manual
URL: https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/5.2/render/shader_nodes/shader/principled.html
Use: interpret documented shader controls including base color, roughness,
metallic response, IOR, transmission and subsurface behavior.
Limit: shader controls are not direct measurements of a material's composition
or microscopic geometry. Verify compatibility with the installed Blender
version and selected render engine.

## SRC-SURF-004 — Physically Based Shading at Disney
URL: https://disneyanimation.com/publications/physically-based-shading-at-disney/
Use: background on measured reflectance, practical reflectance models, and
physically based shading trade-offs.
Limit: a general shading model does not validate an individual material asset.

## Evidence handling rules
1. Preserve the exact source title, URL, access date and relevant section.
2. Distinguish measured, derived, estimated, assumed and artistic values.
3. Record units, measurement conditions, uncertainty and applicability limits.
4. Do not copy a generic roughness value into every member of a material family.
5. A source supporting methodology is not evidence that a particular material
   has a particular grain size, pore distribution, composition or finish.
6. Canonical use, historical availability and physical properties are separate
   questions and require their own evidence.
7. A procedural reconstruction must be labelled as such when measurements are
   unavailable.
8. Do not mark a research task RESOLVED without the actual closure evidence.
