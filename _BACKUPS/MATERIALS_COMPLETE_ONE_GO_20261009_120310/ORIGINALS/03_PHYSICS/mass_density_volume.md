# MASS, DENSITY AND VOLUME

Mass is an extensive property.

Density depends on material composition and physical condition.

rho = m / V

Important dependencies:

- temperature
- pressure
- moisture
- porosity
- void fraction
- composition
- processing
- direction where anisotropy matters

Distinguish:

bulk density
particle density
true density
apparent density

For porous materials, never substitute true density for bulk density.

Volume may change through:

- thermal expansion
- swelling
- compression
- phase change
- moisture uptake
- deformation

All reported values require conditions.
