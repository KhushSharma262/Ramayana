# PHYSICAL MODELS

Models may include:

- analytical models
- empirical models
- constitutive models
- continuum mechanics
- rigid-body dynamics
- discrete element models
- particle methods
- finite element methods
- finite volume methods
- finite difference methods
- molecular/mesoscale models where genuinely required

Model selection must follow the physical phenomenon.

Do not select a method because it is convenient if it changes the relevant physical result.

Every model records:

- assumptions
- governing equations
- parameters
- boundary conditions
- initial conditions
- validity range
- calibration
- validation
- limitations
