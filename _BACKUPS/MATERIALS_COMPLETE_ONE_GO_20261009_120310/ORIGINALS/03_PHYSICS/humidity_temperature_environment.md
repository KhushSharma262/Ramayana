# HUMIDITY AND TEMPERATURE ENVIRONMENT

Environmental state may include:

- air temperature
- material temperature
- relative humidity
- absolute humidity
- pressure
- moisture content
- wind
- solar exposure
- shade

Relative humidity alone does not completely define water availability.

Material response may include:

- swelling
- shrinkage
- corrosion
- condensation
- evaporation
- biological degradation
- thermal expansion
- embrittlement
- softening
