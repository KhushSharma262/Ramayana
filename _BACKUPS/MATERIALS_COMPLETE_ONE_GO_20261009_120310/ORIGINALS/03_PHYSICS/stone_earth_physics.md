# STONE AND EARTH PHYSICS

Stone and earth are not one physical class.

Relevant differences include:

- mineral composition
- porosity
- grain size
- moisture
- density
- fracture structure
- cohesion
- confinement
- particle size

Stone:
fracture, crushing, abrasion, thermal expansion and weathering matter.

Earth/soil:
particle size distribution, moisture, compaction, cohesion, friction and drainage matter.

Mud is a multiphase granular-fluid system and must not be modeled as simply "wet earth".
