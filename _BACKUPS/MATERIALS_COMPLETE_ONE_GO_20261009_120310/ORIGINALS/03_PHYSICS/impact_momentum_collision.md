# IMPACT, MOMENTUM AND COLLISION

Momentum:

p = m v

Impulse:

J = integral(F dt)

Kinetic energy:

E = 1/2 m v^2

Collision behavior depends on:

- mass
- velocity
- geometry
- material stiffness
- damping
- friction
- contact time
- restitution
- deformation
- fracture

Never use a universal bounce coefficient without defining the contact system and regime.

Impact damage must be physically consistent with transferred energy and material strength.
