# OPTICAL PROPERTIES

Potential properties:

- spectral reflectance
- absorption
- transmission
- refractive index
- scattering
- emission
- fluorescence
- surface reflection
- subsurface scattering

Optical behavior depends on:

- wavelength
- angle
- surface roughness
- thickness
- internal structure
- pigmentation
- moisture
- temperature

Do not use RGB appearance as a substitute for physical optical properties.
