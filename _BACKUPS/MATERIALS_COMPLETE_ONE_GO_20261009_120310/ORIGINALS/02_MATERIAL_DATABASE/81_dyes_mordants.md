# DYES AND MORDANTS

## Plant dye families

- turmeric
- madder-type red dyes
- indigo
- safflower
- other documented regional dye plants

## Insect dyes

- lac-derived dye
- other insect-derived colourants only where evidence supports

## Mineral/inorganic colourants

Some colourants are pigments rather than dyes and must remain separate.

## Mordants

Mordants are substances used to improve fixation or alter dye colour/fastness.

Examples historically include alum and other metal-salt/mineral systems, but exact recipes require evidence.

## Rule

Dye != pigment.

Dye penetrates/colours a substrate through a different mechanism from an opaque particulate pigment coating.
