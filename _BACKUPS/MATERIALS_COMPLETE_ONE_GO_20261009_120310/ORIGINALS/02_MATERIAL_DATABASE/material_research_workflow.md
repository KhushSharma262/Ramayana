# Material Research Workflow

Defines a repeatable evidence-led workflow for unresolved material questions.

## Workflow

1. State the precise question and affected material or scene.
2. Search direct textual or production sources first when relevant.
3. Record traditional sources and historical research separately.
4. Compare plausible alternatives without collapsing disagreement.
5. Distinguish sourced facts from inference and technical reconstruction.
6. Record the chosen option, confidence, rationale, and consequences.
7. Leave the question OPEN if evidence is insufficient.
8. Update the material record and continuity constraints only after review.

Research completion requires actual sources and documented reasoning; creating a research file alone is not completion.
