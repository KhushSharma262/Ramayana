# MATERIAL DATABASE — INDEX

## Purpose

This is the master inventory of material identities relevant to the RAMAYAN production world.

This folder is intentionally broader than a small list of generic categories. It is a long-lived reference library: the project should not need a structural redesign merely because a new prop, costume, palace, forest, battlefield, ritual object, vehicle, weapon, ornament, or environmental element introduces another material family.

## Not automatically a material record

Fire is a process/energy phenomenon.
Smoke is a multiphase particulate/gas phenomenon.
Water is a fundamental substance and interaction medium and is handled across physics/environment systems.
Light is not a material.
Magic/divine energy is not silently converted into a material property.
Skin, hair, blood, fur and other biological tissues remain primarily in CHARACTER/ANIMALS/BIOLOGICAL systems and are referenced here only when material behavior is required.

## Evidence rule

A material being historically possible does not mean it was used in the Ramayana narrative.

A material being mentioned in a text does not automatically establish its exact modern mineralogical, botanical, metallurgical or industrial identity.

Every Ramayana-specific assignment must therefore be traceable through `07_HISTORICAL_CANONICAL`.
