# OTHER HISTORICALLY RELEVANT METALS

## Tin

Important especially as an alloying component for bronze.

## Lead

Used historically in metalworking and other crafts; exact object use requires evidence.

## Zinc

Historically important in Indian metallurgy. Metallic zinc production has a distinct technological history; do not project later industrial methods backward.

## Mercury

Historically documented in Indian chemical traditions. It is a toxic liquid metal and must never be visually or physically treated like silver.

## Rule

A material being historically known does not establish that it belongs in a specific Ramayana object.
