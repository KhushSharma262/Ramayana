# COMBUSTION AND FIRE

Fire is a chemical and thermal process.

Important quantities:

- heat release
- ignition
- fuel availability
- oxygen availability
- heat transfer
- pyrolysis
- flame temperature
- char formation
- smoke production
- ash production
- extinction
- cooling

Material-specific differences must be preserved.

Wood, cloth, oil, resin, metal, stone and ceramic do not burn or heat identically.

Do not model fire as a generic orange surface effect.

Combustion models must distinguish:

heating
→ pyrolysis/decomposition
→ ignition
→ flaming/smoldering
→ char
→ ash
→ cooling

Mass conservation applies to the combustion products.
