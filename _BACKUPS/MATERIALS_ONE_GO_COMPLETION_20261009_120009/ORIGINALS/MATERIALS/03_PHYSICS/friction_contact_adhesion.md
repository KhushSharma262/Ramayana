# FRICTION, CONTACT AND ADHESION

Friction is not one universal material constant.

It depends on the contact pair and conditions.

Possible regimes:

- static friction
- kinetic friction
- rolling resistance
- lubricated contact
- wet contact
- dry contact
- rough contact
- adhesive contact

Factors include:

- normal load
- surface roughness
- velocity
- temperature
- moisture
- contamination
- pressure
- contact area
- material pairing

Do not store "friction = X" without defining the interacting surfaces and conditions.

Adhesion must be distinguished from friction.
