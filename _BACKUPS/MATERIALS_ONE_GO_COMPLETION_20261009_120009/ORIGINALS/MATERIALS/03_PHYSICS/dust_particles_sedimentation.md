# DUST, PARTICLES AND SEDIMENTATION

Particle behavior depends on:

- particle size
- density
- shape
- cohesion
- air velocity
- humidity
- electrostatic effects
- surface roughness
- gravity

Processes:

- suspension
- transport
- deposition
- resuspension
- adhesion
- settling
- abrasion

Dust accumulation must have a physically plausible source and transport mechanism.
