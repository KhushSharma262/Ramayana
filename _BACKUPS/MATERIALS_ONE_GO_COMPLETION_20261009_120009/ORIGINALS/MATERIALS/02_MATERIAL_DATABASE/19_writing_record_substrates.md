# WRITING, RECORD AND IMAGE SUBSTRATES

Material identities relevant to manuscripts, records, inscriptions and painted surfaces.

## Candidate families

- palm leaf
- birch bark
- prepared bark
- cloth manuscript substrate
- wooden tablet/board
- clay tablet or clay writing surface
- stone inscription surface
- metal inscription surface
- prepared plaster surface

## Rules

A writing substrate is not automatically a separate material from its raw material. The record must identify both:

1. substrate material
2. preparation/processing that made it suitable for writing

Ink, pigment and binder remain separate material identities.

Historical use must be verified rather than assumed from modern practice.
