# SAND, GRAVEL AND AGGREGATE

## Material families

- quartz-rich sand
- feldspathic sand
- dark mineral sand
- river sand
- desert/arid sand
- gravel
- rounded river gravel
- angular crushed stone
- pebble
- cobble
- mineral aggregate

## Identity

Grain size, mineral composition, particle shape and sorting determine identity.

Do not use "sand" as a universal visual texture.

## Aggregate

Aggregate can be naturally occurring or crushed material used in construction.

Its identity depends on constituent rock/mineral.

## Production rule

Particle size distribution and mineral composition are material facts, not merely texture settings.
