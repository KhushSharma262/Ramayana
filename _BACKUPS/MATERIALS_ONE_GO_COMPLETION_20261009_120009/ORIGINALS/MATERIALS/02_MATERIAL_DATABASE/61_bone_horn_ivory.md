# BONE, HORN AND IVORY

## Bone

Mineralized biological tissue containing a collagen-rich organic component and mineral phase.

## Horn

Keratinous biological material. It is not bone.

## Ivory

Dense dentine from tusks/teeth. Elephant ivory is distinct from bone and horn.

## Historical uses

Tools, handles, ornaments, carvings, inlays and other objects occur in archaeological contexts.

## Rule

Bone, horn and ivory must remain separate material identities because composition, structure and behavior differ.
