# PIGMENTS AND MINERAL COLOURANTS

## Mineral/inorganic families

- red ochre / iron-oxide-rich earth
- yellow ochre / hydrated iron-oxide-rich earth
- carbon black
- lampblack
- charcoal black
- chalk/calcium-carbonate white
- lime-based white
- gypsum-based white
- clay/mineral earth pigments
- malachite/copper-carbonate green where historically justified
- azurite/copper-carbonate blue where historically justified
- realgar/orpiment arsenic sulfide pigments where historically justified
- mineral blue materials such as lapis lazuli where evidence supports

## Rule

A pigment is a particulate colourant, not a finished paint.

Pigment identity depends on mineral/organic composition and processing.

Toxic historical pigments must be treated as historical material identities, not modern recommendations.
