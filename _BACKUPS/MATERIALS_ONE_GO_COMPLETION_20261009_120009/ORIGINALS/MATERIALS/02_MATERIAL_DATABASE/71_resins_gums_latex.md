# RESINS, GUMS AND LATEX

## Material families

- tree resins
- oleoresins
- plant gums
- gum-resin materials
- latex
- lac/resinous insect-derived material

## Identity

Resin and gum are not synonyms.

Some natural materials contain mixed resin/gum/oil fractions.
