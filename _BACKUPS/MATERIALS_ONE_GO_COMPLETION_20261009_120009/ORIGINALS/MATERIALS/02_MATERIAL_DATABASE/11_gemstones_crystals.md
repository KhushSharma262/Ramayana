## Important distinction

Gemstone name, mineral species and historical gem-name are not always equivalent.

A Sanskrit/Indic gem term must not be mapped to a modern mineral species without evidence.

## Rock crystal / quartz

Silicon-dioxide mineral. Transparent crystals may occur naturally; inclusions and fractures are normal.

## Corundum

Aluminium-oxide mineral family. Ruby and sapphire are colour varieties of corundum rather than separate mineral species.

## Beryl

Beryllium-aluminium silicate family. Emerald is a green variety of beryl.

## Diamond

Crystalline carbon. Natural diamond has a different optical and mechanical identity from quartz or crystal.

## Pearl

Biogenic calcium-carbonate material produced by molluscs. It is not a mineral crystal in the same sense as quartz.

## Coral

Biogenic calcium-carbonate skeletal material. It is not a gemstone mineral in the geological sense.

## Production rule

Never assign a modern gem species solely because a text uses a broad traditional gem name. Record the traditional term and possible modern identities separately.
