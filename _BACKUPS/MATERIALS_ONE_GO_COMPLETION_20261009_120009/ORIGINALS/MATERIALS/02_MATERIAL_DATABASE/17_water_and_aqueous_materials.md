# WATER AND AQUEOUS MATERIAL IDENTITIES

Water is a genuine physical substance and should have an identity record even though most of its interaction behavior is owned by PHYSICS and BEHAVIOR.

## Identity

- MAT-WATER-BASE
- water
- freshwater
- saline/brackish water where relevant
- rainwater as a contextual state/source, not a separate substance
- ritual water as a contextual designation, not a chemically different material unless composition differs

## Rules

Do not create separate fake materials merely because water is visually different.

Visual differences may result from:

- dissolved minerals
- suspended sediment
- organic matter
- temperature
- depth/path length
- lighting
- surface disturbance
- container material
- contamination

## Ownership

Identity/composition: this database.

Density, viscosity, surface tension, evaporation, freezing, wetting, flow and interaction: PHYSICS/BEHAVIOR.

Ritual/canonical meaning: HISTORICAL_CANONICAL.
