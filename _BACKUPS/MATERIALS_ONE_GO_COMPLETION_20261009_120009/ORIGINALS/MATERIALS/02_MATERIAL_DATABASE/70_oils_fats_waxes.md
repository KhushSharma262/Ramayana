# OILS, FATS AND WAXES

## Plant oils

Potential historical plant-oil families include:

- sesame
- mustard
- coconut
- castor
- linseed-type oils where historically justified
- other locally available seed/fruit oils

Exact use must be evidenced.

## Animal fats

Animal-derived fats may occur as food, lubricant, finish, binder or processing aid depending on historical evidence.

## Beeswax

Natural wax produced by bees. It can serve as a coating, polish, binder or modelling material.

## Plant waxes

Include only when source and historical use are established.

## Rule

Oil, fat and wax are distinct material identities despite all being hydrophobic organic substances.
