# ADHESIVES AND BINDERS

## Binder families

- plant gums
- animal glues
- starch-based binders
- casein-like protein binders where historically supported
- natural resins
- clay binders
- lime binders
- gypsum binders
- oil-based binders

## Rule

Binder identity must be separated from filler/aggregate/pigment.

Examples:

pigment + binder = paint/coating system
sand + lime + water = mortar system
clay + temper + water + firing = ceramic precursor

The complete physical response belongs in physics/behavior.
