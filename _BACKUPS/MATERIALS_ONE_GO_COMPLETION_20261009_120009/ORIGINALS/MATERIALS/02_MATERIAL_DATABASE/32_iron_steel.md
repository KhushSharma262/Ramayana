# IRON AND STEEL

## Iron

Iron can occur as wrought/forged historical material, but "iron" is not equivalent to one modern industrial grade.

## Wrought iron

Low-carbon iron with characteristic slag inclusions and working history in traditional production. Exact composition varies.

## Cast iron

High-carbon cast material. Historical availability must be established for the relevant period before use.

## Steel

Iron-carbon material with carbon content and processing sufficient to produce steel-like properties. Historical steels may differ substantially from modern standardized grades.

## Rule

Never use "steel" as a generic word for every ancient iron weapon.

The historical production process, carbon level, heat treatment and evidence must be established where relevant.

Ancient Indian archaeological and historical sources document iron technology and iron tools/weapons, with regional chronology requiring separate verification.
