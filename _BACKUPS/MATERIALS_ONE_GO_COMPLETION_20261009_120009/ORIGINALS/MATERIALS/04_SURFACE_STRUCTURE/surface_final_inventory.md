## Inventory rules

Do not delete older documents automatically. Older documents may contain
material-specific research or decisions not represented in the canonical set.

Review unexpected files manually and migrate useful information with traceable
provenance before retiring duplicates.

A file's presence does not mean its records have been completed or approved.
