# Surface Evidence Source Register — Repaired

## Scope and evidence boundary
This register contains general methodological references for surface measurement and shading. These sources do **not** establish the historical use, exact identity, dimensions, or optical parameters of any material assigned to a Ramayana asset. Every asset-specific assignment still requires a traceable material record and a source appropriate to that material, region, chronology, processing state, and claim.

## General methodology references

| ID | Reference | Appropriate use | Does not establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| SRC-SURF-001 | NIST, *Surface Texture and Forensic Topography*: https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/surface-texture-and-forensic-topography | Surface topography measurement methods, traceability, reference materials, measurement uncertainty, comparison methods | Material-specific historic values or Ramayana use |
| SRC-SURF-002 | Vorburger et al. (2014), *NIST Surface Roughness and Step Height Calibrations, Measurement Conditions and Sources of Uncertainty*: https://www.nist.gov/publications/nist-surface-roughness-and-step-height-calibrations-measurement-conditions-and-sources | Roughness parameters, measurement conditions, calibration and uncertainty discipline | Values for a particular stone, wood, textile, metal, or residue without matching specimen data |
| SRC-SURF-003 | Blender 5.2 Manual, Principled BSDF: https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/5.2/render/shader_nodes/shader/principled.html | If Blender 5.2 is confirmed, shader model capabilities and parameter interpretation | Confirmation that this project uses Blender 5.2 or proof that a rendered material is physically validated |
| SRC-SURF-004 | Burley (2012), *Physically-Based Shading at Disney*: https://disneyanimation.com/publications/physically-based-shading-at-disney/ | General production shading methodology and measured-material motivation | Historical material identity, exact material values, or a validation result for this project |

## Required source record for each material-specific claim
Record: source ID; full bibliographic citation/URL; author or issuing institution; publication/specimen date; exact claim supported; material identity and provenance; geographic and chronological scope; processing/aging/moisture state; measurement method and units; uncertainty or limitations; access date; reviewer; and the asset/material IDs to which the claim applies.

## Evidence classification vocabulary
- `DIRECT_MEASUREMENT`: measurement on identified specimen with method and uncertainty recorded.
- `AUTHORITATIVE_MATERIAL_REFERENCE`: traceable source directly about the identified material/state.
- `HISTORICAL_OR_ARCHAEOLOGICAL_EVIDENCE`: supports a historical/cultural claim within stated context.
- `TEXTUAL_MENTION`: a text names a material or term but does not by itself prove modern scientific identity.
- `ANALOGICAL_REFERENCE`: analogous specimen/material used with explicit rationale; not treated as direct evidence.
- `ARTISTIC_RECONSTRUCTION`: production choice made because direct evidence is absent; explicitly not a verified physical fact.
- `UNKNOWN`: evidence not yet established.

Do not use a general rendering reference to close a historical or material-identity question.
