# Master Material Registry Rules

Defines registry governance and record quality.

## Registry governance

Every approved entry must have a stable unique material ID, a name, category, evidence classification, status, and traceable links to supporting records.

Before adding an entry, check for duplicates and conflicts. Keep base identity separate from state variations. Use separate state records or linked state definitions when a material changes condition without changing identity.

Do not populate the registry with illustrative entries that could be mistaken for approved production materials. Unverified candidates must be labeled as candidates and remain unapproved.
