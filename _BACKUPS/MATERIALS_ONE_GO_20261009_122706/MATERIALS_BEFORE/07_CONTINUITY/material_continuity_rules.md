# Material Continuity Rules

Controls identity and state continuity across shots and generations.

## Identity continuity

The approved base material must remain identifiable across shots, lighting, environment, and AI-generated variations. Visual changes caused by illumination, exposure, grading, or camera response must not automatically be classified as material changes.

## State continuity

Every meaningful change of state must have a plausible physical or narrative cause. Track wetness, dust, dirt, wear, damage, repairs, and other relevant states across adjacent shots. Preserve state where no credible transition occurs.

## Substitution control

Record every actual substitution, its reason, evidence status, approval, and continuity impact. Do not silently replace a material because another shader or texture is easier to render.

## Review

Flag unresolved identity changes, unexplained state transitions, scale inconsistencies, AI artifacts, and physically implausible surface responses for review.
