# TEXTILE FIBRES

## Plant fibres

- cotton
- flax-type fibres where historically/geographically justified
- bast fibres
- jute-type fibres
- hemp-type fibres
- banana/coir and other plant fibres where evidenced

## Animal fibres

- silk
- wool
- goat hair fibres
- other animal fibres where evidenced

## Cotton

Cellulosic plant fibre. Fibre length, fineness and preparation affect yarn and fabric identity.

## Silk

Protein fibre produced by silkworms. Wild and domesticated silk must not automatically be treated as identical.

## Wool

Keratin fibre from sheep and other wool-producing animals. Fibre diameter, crimp and processing affect textile identity.

## Rule

Fibre != yarn != fabric.

Do not identify a fabric merely from colour or drape.
