# WOOD AND TIMBER

## Scope

Wood identity is species- and preparation-dependent.

## Important candidate species/families for research

- sal / Shorea robusta
- sandalwood / Santalum spp.
- ashoka / Saraca asoca
- banyan / Ficus benghalensis
- peepal / Ficus religiosa
- arjuna / Terminalia arjuna
- khadira / Acacia/Senegalia category requires taxonomic verification
- palash / Butea monosperma
- neem / Azadirachta indica
- mango / Mangifera indica
- jackfruit / Artocarpus heterophyllus
- kadamba / Neolamarckia cadamba
- bamboo is handled separately
- cane/reed are handled separately

## Record identity

For each species record:

- botanical identity
- accepted/common names
- native/geographic range
- typical wood colour
- grain character
- density range
- hardness range
- durability
- moisture sensitivity
- historical uses where evidenced

Detailed numerical and mechanical properties belong in `03_PHYSICS`.

## Important warning

A Sanskrit plant name may have multiple historical or regional identifications.

Do not silently map a literary tree name to a modern species.

## Material states

Fresh wood, seasoned wood, wet wood, charred wood and finished wood may be separate states/variants but are not automatically separate base materials.
