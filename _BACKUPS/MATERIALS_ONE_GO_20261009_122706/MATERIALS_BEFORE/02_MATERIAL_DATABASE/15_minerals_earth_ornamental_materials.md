# MINERALS, EARTH MATERIALS AND ORNAMENTAL STONES

This file captures mineral/material identities that do not fit safely inside a generic stone or gemstone bucket.

## Material families to research when evidence supports use

- steatite / soapstone
- chlorite and related soft stone materials
- alabaster / gypsum-based decorative stone
- gypsum
- mica
- talc
- calcite
- quartz
- feldspar-rich stone
- laterite
- slate and schist where geographically/historically justified
- limestone
- sandstone
- basalt
- granite and other crystalline rocks
- marble and decorative metamorphic stone
- travertine where supported
- salt / halite
- natural mineral aggregates
- mineral earths used as colourants

## Identity requirements

For each specific material, record mineralogical identity only to the level supported by evidence. Do not convert a historical colour/name automatically into a modern mineral species.

Record:

- material ID
- accepted identity
- historical names
- alternative identifications
- composition/mineral family
- geological origin
- likely source regions when evidenced
- working properties at identity level
- known historical uses
- evidence status
- uncertainty
- links to physics, surface structure and historical/canonical evidence

## Important distinction

A decorative stone, a geological rock, a mineral crystal and a polished ornamental material are not automatically interchangeable records.
