# LEATHER AND HIDE

## Raw hide

Animal skin with hair/fur and natural tissue structure before tanning/preparation.

## Leather

Animal hide/skin transformed through tanning and related processing.

## Identity variables

- animal source
- hide thickness
- grain
- tanning method
- oil/fat content
- surface treatment
- hair retained/removed
- stretching
- drying

## Rule

Leather is not visually interchangeable with cloth, rubber or plastic.

Historical tanning methods and exact animal sources require evidence.
