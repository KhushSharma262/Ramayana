# SPECIAL HISTORICAL MATERIALS

This file prevents unusual but physically real material families from disappearing because they are not common modern categories.

## Candidate identities requiring evidence

- natural bitumen
- asphaltic/natural hydrocarbon residues
- pitch-like materials
- tar-like historical products where evidence exists
- mineral wax-like substances
- natural latex
- lac
- shellac as a processed resin product where historically appropriate
- fermented or proteinaceous binders where materially relevant
- casein/protein adhesives where historically appropriate
- animal glue
- hide glue
- starch paste
- flour/starch binders
- lime-based binders
- gypsum-based binders

## Rule

Historical plausibility is not proof of use. Each entry must carry a source classification and confidence status.
