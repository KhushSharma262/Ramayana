# PRECIOUS METALS

## Gold

- elemental metal
- dense
- malleable
- ductile
- naturally yellow metallic appearance
- purity/alloying strongly affect physical identity

## Silver

- elemental metal
- highly reflective when clean
- tarnish changes surface chemistry and appearance
- alloying changes properties

## Electrum-like gold-silver alloys

Do not assume historical objects were pure gold or pure silver. Natural/alloyed compositions must be considered when evidence supports them.

## Production rule

Jewellery appearance is not enough to identify the metal.

A yellow object can be gold, gold alloy, copper alloy, pigment-coated metal or another material.
