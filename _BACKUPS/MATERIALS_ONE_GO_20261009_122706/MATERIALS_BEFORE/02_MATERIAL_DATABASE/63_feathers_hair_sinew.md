# FEATHERS, HAIR AND SINEW

## Feathers

Keratinous biological structures with species-specific morphology.

## Hair

Keratin fibre. Human and animal hair must not be merged.

## Sinew

Collagen-rich connective tissue historically usable as cord/binding material.

## Scope rule

These are included because props, weapons, costumes, ornaments and animal materials may require their physical identity.

Biological identity itself remains owned by CHARACTER/ANIMALS.
