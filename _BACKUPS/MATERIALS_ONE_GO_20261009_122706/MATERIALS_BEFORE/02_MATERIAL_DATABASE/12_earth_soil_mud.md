# EARTH, SOIL AND MUD

## Material families

- mineral soil
- sandy soil
- silty soil
- clay-rich soil
- loamy soil
- lateritic soil
- alluvial soil
- compacted earth
- loose earth
- mud
- wet clay-rich mud
- dry dust-bearing earth

## Identity

Soil is a multiphase natural material containing mineral particles, organic matter, water, air and biological components.

Mud is not simply "brown soil"; it is soil/sediment with sufficient water to produce a cohesive or flowable wet condition.

## Relevant constituents

- sand
- silt
- clay minerals
- gravel
- organic matter
- salts/minerals
- water
- air

## Production rule

Soil appearance and behavior must depend on actual composition and moisture condition.

A dry sandy surface cannot be represented by the same material definition as wet clay-rich mud.

Detailed moisture, cohesion, adhesion, deformation, drying and footprint behavior belong in `03_PHYSICS`, `05_BEHAVIOR`, and `06_STATES`.
