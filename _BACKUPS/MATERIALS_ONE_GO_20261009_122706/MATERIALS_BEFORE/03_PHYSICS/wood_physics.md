# WOOD PHYSICS

Wood is heterogeneous and anisotropic.

Relevant variables:

- species
- density
- moisture content
- grain direction
- growth structure
- defects
- temperature
- loading direction

Important behaviors:

- longitudinal/radial/tangential differences
- swelling
- shrinkage
- warping
- splitting
- bending
- creep
- fracture
- combustion

Do not assign one isotropic "wood" model to all species.
