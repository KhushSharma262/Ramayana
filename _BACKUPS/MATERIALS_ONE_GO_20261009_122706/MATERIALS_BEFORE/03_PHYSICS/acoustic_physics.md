# ACOUSTIC PHYSICS

Sound response depends on:

- density
- stiffness
- damping
- geometry
- surface structure
- contact
- excitation
- surrounding medium

Important phenomena:

- reflection
- absorption
- transmission
- resonance
- scattering
- damping
- impact sound
- scraping
- cracking
- ringing

Material identity alone does not determine the final sound.

The complete object and environment matter.
