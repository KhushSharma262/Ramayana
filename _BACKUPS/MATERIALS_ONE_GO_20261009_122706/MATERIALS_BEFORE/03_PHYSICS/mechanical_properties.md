# MECHANICAL PROPERTIES

Potential properties:

- Young's modulus
- shear modulus
- bulk modulus
- Poisson ratio
- hardness
- yield strength
- tensile strength
- compressive strength
- shear strength
- flexural strength
- fracture toughness
- fatigue behavior
- creep
- stress relaxation

Properties may depend on:

- direction
- temperature
- moisture
- strain rate
- microstructure
- defects
- age
- processing

Do not assign isotropic properties to anisotropic materials without justification.
