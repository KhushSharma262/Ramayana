# WETTING, ABSORPTION AND PERMEABILITY

Distinguish:

surface wetting
absorption
adsorption
capillary uptake
permeation
diffusion
runoff
drainage

Important factors:

- pore structure
- contact angle
- surface energy
- viscosity
- gravity
- pressure
- material saturation
- temperature

Wetness is not merely a darker color.

Water interaction may change:

- mass
- density
- stiffness
- friction
- dimensions
- thermal properties
- optical response
- acoustic response

The physical cause must precede the visual change.
