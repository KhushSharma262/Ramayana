# WEATHERING AND EROSION PHYSICS

Weathering mechanisms:

- thermal cycling
- wet-dry cycling
- freeze-thaw where applicable
- UV exposure
- oxidation
- chemical dissolution
- abrasion
- wind erosion
- water erosion
- salt crystallization
- biological activity

Rate depends on:

- exposure
- material
- environment
- geometry
- time

Do not treat aging as one generic texture layer.
