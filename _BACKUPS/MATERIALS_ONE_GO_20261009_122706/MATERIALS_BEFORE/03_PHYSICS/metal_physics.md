# METAL PHYSICS

Metals differ by:

- elemental composition
- alloying
- microstructure
- processing
- heat treatment
- impurities
- grain structure
- corrosion state

Relevant behavior:

- elasticity
- plasticity
- yielding
- ductility
- fracture
- fatigue
- thermal expansion
- thermal conductivity
- electrical conductivity
- oxidation
- corrosion

Historical wrought/forged materials must not automatically inherit modern industrial alloy properties.
