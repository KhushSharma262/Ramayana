# TEXTILE AND FIBRE PHYSICS

Textiles are assemblies of fibres.

Relevant variables:

- fibre material
- fibre diameter
- yarn twist
- weave
- knit
- density
- thickness
- moisture
- friction
- fibre orientation
- stitching

Behavior:

- tension
- bending
- drape
- stretching
- compression
- shear
- tearing
- friction
- air permeability

A fabric is not physically equivalent to the raw fibre from which it is made.
