# BONE, HORN AND SHELL PHYSICS

These are heterogeneous biological composites.

Relevant variables:

- mineral fraction
- organic matrix
- porosity
- orientation
- moisture
- geometry
- species/source

Behavior may include:

- anisotropic strength
- brittle/ductile transition
- fracture
- cracking
- bending
- thermal degradation

Ivory, bone, horn and shell are not interchangeable materials.
