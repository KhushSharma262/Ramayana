## Rules

Use SI units where applicable and record conversions. Do not create false
precision for estimated values. Preserve the assumptions of derived values.

An image can support visible morphology under its capture conditions, but may
not uniquely establish physical dimensions, composition, refractive index,
subsurface structure, or roughness statistics.

A record may be structurally complete while its science remains uncertain.
