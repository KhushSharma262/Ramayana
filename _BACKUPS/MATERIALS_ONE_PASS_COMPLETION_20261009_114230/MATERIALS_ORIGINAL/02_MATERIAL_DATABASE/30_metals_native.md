# NATIVE AND BASE METALS

## Material families

- copper
- gold
- silver
- iron
- lead
- tin
- zinc
- mercury
- other metals only when evidence requires

## Native metal

Some metals can occur naturally in metallic form. Native occurrence does not imply that all historical objects were made from native metal.

## Copper

Important ancient Indian metal. Copper objects and copper metallurgy are archaeologically well attested.

## Gold

Naturally occurring gold and refined gold must not be treated as identical. Purity and alloying affect physical identity.

## Silver

Native/ore-derived silver and refined/alloyed silver must remain distinguishable where evidence matters.

## Lead, tin, zinc, mercury

These are historically relevant materials, but exact use in a particular Ramayana scene requires evidence.

## Historical technology

Ancient Indian sources document copper, bronze, gold, silver, lead, tin, zinc and other metallurgical knowledge. The exact date and regional distribution of each use must be checked against the relevant historical layer.
