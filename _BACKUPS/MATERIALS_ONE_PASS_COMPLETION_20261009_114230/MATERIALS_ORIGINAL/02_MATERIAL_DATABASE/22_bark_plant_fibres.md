# BARK AND PLANT FIBRES

## Material families

- bark cloth
- bast fibres
- flax-type fibres
- jute-type fibres
- hemp-type fibres
- nettle-type fibres
- banana fibre
- coconut/coir fibre
- other locally available plant fibres

## Identity

Plant fibre identity depends on source plant, extraction method, fibre length, fineness, processing and spinning.

## Important distinction

Raw fibre != yarn != woven fabric.

The material database records each as a distinct material stage when physical identity changes materially.

## Historical rule

Do not assume a modern commercial fibre name was available or used in the relevant period without evidence.
