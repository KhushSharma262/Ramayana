# CHARCOAL, ASH, SOOT AND PARTICULATE MATERIALS

## Scope

These are physical residues/materials produced by combustion or processing and are important to ritual spaces, kitchens, lamps, fires, cremation contexts, workshops and environmental continuity.

## Material identities

- charcoal
- wood charcoal
- fine charcoal powder
- ash
- mineral ash residues
- soot
- lampblack
- combustion residue mixtures
- furnace/slag residues where material identity is relevant
- kiln residues

## Required distinction

Fire itself is not stored as a material identity. Charcoal and ash are material residues with their own composition and physical behavior.

## Record

- precursor material
- production/formation mechanism
- composition
- particle/fragment character
- colour range
- contamination/inclusions
- historical use
- source confidence
- uncertainty
- physics/behavior/state links

Never assume that all black powder is soot, all ash is identical, or all charcoal has the same structure.
