# GLASS AND MINERAL GLASS-LIKE MATERIALS

## Glass

Amorphous inorganic solid produced by melting/fusing appropriate constituents and cooling without crystallizing into a normal crystalline structure.

## Historical identity

Ancient Indian glassmaking has archaeological and literary evidence, but glass types, recipes and regional chronology vary.

## Faience

Handled separately from true glass despite its glassy surface.

## Quartz crystal

Natural crystalline mineral, not glass.

## Obsidian

Natural volcanic glass. It must not be confused with manufactured glass.

## Rule

Do not use modern float glass, optical glass or industrial safety glass as default ancient materials.

Exact glass type requires historical evidence.
