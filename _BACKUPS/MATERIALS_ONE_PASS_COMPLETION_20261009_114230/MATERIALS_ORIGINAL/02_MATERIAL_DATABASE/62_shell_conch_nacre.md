# SHELL, CONCH AND NACRE

## Shell

Calcium-carbonate-rich biological hard material produced by molluscs.

## Conch

A gastropod shell; the word can refer to the shell itself or the ritual musical object made from it. These are different levels of identity.

## Nacre

Mother-of-pearl inner shell layer with a characteristic layered microstructure.

## Coral

Handled as a separate biogenic calcium-carbonate ornamental material.

## Rule

Shell colour alone does not identify species or material layer.
