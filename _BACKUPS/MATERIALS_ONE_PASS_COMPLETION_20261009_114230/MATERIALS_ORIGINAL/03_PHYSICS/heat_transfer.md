# HEAT TRANSFER

Heat transfer mechanisms:

- conduction
- convection
- radiation
- phase-change heat transfer

Conduction:
q = -k grad(T)

Convection depends on fluid flow and boundary conditions.

Radiation depends on:

- temperature
- emissivity
- geometry
- view factor
- wavelength regime

Thermal contact resistance may matter.

Heat transfer models must conserve energy within numerical tolerance.
