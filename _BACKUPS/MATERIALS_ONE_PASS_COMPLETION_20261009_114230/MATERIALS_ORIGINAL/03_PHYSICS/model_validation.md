# MODEL VALIDATION

Validation hierarchy:

LEVEL 1
Analytical sanity check.

LEVEL 2
Conservation-law check.

LEVEL 3
Convergence check.

LEVEL 4
Comparison with experimental/literature data.

LEVEL 5
Scenario-level physical validation.

LEVEL 6
Production-shot validation.

Passing a lower level does not automatically pass higher levels.

A model that visually matches a shot can still be physically wrong.
