# FLUID MECHANICS

Potential regimes:

- hydrostatic
- laminar
- turbulent
- free-surface
- multiphase
- porous-media flow

Relevant quantities may include:

- Reynolds number
- Froude number
- Weber number
- Mach number
- pressure
- velocity
- viscosity
- density

Models must respect:

mass conservation
momentum conservation
energy conservation where relevant.

Do not select a turbulence model solely for visual appearance.
