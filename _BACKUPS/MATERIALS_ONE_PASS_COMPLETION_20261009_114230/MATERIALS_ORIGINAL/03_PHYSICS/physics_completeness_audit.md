# PHYSICS COMPLETENESS AUDIT

Before a physics record is approved, ask:

## Identity
- Is the correct material identified?
- Is the material variant identified?
- Is the physical condition identified?

## Units
- Are SI units used?
- Are conversions traceable?

## Conditions
- Temperature?
- Pressure?
- Moisture?
- Humidity?
- Loading?
- Direction?
- Strain rate?
- Surface condition?
- Contact pair?
- Scale?

## Data
- Source?
- Provenance?
- Uncertainty?
- Range?
- Precision justified?

## Model
- Governing model?
- Assumptions?
- Validity regime?
- Boundary conditions?
- Initial conditions?

## Numerical
- Resolution?
- Timestep?
- Convergence?
- Stability?
- Conservation?
- Solver limitations?

## Validation
- Real-world reference?
- Measurement uncertainty?
- Model discrepancy?
- Acceptance criterion?

## Causality
- Is every observable change physically caused?
- Is every state transition physically justified?

## Historical
- Is the material historically appropriate?
- Is modern data being transferred backward?
- Is that transfer justified?

## Handoff
- Is the downstream implementation traceable?
- Are approximation losses documented?

A record failing a critical question is not physically approved.
