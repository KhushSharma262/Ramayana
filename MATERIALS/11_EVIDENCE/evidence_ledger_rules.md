# Evidence Ledger Rules

Defines how source evidence must be recorded.

## Evidence classes

- DIRECT_SOURCE
- TRADITIONAL_SOURCE
- HISTORICAL_RESEARCH
- TECHNICAL_RECONSTRUCTION
- DIRECTORIAL_CHOICE

## Minimum evidence fields

Evidence ID, material ID or research question, source title, author or issuing body when known, publication or edition when known, page/section/URL or stable locator, relevant excerpt or observation, access date where applicable, evidence class, supported claim, limitations, and review status.

Only record details actually verified. If a locator, quotation, publication detail, or claim is unknown, leave it unresolved. Technical reconstruction and directorial choice must not be represented as direct source evidence.
