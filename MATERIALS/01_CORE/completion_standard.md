# MATERIALS Completion Standard

Defines when a material record or system component may be treated as complete.

## Completion requirements

A structural file is not proof that the task it describes has been completed.

A material record is complete only when its identity, source classification, uncertainty, properties, construction, state rules, continuity requirements, and applicable validation status are traceable or explicitly marked not applicable or unresolved.

Never infer source approval from a populated template. Never mark a test passed unless it was executed and its result was recorded. Never invent an asset identifier, evidence citation, registry ID, source quotation, research conclusion, or approval.

## Status vocabulary

- NOT_STARTED
- OPEN
- BLOCKED
- IN_PROGRESS
- NEEDS_REVIEW
- PASSED
- FAILED
- NOT_APPLICABLE

Use PASSED only when the acceptance criteria were actually checked. Record the basis for NOT_APPLICABLE.
