# SURFACE EVIDENCE AND RESEARCH REGISTER

## Purpose

Track unresolved surface-structure claims and prevent unsupported claims from
being treated as verified material facts.

## Research status

OPEN: evidence not yet collected.
IN_REVIEW: sources or measurements are being evaluated.
SUPPORTED: evidence supports the stated claim within a documented scope.
CONFLICTED: credible evidence disagrees.
UNRESOLVED: current evidence cannot answer the question.
NOT_APPLICABLE: question does not apply, with a reason.

Do not use SUPPORTED to imply universal validity beyond the evidence.

## Evidence hierarchy

Evaluate sources by relevance and method, not by prestige alone. Prefer
traceable measurements, primary studies, standards, and technical references
appropriate to the claim. Record limitations and contradictory results.

A visual reference can support appearance under known capture conditions
without establishing unique microscopic structure or physical parameters.

## Required uncertainty discipline

Never invent source titles, measurements, citations, or experimental results.
Do not silently replace missing evidence with general knowledge or a plausible
guess. Label inference and artistic reconstruction explicitly.

## Closure criteria

Close a research item only when its conclusion, evidence, applicability,
uncertainty, and downstream consequences are recorded. An unresolved item may
remain open while the production decision is blocked, limited, or explicitly
approved as an artistic reconstruction.
