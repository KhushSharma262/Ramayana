# DEFORMATION

Possible deformation:

- translation
- rotation
- bending
- stretching
- compression
- shear
- torsion
- buckling
- wrinkling
- folding
- crushing
- tearing

Distinguish:

rigid-body motion
from
material deformation.

Deformation must be consistent with:

- stiffness
- geometry
- loading
- boundary conditions
- material law
- mass
- time scale
