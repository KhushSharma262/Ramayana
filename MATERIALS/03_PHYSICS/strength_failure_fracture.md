# STRENGTH, FAILURE AND FRACTURE

Failure modes:

- tension
- compression
- shear
- bending
- buckling
- fatigue
- creep rupture
- brittle fracture
- ductile fracture
- delamination
- tearing
- crushing
- chipping

Failure must have a physical cause.

Record:

- failure criterion
- loading condition
- defect sensitivity
- fracture energy where known
- crack propagation behavior
- directionality
- strain-rate dependence

A procedural crack is not a physics model.

Damage must correspond to a plausible loading history.
