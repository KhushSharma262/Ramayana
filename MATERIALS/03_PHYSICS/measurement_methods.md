# MEASUREMENT METHODS

Physical data must retain its measurement method.

Possible methods:

- direct measurement
- calibrated instrument
- laboratory test
- standardized mechanical test
- thermal measurement
- optical measurement
- acoustic measurement
- dimensional measurement
- field measurement

Measurement metadata should include:

- instrument
- calibration
- specimen
- environment
- method
- repeatability
- uncertainty

Measurement conditions are part of the data.
