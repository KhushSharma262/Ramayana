# UNITS AND DIMENSIONS

## Internal standard

SI is mandatory for quantitative internal representation.

Length: metre (m)
Mass: kilogram (kg)
Time: second (s)
Temperature: kelvin (K)
Amount of substance: mole (mol)
Electric current: ampere (A)

Derived:

Force: newton (N)
Energy: joule (J)
Power: watt (W)
Pressure: pascal (Pa)
Frequency: hertz (Hz)

## Rules

Historical units may be preserved as source data.

They must be converted to SI for internal calculation.

Conversions must be traceable.

Do not mix:

mass and weight
force and mass
energy and power
temperature and temperature difference

Mass:
m [kg]

Weight force:
W = m g [N]

Density:
rho = m / V [kg/m^3]

Pressure:
p = F / A [Pa]

Kinetic energy:
E_k = 1/2 m v^2 [J]

Momentum:
p = m v [kg m/s]

No unitless numerical values are allowed where a physical dimension is required.
