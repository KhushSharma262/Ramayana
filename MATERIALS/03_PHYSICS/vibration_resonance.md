# VIBRATION AND RESONANCE

Relevant properties:

- mass
- stiffness
- damping
- geometry
- boundary conditions

Natural frequency depends on the complete physical system, not material alone.

Distinguish:

material damping
from
structural damping
from
air damping
from
contact damping.

Resonance requires a driving mechanism and compatible frequency conditions.

Do not add vibration merely because an object "looks alive".
