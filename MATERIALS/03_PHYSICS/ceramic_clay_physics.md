# CERAMIC AND CLAY PHYSICS

Important variables:

- clay mineralogy
- water content
- particle size
- temper
- porosity
- firing temperature
- firing atmosphere
- vitrification
- glaze/slip
- thickness

Relevant behavior:

- drying shrinkage
- firing shrinkage
- thermal shock
- brittle fracture
- chipping
- water absorption
- crushing
- cracking

Unfired clay and fired ceramic are different physical materials/states.
