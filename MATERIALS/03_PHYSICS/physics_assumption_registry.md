# PHYSICS ASSUMPTION REGISTRY

Every non-measured assumption must be registered.

Required:

- assumption_id
- statement
- reason
- affected material/system
- evidence
- confidence
- validity
- expected error
- downstream impact
- approval status

Assumptions are not facts.

If an assumption becomes supported by evidence, the record may be upgraded through change control.

If an assumption is disproven, affected downstream results must be identified.
