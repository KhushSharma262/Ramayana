# MATERIAL COMPOSITES AND LAYERED SYSTEMS

Many real objects are not one homogeneous material.

## Composite identity types

- metal + wood
- wood + resin
- fibre + binder
- clay + temper
- plaster + aggregate
- pigment + binder
- textile + dye
- leather + finish
- shell + metal setting
- stone + mortar
- brick + mortar
- ceramic body + slip + glaze
- painted wood
- painted plaster
- gilded surface
- lacquer/resin coated surface
- woven fibre + coating

## Required record

- composite ID
- constituent material IDs
- structural relationship
- relative layer/order
- binder/adhesive
- manufacturing process
- whether constituents remain independently identifiable
- historical evidence
- physical ownership links

A composite must never be represented as a generic texture if the constituent materials materially affect weight, deformation, fracture, wetting, heat, sound or appearance.
