# COATINGS, POLISHES AND SURFACE FINISHES

## Families

- oil finishes
- wax finishes
- resin varnish-like finishes
- shellac/lac-based finishes where historically supported
- limewash
- clay coatings
- pigment coatings
- metallic leaf
- burnished surfaces
- polished stone
- polished metal
- glazed ceramic

## Rule

A coating is an additional material layer.

Never erase the base material's identity.

Example:

wood + oil finish != oil as the material.

The database should preserve the layer stack.
