# BAMBOO, REEDS, CANE AND GRASSES

## Material families

- bamboo
- cane/rattan
- reed
- grass stems
- thatching grasses
- woven grass/reed material

## Identity

These are plant structural materials, but their anatomy and mechanical behavior differ from ordinary timber.

## Uses to investigate

- poles
- baskets
- mats
- roofs/thatching
- screens
- containers
- furniture
- weapons/tools where evidence supports
- temporary structures

## Production rule

Do not represent bamboo as generic cylindrical wood. Hollow internodes, nodes, fibre orientation and wall thickness are fundamental identity features.
