# MATERIAL RESEARCH QUEUE

This is a controlled exception list, not a dumping ground.

Each unresolved material must have:

- temporary ID
- source term or scene requirement
- suspected material family
- why identification matters
- possible identities
- missing evidence
- priority
- downstream impact
- owner folder
- resolution status

Statuses:

OPEN
UNDER_RESEARCH
PROVISIONAL
RESOLVED
REJECTED
SUPERSEDED

An unresolved item must never silently become a production truth.


## MATERIALS_ONE_PASS_RESEARCH_WORKFLOW

Every research item must contain:
- research ID and affected material/feature ID, if assigned;
- precise question and production consequence;
- evidence class and source reference;
- measurement or comparison method where applicable;
- uncertainty and validity context;
- priority, owner, reviewer, and next action;
- closure evidence and resolution rationale.

Permitted statuses: OPEN, UNDER_RESEARCH, PROVISIONAL, RESOLVED, REJECTED, SUPERSEDED.

Do not close an item merely because a search was performed. RESOLVED requires evidence that
answers the stated question. If evidence remains inadequate, keep the item OPEN or PROVISIONAL
and state what would resolve it.
