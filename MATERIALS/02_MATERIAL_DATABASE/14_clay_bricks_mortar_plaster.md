# CLAY, BRICK, MORTAR AND PLASTER

## Clay

Fine-grained natural earth material dominated by clay minerals.

Identity depends on mineralogy, impurities, water content and processing.

## Earthen brick / mud brick

Earth-based building unit shaped and dried, potentially with plant temper or other additives. Do not assume fired ceramic behavior.

## Fired brick

Clay-rich material transformed through firing. Firing temperature and recipe affect resulting ceramic identity.

## Lime

Calcium-based mineral binder. Lime terminology must distinguish raw limestone, quicklime, hydrated lime and lime-based plaster/mortar.

## Gypsum

Calcium-sulfate mineral and binder material where historically supported.

## Mortar

A composite construction material consisting of binder + aggregate + water and possibly additives.

Examples:

- lime mortar
- clay mortar
- gypsum-based material

## Plaster

A surface-applied composite layer. It must not be treated as generic "paint."

## Historical note

Ancient Indian archaeology documents mud brick, fired brick, plaster/binder technologies and controlled pottery firing. Exact Ramayana-period construction recipes require separate evidence.
