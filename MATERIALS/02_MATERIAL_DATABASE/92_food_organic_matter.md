# FOOD AND ORGANIC MATTER

## Why this exists

Food is a physical material in close-up shots and can affect:

- moisture
- viscosity
- deformation
- staining
- adhesion
- spoilage
- heat
- steam
- residue

## Material families to support when scenes require them

- grains
- rice
- wheat/barley/millets
- pulses
- fruits
- vegetables
- roots/tubers
- cooked starches
- milk/curd/butter/ghee
- honey
- oils
- cooked meat where narratively required
- dried foods
- fermented materials

## Rule

Food identity belongs primarily to the relevant scene/prop/culture research. This file prevents food from being incorrectly represented as generic coloured solids/liquids.
