# COPPER ALLOYS

## Bronze

Bronze is a family of copper-based alloys, conventionally associated primarily with tin, but historical bronze compositions can vary.

Do not encode one modern recipe as universal ancient bronze.

## Arsenical copper / arsenical bronze

Copper containing arsenic may occur through ore composition or deliberate alloying. The production history must be established rather than assumed.

## Brass

Copper-zinc alloy. Do not use modern brass as a default ancient material merely because it resembles golden metal.

## Other copper alloys

Historical copper alloys may contain lead, arsenic, tin, zinc or other elements.

## Production rule

For every copper-alloy asset:

- identify alloy family
- identify evidence for the period
- identify whether composition is measured, inferred or unknown
- keep uncertainty

Detailed alloy mechanics belong in `03_PHYSICS`.
