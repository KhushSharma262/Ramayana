# PLANT-DERIVED RAW MATERIALS

## Scope

Plant-derived materials that can become physical production materials but are not adequately represented by timber or textile fibres.

Families include:

- leaves
- bark
- stems
- roots
- seeds
- pods
- flowers
- fruits
- sap
- latex
- plant pith
- plant thorns
- husks
- shells of plant fruits
- dried plant matter
- straw
- thatch

## Production rule

A plant part used as a material must be identified by botanical source when possible.

A generic "leaf" material is insufficient for close-up production when species matters.
