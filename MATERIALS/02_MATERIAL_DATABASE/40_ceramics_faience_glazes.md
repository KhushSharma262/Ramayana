# CERAMICS, EARTHENWARE, FAience AND GLAZES

## Ceramic families

- unfired clay
- earthenware
- terracotta
- fired clay
- stoneware-like historical ceramics where evidence supports
- porcelain is not a generic ancient Indian ceramic and must not be assumed
- faience
- glazed ceramic
- painted ceramic

## Terracotta

Fired clay material. Its porosity and firing history must be represented.

## Faience

Artificially produced silica-rich material with a glassy/glazed surface; historically used for beads and ornaments in South Asia.

Do not equate faience with transparent modern glass.

## Glaze

A vitreous surface layer formed through firing. Glaze identity depends on composition and firing conditions.

## Rule

Clay, fired ceramic, glaze and paint are separate material layers.
