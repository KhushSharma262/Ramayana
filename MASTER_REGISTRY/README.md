# MASTER REGISTRY

The MASTER REGISTRY is the cross-system index for the production.

It connects:

CANON
CHARACTERS
LOCATIONS
ARCHITECTURE
COSTUMES
PROPS
ANIMALS
NATURE
ENVIRONMENT
ACTING
PERFORMANCE
AUDIO
CINEMATOGRAPHY
EDITING
VFX
COMPOSITING
AI
EPISODES
LEGAL
RENDER
DELIVERY

It does not duplicate their content.

Its purpose is to answer:

- What exists?
- What is its unique ID?
- Where is its source of truth?
- Where is it used?
- What does it depend on?
- What depends on it?
- What version is active?
- Is it approved?
- Is it locked?
- Is it legally cleared?
- Is it continuous?
- Is its AI provenance known?
- Are there unresolved problems?

MASTER_REGISTRY is therefore the project's cross-domain control layer.
