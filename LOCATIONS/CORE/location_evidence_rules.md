# LOCATION EVIDENCE AND RECONSTRUCTION

Every significant spatial claim must have a classification.

## DIRECT_SOURCE

Explicitly established by the primary source.

## TRADITIONAL_SOURCE

Established by a recognized traditional/commentarial/iconographic
tradition.

## SECONDARY_SOURCE

Supported by reputable secondary research.

## RECONSTRUCTION

Necessary production reconstruction where the sources do not provide
sufficient spatial detail.

## DIRECTORIAL_CHOICE

A deliberate filmmaking decision.

A reconstruction or directorial choice must never be written as
though it were directly stated by the source.

## Conflict handling

When sources disagree:

1. Record the disagreement.
2. Identify each source.
3. Do not silently merge incompatible versions.
4. Apply the project's CANON hierarchy.
5. Record the production decision.
6. Preserve the rejected alternatives in the decision log.

The location dossier describes what was selected for production.
The CANON system remains authoritative for scriptural interpretation.
