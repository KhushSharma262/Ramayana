# NARRATION QA

## Source

- source identified
- adaptation identified
- original verse preserved where required
- no unsupported claim

## Narrator

- correct narrator
- correct narrative layer
- correct voice
- correct relationship/context

## Placement

- correct scene
- correct sequence
- correct entry point
- correct exit point
- no unnecessary overlap

## Performance

- natural pacing
- appropriate pauses
- appropriate emphasis
- no robotic delivery
- no excessive theatricality
- pronunciation correct

## Continuity

- previous narration connects correctly
- no information repeated unnecessarily
- no contradiction with visual story
- no contradiction with dialogue

## AI

- voice consistency
- script consistency
- no invented words
- no pronunciation drift
- no narrative-layer drift

## Final question

If the narration is removed, does the scene become genuinely
unclear?

If NO, determine whether the narration is actually necessary.
