# NARRATION SYSTEM

NARRATION controls the narrative voice layer of the production.

It defines:

- who is narrating
- when narration occurs
- why narration occurs
- what information narration may communicate
- how narration connects scenes
- how different narrative layers interact
- how scripture/verse is introduced
- how narration is performed
- how narration remains continuous

NARRATION does NOT replace:

CANON
SCRIPT
LANGUAGE
AUDIO
ACTING
EDITING

## Narrative hierarchy

CANON
    ↓
STORY INFORMATION
    ↓
SCRIPT
    ↓
NARRATION
    ↓
VOICE / AUDIO
    ↓
EDITING

## Core principle

Narration should add necessary narrative information,
not describe everything visible on screen.

Prefer:

VISUAL STORYTELLING
        ↓
DIALOGUE
        ↓
SCRIPTURAL VERSE
        ↓
NARRATION

when each can communicate the required information naturally.

## Episode 1 narrator layers

The production distinguishes:

TULSIDAS
    = authorial / framing narrative layer

SHIVA
    = in-world narrator who narrates Rama-katha to Parvati

CHARACTERS
    = dialogue and lived action

These layers must not be accidentally merged.
