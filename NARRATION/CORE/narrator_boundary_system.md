# NARRATOR BOUNDARY SYSTEM

The production has two distinct narrative authorities.

## PRIMARY EXTERNAL NARRATOR

TULSIDAS

Tulsidas narrates the Shiva-Sati-Parvati story from the external
authorial narrative layer.

He is not physically present inside the story world as a character
during this narration.

## SECONDARY IN-WORLD NARRATOR

SHIVA

Shiva becomes the narrator when he personally continues the
Rama-katha to Parvati.

Shiva remains a character inside the story world.

## HARD BOUNDARY

Tulsidas and Shiva must never be treated as the same narrator.

Tulsidas:
    external narrative authority

Shiva:
    character within the narrative who narrates to Parvati

## Narrative chain

TULSIDAS
    ↓
SHIVA-SATI-PARVATI STORY
    ↓
SHIVA + PARVATI
    ↓
NARRATIVE HANDOFF
    ↓
SHIVA
    ↓
RAMA-KATHA

The handoff must be explicitly established.

## Forbidden

Do not:

- make Tulsidas sound like Shiva
- make Shiva sound like an external narrator
- allow Shiva to narrate before the approved handoff
- allow Tulsidas to silently continue over Shiva's Rama-katha
- merge the two voices
- change narrative perspective without recording the transition
