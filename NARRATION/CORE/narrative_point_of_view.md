# NARRATIVE POINT OF VIEW

## TULSIDAS

Point of view:

EXTERNAL / AUTHORIAL

Narrates the Shiva-Sati-Parvati story.

His narration can establish:

- narrative context
- sequence
- transitions
- relevant background
- selected descriptive material
- selected source verses
- movement between narrative sections

## SHIVA

Point of view:

IN-WORLD / CHARACTER NARRATION

Shiva narrates to Parvati.

His narration is part of the story itself.

The Rama-katha therefore exists simultaneously as:

1. a story being told
2. Shiva speaking to Parvati
3. the narrative content being shown to the audience

The production must preserve this layered relationship.

## Important distinction

When Shiva narrates Rama-katha:

The audience should understand that Shiva is telling the story
to Parvati, rather than assuming that an anonymous external narrator
has replaced Tulsidas.

## Narrative authority

Tulsidas:
    external narrative layer

Shiva:
    in-world Rama-katha narrator

Character dialogue:
    immediate story-world speech

Original verse:
    source-text layer
