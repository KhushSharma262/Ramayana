# SHIVA AS NARRATOR

Shiva's narration is an in-world narrative layer.

He is narrating within the story world and specifically in relation
to Parvati.

His narration must therefore retain:

- relationship to Parvati
- context of the conversation
- spiritual gravity
- narrative continuity
- character identity

Shiva's narration must not suddenly sound like an external
authorial narrator.

The transition between:

SHIVA DIALOGUE
    ↓
SHIVA NARRATION
    ↓
RAMAKATHA

must be deliberate and understandable.

The Rama-katha layer must remain connected to the framing scene
rather than becoming an unexplained voice-over.
