# SHIVA - NARRATIVE ROLE

Shiva is the SECONDARY / IN-WORLD NARRATOR.

Shiva does not narrate the Shiva-Sati-Parvati story as an external
voice.

Instead, after the approved narrative handoff, Shiva continues
the story by narrating Rama-katha to Parvati.

This makes Shiva's narration part of the story world.

Narrative structure:

TULSIDAS
    ↓
Shiva-Sati-Parvati story
    ↓
Shiva-Parvati
    ↓
SHIVA
    ↓
Rama-katha

Shiva's narrative identity must remain connected to his relationship
with Parvati.
