# WHEN SHIVA NARRATES

Shiva narrates after the approved Tulsidas-to-Shiva handoff.

Primary function:

Continue the narrative through Rama-katha while speaking to Parvati.

Shiva's narration must remain:

- in-world
- connected to Parvati
- consistent with his character
- consistent with the approved source/script
- distinct from Tulsidas's external narration

Shiva does not become a generic voice-over narrator.

The Rama-katha is presented as Shiva's narration to Parvati.
