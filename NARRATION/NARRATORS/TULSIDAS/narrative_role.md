# TULSIDAS - NARRATIVE ROLE

Tulsidas is the PRIMARY EXTERNAL NARRATOR of the production's
Shiva-Sati-Parvati narrative layer.

For the planned narrative architecture:

TULSIDAS
    ↓
narrates Shiva-Sati-Parvati story
    ↓
reaches Shiva-Parvati narrative situation
    ↓
hands narrative continuation to Shiva

Tulsidas is not merely an opening narrator.

He carries the external narration of the Shiva-Sati-Parvati portion
until the approved handoff.

Tulsidas must remain distinct from Shiva.

He does not become a character speaking from inside the story.
