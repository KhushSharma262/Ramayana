# TULSIDAS NARRATOR

Tulsidas is treated as a distinct authorial narrative voice.

The production must preserve the distinction between:

- source text
- adaptation
- connective narration
- directorial choice

Tulsidas narration must not sound like:

- a modern YouTube narrator
- a news reader
- a trailer announcer
- a documentary presenter
- an omniscient modern commentator

The performance should be rooted in the devotional and literary
character of the source while remaining intelligible to the audience.

When original Sanskrit or Awadhi verse is used, the source text
must remain identifiable.

Any connective Hindi prose created for the adaptation must be
identified as adaptation rather than falsely presented as an
original Tulsidas verse.
