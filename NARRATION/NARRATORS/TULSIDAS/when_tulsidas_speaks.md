# WHEN TULSIDAS SPEAKS

Tulsidas may narrate:

- the Shiva-Sati-Parvati narrative
- necessary narrative transitions
- relevant context
- selected descriptions
- selected verse introductions
- approved connective material

Tulsidas continues as the external narrator until the approved
handoff to Shiva.

After Shiva assumes the Rama-katha:

Tulsidas does not silently continue underneath Shiva's narration
unless the script explicitly returns to the external narrative layer.
