# NARRATION PERFORMANCE

Narration performance controls delivery rather than rewriting the text.

Track:

- breathing
- pauses
- cadence
- rhythm
- emphasis
- sentence duration
- silence
- emotional restraint
- reverence
- gravity
- intimacy
- intensity

Do not apply one emotional setting to the entire narration.

Narrative importance, scene context and narrator identity determine
delivery.

Silence is an intentional part of narration.

Not every sentence requires continuous speech.
