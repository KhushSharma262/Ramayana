# NARRATION

## Production narrative architecture

The series uses two primary narration layers.

### TULSIDAS

Primary external narrator.

Tulsidas narrates the Shiva-Sati-Parvati story.

### SHIVA

Secondary in-world narrator.

Shiva takes over narration and continues with Rama-katha,
speaking to Parvati.

## Master flow

TULSIDAS
    ↓
SHIVA-SATI-PARVATI STORY
    ↓
SHIVA + PARVATI
    ↓
TULSIDAS → SHIVA HANDOFF
    ↓
SHIVA
    ↓
RAMA-KATHA
    ↓
SHIVA continues telling the story to PARVATI

## Critical rule

The Tulsidas-to-Shiva handoff is a deliberate narrative event.

It must remain synchronized across:

- script
- voice
- audio
- editing
- subtitles
- continuity
- AI generation

Tulsidas and Shiva are never interchangeable narrators.

## Domain separation

CANON
    = source truth

SCRIPT
    = exact approved words

LANGUAGE
    = linguistic rules

NARRATION
    = narrator identity, narrative layer, placement and handoff

AUDIO
    = recorded/generated sound

EDITING
    = placement in the final film
