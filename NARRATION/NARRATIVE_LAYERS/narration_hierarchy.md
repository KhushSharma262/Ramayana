# NARRATION LAYERS

## AUTHORIAL NARRATION

Narration originating from the narrative/authorial layer.

For this production, Tulsidas is treated as a distinct narrator layer
when the adaptation calls for authorial narration.

## IN-WORLD NARRATION

Narration spoken by a character within the story world.

Episode 1 includes Shiva narrating Rama-katha to Parvati.

## CHARACTER RECOLLECTION

A character may recount an event from their own experience.

This must not automatically be treated as authorial narration.

## DIALOGUE

Characters speaking to one another in the story.

Dialogue and narration must remain distinguishable.

## VERSE

Original scriptural/poetic material has its own handling rules.

Do not silently rewrite source verses as narration.

## Priority

The production must know which narrative layer is active
before generating a narration segment.
