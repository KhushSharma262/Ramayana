# AI NARRATION RULES

AI-generated narration must preserve:

- narrator identity
- voice identity
- pronunciation
- pacing
- emotional character
- recording continuity
- approved script
- source attribution

AI must not:

- invent lines
- paraphrase locked verses
- change names
- change relationships
- change narrative perspective
- merge Tulsidas and Shiva
- introduce modern commentary

Every generated narration segment must have provenance.
