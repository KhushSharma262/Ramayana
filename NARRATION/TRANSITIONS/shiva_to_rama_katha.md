# SHIVA TO RAMA-KATHA

Shiva's narration must transition from his immediate interaction
with Parvati into the Rama-katha.

The production must preserve:

SHIVA
    ↓
SPEAKING TO PARVATI
    ↓
NARRATIVE CONTINUATION
    ↓
RAMA-KATHA

The audience must understand that the Rama-katha is being narrated
by Shiva to Parvati.

This is not an anonymous voice-over.

The exact wording and verse placement are controlled by the
approved script and CANON records.
