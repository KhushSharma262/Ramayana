# RAMA-KATHA TO FRAME STORY

If the production later returns from Shiva's Rama-katha to the
external frame narrative:

SHIVA
    ↓
RAMA-KATHA PAUSE / COMPLETION
    ↓
FRAME STORY
    ↓
TULSIDAS

The transition must be explicitly written into the relevant
episode narration map.

Do not switch automatically because of an edit.
