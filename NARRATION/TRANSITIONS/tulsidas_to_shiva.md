# TULSIDAS TO SHIVA

## Purpose

Defines the transition from Tulsidas's external narration to
Shiva's in-world narration.

## Narrative sequence

TULSIDAS
    ↓
Shiva-Sati-Parvati story
    ↓
Parvati's journey and Shiva-Parvati narrative
    ↓
Shiva-Parvati conversation
    ↓
approved handoff
    ↓
SHIVA NARRATION
    ↓
RAMA-KATHA

## Tulsidas boundary

The final Tulsidas narration before the handoff must be explicitly
identified in the Episode 1 narration map.

## Shiva entry

Shiva's first narration after the handoff must be explicitly
identified.

## Critical rule

Do not overlap the two narrator identities in a way that makes
the audience uncertain who is speaking.

## Voice distinction

Tulsidas:
    external narrator

Shiva:
    character speaking within the story

Their vocal identity, delivery and narrative position must remain
distinct.
