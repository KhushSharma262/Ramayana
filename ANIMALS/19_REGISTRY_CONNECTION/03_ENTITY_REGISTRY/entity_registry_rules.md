# ENTITY REGISTRY

Entity registry identifies persistent entities.

Examples:
- animal species/taxonomic entity
- individual animal
- human-associated animal
- population
- ecological entity
- object when materially relevant
- scene entity

Entity registration does not determine biological truth.

Broad terms such as "monkey", "deer", "bird", "snake", or "fish"
must remain unresolved unless an authoritative system has resolved them.

Unknown taxonomy remains UNKNOWN.
