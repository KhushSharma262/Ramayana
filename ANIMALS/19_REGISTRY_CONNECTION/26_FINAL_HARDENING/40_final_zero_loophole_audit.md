# FINAL ZERO-LOOPHOLE AUDIT

The Registry must prevent:

- duplicate identity
- identity guessing
- orphan references
- stale references
- wrong snapshot resolution
- version mismatch
- scope leakage
- authority inversion
- truth creation
- AI contamination
- proximity-based inference
- causality invention
- continuity drift
- silent replacement
- conflict guessing
- QA bypass
- production handoff bypass
- rejection reuse
- lineage loss
- circular dependencies
- historical mutation
- status confusion
- forward audit failure
- reverse audit failure

No known architecture-level or pipeline-level registry loophole may remain before freeze.
