# HUSBANDRY INDEX

## DOMAIN

Husbandry describes human management and care of domestic animals.

## AREAS

- feeding
- watering
- grooming
- cleaning
- housing
- stabling
- pasture
- tethering
- milking
- breeding
- offspring management
- hoof/foot care
- veterinary care
- rest
- transport
- waste management

## RULE

Husbandry must be historically and culturally appropriate to the selected
world state.
