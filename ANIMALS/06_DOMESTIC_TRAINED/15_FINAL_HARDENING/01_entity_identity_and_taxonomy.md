# ENTITY IDENTITY AND TAXONOMY

Every production-relevant animal requires a stable identity.

## ENTITY TYPES

- species
- domestic population
- individual animal
- human handler
- equipment
- training program
- work role
- husbandry system
- historical practice
- continuity state

## BIOLOGICAL IDENTITY

Species-level records require:

species_id:
accepted_name:
scientific_name:
taxonomy_authority:
taxonomy_version:
synonyms:
geographic_scope:
temporal_scope:

## INDIVIDUAL IDENTITY

Important recurring animals require:

animal_id:
species_id:
origin:
sex:
age:
owner:
handler:
training:
equipment:
continuity_state:

## HARD RULE

Common names are not sufficient for species-specific historical claims.

Unresolved identity → BLOCKED.
