# EVIDENCE / INFERENCE FIREWALL

## Evidence hierarchy

Every claim must distinguish:

SOURCE
EVIDENCE
INTERPRETATION
INFERENCE
DECISION
APPROVAL

## Forbidden conversions

INFERENCE != EVIDENCE

PLAUSIBLE != SUPPORTED

COMMON != HISTORICALLY ESTABLISHED

MODERN PRACTICE != ANCIENT PRACTICE

AI IMAGE != EVIDENCE

FILM DEPICTION != EVIDENCE

POPULAR CULTURE != EVIDENCE

UNSPECIFIED != ABSENT

## Uncertainty

Uncertainty must remain attached to the claim.

It must not disappear when the claim enters:

- individual records
- scene records
- prompts
- assets
- continuity
- final shots

## Production

Unsupported claims are BLOCKED.
