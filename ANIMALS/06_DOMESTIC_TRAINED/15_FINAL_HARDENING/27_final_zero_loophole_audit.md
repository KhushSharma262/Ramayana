# FINAL ZERO-LOOPHOLE AUDIT

The Domestic / Trained architecture is valid only when all controls below are satisfied.

## Identity

- unique animal_id
- species identity resolved or explicitly unresolved
- individual/population distinction
- permanent identity
- replacement control
- death control
- duplicate prevention

## Domestication

- wild distinguished from domesticated
- taming distinguished from domestication
- captivity distinguished from domestication
- feral distinguished from wild
- semi-domestic state controlled

## Training

- training state explicitly defined
- progression controlled
- training history preserved
- retraining controlled
- retirement controlled
- training does not erase species behavior

## Roles

- working
- mounted
- riding
- military
- ceremonial
- royal
- companion
- draft
- pack
- herding
- guard

are contextual roles, not biological identities.

## Human Relationships

- owner
- trainer
- rider
- keeper
- handler
- herder
- trusted handler
- feared handler

remain independently controlled.

## Equipment

- equipment has independent identity
- compatibility checked
- assignment controlled
- transfer controlled
- historical equipment controlled
- continuity preserved
- equipment cannot imply training

## Historical Reconstruction

- temporal context controlled
- geographical context controlled
- modern practice cannot silently enter ancient scenes
- historical evidence separated from inference
- canonical evidence separated from archaeological evidence
- plausible is not supported
- unknown is not absent

## Biology

Biology remains authoritative for:

- anatomy
- morphology
- physiology
- health
- reproduction
- biological limits

## Behavior

Behavior remains authoritative for:

- behavioral state
- motivation
- fear
- stress
- social behavior
- learning
- communication

## Movement

Movement remains authoritative for:

- biomechanics
- gait
- load
- balance
- equipment effects
- physical execution

## Ecology

Ecology remains authoritative for:

- habitat
- resources
- abundance
- distribution
- seasonality
- environmental constraints

## Scene

Scenes consume approved snapshots.

Scenes cannot silently rewrite canonical state.

## AI

AI-generated:

- images
- videos
- prompts
- concept art
- visual references

are never evidence.

## Continuity

- previous snapshot preserved
- state transitions recorded
- equipment continuity preserved
- handler continuity preserved
- replacement continuity preserved
- rollback supported
- invalidated states cannot remain production-valid

## Evidence

Every important claim has:

SOURCE
→ EVIDENCE
→ INTERPRETATION
→ INFERENCE where required
→ DECISION
→ APPROVAL
→ SNAPSHOT

## Fail Closed

Any unresolved critical dependency results in:

PRODUCTION = BLOCKED

## Final Rule

If a loophole permits an unsupported animal state to reach the final shot without traceable evidence and approval:

ARCHITECTURE = INVALID
