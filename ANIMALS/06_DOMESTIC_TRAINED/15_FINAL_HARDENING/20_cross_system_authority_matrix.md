# CROSS-SYSTEM AUTHORITY MATRIX

## Purpose

Define exactly which animal system owns which type of truth.

No system may silently overwrite another system's authority.

---

# BIOLOGY

## Biology Owns

- taxonomy
- anatomy
- morphology
- body structure
- physiological limits
- age-related biological changes
- sex biology
- reproductive biology
- growth
- biological development
- physical health constraints

## Biology Does NOT Own

- training history
- human relationships
- work roles
- equipment assignment
- cinematic framing
- prompt generation

---

# BEHAVIOR

## Behavior Owns

- behavioral state
- behavioral motivation
- social behavior
- fear
- aggression
- curiosity
- learning behavior
- communication
- species-typical behavioral expression
- behavioral response to stress
- behavioral response to humans

## Behavior Does NOT Own

- anatomical limits
- equipment ownership
- historical equipment reconstruction
- cinematic rendering
- AI generation

---

# MOVEMENT

## Movement Owns

- physical movement execution
- locomotion
- gait
- acceleration
- turning
- stopping
- balance
- load mechanics
- physical equipment interaction
- movement restrictions
- biomechanical consequences of equipment
- terrain interaction

## Movement Does NOT Own

- training history
- domestication status
- human relationship
- historical evidence
- habitat ecology
- visual generation

---

# ECOLOGY

## Ecology Owns

- habitat
- environment
- resources
- distribution
- abundance
- population context
- seasonality
- climate
- water
- vegetation
- ecological interactions
- human-wildlife ecological context

## Ecology Does NOT Own

- individual training
- equipment assignment
- handler relationships
- detailed biomechanics
- AI generation

---

# DOMESTIC / TRAINED

## Domestic / Trained Owns

- domestication status
- taming context
- habituation context
- training history
- training progression
- work role
- human-animal relationship context
- equipment assignment
- equipment continuity
- husbandry context
- historical animal-use reconstruction
- domestic-use continuity
- training continuity
- handler continuity

## Domestic / Trained Does NOT Own

- anatomy
- physiological limits
- species taxonomy
- detailed biomechanics
- ecological habitat truth
- biological safety limits
- independent behavioral truth

---

# AI PRODUCTION

## AI Production Owns

- generation
- rendering
- asset construction
- prompt implementation
- visual production

## AI Production Does NOT Own

- biological truth
- behavioral truth
- movement truth
- ecological truth
- historical truth
- domestication truth
- training truth
- animal identity

AI-generated material is never authoritative evidence.

---

# CONFLICT RESOLUTION

When two systems disagree:

1. identify the disputed claim
2. identify the owning system
3. inspect the claim provenance
4. inspect supporting evidence
5. invalidate the non-authoritative interpretation
6. resolve the contradiction
7. create a corrected approved snapshot
8. propagate the correction downstream

No downstream system may silently override an upstream authoritative claim.

---

# EXAMPLES

## Training vs Biology

Domestic / Trained:

"Animal is trained to perform this task."

Biology:

"Animal's body cannot safely perform the required load."

Result:

BIOLOGY WINS.

Production = BLOCKED unless the task is changed.

---

## Training vs Movement

Domestic / Trained:

"Animal is trained for mounted work."

Movement:

"The proposed equipment/load prevents the required movement."

Result:

MOVEMENT WINS on physical execution.

Domestic / Trained retains the training/use context.

---

## Training vs Behavior

Domestic / Trained:

"Animal has advanced training."

Behavior:

"Animal is currently frightened and unlikely to obey the command."

Result:

Both states remain valid.

Training history does not erase immediate behavioral state.

---

## Domestic Use vs Ecology

Domestic / Trained:

"Animals are used for grazing."

Ecology:

"The specific landscape/season/resource conditions cannot support the proposed concentration."

Result:

ECOLOGY WINS on environmental carrying capacity.

---

## Historical Use vs Biology

Historical reconstruction:

"Historical sources describe this use."

Biology:

"The proposed physical implementation exceeds the animal's biological limits."

Historical existence of a practice does not override biological reality.

---

## Historical Use vs Movement

Historical reconstruction:

"Equipment/use is historically supported."

Movement:

"The proposed physical execution is incompatible with the animal's biomechanics."

Result:

Historical context may remain supported, but the physically impossible implementation is BLOCKED.

---

# AUTHORITY PRIORITY

Authority is claim-specific, not a single universal ranking.

For a claim about:

TAXONOMY → BIOLOGY

ANATOMY → BIOLOGY

PHYSIOLOGICAL LIMIT → BIOLOGY

BEHAVIOR → BEHAVIOR

MOTIVATION → BEHAVIOR

GAIT → MOVEMENT

LOAD MECHANICS → MOVEMENT

HABITAT → ECOLOGY

RESOURCE AVAILABILITY → ECOLOGY

DOMESTICATION → DOMESTIC / TRAINED

TRAINING → DOMESTIC / TRAINED

HANDLER RELATIONSHIP → DOMESTIC / TRAINED

EQUIPMENT ASSIGNMENT → DOMESTIC / TRAINED

HISTORICAL ANIMAL USE → DOMESTIC / TRAINED + historical evidence controls

VISUAL RENDERING → AI PRODUCTION

ANIMAL IDENTITY → CANONICAL ANIMAL STATE

---

# NO SILENT OVERRIDE

A downstream prompt cannot change:

- species
- anatomy
- biological limits
- behavior
- movement capability
- habitat
- domestication
- training
- identity

merely because the desired shot requires it.

If a creative requirement conflicts with an authoritative constraint, the conflict must be explicitly resolved.

---

# FAIL-CLOSED RULE

If ownership of a claim is unclear:

CLAIM_OWNER = UNRESOLVED

If the disputed claim affects production:

PRODUCTION = BLOCKED

No system may claim ownership merely because it contains the relevant text.

---

# MASTER RULE

Each system owns its domain of truth.

Systems may exchange information.

Systems may depend on one another.

Systems may influence one another.

But no system may silently rewrite another system's authoritative truth.
