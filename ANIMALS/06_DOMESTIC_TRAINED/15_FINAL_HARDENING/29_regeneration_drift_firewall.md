# REGENERATION DRIFT FIREWALL

## Purpose

Prevent AI regeneration from silently changing an established animal.

## Regeneration Must Preserve

Where continuity requires it:

- animal_id
- species
- identity
- sex
- life stage
- approved physical identifiers
- domestication state
- training state
- role
- human relationships
- equipment
- equipment condition
- injuries
- fatigue
- relevant continuity state

## Allowed Visual Variation

Natural variation may occur in:

- minor posture
- expression
- lighting response
- fur/hair arrangement
- temporary dirt
- minor environmental state

provided it does not contradict the approved state.

## Forbidden Drift

Regeneration must not silently change:

- species
- identity
- breed/type classification
- age
- sex
- training level
- role
- equipment
- handler
- injury
- continuity
- historical context

## Comparison

Before accepting a regenerated asset:

APPROVED SNAPSHOT
vs
GENERATED ASSET

must be checked.

## Drift

If the generated asset conflicts with the snapshot:

ASSET = REJECTED

Do not modify the canonical state merely to accept a generated asset.

## Master Rule

THE MODEL MUST FOLLOW THE ANIMAL STATE.

THE ANIMAL STATE MUST NOT FOLLOW THE MODEL.
