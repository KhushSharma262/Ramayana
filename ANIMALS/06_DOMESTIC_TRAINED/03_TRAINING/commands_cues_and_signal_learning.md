## HARD RULE

A cue must be learned before the animal can be expected to respond reliably.

## INDIVIDUAL VARIATION

Two animals of the same species may respond differently to the same cue.

## PRODUCTION

AI generation must not invent cue-response relationships that were never
established in the approved animal state.
