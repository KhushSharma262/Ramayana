## TRAINING MEMORY

Training persists unless evidence or continuity records establish:

- loss of conditioning
- prolonged neglect
- injury
- age-related change
- retraining
- deliberate de-training
- major environmental change

## HARD RULE

A trained animal cannot randomly revert to untrained behavior without
a documented reason.

## HARD LIMIT

Training cannot override biological impossibility.
