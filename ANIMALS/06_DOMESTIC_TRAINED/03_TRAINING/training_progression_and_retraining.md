# TRAINING PROGRESSION AND RETRAINING

## TRAINING IS TEMPORAL

Training can improve, plateau, deteriorate, or be restored.

## POSSIBLE TRANSITION

UNTRAINED
→ TAMING
→ HABITUATION
→ BASIC
→ INTERMEDIATE
→ ADVANCED
→ SPECIALIZED
→ WORK_READY

Possible later transitions:

WORK_READY
→ NEGLECTED
→ CONDITIONING_LOSS
→ RETRAINING
→ RESTORED
