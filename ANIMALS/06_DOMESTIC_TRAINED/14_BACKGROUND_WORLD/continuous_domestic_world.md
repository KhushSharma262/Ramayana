# CONTINUOUS DOMESTIC WORLD

## PURPOSE

Domestic animals must exist as living populations rather than isolated props.

## BACKGROUND SYSTEMS

- village livestock
- grazing herds
- pack animals
- transport animals
- household animals
- working animals
- poultry
- guard animals
- camp animals
- market animals
- agricultural animals

## NATURAL VARIATION

Animals may differ in:

- age
- sex
- size
- condition
- training
- work status
- equipment
- handler relationship

## RULE

Background animals must still obey the approved domestic/ecological context.
