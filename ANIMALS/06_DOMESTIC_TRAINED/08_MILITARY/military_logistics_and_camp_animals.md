# MILITARY LOGISTICS AND CAMP ANIMALS

## FUNCTIONS

- carrying supplies
- pulling equipment
- transport
- camp support
- food production
- water logistics
- messenger functions

## ECOLOGY CONNECTION

Large concentrations of military animals create ecological consequences:

- fodder demand
- water demand
- grazing pressure
- waste
- disease risk
- landscape disturbance

These consequences must be handed to Ecology.

## RULE

Military animal populations cannot be treated as decorative background.
