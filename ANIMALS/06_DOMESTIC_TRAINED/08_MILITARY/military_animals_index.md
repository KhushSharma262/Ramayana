# MILITARY ANIMALS

## DOMAIN

Dedicated system for animals used in warfare, royal forces, military
transport, logistics, scouting, messaging, baggage, and camp systems.

## POSSIBLE ROLES

- war elephant
- cavalry horse
- messenger horse
- baggage animal
- pack animal
- supply animal
- camp animal
- transport animal
- scouting animal

## RULE

Military status does not automatically imply combat behavior.

Behavior and Movement remain authoritative for actual physical and behavioral
manifestation.
