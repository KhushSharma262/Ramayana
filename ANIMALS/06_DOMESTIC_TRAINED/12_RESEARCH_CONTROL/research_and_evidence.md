# DOMESTIC / TRAINED RESEARCH AND EVIDENCE

## SOURCE → CLAIM

Every important claim requires:

source_id:
evidence_id:
claim_id:
interpretation:
confidence:
applicability:
temporal_scope:
geographic_scope:

## AI FIREWALL

AI images, videos, prompts, generated text, generated animal behavior,
and generated reconstructions are NOT biological, historical, or training
evidence.

## SOURCE INDEPENDENCE

Multiple sources derived from one source do not count as independent evidence.

## NEGATIVE EVIDENCE

No record ≠ absence.

## UNKNOWN

UNKNOWN is a valid result.

## HARD RULE

Unsupported domestic/training practices remain UNKNOWN or BLOCKED.
