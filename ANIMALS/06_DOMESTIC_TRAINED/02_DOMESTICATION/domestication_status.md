# DOMESTICATION STATUS

## REQUIRED DISTINCTION

Individual taming does not equal species domestication.

Individual training does not equal species domestication.

Captivity does not equal domestication.

Habituation does not equal domestication.

## RULE

A species-level domestication claim requires population-level evidence.

An individual animal may be trained even when the broader population is not
fully domesticated.

## HISTORICAL CONTROL

Modern domestication status must not automatically be projected backward
into the Ramayana period.
