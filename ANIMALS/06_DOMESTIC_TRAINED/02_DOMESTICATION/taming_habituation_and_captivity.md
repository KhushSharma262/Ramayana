# TAMING, HABITUATION AND CAPTIVITY

## TAMING

Individual reduction in fear or increased tolerance.

## HABITUATION

Repeated exposure resulting in reduced response to a harmless stimulus.

## CAPTIVITY

Human containment or management.

## DOMESTICATION

Population-level evolutionary change.

## DO NOT CONFLATE

CAPTIVE ≠ TAMED

TAMED ≠ TRAINED

TRAINED ≠ DOMESTICATED

DOMESTICATED ≠ PERFECTLY OBEDIENT

## PRODUCTION RULE

The exact status of an individual must be explicitly recorded.
