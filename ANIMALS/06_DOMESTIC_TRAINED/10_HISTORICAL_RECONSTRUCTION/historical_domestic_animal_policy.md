# HISTORICAL DOMESTIC ANIMAL POLICY

## EVIDENCE HIERARCHY

1. Direct primary historical/archaeological evidence
2. Specialist peer-reviewed research
3. Government/museum/archaeological institutions
4. Specialist secondary sources
5. Ethnographic analogy
6. Expert reconstruction
7. Production inference

## INDIA-FIRST

India-specific evidence is preferred where applicable.

Then:

South Asia
→ Asia
→ broader comparative evidence

Global evidence must not silently become India-specific evidence.

## TEMPORAL CONTROL

Modern practices cannot automatically become ancient practices.

## RULE

If the exact historical practice is unknown:

SUPPORTED
PLAUSIBLE
CONTESTED
UNKNOWN
BLOCKED

Do not invent certainty.
