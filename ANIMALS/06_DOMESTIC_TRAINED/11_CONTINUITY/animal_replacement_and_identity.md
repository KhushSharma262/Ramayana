# ANIMAL REPLACEMENT AND IDENTITY

## IDENTITY STATES

- SAME_ANIMAL
- SIMILAR_ANIMAL
- REPLACEMENT_ANIMAL
- BACKGROUND_MEMBER
- UNKNOWN_IDENTITY

## RULE

Visual resemblance does not prove identity.

A recurring animal must retain its animal_id.

If identity is intentionally changed, the change must be documented.

## PRODUCTION

A regenerated asset that looks different is not automatically a new animal.

Identity is determined by continuity records, not AI appearance alone.
