# TRAINING, EQUIPMENT AND HANDLER CONTINUITY

## PERSISTENCE

Unless changed:

training state persists
equipment persists
handler relationship persists
work role persists
conditioning persists

## CROSS-SCENE CHECK

Before approving a scene:

CHECK ANIMAL
CHECK TRAINING
CHECK HANDLER
CHECK EQUIPMENT
CHECK WORK ROLE
CHECK PHYSICAL STATE

## BLOCK

If a later scene contradicts an earlier approved state without a change event.
