## PURPOSE

Model how animals exist within human-managed, domesticated, trained,
working, companion, ceremonial, agricultural, transport, military,
and husbandry systems.

## MASTER RULE

A trained animal is still an animal.

Training does not erase:

- species biology
- natural behavior
- physical limits
- fatigue
- fear
- pain
- startle responses
- individual variation
- environmental effects
