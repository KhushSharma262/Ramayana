# DOMESTIC / TRAINED DEFINITION

## DOMESTICATION

Domestication is a population-level evolutionary process.

It is NOT synonymous with:

- taming
- training
- habituation
- captivity

## TAMING

Taming refers to reduction of fear or increased tolerance in an individual.

## HABITUATION

Habituation refers to reduced response to repeated harmless exposure.

## TRAINING

Training is learned conditioning of an individual to respond to human cues,
tasks, routines, equipment, or environmental signals.

## DOMESTIC STATUS

Possible states include:

- WILD
- WILD_HABITUATED
- CAPTIVE
- TAMED
- DOMESTICATED
- BORN_DOMESTIC
- TRAINED
- WORKING
- COMPANION
- FERAL
- SEMI_DOMESTIC

These states are NOT interchangeable.
