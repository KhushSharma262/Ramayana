# HUMAN-ANIMAL TRUST AND FEAR

## STATES

- unfamiliar
- familiar
- neutral
- cooperative
- trusting
- cautious
- fearful
- conditioned_to_avoid
- stressed
- unknown

## CAUSES

Possible causes include:

- training history
- mistreatment
- unfamiliar handler
- unfamiliar environment
- equipment
- pain
- previous negative experience
- social familiarity
- routine

## HARD RULE

Trust/fear must not become an arbitrary personality label.

It must have a plausible history or contextual cause.
