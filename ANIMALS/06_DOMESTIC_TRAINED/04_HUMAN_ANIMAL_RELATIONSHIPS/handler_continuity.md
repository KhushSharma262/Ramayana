# HANDLER CONTINUITY

## PURPOSE

Maintain persistent human-animal relationships across scenes.

## REQUIRED

animal_id:
human_id:
relationship_id:
continuity_snapshot:
last_known_state:
current_state:
change_event:

## RULE

If no change event occurs, the approved relationship state persists.

## EXCEPTION

A documented event may change:

- trust
- familiarity
- training
- handling
- equipment
- work role
