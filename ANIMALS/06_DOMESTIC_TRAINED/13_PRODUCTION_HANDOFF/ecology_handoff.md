# ECOLOGY HANDOFF

## DOMESTIC_TRAINED PROVIDES

- domestic population
- herd/flock management
- grazing management
- human provisioning
- water provisioning
- livestock concentration
- resource demand

## ECOLOGY PROVIDES

- environmental resource availability
- landscape
- habitat
- wild population context
- water systems
- ecological carrying capacity

## RULE

Human-managed resources must not be confused with wild ecological resources.
