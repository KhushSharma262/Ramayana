# THREAT SEVERITY AND EXPOSURE

Severity and exposure are separate variables.

## Severity
Describes the potential magnitude of a threat.

## Exposure
Describes what the individual actually experiences.

Record:
- threat magnitude
- distance
- duration
- frequency
- directness
- sensory exposure
- physical contact
- escape availability
- refuge availability
- social buffering
- prior habituation

Stress cannot be assigned from threat severity alone.

A severe threat may cause avoidance without panic.
A moderate threat may cause severe response when escape is blocked or the animal is already injured, exhausted or highly aroused.
