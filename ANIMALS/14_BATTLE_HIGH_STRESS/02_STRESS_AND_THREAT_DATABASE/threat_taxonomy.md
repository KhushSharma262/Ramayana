# THREAT TAXONOMY

## Biological
Predator, competitor, hostile animal, disease-related threat where applicable.

## Environmental
Fire, smoke, flood, unstable terrain, extreme heat, cold, falling objects, severe weather, darkness or sudden visibility loss.

## Human-Associated
Crowds, capture, restraint, unfamiliar human activity, weapons, machinery or vehicles where historically and contextually valid.

## Social
Separation, offspring disruption, herd disruption, territorial intrusion and group collapse.

## Physical
Injury, pain, exhaustion, dehydration, overheating and confinement.

Threat classification describes the source of danger. It does NOT determine the animal's response.
