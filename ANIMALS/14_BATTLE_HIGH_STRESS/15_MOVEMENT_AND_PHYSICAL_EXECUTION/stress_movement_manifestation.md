# STRESS MOVEMENT MANIFESTATION

Stress can modify movement through:
- altered readiness
- acceleration
- hesitation
- route changes
- freezing
- evasive movement
- reduced coordination
- fatigue
- injury

Movement remains responsible for:
- gait
- acceleration
- balance
- turning
- jumping
- physical contact
- biomechanics

Battle/High-Stress supplies the approved state; Movement supplies the physical truth.
