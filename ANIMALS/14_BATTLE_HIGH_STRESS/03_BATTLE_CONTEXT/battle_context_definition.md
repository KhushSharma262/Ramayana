# BATTLE CONTEXT

Battle is an organized violent event involving human, animal or supernatural participants where animals may be directly or indirectly exposed.

## Possible Animal Roles
- mount
- draft animal
- pack animal
- transport animal
- ceremonial animal
- messenger-associated animal
- camp animal
- livestock
- displaced wildlife
- scavenger
- predator
- incidental wildlife

Presence does not imply participation.

An animal role must be independently established.
