# BATTLE PHASE AND ESCALATION

Stress events may progress through:

PRE-THREAT
-> THREAT EMERGENCE
-> INITIAL DISTURBANCE
-> ESCALATION
-> PEAK EXPOSURE
-> DISENGAGEMENT
-> AFTERMATH
-> RECOVERY

Transitions require causal events.

An animal cannot be moved from calm to peak panic merely because the shot requires dramatic intensity.

Sudden escalation is allowed only when the triggering event itself is sufficiently sudden and supported.
