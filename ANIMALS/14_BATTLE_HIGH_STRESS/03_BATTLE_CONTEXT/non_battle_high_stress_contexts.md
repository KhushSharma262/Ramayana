# NON-BATTLE HIGH-STRESS CONTEXTS

High stress is broader than warfare.

Supported contexts may include:

- predator encounters
- wildfire
- flood
- capture
- restraint
- separation
- shelter collapse
- extreme weather
- accidental injury
- stampede
- resource conflict
- severe human disturbance
- environmental collapse

These contexts must not be mislabeled as battle merely because they produce dramatic behavior.
