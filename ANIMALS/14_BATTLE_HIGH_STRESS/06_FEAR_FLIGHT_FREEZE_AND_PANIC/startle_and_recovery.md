# STARTLE AND RECOVERY

Startle is a rapid response to a sudden stimulus.

Record:
- stimulus
- latency
- immediate response
- response duration
- recovery
- residual vigilance

Startle does not automatically become panic.

Recovery depends on:
- continued threat
- species
- individual state
- environment
- injury
- fatigue
- social support

A startle response must not remain permanently exaggerated without a causal reason.
