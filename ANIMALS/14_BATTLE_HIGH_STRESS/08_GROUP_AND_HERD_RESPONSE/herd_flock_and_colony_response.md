# HERD / FLOCK / COLONY RESPONSE

Group response must reflect the actual social organization of the species.

Possible group responses:
- vigilance
- clustering
- following
- dispersal
- coordinated flight
- freezing
- regrouping
- separation
- route changes

Herd, flock and colony are not interchangeable concepts.

Species-specific social organization must be established before a group behavior is approved.
