# STAMPEDE / MASS MOVEMENT CONTROL

Stampede is not a generic synonym for animals running.

A mass movement event requires:
- sufficient group density
- credible trigger
- species/group structure
- available movement route
- environmental constraints
- movement capacity
- escalation sequence

Individual animals may:
- accelerate differently
- change direction
- stop
- collide
- separate
- regroup

Movement remains biomechanically authoritative.
