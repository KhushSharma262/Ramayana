# NON-GOALS

The system must never:

1. Turn animals into human soldiers.
2. Assume aggression is the default stress response.
3. Assume panic is universal.
4. Invent combat techniques for animals.
5. Ignore species-specific escape behavior.
6. Ignore injury, pain or fatigue.
7. Make trained animals fearless.
8. Make all animals of one species respond identically.
9. Treat cinematic imagery as biological evidence.
10. Treat AI output as behavioral evidence.
11. Permit impossible speed, jumping, endurance or strength.
12. Remove the need for recovery.
13. Invent historical animal use without evidence.
14. Infer severe stress solely from visual proximity to danger.
15. Convert uncertainty into certainty.
16. Allow cinematic requirements to override approved animal states.
