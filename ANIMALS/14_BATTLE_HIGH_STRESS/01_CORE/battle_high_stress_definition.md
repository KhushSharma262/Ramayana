# BATTLE / HIGH-STRESS DEFINITION

Battle/High-Stress is a specialized state and integration layer for circumstances in which an animal is exposed to unusually intense threat, disturbance, exertion, injury, environmental danger or social disruption.

## Covered Conditions
- battle exposure
- predator attack
- capture
- restraint
- sudden threat
- fire and smoke
- extreme noise
- crowd pressure
- separation
- injury
- pain
- exhaustion
- escape
- stampede
- environmental disaster
- post-event recovery

## Important Distinction
Battle is a context.
Stress is a biological and behavioral response.
They must never be treated as synonyms.

An animal may be present during a battle without participating in combat.
An animal may experience extreme stress without a battle.
