# MASTER STRESS PRINCIPLES

1. EXTREME STRESS CHANGES RESPONSE; IT DOES NOT CREATE A HUMAN-LIKE COMBATANT.
2. FEAR IS NOT SYNONYMOUS WITH AGGRESSION.
3. FLIGHT, FREEZE, AVOIDANCE, DEFENSE AND SOCIAL FOLLOWING ARE VALID RESPONSES.
4. EVERY STRESS RESPONSE REQUIRES A CAUSAL TRIGGER.
5. STRESS LOAD MAY ACCUMULATE ONLY WHEN TEMPORAL AND BIOLOGICAL CONDITIONS SUPPORT IT.
6. SPECIES, AGE, HEALTH, EXPERIENCE, TRAINING AND INDIVIDUAL VARIATION MATTER.
7. TRAINING MODIFIES RESPONSE BUT DOES NOT DELETE INSTINCT.
8. INJURY AND FATIGUE REDUCE AVAILABLE PERFORMANCE.
9. RECOVERY IS PART OF THE EVENT.
10. GROUP RESPONSE MUST NOT ERASE INDIVIDUAL VARIATION.
11. BATTLE CONTEXT REQUIRES HISTORICAL, CANONICAL OR SCENE EVIDENCE.
12. UNKNOWN REMAINS UNKNOWN.
13. CINEMA MAY EMPHASIZE APPROVED REALITY BUT MAY NOT CHANGE IT.
14. AI MAY REPRESENT APPROVED STATES BUT MAY NOT DEFINE THEM.
15. EVERY APPROVED STATE MUST REMAIN TRACEABLE.
