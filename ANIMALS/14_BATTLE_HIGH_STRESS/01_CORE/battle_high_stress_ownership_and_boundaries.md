# OWNERSHIP AND BOUNDARIES

## This System Owns
- high-stress event classification
- threat exposure state
- stress-load state
- escalation and de-escalation
- extreme-stress scenario integration
- recovery windows
- battle-specific stress context
- stress validation gates

## This System Does Not Own
- anatomy
- species physiology
- generic behavior
- biomechanics
- habitat
- domestication
- training
- equipment construction
- interaction identity
- continuity history
- canonical truth
- visual identity
- sound identity
- cinematic presentation
- AI generation

## Boundary Rule
This system may combine approved upstream states, but it may not redefine the truth owned by another domain.
