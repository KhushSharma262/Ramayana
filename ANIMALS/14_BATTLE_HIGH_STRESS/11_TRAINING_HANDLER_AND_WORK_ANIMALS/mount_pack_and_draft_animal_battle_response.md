# MOUNT / PACK / DRAFT ANIMAL BATTLE RESPONSE

Battle-associated working animals may experience:

- loud noise
- sudden movement
- smoke
- unfamiliar animals
- crowd pressure
- weapon-related disturbance
- rider/handler stress
- terrain disruption
- injury
- separation

Their response must remain animal behavior.

A mount does not automatically charge.
A pack animal does not automatically remain obedient.
A draft animal does not automatically continue after exhaustion.

Training, physical capacity, equipment and stress state jointly determine outcome.
