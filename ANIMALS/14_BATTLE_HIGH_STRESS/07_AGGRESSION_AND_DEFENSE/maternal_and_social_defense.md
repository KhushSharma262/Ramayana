# MATERNAL AND SOCIAL DEFENSE

Maternal or social defense may alter normal risk tolerance.

Relevant variables:
- offspring presence
- offspring age
- group structure
- threat proximity
- escape options
- individual condition
- species-specific parental behavior

The presence of offspring does not guarantee aggression.

Possible responses include:
- concealment
- relocation
- threat display
- attack
- defensive positioning
- flight
- abandonment where biologically supported

No response is selected solely for dramatic effect.
