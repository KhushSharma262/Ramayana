# DEFENSIVE BEHAVIOR

Defensive behavior is a response to perceived threat, not proof of hostility.

Possible responses:
- withdrawal
- threat display
- protective positioning
- defensive strike
- bite
- kick
- charge
- bluff
- escape
- concealment

The exact response is species-specific.

Defensive behavior must be distinguished from:
- predatory behavior
- territorial behavior
- social conflict
- trained attack behavior
- accidental collision

The Behavior system owns the behavioral motivation.
Movement owns physical execution.
