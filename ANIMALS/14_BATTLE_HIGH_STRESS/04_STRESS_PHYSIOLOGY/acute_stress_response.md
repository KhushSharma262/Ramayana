# ACUTE STRESS RESPONSE

Acute stress is a rapid response to an immediate threat.

This file does not replace Biology.

Potential approved consequences include:
- increased arousal
- altered attention
- increased respiratory demand
- increased locomotor readiness
- altered feeding behavior
- altered vigilance
- autonomic activation
- fatigue after prolonged exertion

Species-specific physiological details must come from approved biological evidence.

No invented physiological measurement may become canonical.
