# FIRE / SMOKE / HEAT STRESS

Fire and smoke can create:
- heat exposure
- respiratory difficulty
- visibility reduction
- route obstruction
- startle
- escape pressure
- dehydration
- fatigue

Ecology and Biology remain authoritative for environmental and physiological effects.

The visual presence of flames does not automatically mean an animal is physically exposed to lethal heat.
Exposure must be spatially and causally established.
