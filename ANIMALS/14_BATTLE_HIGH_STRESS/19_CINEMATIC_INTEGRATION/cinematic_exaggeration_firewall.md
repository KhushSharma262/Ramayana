# CINEMATIC EXAGGERATION FIREWALL

Cinematic emphasis is permitted only when the underlying behavior remains physically plausible.

ALLOWED:
- longer observation of an existing response
- selective framing
- controlled pacing
- visual emphasis
- heightened but plausible environmental presentation

BLOCKED:
- invented aggression
- impossible speed
- impossible jumping
- impossible endurance
- artificial panic
- synchronized animal choreography without cause
- anthropomorphic emotional acting
- injury exaggeration beyond the approved state

Cinematic intensity must never become a new biological fact.
