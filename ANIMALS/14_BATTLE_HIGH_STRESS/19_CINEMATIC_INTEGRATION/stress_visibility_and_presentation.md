# STRESS VISIBILITY / PRESENTATION

Not every internal stress state must be visually obvious.

A scene may communicate stress through:
- posture
- movement
- attention
- spacing
- breathing where observable
- vocalization where appropriate
- avoidance
- group behavior
- environmental interaction

Avoid exaggerated:
- facial expressions
- human-like crying
- human-like anger
- theatrical fear
- artificial trembling

Visual presentation must remain consistent with the approved species response.
