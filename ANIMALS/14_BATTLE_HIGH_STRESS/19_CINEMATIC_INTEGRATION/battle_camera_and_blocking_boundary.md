# BATTLE CAMERA / BLOCKING BOUNDARY

Camera blocking must be downstream of approved animal states.

Do not place an animal:
- where terrain makes its movement impossible
- where its escape route contradicts the approved ecology
- in a physical position incompatible with injury
- in a crowd density that contradicts the scene state
- in a location that creates impossible interaction geometry

Camera may reveal approved behavior from different viewpoints.

Camera cannot create behavior by implication.
