# STRESS CINEMATIC INTEGRATION

Cinema presents an approved stress state.

It may control:
- framing
- shot duration
- camera position
- lens
- composition
- visibility
- pacing
- lighting
- emphasis

It may NOT change:
- animal identity
- species behavior
- physiological limits
- injury
- fatigue
- stress causality
- ecological reality
- historical truth

CINEMA SHAPES HOW THE APPROVED STRESS STATE IS EXPERIENCED. IT DOES NOT CHANGE WHAT THE ANIMAL IS EXPERIENCING.
