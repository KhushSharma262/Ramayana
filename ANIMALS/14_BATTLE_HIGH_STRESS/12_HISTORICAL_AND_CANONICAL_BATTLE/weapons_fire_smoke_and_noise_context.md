# WEAPONS / FIRE / SMOKE / NOISE CONTEXT

Weapons and battlefield disturbances may affect animals through:
- sudden sound
- vibration
- smoke
- fire
- flashes
- movement
- crowd pressure
- debris
- physical impact

The exact effect depends on:
- species
- distance
- duration
- prior exposure
- environment
- individual state

Do not assign a universal reaction to every weapon or loud sound.

Historical technology must be period-compatible.
