# CANONICAL BATTLE CONTEXT

Canonical battle scenes must be derived from the approved textual/canonical record.

Track:
- source
- passage/event
- participants
- animal involvement
- stated action
- implied action
- interpretation
- uncertainty
- approval

Literal textual evidence and cinematic reconstruction must remain distinguishable.

An animal not mentioned in the source must not be inserted as a canonical participant merely because the scene would look more complete.
