# AI STRESS OUTPUT BOUNDARY

AI output is a representation.

It is NOT:
- behavioral evidence
- biological evidence
- historical evidence
- canonical evidence
- ecological evidence
- proof of species behavior

If generated output contradicts an approved stress state:
1. reject the output;
2. do not rewrite the approved state to fit the output;
3. regenerate or change the production implementation;
4. preserve the original approved truth.
