# ANIMAL NATURALISM AUDIT

Reject output showing:

- human-like expressions
- human-like emotional acting
- heroic posing
- artificial combat stance
- unnecessary aggression
- synchronized choreography
- impossible locomotion
- impossible reactions
- generic wildlife behavior
- species-inappropriate threat response
- unexplained vocalization
- unexplained fear
- unexplained panic

The animal must behave according to the approved state, not according to cinematic convention.
