# STRESS VALIDATION GATE

A stress asset may pass only if:

- upstream snapshots are approved
- species identity is correct
- stress trigger is causal
- stress state is correct
- behavior is species-appropriate
- movement is physically possible
- injury is consistent
- fatigue is consistent
- environment is correct
- equipment is correct
- continuity is preserved
- cinematic presentation does not alter truth
- AI has not introduced unsupported behavior

Any failed mandatory gate = BLOCKED.
