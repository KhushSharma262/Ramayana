# BATTLE REALISM AUDIT

Check:

1. Is the animal actually supposed to be present?
2. Is its role supported?
3. Is the battle environment historically/canonically valid?
4. Is the threat real within the scene?
5. Is the stress response species-appropriate?
6. Is the response physically possible?
7. Are injury and fatigue consistent?
8. Does the animal remain an animal rather than a human actor?
9. Is equipment historically/contextually valid?
10. Does the scene preserve continuity?

Any unresolved critical issue blocks approval.
