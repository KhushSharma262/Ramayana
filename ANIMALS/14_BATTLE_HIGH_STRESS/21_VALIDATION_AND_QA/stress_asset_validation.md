# STRESS ASSET VALIDATION

## Identity

visual_identity_id

## State
stress_state

injury_state
fatigue_state
equipment_state
continuity_state

## Context
scene_id
event_id
environment_id
timeline_position

## Production
prompt_id
model_id
generation_id
reference_set_id
asset_id

No final asset may bypass validation.
