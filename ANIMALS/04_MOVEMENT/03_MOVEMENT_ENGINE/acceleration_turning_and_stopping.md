# ACCELERATION TURNING AND STOPPING

## Acceleration

Must account for:
- body mass
- traction
- limb mechanics
- propulsion
- surface
- incline
- condition
- load

## Turning

Must account for:
- speed
- body mass
- limb placement
- center of mass
- traction
- body flexibility
- species mechanics

## Stopping

Must account for:
- current speed
- braking mechanics
- traction
- momentum
- surface
- body position
- load

## Hard Rule

Animals must not:
- teleport
- instantaneously reverse momentum
- make impossible turns
- stop without plausible deceleration
- exceed physical traction
- exceed species-specific mechanical limits
