# MOVEMENT HANDOFFS

## Upstream

### BIOLOGY → MOVEMENT

Provides:
- anatomy
- morphology
- body dimensions
- joints
- musculature
- physiological limits

### BEHAVIOR → MOVEMENT

Provides:
- behavioral context
- intent
- state
- movement-relevant behavior

### ECOLOGY → MOVEMENT

Provides:
- terrain
- surface
- environmental conditions
- water conditions

### DOMESTIC_TRAINED → MOVEMENT

Provides:
- training context
- working role
- riding context
- equipment context
- trained physical responses

## Downstream

### MOVEMENT → DIRECTING

Provides:
- physically valid movement possibilities
- movement timing
- physical limitations
- interaction constraints

### MOVEMENT → AI_PRODUCTION

Provides:
- movement specifications
- physical constraints
- species-specific locomotion
- validation criteria

AI_PRODUCTION may not redefine Movement truth.
