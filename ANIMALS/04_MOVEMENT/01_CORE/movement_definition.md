# MOVEMENT DEFINITION

## Definition

Animal movement is the physically observable execution of locomotion, posture, balance, body displacement and interaction with the physical environment.

## Movement Is Not

Movement is not:
- animation style
- AI prompting
- camera direction
- cinematography
- arbitrary visual exaggeration
- invented biomechanics
- generic human motion applied to animals

## Movement Sources

Movement truth may depend on:

1. Biology
2. Behavior
3. Physics
4. Environment
5. Species morphology
6. Age
7. Sex where relevant
8. Body condition
9. Training
10. Load
11. Injury
12. Fatigue
13. Individual variation

## Boundary

Behavior may establish:
- intent
- response
- behavioral movement pattern
- context

Movement establishes:
- physical execution
- biomechanics
- gait or locomotion mechanics
- body coordination
- balance
- acceleration
- turning
- stopping
- physical environmental interaction

## Production Rule

AI-generated movement must be evaluated against the approved Movement truth.

AI output can never become biological evidence.
