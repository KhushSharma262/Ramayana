# MOVEMENT SYSTEM INDEX

## Purpose

This system defines how animals physically move within the Ramayana production.

Movement represents:
- locomotion
- posture
- balance
- acceleration
- turning
- stopping
- terrain interaction
- obstacle negotiation
- water interaction
- flight
- swimming
- climbing
- jumping
- physical interaction with humans, animals and equipment
- physical effects of fatigue, injury and load

Movement must remain biologically and physically plausible unless an explicitly approved cinematic or supernatural exception exists.

## Authority Chain

BIOLOGY
→ BEHAVIOR
→ MOVEMENT
→ DIRECTING
→ AI_PRODUCTION

Movement does not replace Biology or Behavior.

## Core Principle

Movement describes physical execution and physical manifestation.

Behavior may describe movement as an observable behavioral component, but Movement owns the detailed physical mechanics.

## Scope

This system applies to animals.

Human biomechanics are outside ANIMALS.

Animal-side effects of:
- riders
- handlers
- loads
- harnesses
- yokes
- saddles
- carts
- chariots
- other equipment

may be represented where they physically affect the animal.
