# TERRAIN AND SURFACE INTERACTION

ECOLOGY owns the environment.

MOVEMENT owns how the animal physically interacts with it.

Examples:

ECOLOGY:
mud exists.

MOVEMENT:
hoof sinks,
traction decreases,
stride changes,
balance compensation occurs.

Relevant surfaces include:
- hard ground
- soft soil
- mud
- sand
- grass
- rock
- wet surfaces
- uneven terrain
- slopes
- vegetation
