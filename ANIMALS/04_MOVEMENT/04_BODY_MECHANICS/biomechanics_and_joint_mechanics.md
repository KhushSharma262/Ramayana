# BIOMECHANICS AND JOINT MECHANICS

Biology owns anatomical truth.

Movement uses biological constraints.

Do not duplicate:
- skeletal anatomy
- muscle anatomy
- joint inventory
- body dimensions

Movement records their functional consequences where necessary.

## Hard Constraints

Movement cannot:
- reverse joint mechanics
- exceed plausible range of motion
- create impossible limb articulation
- detach or distort anatomy
- use human joint mechanics for animals
