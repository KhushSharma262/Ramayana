# ANATOMY TO MOVEMENT

This document defines the interface between Biology and Movement.

BIOLOGY supplies:
- anatomy
- morphology
- proportions
- joints
- musculature
- body mass
- physiological limits

MOVEMENT translates these into:
- locomotion
- posture
- balance
- stride
- propulsion
- turning
- acceleration
- braking
- terrain interaction

No anatomical fact may be silently invented inside Movement.
