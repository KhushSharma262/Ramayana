# STRENGTH LOAD FATIGUE AND RECOVERY

Movement consequences of physical condition must be represented.

## Load

Where applicable:
- carried load
- rider
- draft load
- equipment
- uneven loading

## Fatigue

Movement may progressively change through:
- reduced speed
- altered stride
- reduced coordination
- increased recovery time
- reduced jumping/climbing capability
- altered posture

## Recovery

Recovery must be biologically plausible and context dependent.

Causes and physiological states remain upstream in Biology/Behavior where applicable.
Movement records their physical manifestations.
