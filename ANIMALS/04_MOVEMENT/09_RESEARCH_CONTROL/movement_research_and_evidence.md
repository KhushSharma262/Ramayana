# MOVEMENT RESEARCH AND EVIDENCE

Every production-relevant movement claim must have traceability.

Required chain:

SOURCE
→ EVIDENCE
→ INTERPRETATION
→ CLAIM
→ DECISION
→ APPROVAL
→ VERSION
→ SNAPSHOT

## Evidence Priority

Prefer:
1. authoritative biological literature
2. peer-reviewed research
3. authoritative species references
4. professional zoological/veterinary sources
5. documented field observation
6. secondary sources with clear provenance

AI-generated imagery/video is never biological evidence.

## Geography

India-specific evidence should be preferred where relevant.

Never silently relabel global evidence as India-specific evidence.
