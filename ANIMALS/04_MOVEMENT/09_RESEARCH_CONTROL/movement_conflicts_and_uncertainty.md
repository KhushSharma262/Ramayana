# MOVEMENT CONFLICTS AND UNCERTAINTY

Conflicts must be recorded rather than silently resolved.

Possible states:

UNKNOWN
MISSING
AMBIGUOUS
UNSUPPORTED
UNVERIFIED
CONFLICTED
STALE
UNAPPROVED
UNSNAPSHOTTED
BLOCKED

A conflict requires:
- competing claims
- sources
- interpretation
- resolution decision
- approval
- version

If unresolved, production remains BLOCKED.
