# SUPERNATURAL MOVEMENT POLICY

## Purpose

Keep ordinary zoological movement separate from mythological or supernatural movement.

## Ordinary Animal

Ordinary animals use:

BIOLOGY
+
PHYSICS
+
SPECIES MOVEMENT
+
BEHAVIOR
+
ENVIRONMENT

## Supernatural / Mythological Entity

Supernatural movement requires:

CANONICAL_SOURCE
→ CANONICAL_CAPABILITY
→ INTERPRETATION
→ PRODUCTION_DECISION
→ APPROVAL

## Hard Rule

A supernatural capability must never silently enter an ordinary species movement profile.

Examples of separate treatment may include:

- supernatural speed
- supernatural flight
- impossible size changes
- impossible movement across space
- divine manifestation
- magical transformation

## Status

Ordinary zoological movement and supernatural movement are separate authority domains.

APPROVAL_REQUIRED: YES
