# CINEMATIC EXAGGERATION POLICY

## Purpose

Define the boundary between natural animal movement and deliberate cinematic emphasis.

## Allowed

Cinematic emphasis may alter presentation only when:

- the underlying movement remains physically plausible
- species mechanics remain correct
- anatomy is respected
- acceleration remains plausible
- balance remains plausible
- contact and momentum remain plausible
- the exaggeration is explicitly documented

## Required Labels

NATURAL
CINEMATICALLY_EMPHASIZED
SUPERNATURAL

These states must never be silently interchangeable.

## Prohibited

Do not present:

- impossible acceleration
- impossible stopping
- impossible turning
- impossible joint movement
- impossible weight transfer
- impossible gait transitions

as ordinary natural movement.

## Authority

Cinematic emphasis is a production decision.

It is not biological evidence.
