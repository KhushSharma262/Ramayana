# MOVEMENT SCOPE AND OWNERSHIP

## Biology

Owns:

- anatomy
- morphology
- skeletal structure
- muscles
- joints
- proportions
- physiological limits

## Behavior

Owns:

- behavioral state
- motivation
- intent
- behavioral response
- movement-related behavioral pattern

## Movement

Owns:

- physical execution
- locomotion
- posture
- balance
- acceleration
- deceleration
- turning
- stopping
- physical terrain interaction
- physical load effects

## Ecology

Owns:

- environment
- habitat
- terrain existence
- environmental conditions

Movement owns only the animal's physical response to those conditions.

## Domestic / Trained

Owns:

- training
- commands
- conditioning
- work roles
- riding systems
- equipment context

Movement owns the physical execution.

## AI Production

Owns:

- generation
- animation implementation
- rendering
- prompt construction
- production controls

AI Production cannot redefine biological or movement truth.
