# HISTORICAL RECONSTRUCTION POLICY

## Purpose

Separate historical equipment/use from animal biomechanics.

## Historical Layer Owns

- existence of equipment
- historical period
- cultural context
- construction
- usage
- archaeological/textual evidence

## Movement Owns

Once equipment is approved:

- physical load
- balance effects
- traction effects
- movement restriction
- altered gait
- turning effects
- fatigue effects

## Rule

Historical equipment cannot be assumed merely because it is visually plausible.

Historical claims require independent evidence.
