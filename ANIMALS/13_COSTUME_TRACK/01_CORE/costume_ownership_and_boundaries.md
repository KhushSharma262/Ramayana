# COSTUME OWNERSHIP AND BOUNDARIES

Historical/Canonical systems:
What is supported by historical or canonical evidence.

How approved costume appears on the persistent entity.

wearing state, continuity, and costume snapshots.

Continuity:
Persistence and state transitions.

Movement:
Physical consequences of garments and equipment.

Cinematic Integration:
Presentation and camera treatment.

Representation only.
