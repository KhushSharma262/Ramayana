# COSTUME NON-GOALS

Costume Track must not:

- invent historical clothing
- use modern fashion as ancient evidence
- equate beauty with accuracy
- infer status without evidence
- convert uncertainty into fact
- use AI images as evidence
- add unsupported jewelry
- add fantasy costume without authorization
- silently change costume between shots
- ignore damage or environmental effects
- create physically impossible clothing
- erase animal equipment continuity
