# COSTUME DEFINITION

Costume Track controls:

- garments
- textiles
- materials
- construction
- dyes and colors
- jewelry
- ornaments
- head adornment
- footwear
- accessories
- status/role costume
- animal tack and equipment
- costume condition
- scene-specific costume state
- historical/canonical costume applicability
- continuity
- production handoff

It does not own:
- anatomy
- character identity
- psychology
- habitat
- biomechanics
- camera truth
- AI model behavior

Those remain under their authoritative systems.
