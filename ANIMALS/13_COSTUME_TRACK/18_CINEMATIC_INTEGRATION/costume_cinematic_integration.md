# COSTUME CINEMATIC INTEGRATION

Cinematic Integration determines how approved costume is seen.

It may control:

- framing
- visibility
- focus
- depth of field
- lighting
- composition
- camera distance
- movement presentation

It may NOT:

- add unsupported garments
- remove canonical garments
- change costume identity
- change historical applicability
- rewrite continuity
- redefine approved colors or materials
