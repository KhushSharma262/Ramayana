# COSTUME VALIDATION GATE

A costume asset passes only when:

1. Identity matches.
2. Garment configuration matches.
3. Layering matches.
4. Material is compatible.
5. Color is compatible.
6. Ornament identity matches.
7. Status/context is compatible.
8. Period/region is compatible.
9. Scene condition is compatible.
10. Continuity is compatible.
11. Movement and physics are compatible.
12. AI has not introduced unsupported elements.

Any blocking failure = REJECT.
