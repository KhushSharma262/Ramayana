# EPISODE QA INTEGRATION

Episode QA verifies the integrated episode.

QA must verify:
- scene ordering
- cross-scene continuity
- entity identity
- population consistency
- ecological continuity
- behavioral continuity
- movement continuity
- interaction continuity
- audio continuity
- equipment continuity
- production handoff
- asset lineage
- episode-level conflicts

Episode Integration cannot self-approve.

QA approval remains owned by QA.
