## Purpose

## Content

Age affects behavioral capability and independence.

Possible developmental changes include:

- dependence on parents
- locomotor ability
- feeding independence
- play
- social learning
- predator avoidance
- exploration
- vigilance
- territorial behavior
- reproductive behavior.

Young animals may have different:

- coordination
- endurance
- risk response
- social dependence
- sensory development.

Adult behavior must not be assigned automatically to juveniles.

Likewise, old animals must not automatically be portrayed as weak or inactive.

Species-specific developmental evidence controls the behavior.
