## Content

Limb anatomy determines movement.

Record where relevant:

- forelimb structure
- hindlimb structure
- joints
- limb orientation
- digits
- claws
- hooves
- pads
- webbing
- talons
- weight-bearing surfaces.

Species differ fundamentally in:

- plantigrade locomotion
- digitigrade locomotion
- unguligrade locomotion
- wing-based locomotion
- limbless locomotion.

AI must preserve actual joint directions.

Feet must contact surfaces correctly.

No floating, sliding or impossible joint bending.
