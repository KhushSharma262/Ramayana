# BIOLOGY HANDOFF CONTRACT

BIOLOGY supplies verified biological constraints.

BEHAVIOR receives:
- sensory capabilities
- internal biological state
- social constraints
- hunger
- thirst
- fear/stress response constraints
- health.

MOVEMENT receives:
- anatomy
- locomotion
- age
- health
- injury
- fatigue
- physiological limits.

ECOLOGY receives:
- resource requirements
- habitat requirements
- ecological interactions.

DOMESTIC_TRAINED receives:
- biological capability
- physical limitations
- species identity.

VISUAL_IDENTITY receives:
- morphology
- age
- sex
- health
- body condition
- natural variation.

CONTINUITY receives:
- persistent biological state.

PRODUCTION receives:
- approved final biological constraints.

No downstream system may silently alter biological truth.
