# TAXONOMY CONTRACT

Required for a species-level production entity:

entity_id:
common_name:
scientific_name:
accepted_name:
taxonomic_rank:
family:
genus:
species:
subspecies_if_relevant:
taxonomy_source:
taxonomy_date:
taxonomy_confidence:

## BROAD NAMES

Examples:

monkey
deer
snake
bird
fish
civet
otter
mongoose
rat
mouse
crow
eagle

These do NOT automatically identify a species.

## RULE

If the source itself is broad, preserve the broad identity until evidence permits
species resolution.

Do not select the most visually convenient species.
