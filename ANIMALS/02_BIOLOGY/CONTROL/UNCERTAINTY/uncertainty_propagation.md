# UNCERTAINTY PROPAGATION CONTROL

Uncertainty must propagate downstream.

Example:

species identification = MEDIUM CONFIDENCE

may affect:

anatomy
body size
coat
movement
diet
behavior
habitat
sensory biology

Therefore downstream records must not represent uncertain upstream decisions
as certain facts.
