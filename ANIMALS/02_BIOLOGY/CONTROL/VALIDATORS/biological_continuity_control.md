# BIOLOGICAL CONTINUITY CONTROL

## LOCKED VARIABLES

For a tracked animal:

species
sex
age
body proportions
major markings
major scars
horns/antlers/tusks
health
injury
body condition
major reproductive state.

## STATE VARIABLES

These may change gradually:

hunger
thirst
hydration
energy
fatigue
stress
thermal state
feeding state
recovery state.

## EVENT REQUIRED

Material changes require:

event
transition
scene
shot
timestamp.

## VISUAL CONTINUITY

The animal must not:

change species
change sex
change age
lose injury
gain injury
change markings
change body size
change limb count
change anatomy

without an approved biological cause.
