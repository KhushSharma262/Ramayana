# BIOLOGICAL COMPATIBILITY RULES

These rules define conditions that must be checked before production.

---

## SPECIES

species_id must be resolved before final asset approval.

Generic labels such as:

deer
monkey
buffalo
snake
bird

are insufficient when species materially affects:

anatomy
scale
movement
behavior
ecology
appearance.

---

## AGE

life_stage must be compatible with:

chronological age
biological development
reproductive status
anatomical development.

---

## SEX

Sex-specific anatomy must match sex.

Sex-specific physiology must match sex.

Sex-specific reproductive state must match sex.

---

## REPRODUCTION

Reproductive state must be compatible with:

species
sex
age
life stage.

---

## HEALTH

Severe injury must affect relevant movement.

Disease must have biologically plausible consequences.

---

## NUTRITION

Long-term nutritional deficit must be compatible with:

body condition
energy
health.

---

## HYDRATION

Severe dehydration must not coexist with an unexplained perfectly normal high-endurance state.

---

## FATIGUE

High exertion must produce an appropriate recovery state.

---

## ANATOMY

Anatomy must match:

species
sex
age
individual.

---

## MOVEMENT

Movement must remain within:

anatomical capability
musculoskeletal capability
physiological capability
health state
terrain.

---

## SENSORY

A response must be possible through a biologically available sensory channel.

---

## SCALE

Individual scale must remain consistent.

---

## ENVIRONMENT

Biological state must be compatible with:

temperature
water
food
terrain
season
habitat.

---

## BLOCK CONDITIONS

BLOCK if:

species conflict
anatomical impossibility
sex contradiction
age contradiction
reproductive contradiction
severe health/movement contradiction
impossible sensory capability
impossible scale
impossible locomotion
unsupported supernatural ability.
