# POPULATION VARIATION CONTROL

Background animals are not individually tracked unless necessary.

However, they must still obey population-level biology.

## ALLOWED VARIATION

- body size
- age
- sex
- coat/plumage
- markings
- posture
- activity
- body condition
- minor asymmetry.

## FORBIDDEN

Background populations must not contain:

- anatomically impossible individuals
- impossible species mixtures
- identical clones in every position
- impossible sex ratios where the scene requires a realistic population
- impossible age distribution
- impossible body sizes.

## IMPORTANT

Population variation does not mean random variation.

Variation remains constrained by:

species
population
sex
age
environment
season
health.

## INDIVIDUAL TRACKING

Prominent animals must receive:

animal_id

and an individual record.
