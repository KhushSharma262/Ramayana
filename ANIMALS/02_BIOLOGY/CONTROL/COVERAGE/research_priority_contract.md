# RESEARCH DEPTH AND PRODUCTION IMPACT

Every animal/species receives a research tier.

## RESEARCH TIERS

TIER_0
Background organism with no meaningful interaction.

TIER_1
Visible background animal.

TIER_2
Repeated visible animal.

TIER_3
Important interaction / riding / working / environmental role.

TIER_4
Named or narratively important individual animal.

TIER_5
Animal central to a scene or major narrative event.

## PRODUCTION IMPACT

LOW
Minimal visual/behavioral effect.

MEDIUM
Visible effect.

HIGH
Affects anatomy, movement, interaction, continuity or realism.

CRITICAL
Wrong information could materially break the scene.

## RULE

Research depth must increase with production impact.

A Tier-5 animal cannot receive the same research standard as a distant insect.
