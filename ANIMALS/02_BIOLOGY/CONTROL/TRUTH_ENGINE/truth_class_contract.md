# TRUTH CLASS CONTRACT

Four independent truth classes exist.

## CANON_TRUTH

What an authoritative Ramayana/traditional source actually states.

It does not automatically establish modern zoological biology.

## ZOOLOGICAL_TRUTH

Evidence-backed facts about real animals.

Examples:

- anatomy
- physiology
- senses
- movement
- feeding
- reproduction
- natural variation
- health

## RECONSTRUCTED_TRUTH

A reasoned reconstruction where direct evidence is incomplete.

Every reconstruction must state:

- evidence
- inference
- assumptions
- alternatives
- uncertainty
- confidence

## PRODUCTION_CHOICE

A filmmaking decision.

Examples:

- visual emphasis
- framing
- staging
- camera
- AI generation choice
- artistic interpretation

## ABSOLUTE RULES

AI output ≠ evidence.

AI image ≠ anatomy evidence.

AI video ≠ behavior evidence.

Visual reference ≠ zoological proof.

Canon ≠ zoology.

Zoology ≠ canon.

Reconstruction ≠ direct fact.

Production choice ≠ biological truth.
