## CRITICAL RULE

One citation attached to a paragraph does not automatically support every claim
inside the paragraph.

Each independently meaningful biological assertion requires traceable evidence.

## APPROVAL

A claim may be APPROVED only if:

- source exists
- evidence exists
- evidence points to source
- evidence explicitly supports the claim
- applicability is established
- uncertainty is declared
- contradiction status is resolved
