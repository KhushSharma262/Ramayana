## AI RULE

AI_SYNTHESIS can assist research organization.

AI_SYNTHESIS cannot independently establish zoological truth.

AI-generated answers must point to their underlying sources.

## VISUAL RULE

VISUAL_REFERENCE cannot establish anatomy or biology by itself.

It can inform visual reconstruction only.

## PRIMARY RULE

Primary scientific evidence receives priority over unsourced summaries.

## VERIFICATION RULE

UNVERIFIED sources cannot support an APPROVED production claim.
