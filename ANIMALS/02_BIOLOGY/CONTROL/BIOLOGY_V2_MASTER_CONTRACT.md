# BIOLOGY V2 MASTER CONTRACT

## SYSTEM PURPOSE

BIOLOGY defines real-world biological truth for every animal used by the Ramayana production.

It is a constraint system, not merely a documentation system.

---

# 1. BIOLOGICAL IDENTITY

Every animal must have:

species_id

and, where individually tracked:

animal_id

Species identity must be resolved as narrowly as evidence permits.

---

# 2. BIOLOGICAL STATE

A tracked animal may have:

species
individual
age
sex
life_stage
anatomy
health
injury
pain
body_condition
hydration
hunger
energy
fatigue
thermal_state
reproductive_state
sensory_state.

---

# 3. BIOLOGICAL HIERARCHY

Species facts:

SPECIES BASELINE

Individual facts:

INDIVIDUAL STATE

Individual state overrides generic species assumptions.

---

# 4. EVIDENCE HIERARCHY

Preferred:

A = direct authoritative/scientific evidence
B = strong scientific evidence
C = credible secondary synthesis
D = indirect evidence
E = cultural/iconographic inference
U = unknown.

Exact numerical claims normally require A/B evidence.

---

# 5. NO FALSE PRECISION

Do not invent:

height
weight
speed
lifespan
sensory range
gestation
sleep duration
calorie requirement
temperature tolerance
population size
disease prevalence
parasite prevalence.

If not supported:

UNKNOWN
or
RANGE
or
ESTIMATE.

---

# 6. AGE

Age must affect biology where relevant.

No juvenile may automatically receive adult anatomy.

---

# 7. SEX

Sex must be independently recorded.

Sex cannot be inferred from personality.

---

# 8. ANATOMY

Anatomy constrains:

movement
posture
feeding
interaction
injury
visual identity.

---

# 9. PHYSIOLOGY

Physiology constrains:

energy
fatigue
breathing
thermoregulation
hydration
recovery.

---

# 10. HEALTH

Health must have consequences.

An injury cannot exist only in documentation while movement remains unaffected.

---

# 11. NUTRITION

Nutrition must correspond to species and state.

---

# 12. SENSES

Sensory capability must be species-specific.

No supernatural perception.

---

# 13. REPRODUCTION

Reproductive state must be compatible with:

species
sex
age
life stage.

---

# 14. VARIATION

Animals must not appear as identical clones.

---

# 15. ENVIRONMENT

Environment modifies biological state.

Examples:

heat
cold
water availability
food availability
terrain
humidity
predator exposure.

---

# 16. CONTINUITY

Biological state cannot change between shots without a causal transition.

---

# 17. AI GENERATION

AI is not allowed to redefine biological truth.

AI output must conform to biological records.

---

# 18. BLOCKING

Generation must be BLOCKED when a biological contradiction exists.

---

# 19. UNKNOWN

UNKNOWN is an acceptable scientific state.

It is preferable to fabricated certainty.

---

# 20. APPROVAL

Only APPROVED biological records may become final production truth.
