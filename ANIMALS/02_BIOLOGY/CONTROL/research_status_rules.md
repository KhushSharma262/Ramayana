# STATUS AND UNKNOWN CONTROL

## RESEARCH_REQUIRED

Means:
The project has not yet established the required biological fact.

It must not be interpreted as UNKNOWN.

It must create an open research gap.

## UNKNOWN

Allowed only when:

1. Research has been attempted or the information is inherently unavailable.
2. The reason is documented.
3. The missing knowledge is explicitly identified.
4. No unsupported value is substituted.

## NOT_APPLICABLE

Allowed only when:

1. The field genuinely does not apply.
2. The biological reason is stated.
3. The decision is species-appropriate.

## CONFLICTED

Used when credible evidence disagrees.

A conflicted claim cannot be silently averaged.

## APPROVED

Only after:

- source validation
- evidence validation
- applicability validation
- taxonomy validation
- measurement validation
- plausibility validation
- cross-domain validation
- uncertainty validation
- dependency validation
- downstream acknowledgement
- human/project approval
