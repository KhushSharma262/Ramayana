# MEASUREMENT CONTROL

Every quantitative biological claim should specify:

value:
unit:
measurement_type:
method:
conditions:
population:
sex:
age:
sample_size:
source:

## Unit rule

Units must be dimensionally appropriate.

Examples:

mass → g, kg
length → mm, cm, m
speed → m/s, km/h
temperature → °C, K
time → s, min, h, day
volume → mL, L
frequency → Hz or events/time

## Precision rule

Do not preserve false precision.

If a source reports an approximate range, retain the range.

Do not convert an approximate value into a falsely exact value.
