# CONFLICT CONTRACT

Conflicting evidence must remain visible.

Required:

conflict_id:
claim_id:
alternative_a:
alternative_b:
evidence_a:
evidence_b:
scope_comparison:
source_quality_comparison:
resolution_method:
final_decision:
decision_reason:
uncertainty:
approval_id:

## RULE

Do not average conflicting scientific values simply to produce one number.

No conflict may become RESOLVED merely by changing a status field.
