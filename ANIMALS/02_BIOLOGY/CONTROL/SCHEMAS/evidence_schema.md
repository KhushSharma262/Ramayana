## PURPOSE

Evidence must be specific enough to determine whether a claim actually applies.

## IMPORTANT

A study on one population must not automatically be generalized to every population.

A study on adults must not automatically be applied to juveniles.

A study on captive animals must not automatically be treated as wild-animal behavior.

A study on one sex must not automatically be applied to both sexes.
