# BIOLOGICAL APPLICABILITY CONTROL

Every claim must declare the context in which it is valid.

## Required dimensions

species
subspecies_if_relevant
population_if_relevant
geography
sex
age
life_context
season
environment
captive_or_wild

## Life context

WILD
DOMESTIC
CAPTIVE
WORKING
FERAL
HUMAN_HABITUATED
UNKNOWN

## Critical distinction

DOMESTICATION is not equivalent to:

- captivity
- training
- habituation
- taming
- individual familiarity with humans

These must not be merged.

## Generalization rule

An observation from one individual must not automatically become a species-wide
biological rule.

A small sample must not automatically become universal species biology.

Population, sample size and study conditions must remain attached to the evidence.
