# APPLICABILITY HIERARCHY

Evidence scope must be preserved.

LEVEL 1:
species-wide

LEVEL 2:
subspecies

LEVEL 3:
population

LEVEL 4:
geographic population

LEVEL 5:
sex

LEVEL 6:
age/life-stage

LEVEL 7:
individual

## RULE

Information cannot move upward in this hierarchy without justification.

Individual observation
≠
species fact

One population
≠
all populations

Captive animal
≠
wild animal

Habituated animal
≠
domesticated animal

Domestic breed
≠
wild species phenotype

## INDIVIDUAL OVERRIDE

Individual biological records override generic species assumptions only for
that individual.

They must never rewrite the species record automatically.
