# EVIDENCE SCOPE HIERARCHY

Evidence scope may be:

SPECIES
SUBSPECIES
POPULATION
GEOGRAPHIC_POPULATION
SEX
AGE
INDIVIDUAL

## UPWARD GENERALIZATION RULE

Evidence may not silently move upward.

INDIVIDUAL
≠ SPECIES

CAPTIVE
≠ WILD

HABITUATED
≠ DOMESTICATED

ONE POPULATION
≠ ALL POPULATIONS

ONE SEX
≠ BOTH SEXES

ONE LIFE STAGE
≠ ALL LIFE STAGES

If upward generalization is scientifically justified, it requires an explicit
research decision with evidence and uncertainty.
