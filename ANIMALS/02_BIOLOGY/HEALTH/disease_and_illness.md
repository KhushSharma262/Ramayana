# DISEASE AND ILLNESS

## Purpose

Defines disease and illness states.

## Status

ACTIVE

## Biological Scope

This file belongs to the ANIMALS → BIOLOGY system.

It defines animal biological truth and production constraints.

The baseline is modern real-world zoology unless another project-level decision explicitly changes the scope.

## Content

Disease is a deviation from normal biological function caused by pathological processes.

Records should distinguish:

- infectious disease
- parasitic disease
- nutritional disorder
- genetic disorder
- degenerative disease
- traumatic condition
- environmental illness.

Disease may affect:

- energy
- appetite
- movement
- breathing
- hydration
- thermoregulation
- senses
- behavior.

Do not diagnose an individual without evidence.

If a disease is included for a story requirement, its biological consequences must be researched separately.
