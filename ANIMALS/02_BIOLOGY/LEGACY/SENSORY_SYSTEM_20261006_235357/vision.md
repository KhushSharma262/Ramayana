## Content

Vision differs among animals in:

- eye placement
- visual field
- acuity
- color perception
- low-light sensitivity
- motion detection
- depth perception.

Predatory and prey-oriented visual systems may have different priorities.

Camera perspective must not be treated as the animal's biological visual field.

If a scene shows an animal detecting something, the stimulus must be accessible through a biologically plausible sensory route.
