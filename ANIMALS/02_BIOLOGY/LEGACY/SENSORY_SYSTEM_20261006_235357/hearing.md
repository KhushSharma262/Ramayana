## Content

Hearing differs across species.

Relevant properties include:

- frequency sensitivity
- sound localization
- directional hearing
- environmental noise tolerance
- sensitivity to sudden sounds.

Animal response to sound depends on:

species
+ stimulus
+ distance
+ environment
+ previous habituation
+ internal state.

Loudness perceived by humans is not automatically equivalent to loudness perceived by an animal.
