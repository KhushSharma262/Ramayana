## Rules

1. A common group such as "monkey", "deer", "buffalo", "snake", "civet",
   "otter" or "eagle" is not automatically a species.

2. If canon identifies only a broad group, preserve that uncertainty.

3. Do not select a species solely because it visually resembles the broad term.

4. If multiple taxa remain plausible, record the alternatives.

5. A final species selection requires a documented decision.

6. Taxonomy changes must not silently rewrite historical research.

7. A taxonomy decision must identify its scope and confidence.
