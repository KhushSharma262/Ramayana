# SPECIES BIOLOGY MASTER REGISTRY

DATA STATUS:
CURRENT BIOLOGY BASELINE

IMPORTANT:
A common name is NOT sufficient for a final biological lock.

Every production animal must ultimately resolve to:

species_id
common_name
scientific_name
taxonomic_status
sex
age
individual_id

Generic categories such as:

BIRD
BUTTERFLY
FISH
FROG
DEER
MONKEY
INSECT
SNAKE

are temporary identification states only.

They must not be used as final biological identities when species-level
identification is required.
