# RESEARCH STATUS DEFINITIONS

NOT_STARTED

No meaningful research exists.

RESEARCH_REQUIRED

The subject is known, but required evidence is missing.

PARTIAL

Some domains are researched but important gaps remain.

RESEARCHED

Required domains have evidence.

REVIEWED

Evidence has been reviewed for conflicts and applicability.

APPROVED

The biological record passed the project approval gate.

BLOCKED

A contradiction or missing requirement prevents approval.

SUPERSEDED

A newer approved record replaced this record.

## IMPORTANT

RESEARCHED != APPROVED

Approval requires cross-domain validation.
