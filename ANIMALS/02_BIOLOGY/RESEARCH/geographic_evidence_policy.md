# GEOGRAPHIC EVIDENCE POLICY

## PRIMARY POLICY

For animals relevant to the Indian subcontinent:

1. Prefer authoritative Indian geographic/population evidence.
2. Prefer Indian zoological studies where available.
3. Prefer regional population data when the animal is geographically variable.
4. Use global scientific evidence when Indian-specific evidence is unavailable.
5. Never silently label global data as Indian data.

## IMPORTANT

Modern Indian zoology is used for biological modeling.

This does NOT automatically establish the exact historical population of an
ancient Ramayana setting.

Historical reconstruction belongs outside the modern BIOLOGY truth layer.
