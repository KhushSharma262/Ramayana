# COSTUME / EQUIPMENT HANDOFF

Preserve approved:

- garment identity
- layering
- material
- construction
- color/dye
- ornament
- status/role
- historical/canonical compatibility
- condition
- equipment
- animal tack
- fit and welfare
- movement consequences

Costume/equipment must remain consistent with character, period, scene state, and physical reality.

Cinematic preference cannot override approved costume state.
