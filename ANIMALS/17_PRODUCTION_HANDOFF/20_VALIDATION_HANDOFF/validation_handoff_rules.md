# VALIDATION HANDOFF

Validation must independently verify:

1. upstream snapshot integrity
2. scene compatibility
3. shot compatibility
4. identity continuity
5. biological plausibility
6. behavioral plausibility
7. movement plausibility
8. ecological integrity
9. interaction integrity
10. continuity integrity
11. audio integrity
12. costume/equipment integrity
13. cinematic compliance
14. AI output compliance
15. lineage completeness

Passing one category does not imply passing the others.

Validation failure blocks approval.
