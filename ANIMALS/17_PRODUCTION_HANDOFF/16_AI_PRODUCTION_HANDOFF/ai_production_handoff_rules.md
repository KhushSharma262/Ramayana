# AI PRODUCTION HANDOFF

AI receives a compiled package containing approved requirements and authorized references.

AI may determine representation mechanics.

AI may NOT:

- invent canonical facts
- invent biological traits
- invent behavior
- invent ecology
- invent identity
- invent continuity state
- treat generated output as evidence
- silently alter approved requirements

If generated output conflicts with the handoff package, the output is rejected.

The handoff package is never rewritten to justify AI failure.
