# RESEARCH / CONFLICT HANDOFF

Research and uncertainty states must be preserved.

UNKNOWN is not converted into a production fact.

EVIDENCE GAP remains an evidence gap.

CONFLICT remains blocked until resolved by the owning authority.

AMBIGUITY remains distinct from conflict.

Natural variability remains distinct from uncertainty.

If material uncertainty affects production requirements, the handoff is BLOCKED.

The handoff system does not resolve upstream research conflicts.
