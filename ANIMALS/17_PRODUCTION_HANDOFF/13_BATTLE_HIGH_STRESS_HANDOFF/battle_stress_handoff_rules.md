# BATTLE / HIGH-STRESS HANDOFF

Where applicable, preserve approved:

- stressor
- exposure
- stress state
- fear response
- flight/freeze/panic state
- aggression/defense state
- group response
- injury
- pain
- fatigue
- recovery state
- battlefield role
- environmental hazards

Extreme stress changes response; it does not transform an animal into a human combatant.

Battle presence does not automatically mean battle participation.
