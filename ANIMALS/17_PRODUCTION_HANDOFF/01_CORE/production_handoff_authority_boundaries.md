# AUTHORITY BOUNDARIES

BIOLOGY
Owns physical and physiological truth.

BEHAVIOR
Owns motivation, behavioral state, and behavioral response.

MOVEMENT
Owns physical execution and biomechanics.

ECOLOGY
Owns habitat, environment, resources, and ecological context.

DOMESTIC/TRAINED
Owns domestication, training, handlers, work roles, and equipment context.

INTERACTION
Owns interaction-event identity, participants, event lifecycle, and interaction consequences.

VISUAL IDENTITY
Owns approved visual identity.

CONTINUITY
Owns persistence, temporal state, event ordering, and snapshot reconciliation.

AUDIO
Owns acoustic and animal-sound truth.

BATTLE/HIGH-STRESS
Owns approved extreme-stress state and battle-context requirements.

COSTUME
Owns costume, garment, ornament, tack, equipment, and their approved states.

CINEMATIC INTEGRATION
Owns cinematic presentation and shot orchestration.

AI PRODUCTION
Owns representation and generation implementation.

PRODUCTION HANDOFF
Owns transfer integrity only.

It cannot override any upstream authority.
