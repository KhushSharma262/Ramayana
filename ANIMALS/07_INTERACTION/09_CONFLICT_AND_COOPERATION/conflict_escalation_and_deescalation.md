# CONFLICT ESCALATION / DE-ESCALATION

Conflict may progress through:

PRESENCE
→ AWARENESS
→ WARNING
→ TENSION
→ APPROACH
→ THREAT
→ CONTACT
→ ESCALATION
→ INTERRUPTION / DE-ESCALATION
→ SEPARATION

Not every conflict reaches physical contact.

Warning behavior belongs to Behavior.

Physical execution belongs to Movement.

Interaction owns the event progression.
