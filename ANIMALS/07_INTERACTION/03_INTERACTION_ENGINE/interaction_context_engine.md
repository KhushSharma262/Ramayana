# CONTEXT ENGINE

Interaction is context-dependent.

Relevant context may include:

species
age/life stage
sex where relevant
physiological state
health
fatigue
social group
prior history
territory
resource availability
habitat
season
weather
time
human presence
training
domestication
equipment
population density
predator/prey context

The same entities may produce different interactions under different conditions.

Context cannot be silently borrowed from another scene or entity.
