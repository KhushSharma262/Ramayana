# OUTCOMES AND CONSEQUENCES

Possible outcomes:

NO_CHANGE
CONTACT_ESTABLISHED
CONTACT_AVOIDED
COOPERATION
CONFLICT
ESCALATION
DE_ESCALATION
SEPARATION
INJURY
DEATH
MATING
OFFSPRING
RESOURCE_TRANSFER
LEARNING_CONSEQUENCE
RELATIONSHIP_CHANGE
TERRITORIAL_CHANGE
TRAINING_CONSEQUENCE
OTHER_APPROVED

Consequences must be assigned to the system that owns their truth.

Injury → Biology/health.
Physical mechanics → Movement.
Behavioral response → Behavior.
Habitat/resource effect → Ecology.
Training consequence → Domestic/Trained.

Interaction records the consequence linkage but does not replace the owner.
