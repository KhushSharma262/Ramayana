# SUPERNATURAL INTERACTION

Supernatural/canonical interactions use a separate controlled pathway.

They must not contaminate ordinary zoological interaction rules.

A supernatural interaction must contain:

supernatural_interaction_id
canonical_basis
event_description
entities
canonical_context
supernatural_mechanism_or_status
ordinary_physical_conflict_if_any
interpretation
approval
snapshot
scene_reference

Supernatural status cannot be inferred merely because an ordinary interaction is visually dramatic.

Ordinary animal interaction remains governed by ordinary biological/behavioral/movement rules unless explicitly overridden by approved canonical/supernatural logic.
