# INTERACTION DEFINITION

Interaction is the structured representation of an event, encounter, relationship change, or consequential non-event involving at least one animal.

## Interaction Includes

- participants
- participant roles
- initiator where applicable
- recipient/participants
- interaction type
- context
- temporal state
- spatial context
- interaction lifecycle
- observable event
- outcome
- consequences
- relationship consequences
- continuity
- evidence
- approval
- snapshot

## Interaction Does Not Own

Biology:
- anatomy
- physiology
- health
- physical limits

Behavior:
- motivation
- internal behavioral state
- emotional/behavioral response
- communication mechanisms

Movement:
- biomechanical execution
- gait
- balance
- physical mechanics

Ecology:
- habitat
- resources
- population ecology
- environmental relationships

Domestic/Trained:
- domestication
- training
- handler identity
- work role
- equipment ownership/assignment

AI Production:
- generation parameters
- rendering
- image/video construction

## Boundary Rule

Interaction may reference information owned by another system but may not redefine that information as its own canonical truth.
