# INTERACTION IMMUTABILITY / RETCON CONTROL

Approved snapshots are immutable.

An interaction cannot simply be deleted after downstream scenes consume it.

Allowed corrections:

NEW VERSION
→ INVALIDATION REASON
→ DEPENDENCY AUDIT
→ APPROVAL
→ NEW SNAPSHOT

If an interaction is invalidated:

- dependent relationship states are reviewed
- dependent scenes are reviewed
- dependent prompts are reviewed
- dependent assets are reviewed
- dependent shots are reviewed

Historical record of the invalid interaction remains auditable.

No silent deletion.

No silent replacement.

No retcon without dependency review.
