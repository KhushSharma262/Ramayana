# POPULATION INTERACTION SAMPLING FIREWALL

Background population interaction is rule-driven, not arbitrary.

Generation must respect:

species
population density
group structure
sex ratio where relevant
age structure
season
resource availability
habitat
predator pressure
human disturbance
social system
time
weather

Population rules generate plausible interaction opportunities.

They do not guarantee that every possible interaction occurs.

A generated individual event must pass:
species plausibility
population plausibility
behavior plausibility
movement plausibility
ecological plausibility
identity rules

Population-level probability cannot override individual constraints.
