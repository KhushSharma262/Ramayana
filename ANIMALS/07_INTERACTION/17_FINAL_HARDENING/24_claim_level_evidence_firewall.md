# CLAIM-LEVEL EVIDENCE FIREWALL

One source does not automatically support every part of an interaction.

Each important claim requires claim-level provenance.

Example:

CLAIM A:
Animal species interacted.

CLAIM B:
Interaction was aggressive.

CLAIM C:
Interaction caused injury.

CLAIM D:
Relationship changed.

Each may require separate evidence.

Required:

claim_id
interaction_id
source_id
source_version
evidence_scope
evidence_text/reference
interpretation
confidence
decision
approval
snapshot

Unsupported subclaims remain UNKNOWN/BLOCKED.

Evidence cannot be broadened beyond what it actually supports.
