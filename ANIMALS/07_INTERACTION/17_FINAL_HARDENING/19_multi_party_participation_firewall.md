# MULTI-PARTY PARTICIPATION FIREWALL

Presence is not participation.

For group interactions distinguish:

OBSERVERS
PARTICIPANTS
PRIMARY_PARTICIPANTS
SECONDARY_PARTICIPANTS
NON_PARTICIPANTS

Example:

20 animals visible
3 participants
17 observers/non-participants

The system must not assign the interaction to all 20 animals.

Population/group interaction may remain aggregate when individual participation is unknown.

Individual participation requires:
animal_id
or an approved population/group participant identity.

Partial participation is explicitly supported.

Unknown participation must remain UNKNOWN, not silently expanded to the whole group.
