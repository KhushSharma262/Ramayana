# CONSEQUENCE OWNERSHIP FIREWALL

Interaction records consequences but does not become the canonical owner of every consequence.

Examples:

INJURY
→ Biology / Health

PHYSICAL DAMAGE
→ Biology + Movement where applicable

BEHAVIORAL CHANGE
→ Behavior

HABITAT CHANGE
→ Ecology

TRAINING CHANGE
→ Domestic/Trained

EQUIPMENT CHANGE
→ Domestic/Trained

RELATIONSHIP CHANGE
→ Appropriate relationship authority

CONTINUITY EVENT
→ Continuity

Interaction stores:
consequence_type
consequence_reference
owning_system
validation_state

A consequence without an owner is BLOCKED.

Interaction cannot self-approve a consequence owned by another system.
