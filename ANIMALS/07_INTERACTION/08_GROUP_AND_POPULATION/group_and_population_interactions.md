# GROUP AND POPULATION INTERACTIONS

Supports:
- herd
- flock
- troop
- colony
- pack
- school
- swarm
- human group
- mixed populations

Background interactions may be represented at population/group level.

Do not generate individual interaction IDs for every background animal unless individual identity is narratively or continuously relevant.

Population-level rules must not silently establish individual relationships.

Individualization requires an explicit identity transition.
