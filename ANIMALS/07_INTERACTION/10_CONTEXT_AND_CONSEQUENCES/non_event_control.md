# NON-EVENT CONTROL

Important non-events may be recorded.

Examples:
- predators encounter prey but do not attack
- two territorial animals encounter and separate
- human approaches animal and animal avoids contact
- animal is present but does not interact with another animal

NON_INTERACTION is not the same as UNKNOWN.

Do not record every absence of interaction.

Record only non-events relevant to continuity, causality, scene logic, or validation.
