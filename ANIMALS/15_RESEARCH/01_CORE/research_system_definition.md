# 15_RESEARCH — RESEARCH SYSTEM

## Purpose

15_RESEARCH is the evidence and knowledge-control layer for the entire ANIMALS system.

It does not replace:
- Biology
- Behavior
- Movement
- Ecology
- Domestic/Trained
- Interaction
- Visual Identity
- Continuity
- Audio
- Cinematic Integration
- AI Production

It supplies evidence to those systems.

## Master Chain

SOURCE
-> EVIDENCE
-> INTERPRETATION
-> CLAIM
-> DECISION
-> APPROVAL
-> RESEARCH SNAPSHOT
-> DOMAIN SNAPSHOT
-> SCENE

## Core Rule

RESEARCH DISCOVERS AND JUSTIFIES TRUTH.
DOMAIN SYSTEMS OWN APPROVED OPERATIONAL TRUTH.

## Fail Closed

A claim remains BLOCKED when:
- source is missing
- evidence is insufficient
- taxonomy is unresolved
- geography is unresolved
- chronology is unresolved
- sources conflict without a decision
- inference is presented as fact
- AI output is used as evidence
- confidence is below the required threshold
