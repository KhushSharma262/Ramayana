# RESEARCH AUTHORITY BOUNDARIES

## Research Owns

- source discovery
- source classification
- evidence extraction
- evidence quality
- claim formation
- interpretation
- uncertainty
- contradiction recording
- research snapshots
- handoff to domain systems

## Research Does Not Own

- final anatomy
- final behavior state
- final movement state
- final ecology state
- final continuity state
- final cinematic presentation
- AI generation

Those are owned by the respective domain systems after approval.

## Conflict Rule

If research conflicts with an existing approved domain claim:

RESEARCH CONFLICT
-> REVIEW
-> AUTHORITY CHECK
-> DECISION
-> NEW VERSION IF REQUIRED

No silent rewrite is permitted.
