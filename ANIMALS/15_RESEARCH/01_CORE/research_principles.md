# RESEARCH PRINCIPLES

1. Primary evidence is preferred over secondary summaries.
2. Scientific evidence and canonical evidence are separate evidence classes.
3. Historical evidence and modern biological evidence are separate evidence classes.
4. Geographic relevance must be preserved.
5. Temporal relevance must be preserved.
6. Species must be resolved before species-specific claims are approved.
7. Broad names must remain unresolved when evidence cannot resolve them.
8. Direct observation and inference must never be silently merged.
9. Contradictory sources remain contradictory until explicitly reconciled.
10. Absence of evidence is not automatically evidence of absence.
11. UNKNOWN is different from ABSENT.
12. AI-generated media is never biological, historical or canonical evidence.
13. Popular visual depictions are not evidence merely because they are widespread.
14. Every production claim must remain traceable to research.
15. Research may remain incomplete; unsupported certainty is prohibited.
