# RESEARCH VALIDATION GATE

A research claim may be APPROVED only when:

[ ] source exists
[ ] source identity is verified
[ ] evidence is extracted
[ ] subject is resolved
[ ] geography is resolved where relevant
[ ] chronology is resolved where relevant
[ ] evidence and inference are separated
[ ] contradictions are recorded
[ ] confidence is assigned
[ ] domain owner is identified
[ ] claim scope is defined
[ ] unsupported extrapolation is removed

Failure of any mandatory requirement:
BLOCKED
