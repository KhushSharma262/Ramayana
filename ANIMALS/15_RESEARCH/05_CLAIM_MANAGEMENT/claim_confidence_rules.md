# CLAIM CONFIDENCE RULES

HIGH:
Directly supported by strong primary or authoritative evidence.

MEDIUM:
Supported by credible evidence but requiring interpretation or limited extrapolation.

LOW:
Plausible but insufficiently supported.

BLOCKED:
Unsupported, contradicted, unresolved or outside source scope.

## Important

Confidence is attached to the CLAIM, not merely the source.

A high-quality source can still fail to support a particular claim.
