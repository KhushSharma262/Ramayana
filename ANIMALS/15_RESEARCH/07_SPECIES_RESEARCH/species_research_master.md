Priority 2:
Animals with major domestic, military, ecological or human-associated roles.

Background wildlife.

## Rule

Each species receives:
- taxonomy
- morphology
- sensory ecology
- locomotion
- normal behavior
- stress behavior
- social behavior
- human relationship
- habitat
- geographic relevance
- historical relevance
- canonical relevance
- evidence confidence

No species is considered researched merely because a common-name paragraph exists.
