# QUEUE RULE

NOT_STARTED:
Research has not begun.

BLOCKED:
The requested label is too broad to support species-specific research.

RESEARCHING:
Evidence collection underway.

EVIDENCE_COLLECTED:
Evidence exists but has not passed validation.

APPROVED:
Claim approved for domain use.

SNAPSHOTTED:
Approved claim is immutable and available to downstream systems.

No other status may be interpreted as production-ready.
