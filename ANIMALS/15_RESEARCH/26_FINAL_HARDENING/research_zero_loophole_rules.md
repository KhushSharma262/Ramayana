# RESEARCH ZERO-LOOPHOLE RULES

1. No source -> no claim.
2. No evidence -> no approval.
3. Unresolved species -> no species-specific claim.
4. Geographic mismatch -> reduced confidence or BLOCKED.
5. Temporal mismatch -> explicit uncertainty.
6. Inference must be labeled.
7. Contradictions must be recorded.
8. UNKNOWN != ABSENT.
9. Popular depiction != evidence.
10. AI output != evidence.
11. One source cannot automatically support every related claim.
12. Canonical evidence does not automatically provide scientific evidence.
13. Scientific evidence does not automatically become canonical fact.
14. Modern practice does not automatically become ancient practice.
15. A claim must have an owner.
16. Approved research is versioned.
17. Superseded research remains auditable.
18. Domain systems may reject research claims outside their authority.
19. Production cannot bypass research status.
20. Final shots must remain reverse-auditable to research evidence.
