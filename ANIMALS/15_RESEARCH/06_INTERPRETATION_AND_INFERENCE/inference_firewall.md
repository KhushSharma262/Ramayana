# INFERENCE FIREWALL

Inference is permitted only when:

1. Evidence exists.
2. The inference is logically connected to the evidence.
3. The inference is explicitly labeled.
4. No stronger contradictory evidence exists.
5. The domain owner reviews the inference.

## Example

Evidence:
Battle contains horses and elephants.

Valid inference:
They were exposed to a battlefield environment.

Invalid inference:
Every horse was terrified.

Why invalid:
The evidence does not establish individual emotional state.

## Rule

PLAUSIBLE != ESTABLISHED
