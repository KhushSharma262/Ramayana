# RESEARCH DECISION FRAMEWORK

For every important claim:

1. Identify subject.
2. Resolve species/taxonomy.
3. Resolve geography.
4. Resolve time period.
5. Identify strongest sources.
6. Extract direct evidence.
7. Separate evidence from inference.
8. Identify contradictions.
9. Assign confidence.
10. Determine domain owner.
11. Submit for review.
12. Approve or block.
13. Create research snapshot.

No production claim skips these steps.
