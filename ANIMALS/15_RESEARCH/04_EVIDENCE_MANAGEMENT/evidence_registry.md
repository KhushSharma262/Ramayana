# EVIDENCE REGISTRY — INITIAL

## E001

Source:
S001

Evidence:
Animal stress is assessed through multiple physiological, hormonal, immunological, behavioral and physical-condition measures.

Interpretation:
No single visual cue should automatically be treated as complete evidence of stress.

Applies:
General animal stress research.

Confidence:
HIGH

## E002

Source:
S001

Evidence:
Acute stress is associated with sudden changes in predictability and/or controllability; chronic stress involves prolonged low predictability or controllability.

Interpretation:
Stress duration and context must be tracked.

Confidence:
HIGH

---

## E003

Source:
S002

Evidence:
Animals can experience acute stress from brief threats and chronic stress from prolonged or repeated stressors.

Interpretation:
Battle scenes must distinguish acute event exposure from cumulative stress.

Confidence:
HIGH
