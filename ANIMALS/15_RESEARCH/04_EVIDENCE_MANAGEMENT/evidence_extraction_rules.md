# EVIDENCE EXTRACTION RULES

Every extracted evidence item must preserve:

SOURCE
PASSAGE / LOCATION
DIRECT CONTENT
CONTEXT
WHAT IT ESTABLISHES
WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH

## Example

Evidence:
"Horses are being prepared for battle."

Establishes:
battle preparation of horses.

Does NOT automatically establish:
- exact harness design
- exact training method
- exact breed
- exact stress response
- exact physiological state

The distinction is mandatory.
