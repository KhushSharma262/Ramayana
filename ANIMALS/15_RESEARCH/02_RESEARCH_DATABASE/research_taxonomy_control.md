# TAXONOMY CONTROL

Species-specific research requires a resolved taxonomic identity.

Required:
- common_name
- scientific_name
- taxonomic_authority
- geographic_range
- evidence_scope

Broad labels such as:
monkey
deer
snake
bird
fish
frog
cannot silently become a particular species.

If the source itself is broad, the research record remains broad.

No species-specific production claim may be derived from an unresolved broad label.
