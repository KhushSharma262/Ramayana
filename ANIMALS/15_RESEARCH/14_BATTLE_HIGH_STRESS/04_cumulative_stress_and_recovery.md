# CUMULATIVE STRESS AND RECOVERY

## Evidence

Stress effects depend on:
- duration
- frequency
- predictability
- controllability
- severity
- individual characteristics

## Research Finding

Repeated exposure cannot be treated as identical to one isolated exposure.

The project therefore requires separate states for:

ACUTE
REPEATED
CUMULATIVE
CHRONIC

## Continuity Implication

If an animal survives a battle scene, the next scene cannot automatically assume:

- zero fatigue
- zero injury
- zero arousal
- zero environmental effects
- complete behavioral reset

The actual recovery state must be established.

## Important Scientific Limitation

The research does not justify a universal recovery timer.

Recovery depends on:
- species
- intensity
- injury
- exertion
- environmental conditions
- physiological state
- continued threat
- opportunity for rest

Therefore recovery duration remains a species/context-specific research question.
