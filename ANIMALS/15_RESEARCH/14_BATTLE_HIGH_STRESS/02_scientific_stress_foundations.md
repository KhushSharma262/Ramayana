# SCIENTIFIC STRESS FOUNDATIONS

## 1. Stress Is Context-Dependent

Animal stress response is not determined by the presence of a stimulus alone.

Relevant variables include:
- severity
- duration
- frequency
- predictability
- controllability
- individual condition
- previous experience
- environmental context

Therefore:

VISIBLE THREAT != AUTOMATIC PANIC

Implications of stress for animal welfare and animal-based research.

## 2. Acute And Chronic Stress Are Distinct

Acute stress can follow sudden environmental change or threat.

Chronic stress involves prolonged or repeatedly unresolved conditions.

The distinction matters for cinematic continuity.

A single battle scene cannot automatically establish chronic psychological damage.

Repeated exposure must be tracked through time.

## 3. Stress Has Physiological And Behavioral Components

Stress can be evaluated through combinations of:
- physiological measures
- hormonal measures
- immunological measures
- behavioral changes
- physical condition

Therefore visual appearance alone is insufficient to establish the complete internal stress state.

SOURCE:
PMC review on animal welfare and stress.

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13187226/

## 4. Individual Differences Matter

Stress response is influenced by:
- developmental history
- experience
- genetics
- social environment
- previous exposure
- physical condition

Therefore two animals exposed to the same event should not automatically be represented identically.
