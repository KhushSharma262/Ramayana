# 14_BATTLE_HIGH_STRESS RESEARCH INDEX

## Research Purpose

Establish evidence for realistic animal responses to:
- acute threat
- fear
- freezing
- flight
- defensive behavior
- cumulative stress
- injury and fatigue
- environmental disturbance
- human-associated stress
- battle exposure
- historical animal use
- canonical Ramayana battle scenes

## Research Separation

SCIENTIFIC EVIDENCE
Answers:
"What can real animals plausibly do?"

HISTORICAL EVIDENCE
Answers:
"What animals, equipment and roles existed in the relevant historical context?"

CANONICAL EVIDENCE
Answers:
"What does the Ramayana actually describe?"

PRODUCTION INFERENCE
Answers:
"How can approved evidence be represented cinematically?"

These four layers must never be silently merged.

## Research Rule

AI-generated imagery, video, audio or prompts are not evidence.

A plausible-looking AI animal does not establish biological truth.
