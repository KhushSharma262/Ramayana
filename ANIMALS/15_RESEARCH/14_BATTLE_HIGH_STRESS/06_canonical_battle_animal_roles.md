# CANONICAL BATTLE ANIMAL ROLES

## Horses

Canonical battle descriptions establish horses in:
- chariot use
- mounted use
- battle preparation
- battlefield exposure

Aranya Kanda 3.25 explicitly describes horses yoked to chariots and horses with riders.

Yuddha Kanda 6.33 describes chariot horses being prepared for battle.

## Elephants

Canonical descriptions establish elephants:
- with riders
- prepared/caparisoned for war
- present in battlefield formations
- exposed to battlefield violence

## Camels And Donkeys

Yuddha Kanda 6.60 contains a passage describing horses, camels, donkeys and elephants being driven/used amid intense battlefield action.

This establishes textual presence in that battle context but does not by itself provide species-specific stress behavior.

SOURCE:
https://www.valmikiramayan.net/yuddha/sarga60/yuddhasans60.htm

## Important Distinction

The text establishes role/presence.

It does not automatically establish:
- exact training method
- exact tack construction
- exact breed
- exact physiological response
- exact behavioral sequence

Those claims require separate research.

## Research Classification

CANONICALLY ESTABLISHED:
animal presence and role where explicitly stated.

SCIENTIFICALLY INFERRED:
possible real-world responses.

CINEMATICALLY INTERPRETED:
how the approved response is visually presented.

UNKNOWN:
anything not adequately supported.
