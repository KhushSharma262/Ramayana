# INDIA TAXONOMY AUTHORITIES

## ZSI — Zoological Survey of India

Official:
https://zsi.gov.in/

Fauna of India:
https://www.zsi.gov.in/fauna-of-india

Checklist:
https://zsi.gov.in/checklist

Role:
Primary Indian taxonomic and faunal reference.

Use for:
- taxonomy
- nomenclature
- Indian distribution
- species checklists
- faunal literature
- regional fauna

## National Biodiversity Portal / BIOMIS

India Biodiversity Portal:
https://indiabiodiversity.org/

National Biodiversity Portal:
https://biomis.nic.in/

Role:
Indian species records, taxonomy, occurrence and biodiversity information.

## Taxonomy Rule

Where modern Indian taxonomy is required:

ZSI / authoritative taxonomic literature
-> India Biodiversity / authenticated records
-> specialist literature
-> secondary sources

Common names alone are insufficient.
