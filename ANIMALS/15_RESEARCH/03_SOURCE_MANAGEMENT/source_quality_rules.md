# SOURCE QUALITY RULES

## Tier A — Primary / Highest Authority

- canonical primary text
- peer-reviewed original scientific study
- authoritative taxonomic authority
- archaeological primary evidence
- contemporaneous historical record

## Tier B

- systematic review
- scientific review
- specialist monograph
- institutional technical publication

## Tier C

- reputable secondary synthesis
- museum material
- educational institutional source

## Tier D

- popular article
- unsourced web page
- forum
- social media

Tier D may assist discovery.

Tier D cannot independently establish a critical canonical, biological or historical production claim.

## Prohibited As Evidence

- AI-generated images
- AI-generated video
- AI-generated audio
- movie depictions
- fantasy artwork
- unsourced social posts
- copied claims without traceable source
