# AUTHORITY ROLES

Define project authority separately from source authority.

Project roles may include:

RESEARCHER
Collects and documents evidence.

REVIEWER
Checks evidence, interpretation, and conflicts.

DECISION_OWNER
Owns an approved project decision.

QA
Checks downstream compliance.

PRODUCTION_OWNER
Implements approved decisions in production systems.

FINAL_AUTHORITY
Resolves unresolved project-level conflicts.

No role may silently perform another role's approval function.

Source authority and project authority are separate concepts.
