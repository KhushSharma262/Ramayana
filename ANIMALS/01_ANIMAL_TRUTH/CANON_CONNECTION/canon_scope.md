# CANON SCOPE

## PURPOSE

Determines whether an animal belongs in the canonical or reconstructed world of the series.

## SCOPE CLASSES

CANON_REQUIRED
CANON_SUPPORTED
HISTORICALLY_PLAUSIBLE
ECOLOGICALLY_PLAUSIBLE
PRODUCTION_BACKGROUND
UNCERTAIN
EXCLUDED

## CANON_REQUIRED

The animal is explicitly required by the selected canonical material.

## CANON_SUPPORTED

The animal is supported by the source/context but may not be individually named.

## HISTORICALLY_PLAUSIBLE

The animal is supported by historical reconstruction but not established as canon.

## ECOLOGICALLY_PLAUSIBLE

The animal is biologically/environmentally plausible but requires production justification.

## PRODUCTION_BACKGROUND

The animal exists only as background population support and must not be presented as a canonical event.

## UNCERTAIN

Evidence is insufficient or conflicting.

## EXCLUDED

The animal should not be used for the current canonical representation.

## IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

"Could have existed" is not equivalent to:

"Did exist in this scene."

"Common in the region" is not equivalent to:

"Canonically present."

## SCENE INSERTION RULE

An animal should not be inserted merely to make a scene feel more alive.

Every non-canonical background animal must have a production/environmental reason.

## MODERN BREEDS

Modern breeds/types must not automatically be projected backward into the Ramayana setting.

Species identity remains separate from domestic breed/type.
