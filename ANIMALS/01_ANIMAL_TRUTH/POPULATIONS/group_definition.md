# GROUP DEFINITION

A group is a biologically or production-relevant collection of animals represented collectively.

Group representation must preserve species-appropriate behavior.

Do not assume:

- all animals behave identically
- group movement is perfectly synchronized
- all individuals share identical fear or hunger states
- social hierarchy is visually obvious
- background animals are interchangeable

Population-level variation should be used to prevent repeated AI patterns while remaining biologically plausible.

Important individuals must be separated into individual records.
