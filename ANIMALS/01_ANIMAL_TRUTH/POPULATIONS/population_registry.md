# POPULATION REGISTRY

Purpose:
Represent groups of animals without creating unnecessary individual records.

Population/group records may represent:

- wild population
- herd
- flock
- troop
- pack
- colony
- stable population
- working group
- battlefield animal group
- background population

Required fields:

population_id:
species_id:
group_type:
geographic_scope:
temporal_scope:
estimated_size:
age_structure:
sex_structure:
social_structure:
environment:
human_association:
individual_ids_if_tracked:
source_ids:
claim_ids:
behavioral_relevance:
continuity_requirements:
status:
version:

Do not create individual records for background animals unless individual continuity is important to the story.
