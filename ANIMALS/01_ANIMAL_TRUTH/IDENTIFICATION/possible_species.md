# POSSIBLE SPECIES

## PURPOSE

Maintains the candidate pool before a final species identification is selected.

## REQUIRED FIELDS

animal_id
source_term
candidate_id
candidate_species
scientific_name
supporting_evidence
contradicting_evidence
linguistic_fit
contextual_fit
geographic_fit
historical_fit
zoological_fit
traditional_support
confidence
status

## STATUS VALUES

candidate
strong_candidate
weak_candidate
rejected
unresolved

## CANDIDATE RULE

A candidate must have a documented reason for inclusion.

## REJECTION RULE

Rejected candidates should remain recorded when their rejection helps explain the final decision.

## COMPARISON

Candidates should be compared on evidence rather than visual attractiveness.

## NO FORCED SELECTION

If Candidate A and Candidate B remain similarly plausible:

selected_species = unresolved

Do not force a species simply because production requires a final model.

## DOWNSTREAM CONSEQUENCE

An unresolved species may require:

- broader visual constraints
- multiple reference possibilities
- conditional production design
- later research

The uncertainty must remain visible.
