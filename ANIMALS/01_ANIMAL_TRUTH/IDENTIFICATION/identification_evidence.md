## EVIDENCE RULE

Evidence must be independently understandable.

A future researcher should be able to determine:

- what source was used
- what it says
- what conclusion it supports
- what conclusion it does not support

## EVIDENCE VS INTERPRETATION

Evidence:
"The source uses term X."

Interpretation:
"Term X may correspond to species Y."

Do not store the second statement as though it were the first.

## SOURCE QUALITY

Prefer project-approved authoritative sources.

Do not treat:

- unsourced blogs
- AI answers
- generated images
- random social posts
- adaptation artwork

as primary evidence.

They may be leads for research but not automatic authority.

## CONFLICTING EVIDENCE

Contradictory evidence must remain recorded.
