# IDENTIFICATION CONFIDENCE

## PURPOSE

Standardizes confidence in species identification.

## LEVELS

VERY_HIGH
HIGH
MEDIUM
LOW
VERY_LOW
UNKNOWN

## VERY_HIGH

Use only when:

- source identity is clear
- terminology is unambiguous
- context supports the identification
- competing interpretations are negligible

## HIGH

Evidence strongly supports one identification, but minor uncertainty remains.

## MEDIUM

One interpretation is preferred, but meaningful alternatives exist.

## LOW

Evidence is weak or significantly conflicting.

## VERY_LOW

Identification is speculative and should not be treated as established.

## UNKNOWN

Insufficient evidence exists to assign meaningful confidence.

## CONFIDENCE INPUTS

Consider:

source clarity
term clarity
translation reliability
contextual clarity
geographic plausibility
historical plausibility
zoological plausibility
traditional evidence
competing interpretations

## IMPORTANT

Confidence measures certainty of identification.

It does NOT measure:

- image quality
- AI quality
- cinematic quality
- model confidence
- production convenience

## PROHIBITION

Do not increase confidence merely because the same assumption has been repeated many times.
