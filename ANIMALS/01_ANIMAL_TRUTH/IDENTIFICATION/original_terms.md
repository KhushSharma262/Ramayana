# ORIGINAL TERMS

## PURPOSE

Preserves source terminology before translation or interpretation.

## REQUIRED FIELDS

term_id
animal_id
original_term
script
language
transliteration
grammatical_form
source_name
source_section
source_reference
context
quoted_or_paraphrased_evidence
semantic_notes

## WHY THIS MATTERS

Animal identification can fail during translation.

A single translated English/Hindi word may hide:

- multiple Sanskrit terms
- historical meanings
- contextual distinctions
- regional meanings
- taxonomic ambiguity

Therefore the original term is the primary linguistic anchor.

## RULE

Never delete the original term after a species has been selected.

## TRANSLATION SEPARATION

Original term:
SOURCE DATA

Translation:
INTERPRETATION

Species mapping:
BIOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION

These are different layers.

## SPELLING

Preserve the source spelling/transliteration used by the research source.

Alternative transliterations may be recorded separately.

## UNCERTAINTY

If the source term cannot be confidently interpreted:

status = UNCERTAIN

Do not force a modern animal name into the record.
