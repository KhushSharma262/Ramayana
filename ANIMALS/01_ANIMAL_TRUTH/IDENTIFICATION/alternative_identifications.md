# ALTERNATIVE IDENTIFICATIONS

## PURPOSE

Preserves competing interpretations so that rejected or secondary possibilities are not lost.

## REQUIRED FIELDS

alternative_id
animal_id
interpretation
basis
source
supporting_evidence
contradicting_evidence
limitations
historical_context
zoological_context
status
decision

## WHY ALTERNATIVES MATTER

Animal terminology can be ambiguous.

An interpretation selected today may later be revised when:

- a better source is found
- a translation is corrected
- taxonomy changes
- additional context appears
- another canonical passage clarifies the term

## STATUS

active
secondary
rejected
unresolved
historical
traditional

## RULE

Never erase an important alternative merely because another interpretation is currently preferred.

## DECISION RECORD

The selected interpretation must state:

WHY IT WAS SELECTED
WHAT EVIDENCE SUPPORTS IT
WHAT EVIDENCE OPPOSES IT
WHAT REMAINS UNKNOWN

## ANTI-HALLUCINATION RULE

An alternative is not a fact.

It is a documented interpretation with a defined evidence level.
