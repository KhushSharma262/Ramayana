# TRANSLATION MAPPING

## PURPOSE

Controls the transition from original terminology to translated terminology.

## REQUIRED FIELDS

term_id
source_term
source_language
translation
translator_or_source
translation_type
literal_meaning
contextual_meaning
species_implication
confidence
limitations
notes

## TRANSLATION TYPES

literal
contextual
traditional
modern
approximate
disputed

## IMPORTANT RULE

Translation is not taxonomy.

For example:

A translated term may identify a broad animal category without proving one modern species.

Therefore:

TRANSLATION
!=
SPECIES CONFIRMATION

## MULTIPLE TRANSLATIONS

When reputable sources translate the same term differently:

- retain each translation
- identify source
- identify reason
- compare contextual fit
- preserve disagreement

## CONFIDENCE

Translation confidence and species-identification confidence must be separate.

A highly reliable translation can still lead to an uncertain species identification.

## PROHIBITED BEHAVIOR

Do not choose the translation that produces the most visually convenient animal.

Do not modify the translation simply to match an already-generated image.
