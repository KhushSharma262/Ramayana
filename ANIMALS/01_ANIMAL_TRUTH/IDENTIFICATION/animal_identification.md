# ANIMAL IDENTIFICATION

## PURPOSE

Primary decision record for mapping a source term or described animal to a biological entity.

## IDENTIFICATION PROCESS

STEP 1
Preserve original terminology.

STEP 2
Record language and transliteration.

STEP 3
Record literal meaning.

STEP 4
Record contextual meaning.

STEP 5
Identify all reasonable biological candidates.

STEP 6
Record evidence supporting each candidate.

STEP 7
Record evidence contradicting each candidate.

STEP 8
Check historical/geographic plausibility.

STEP 9
Check zoological plausibility.

STEP 10
Check traditional interpretations.

STEP 11
Record conflicts.

STEP 12
Select candidate only when justified.

STEP 13
Assign confidence.

## REQUIRED RECORD

animal_id
source_term
language
transliteration
literal_translation
contextual_translation
source_reference
candidate_species
selected_species
identification_status
confidence
evidence_ids
conflict_ids
decision_reason

## FORBIDDEN SHORTCUTS

Never identify an animal solely from:

- a modern English translation
- a Hindi adaptation
- a visual stereotype
- an AI-generated image
- a popular television depiction
- a modern breed
- personal intuition

## IDENTIFICATION PRINCIPLE

The question is not:

"What animal looks right?"

The question is:

"What animal does the available evidence justify?"

## LOW CONFIDENCE

If evidence does not distinguish two species, preserve the ambiguity.

Do not manufacture certainty for downstream convenience.
