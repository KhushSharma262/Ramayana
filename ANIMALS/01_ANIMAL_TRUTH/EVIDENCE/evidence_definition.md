# EVIDENCE DEFINITION

Evidence is an identifiable observation, textual passage, artifact, scholarly finding, scientific result, or other traceable material that can be evaluated independently.

Evidence must not be silently converted into a conclusion.

Distinguish:

1. SOURCE
The originating work, publication, artifact, dataset, observation, or authority.

2. SOURCE MENTION
The specific place where an animal, term, characteristic, event, or related information occurs.

3. EVIDENCE
What that source mention actually provides.

4. CLAIM
A proposition supported, partially supported, or challenged by evidence.

5. INTERPRETATION
Reasoned explanation of what the evidence may mean.

6. DECISION
The project-approved conclusion used downstream.

Evidence must preserve uncertainty and limitations.

If evidence does not support a conclusion, the system must not manufacture support.
