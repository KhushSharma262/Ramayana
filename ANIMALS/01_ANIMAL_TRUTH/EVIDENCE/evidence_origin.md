# EVIDENCE ORIGIN

Every evidence record must identify where it came from.

Required origin classes:

- primary_text
- traditional_commentary
- historical_record
- archaeological_record
- scientific_literature
- zoological_reference
- ecological_reference
- linguistic_reference
- iconographic_reference
- expert_observation
- project_observation
- other_traceable_source

Origin must not be confused with authority.

A source may be authoritative for one question and irrelevant for another.

Project-generated material is not automatically external evidence.

Generated AI media must remain explicitly classified as synthetic/production material.
