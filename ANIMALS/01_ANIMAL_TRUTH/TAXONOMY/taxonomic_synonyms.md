# TAXONOMIC SYNONYMS

## PURPOSE

Prevents duplicate species records caused by different scientific names.

## REQUIRED FIELDS

species_id
synonym
synonym_type
authority
source
date_or_period
validity_status
relationship_to_accepted_name
notes

## SYNONYM TYPES

historical
junior_synonym
former_name
regional_taxonomic_usage
disputed
unresolved

## RULE

A synonym normally points to an existing biological entity.

Do not create a second species record unless evidence indicates that it is genuinely a different taxon.

## HISTORICAL SOURCES

Older scientific literature may use names that are no longer accepted.

Preserve those names because they may be necessary to understand research evidence.

## IDENTIFICATION CONSEQUENCE

A synonym does not automatically change the original Ramayana terminology.

Historical source terminology remains historically recorded.

## UNKNOWN

If synonymy cannot be established confidently:

status = unresolved

Do not merge entities merely because their names look similar.
