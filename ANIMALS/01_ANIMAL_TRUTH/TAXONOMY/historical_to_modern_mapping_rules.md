# HISTORICAL TO MODERN TAXONOMIC MAPPING RULES

Historical animal terminology and modern scientific taxonomy must be
treated as separate layers.

A historical term must NOT automatically be mapped to a modern species.

Required mapping states:

DIRECT_EQUIVALENCE
Strong evidence supports a specific modern taxon.

PROBABLE_EQUIVALENCE
A modern taxon is strongly suggested but alternatives remain.

POSSIBLE_EQUIVALENCE
A modern taxon is plausible but insufficiently established.

MULTIPLE_POSSIBILITIES
Several modern taxa remain viable.

UNRESOLVED
No defensible modern mapping can currently be established.

NO_VALID_MAPPING
The historical term cannot responsibly be mapped to a modern taxon.

Never use modern taxonomy to retroactively rewrite what the historical
source itself says.

Historical terminology must remain recoverable even after a modern
scientific mapping is selected.
