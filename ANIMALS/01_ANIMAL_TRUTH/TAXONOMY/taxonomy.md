# TAXONOMY

## PURPOSE

Defines the scientific classification of the selected biological animal.

## REQUIRED FIELDS

species_id
accepted_scientific_name
taxonomic_rank
kingdom
phylum
class
order
family
genus
species
taxonomy_source
taxonomy_version
classification_status
uncertainty

## PURPOSE OF TAXONOMY

Taxonomy provides biological identity.

Taxonomy does NOT prove:

- canonical presence
- historical presence
- scene presence
- behavior
- appearance
- breed
- cultural meaning

Those require separate evidence.

## TAXONOMIC SOURCE

Every scientific classification should identify the classification source.

Where practical record:

source name
version/date
access/reference information

## TAXONOMIC CONFLICT

If scientific authorities differ:

- record both positions
- identify the preferred classification
- explain why
- preserve uncertainty where necessary

## MODERN TAXONOMY

Modern scientific classification may not correspond directly to historical terminology.

Do not force the historical source into modern taxonomy without an identification chain.

## CHANGE

Taxonomy may change over time.

Historical taxonomic names should remain recorded as synonyms rather than silently rewriting historical evidence.
