# NEGATIVE EVIDENCE RULES

Absence of evidence is not automatically evidence of absence.

The following states must remain distinct:

NOT_FOUND
The research process did not find the expected information.

NOT_MENTIONED
The examined source does not mention the item.

EXPLICITLY_DENIED
The source explicitly states that the item does not exist or did not occur.

CONTRADICTED
Evidence exists that conflicts with the proposed claim.

ABSENT_FROM_EXPECTED_CONTEXT
The item is absent where its presence might reasonably have been expected.

UNRESEARCHED
The relevant source or domain has not been adequately examined.

Never convert:

NOT_FOUND → DOES_NOT_EXIST

NOT_MENTIONED → DID_NOT_EXIST

unless explicit evidence supports that conclusion.
