# SOURCE MENTION DEFINITION

## PURPOSE

Defines the smallest traceable unit connecting a source passage to an animal interpretation.

## REQUIRED CHAIN

source_id
source_reference
source_mention_id
original_term
context
evidence_id
interpretation
decision_id

## SOURCE MENTION MUST ANSWER

Where did the animal information originate?

What exactly was mentioned?

What context surrounded the mention?

What does the source actually establish?

What remains unstated?

## IMPORTANT

Do not attach later scientific facts directly to the source as though the source stated them.

Example:

SOURCE:
Animal term appears.

ZOOLOGY:
Modern species has specific behavior.

These are separate facts.

## MENTION STATUS

not_researched
researched
reviewed
approved
disputed
reopened

## MULTIPLE ENTITIES

A mention may connect to:

one species
multiple candidate species
one individual
a group
an unresolved entity

## RULE

The source mention is the provenance anchor for identification.
