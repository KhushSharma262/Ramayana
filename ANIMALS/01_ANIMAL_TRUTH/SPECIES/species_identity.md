# SPECIES IDENTITY

## PURPOSE

Provides the stable identity definition that all downstream systems consume.

## REQUIRED FIELDS

species_id
preferred_name
scientific_name
accepted_taxonomy
source_terms
identification_basis
canon_status
scope_status
confidence
identity_notes

## IDENTITY LOCK

Once approved, species identity becomes an upstream lock.

Visual variation must not change it.

Behavioral variation must not change it.

Lighting must not change it.

Camera angle must not change it.

AI generation must not change it.

## CHANGE CONTROL

A species identity change requires:

1. new evidence
2. conflict review
3. impact analysis
4. downstream review
5. user approval
6. updated identity lock

## IMPORTANT

The species identity should be expressed independently of visual design.

A future model should be able to understand the animal identity even if all reference images are removed.

## UNKNOWN

If the species cannot be established confidently, the identity must remain qualified.

Never silently convert a candidate into an approved species.
