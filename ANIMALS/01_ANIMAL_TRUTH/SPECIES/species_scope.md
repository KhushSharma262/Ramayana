# SPECIES SCOPE

## PURPOSE

Defines why a species is permitted to exist within the project's animal world.

## SCOPE STATUS

CANON_REQUIRED
CANON_SUPPORTED
HISTORICALLY_PLAUSIBLE
ECOLOGICALLY_PLAUSIBLE
PRODUCTION_BACKGROUND
UNCERTAIN
EXCLUDED

## CANON_REQUIRED

Explicitly required by source material.

## CANON_SUPPORTED

Supported by source context but not necessarily explicitly named.

## HISTORICALLY_PLAUSIBLE

Supported by historical reconstruction.

## ECOLOGICALLY_PLAUSIBLE

Biologically/environmentally plausible but not established as canonical.

## PRODUCTION_BACKGROUND

May be used as environmental background under project rules.

## UNCERTAIN

Evidence is insufficient.

## EXCLUDED

Should not be used for the intended representation.

## IMPORTANT

Plausibility is not canon.

Presence elsewhere in the historical region does not prove presence in a specific scene.

## REQUIRED DECISION

Every species introduced into a scene should be traceable to:

WHY IT EXISTS
WHO AUTHORIZED IT
WHAT EVIDENCE SUPPORTS IT
WHETHER IT IS CANON OR RECONSTRUCTION
