# REGIONAL NAMES

## PURPOSE

Stores regional and historical terminology relevant to animal identity.

## REQUIRED FIELDS

species_id
name
language
region
historical_or_modern
period
source
meaning
confidence
notes

## USE

Regional names may help establish:

- historical terminology
- cultural context
- translation differences
- geographic plausibility
- identification alternatives

## RULE

Regional terminology must not automatically be treated as evidence of a different species.

## HISTORICAL NAMES

Historical terminology should be preserved even when modern taxonomy uses a different name.

## UNKNOWN

If a regional name is suspected but not sourced:

do not add it as fact.

Mark it as:

UNVERIFIED LEAD

until a reliable source confirms it.

## TRANSLITERATION

Preserve the original regional form where available.

Do not normalize away meaningful linguistic information.
