# CONFIDENCE MODEL

Confidence must use a common scale throughout ANIMAL_TRUTH.

LEVEL 0 — NONE
No usable supporting basis.

LEVEL 1 — VERY LOW
Weak possibility; substantial unresolved alternatives.

LEVEL 2 — LOW
Some supporting basis but major uncertainty remains.

LEVEL 3 — MODERATE
Reasonable support with meaningful limitations.

LEVEL 4 — HIGH
Strong support with limited meaningful alternatives.

LEVEL 5 — VERY HIGH
Multiple strong independent sources or exceptionally direct evidence.

Confidence must always identify WHAT is being rated.

Examples:

source_confidence:
identification_confidence:
claim_confidence:
interpretation_confidence:
decision_confidence:

These dimensions must not be collapsed into one unexplained number.

A HIGH confidence interpretation does not automatically mean HIGH
confidence in the underlying species identification.

Confidence is not probability unless explicitly defined as probability.
