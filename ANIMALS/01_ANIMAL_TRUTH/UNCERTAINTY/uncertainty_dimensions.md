# UNCERTAINTY DIMENSIONS

Uncertainty must be classified by domain.

Required dimensions:

SOURCE_UNCERTAINTY
Uncertainty about source authenticity, reliability, completeness,
or transmission.

TEXT_UNCERTAINTY
Uncertainty in the wording or textual reading.

TRANSLATION_UNCERTAINTY
Uncertainty introduced by translation.

IDENTIFICATION_UNCERTAINTY
Uncertainty about what animal/entity is being referred to.

TAXONOMIC_UNCERTAINTY
Uncertainty in modern scientific classification.

HISTORICAL_UNCERTAINTY
Uncertainty about historical context.

BIOLOGICAL_UNCERTAINTY
Uncertainty about reconstructed biological characteristics.

BEHAVIORAL_UNCERTAINTY
Uncertainty about natural behavior.

ECOLOGICAL_UNCERTAINTY
Uncertainty about habitat or ecological context.

TEMPORAL_UNCERTAINTY
Uncertainty about when a state or characteristic applied.

INDIVIDUAL_UNCERTAINTY
Uncertainty about a specific animal's identity or state.

PRODUCTION_UNCERTAINTY
Uncertainty introduced during visual or technical reconstruction.

IMPORTANT:

These dimensions must remain separate.

Reducing one uncertainty does not automatically eliminate another.
