# IDENTITY LOCK RULES

Rules:

1. Canonical identity cannot be changed because an AI model produced a different visual.

2. Scientific identification cannot be changed solely because a generated image looks more convincing.

3. Visual references cannot override approved textual or scientific evidence without review.

4. Identity ambiguity must remain explicit.

5. Alternative identifications must remain recoverable.

6. An approved identity change must propagate to all dependent records.

7. No downstream asset may silently retain an obsolete identity after a confirmed change.

8. Identity lock protects truth; it does not freeze legitimate research forever.

9. Reopening requires an explicit reason and evidence.

10. A production convenience is never sufficient justification for changing identity.
