# RECORD LIFECYCLE

## PURPOSE

Defines the lifecycle of animal-truth records.

## STATES

DRAFT
RESEARCHING
REVIEWED
PROVISIONAL
APPROVED
LOCKED
REOPENED
SUPERSEDED
DEPRECATED
REJECTED
ARCHIVED

## DRAFT

Record exists but is incomplete.

## RESEARCHING

Evidence gathering is active.

## REVIEWED

Evidence has undergone review.

## PROVISIONAL

A working interpretation exists but remains qualified.

## APPROVED

Authorized for defined downstream use.

## LOCKED

Identity is protected from silent modification.

## REOPENED

New evidence requires reassessment.

## SUPERSEDED

Replaced by a newer record.

## DEPRECATED

No longer recommended but historically preserved.

## REJECTED

Explicitly rejected.

## ARCHIVED

Retained for historical/audit purposes.

## RULE

Records are never silently deleted because their status changes.

## ID STABILITY

IDs remain resolvable even after supersession.

## PRODUCTION RULE

Only records permitted by approval and lifecycle status may be consumed
by downstream production systems.
