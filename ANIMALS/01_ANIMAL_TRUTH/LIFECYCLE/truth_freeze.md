# TRUTH FREEZE

Purpose:
Define when 01_ANIMAL_TRUTH is considered sufficiently reviewed for
production use.

Subsystem lifecycle:

OPEN
Research and definition are active.

RESEARCH_COMPLETE
Known research scope has been completed for the current target.

REVIEWED
Evidence, claims, interpretations, conflicts, and uncertainties have
been reviewed.

APPROVED
Relevant records are approved for downstream use.

FROZEN
The approved truth state is frozen for production.

REOPENED
New evidence or an identified error requires review.

SUPERSEDED
The record/subsystem state has been replaced by a newer approved state.

FREEZE REQUIREMENTS:

- required research scope documented
- unresolved conflicts explicitly recorded
- important claims traceable
- identity status established
- uncertainty recorded
- downstream restrictions established
- approval recorded
- version recorded
- handoff requirements established

FROZEN does NOT mean “eternally proven.”

It means:

“This is the currently approved project truth state for production.”

New authoritative evidence may reopen it.

No silent edits are permitted to a frozen record.
