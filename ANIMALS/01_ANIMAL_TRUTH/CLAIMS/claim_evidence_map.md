# CLAIM EVIDENCE MAP

## PURPOSE

Maps individual claims to the evidence that supports or contradicts them.

## REQUIRED

claim_id
evidence_id
relationship
strength
notes

## RELATIONSHIP VALUES

supports
strongly_supports
weakly_supports
contradicts
strongly_contradicts
contextualizes
does_not_resolve

## RULE

Never assume that all evidence attached to an animal applies equally
to every claim about that animal.

## CONFLICT

A claim may have both supporting and contradicting evidence.

That is permitted.

The conflict must be visible.

## CONFIDENCE

Confidence must reflect the total evidence relevant to that claim,
not the number of citations.

## PROHIBITION

Citation count is not evidence strength.

Ten copies of the same unsourced statement do not equal ten independent sources.
