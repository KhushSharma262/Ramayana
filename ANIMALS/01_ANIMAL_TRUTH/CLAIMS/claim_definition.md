# CLAIM DEFINITION

A CLAIM is a specific proposition about an animal, animal identity,
animal characteristic, animal occurrence, or animal-related fact.

A claim is NOT automatically:

- a source
- a source mention
- evidence
- an interpretation
- a decision
- a production choice

A claim must be independently traceable to its supporting and/or
contradicting evidence.

Required fields:

claim_id:
claim_text:
claim_type:
entity_scope:
world_time:
knowledge_time:
evidence_ids:
relationship_types:
interpretation_ids:
decision_ids:
uncertainty_id:
confidence:
status:
researcher:
reviewer:
approval:
version:

Allowed claim types include:

- textual_claim
- identity_claim
- species_claim
- taxonomy_claim
- anatomy_claim
- physiology_claim
- behavior_claim
- ecological_claim
- geographic_claim
- historical_claim
- biological_claim
- individual_claim
- population_claim
- relationship_claim
- temporal_claim
- negative_claim
- production_relevant_claim

A claim must not contain hidden production assumptions.

If a proposition cannot be supported, it must remain:

UNKNOWN
UNCERTAIN
UNRESOLVED
or
REJECTED

rather than being silently promoted to fact.
