# INDIVIDUAL REGISTRY

## PURPOSE

Tracks important individual animals whose identity persists across production.

## WHEN TO CREATE AN INDIVIDUAL

Create an individual record when:

- the animal is explicitly named
- the animal recurs
- continuity matters
- the animal is mounted/ridden
- the animal has a meaningful human relationship
- the animal has a tracked injury/state
- the animal is narratively important
- the same animal must be recognized across scenes

## WHEN NOT TO CREATE ONE

Do not create records for every background animal.

Background populations should normally be represented through species and population variation.

## REQUIRED FIELDS

individual_id
species_id
individual_name
individual_role
sex
age_class
canon_status
tracking_status
first_appearance
last_known_appearance
continuity_requirement
identity_basis

## ID FORMAT

Recommended:

IND-0001
IND-0002
IND-0003

## STABILITY

individual_id must remain stable across all episodes.

A visual regeneration does not create a new individual.

## BACKGROUND VARIATION

Background animals may vary naturally without receiving individual IDs.

This prevents unnecessary database growth while preserving realism.
