# INDIVIDUAL RELATIONSHIPS

## PURPOSE

Defines documented relationships between an individual animal and other entities.

## IMPORTANT

A relationship must be evidence-based or explicitly identified as production reconstruction.

## ANTHROPOMORPHISM CONTROL

Do not infer human-style emotional relationships simply because an animal remains near a person.

Observed proximity can have multiple causes:

- habituation
- training
- food association
- safety
- social bonding
- environmental dependence
- learned response

The biological interpretation belongs to the behavior system.

## SOURCE STATUS

Relationship may be:

direct_canon
derived_canon
traditional_interpretation
historical_reconstruction
production_choice
unknown

## CONTINUITY

Recurring relationships must remain consistent unless the story explicitly changes them.
