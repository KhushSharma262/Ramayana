# INDIVIDUAL DEFINITION

## PURPOSE

Defines the persistent identity of one specific animal.

## REQUIRED

individual_id
species_id
sex
age_class
role
source_basis
identity_basis
tracking_status
continuity_requirement

## INDIVIDUAL IDENTITY

An individual may have:

- distinctive markings
- coloration
- scars
- body proportions
- documented physical characteristics
- known relationship
- narrative identity

Only evidence-supported characteristics should become identity locks.

## DO NOT CONFUSE

Individual identity
with

momentary physical state.

For example:

injury
fatigue
hunger
stress
wetness
dust
mud

are not necessarily permanent identity traits.

Those belong to downstream state systems.

## AI RULE

A generated accidental marking is not automatically part of the individual's identity.

## CHANGE CONTROL

If an identity-defining characteristic changes intentionally:

record reason
record scene
record source/decision
review continuity impact

## IDENTITY LOCK

Once approved, the individual must remain recognizable across relevant scenes.
