# INDIVIDUAL STATUS

## PURPOSE

Stores identity-level status of tracked animals.

## ALLOWED VALUES

alive
dead
unknown
absent
introduced
retired
continuity_uncertain

## IMPORTANT SEPARATION

This file does NOT control moment-to-moment biological states.

It does not define:

hunger
thirst
fatigue
stress
fear
pain
alertness
temperature
hydration
injury progression
behavior

Those belong to biological/behavioral systems.

## STATUS TIMELINE

Where relevant, record:

status
effective_scene
effective_episode
source
confidence

## DEATH

If an animal's death is canonically relevant, record the source.

Do not assume death merely because an animal disappears from a later scene.

## ABSENCE

ABSENT means the individual is not currently present in the relevant production context.

It does not mean dead.

## UNKNOWN

Use UNKNOWN when the source does not establish the animal's later status.

## CONTINUITY

Status changes must be reflected in downstream continuity.
