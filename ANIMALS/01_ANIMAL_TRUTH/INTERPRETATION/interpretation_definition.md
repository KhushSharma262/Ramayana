# INTERPRETATION DEFINITION

Interpretation is the reasoned bridge between evidence and decision.

Allowed interpretation states:

- direct_reading
- linguistic_interpretation
- historical_interpretation
- zoological_interpretation
- ecological_interpretation
- comparative_interpretation
- contextual_interpretation
- reconstruction
- unresolved

Interpretation must state:

WHAT IS KNOWN
WHAT IS INFERRED
WHAT IS UNCERTAIN
WHAT ALTERNATIVES EXIST

When evidence is insufficient, the correct interpretation may be:

UNKNOWN
or
UNRESOLVED

rather than an invented answer.
