# ASSUMPTIONS

## PURPOSE

Separates temporary working assumptions from established facts.

## ASSUMPTION STATUS

PROPOSED
ACTIVE
QUALIFIED
REJECTED
CONVERTED_TO_FACT
EXPIRED

## REQUIRED

assumption_id
entity_id
assumption
reason
why_needed
supporting_evidence_ids
limitations
affected_systems
status
created_date
review_date
decision_id

## HARD RULE

An assumption is NOT a fact.

It must never silently enter:

canon
species registry
identity lock
production prompt
continuity lock

without explicit conversion and approval.

## USE

Assumptions are permitted when production work must temporarily continue
while research remains incomplete.

## EXPIRY

Temporary assumptions must have a review condition.

## CONVERSION

To convert an assumption into an approved fact:

research
evidence
claim
decision
approval
change_history

are required.
