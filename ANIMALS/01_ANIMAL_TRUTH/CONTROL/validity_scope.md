# VALIDITY SCOPE

## PURPOSE

Prevents a fact from being applied outside the context in which it is valid.

## REQUIRED DIMENSIONS

world_time
knowledge_time
geography
source_context
species_scope
individual_scope
scene_scope
episode_scope

## WORLD TIME

The historical/fictional period represented by the production.

## KNOWLEDGE TIME

The date/period of the scientific, historical or scholarly knowledge being used.

## IMPORTANT

Modern scientific knowledge must not be presented as though historical
people necessarily possessed that knowledge.

## GEOGRAPHY

A zoological fact may be valid for one geographic region but not another.

## CONTEXT

A behavior or terminology claim may be valid only within a specific context.

## RULE

Never universalize a context-specific fact without justification.

## REQUIRED STATUS

GLOBAL
REGIONAL
TEMPORAL
CONTEXTUAL
INDIVIDUAL
SCENE_SPECIFIC
UNKNOWN
