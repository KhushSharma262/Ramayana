# DECISION DEFINITION

## PURPOSE

Defines the structure of a defensible animal-related decision.

## REQUIRED CHAIN

QUESTION
→ CLAIMS
→ EVIDENCE
→ ALTERNATIVES
→ CONFLICTS
→ DECISION
→ APPROVAL
→ DOWNSTREAM IMPACT

## DECISION MUST ANSWER

What question was being answered?

What evidence was considered?

What alternatives existed?

Why was one position selected?

What remains uncertain?

What systems are affected?

What restrictions apply?

## DECISION QUALITY

A decision is not stronger merely because:

- it is older
- it is repeated often
- an AI model agrees
- an adaptation uses it
- it is visually convenient

## QUALIFIED DECISION

A decision may be accepted with qualification.

Example:

Species A selected for production,
while Species B remains a documented alternative.

## FINAL AUTHORITY

User approval is required where the project identifies the decision
as requiring creative/canonical final authority.
