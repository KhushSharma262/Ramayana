# DECISION REGISTRY

## PURPOSE

Defines formal decisions made from evidence.

A decision is different from a claim.

CLAIM:
What is being asserted.

DECISION:
What the project chooses to do with that claim.

## REQUIRED

decision_id
entity_id
decision_type
question
selected_position
alternative_positions
reason
claim_ids
evidence_ids
conflict_ids
confidence
research_status
approval_status
decision_date
decision_scope
downstream_impact

## DECISION TYPES

species_identification
taxonomy
canon_status
scope
individual_identity
relationship
translation
source_interpretation
production_use

## DECISION RULE

A decision must explain WHY the selected position was chosen.

## ALTERNATIVES

Material rejected or secondary interpretations must remain recorded.

## APPROVAL

A research decision is not automatically a production approval.

## REOPENING

A decision may be reopened when:

- new evidence appears
- stronger source is discovered
- contradiction appears
- taxonomy changes materially
- user requests reassessment

## IMMUTABILITY

Historical decisions must not be silently overwritten.
