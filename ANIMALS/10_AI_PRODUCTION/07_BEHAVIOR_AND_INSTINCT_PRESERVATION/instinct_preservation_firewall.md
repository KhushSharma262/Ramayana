# INSTINCT PRESERVATION FIREWALL

The AI must preserve approved species-appropriate instincts.

Examples:

- prey animals remain alert to credible threats
- predators do not attack without contextual motivation
- herd/social animals respond to group state
- territorial animals respect territorial context
- mothers respond to offspring appropriately
- animals avoid physically dangerous situations when plausible
- unfamiliar humans/equipment may produce appropriate caution
- habituated/trained animals retain animal instincts
- domestic animals do not become psychologically human

Training modifies learned responses; it does not erase species biology.
