# ANIMATION TEMPORAL CONSISTENCY

Animation must preserve physical causality.

Reject:

- teleportation
- impossible acceleration
- discontinuous limb positions
- disappearing anatomy
- changing species morphology between frames
- changing markings between frames
- impossible weight transfer
- foot sliding
- floating
- broken contact with terrain
- inconsistent equipment attachment
- unexplained behavioral state changes

Movement truth remains owned by MOVEMENT.

AI animation is implementation only.
