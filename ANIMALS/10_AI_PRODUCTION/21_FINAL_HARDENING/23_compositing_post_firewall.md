# COMPOSITING AND POST FIREWALL

Every derived asset remains subordinate to the source asset state.

This includes:

- compositing
- masking
- rotoscoping
- inpainting
- outpainting
- frame interpolation
- stabilization
- denoising
- sharpening
- restoration
- upscaling
- color processing
- visual effects
- cleanup

Post-processing cannot silently introduce:

identity drift
anatomy drift
behavior drift
movement drift
ecology drift
equipment drift
continuity drift
historical contamination
canonical contamination

DERIVED ASSET != NEW TRUTH.
