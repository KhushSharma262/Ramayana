# TEMPORAL FRAME CONTINUITY FIREWALL

Video is not merely a sequence of independent images.

Across frames, preserve:

- animal identity
- anatomy
- body proportions
- markings
- equipment
- position continuity
- motion continuity
- gait
- limb state
- environmental relationship
- lighting continuity where required
- interaction continuity
- behavioral state
- fatigue state
- temporal state

Frame-to-frame identity drift is an asset failure.

A single beautiful frame cannot justify an inconsistent sequence.
