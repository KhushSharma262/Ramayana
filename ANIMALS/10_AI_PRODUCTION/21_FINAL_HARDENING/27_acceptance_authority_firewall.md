# ACCEPTANCE AUTHORITY FIREWALL

Approval cannot override factual ownership.

A production approver may approve implementation quality only
within the boundaries of approved upstream state.

Approval cannot legitimize:

- false anatomy
- false species
- unsupported behavior
- unsupported ecology
- unsupported history
- unsupported canonical interpretation
- identity drift
- continuity drift

If an approver detects an upstream conflict:

RETURN TO UPSTREAM OWNER.
DO NOT FORCE APPROVAL.
