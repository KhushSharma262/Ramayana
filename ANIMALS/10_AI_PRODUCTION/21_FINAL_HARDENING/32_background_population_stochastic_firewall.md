# BACKGROUND POPULATION STOCHASTIC FIREWALL

Background populations may vary naturally between generations,
but variation must remain within approved ecological and behavioral bounds.

Permitted variation:

- position
- orientation
- minor posture
- natural spacing
- individual appearance variation
- non-critical movement

Forbidden variation:

- impossible density
- unsupported species
- wrong habitat
- unexplained social structure
- sudden individualization
- continuity-critical identity changes

Population randomness must remain ecologically plausible.
