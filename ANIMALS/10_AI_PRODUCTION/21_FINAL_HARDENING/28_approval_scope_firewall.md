# APPROVAL SCOPE FIREWALL

Approval must have explicit scope.

An approval applies to:

asset_id
generation_id
prompt_version
model_version
reference_set_version
scene_id
shot_id
continuity_snapshot_id
validation_version

Approval for one asset does not automatically approve:

- another generation
- another model
- another prompt
- another scene
- another shot
- another animal
- another continuity state

APPROVAL IS NOT GENERIC.
