# FORBIDDEN PRODUCTION SHORTCUTS

The following shortcuts are prohibited:

1. Prompt-only approval.
2. Reference-only approval.
3. Model-confidence approval.
4. Visual-beauty approval.
5. "Looks realistic" approval.
6. Copying another animal's approved identity.
7. Copying another scene's state without continuity validation.
8. Treating a regenerated asset as the same asset automatically.
9. Editing an asset until it matches an incorrect prompt.
10. Changing canonical state to accommodate AI output.
11. Skipping biological QA for background animals when they are
    visible enough to affect realism.
12. Skipping continuity QA because the animal is off-center.
13. Treating post-processing as harmless.
14. Treating interpolation as neutral.
15. Treating upscaling as truth-preserving without validation.

ANY SHORTCUT THAT BYPASSES A REQUIRED GATE = BLOCKED.
