# MULTI-ASSET SCENE CONSISTENCY

A scene containing multiple assets must be validated as a system.

Check:

- animal identity
- relative positions
- interaction state
- environmental state
- lighting where continuity matters
- equipment
- shadows/contact
- scale
- spatial relationships
- temporal ordering
- behavior compatibility
- movement compatibility

An individually valid asset can still create an invalid scene.

SCENE VALIDITY != SUM OF INDIVIDUAL ASSET VALIDITY.
