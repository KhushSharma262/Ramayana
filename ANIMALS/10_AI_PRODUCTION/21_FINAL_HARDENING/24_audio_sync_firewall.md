# AUDIO SYNC FIREWALL

Animal audio must remain synchronized with:

- visible action
- species
- behavioral state
- interaction
- environment
- temporal position

Audio cannot be used to falsely imply an animal state that is not
supported by the visual and behavioral state.

Dramatic sound design cannot become behavioral evidence.

A roar, growl, cry or call must not automatically imply aggression,
fear or distress without contextual support.
