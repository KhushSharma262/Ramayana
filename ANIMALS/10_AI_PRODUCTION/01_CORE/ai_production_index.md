# AI PRODUCTION INDEX

AI Production is the implementation layer of the animal system.

AI Production does NOT define biological, behavioral, ecological,
domestic/training, interaction, continuity, or visual truth.

It consumes approved upstream states and converts them into:
PROMPTS -> GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS -> ASSETS -> SHOTS.

MASTER RULE:

AI CREATES THE REPRESENTATION.
THE APPROVED ANIMAL WORLD DETERMINES WHAT MUST BE REPRESENTED.

Production must preserve realism, natural instincts, species-specific
behavior, physical plausibility, environmental response, and continuity.
