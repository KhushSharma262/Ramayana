# AI PRODUCTION OWNERSHIP AND BOUNDARIES

BIOLOGY:
physical and physiological truth.

BEHAVIOR:
motivation, behavioral state, instinct, response and learning.

MOVEMENT:
physical movement, biomechanics, gait and physical execution.

ECOLOGY:
habitat, resources, environmental and population context.

DOMESTIC / TRAINED:
domestication, training, work, human-animal relationships and equipment.

INTERACTION:
interaction event identity, participants, outcome and consequences.

VISUAL IDENTITY:
canonical visual representation.

CONTINUITY:
persistence, snapshots, timeline and state history.

AI PRODUCTION:
implementation only.

AI Production cannot override upstream ownership.
