# SCOPE AND NON-GOALS

AI Production SHALL NOT:

- invent species traits
- invent animal instincts
- invent unsupported behavior
- invent historical equipment
- redefine anatomy
- redefine habitat
- redefine identity
- redefine continuity
- convert AI artifacts into evidence
- silently correct upstream contradictions
- use cinematic drama as justification for biological impossibility

If an upstream requirement is unresolved, production is BLOCKED.
