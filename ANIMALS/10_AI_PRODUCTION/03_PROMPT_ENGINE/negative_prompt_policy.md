# NEGATIVE PROMPT POLICY

Negative prompts may prevent known generation failures.

They must NOT be used to redefine canonical truth.

Examples of valid controls:

- no anthropomorphic posture
- no human facial expression
- no impossible anatomy
- no extra limbs
- no incorrect species morphology
- no unexplained equipment
- no modern objects in historical context
- no exaggerated aggression without cause
- no artificial symmetry
- no plastic-looking skin or fur

Negative prompts are implementation controls, not evidence.
