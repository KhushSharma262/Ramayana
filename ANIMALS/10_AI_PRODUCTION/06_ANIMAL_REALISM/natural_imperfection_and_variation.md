# NATURAL IMPERFECTION AND VARIATION

Real animals are not perfectly symmetrical or static.

Permitted natural variation includes:

- minor asymmetry
- fur/feather irregularity
- dirt
- wear
- scars where approved
- variable posture
- breathing
- blinking
- ear movement
- tail movement
- weight shifts
- imperfect gait
- small environmental interactions

Variation must remain species-appropriate and continuity-safe.
