# ANIMAL REALISM MASTER RULE

ANIMALS MUST BE TREATED AS LIVING ANIMALS, NOT HUMAN ACTORS IN ANIMAL BODIES.

Every animal generation must preserve:

- species morphology
- realistic proportions
- musculature
- skeletal constraints
- balance
- sensory orientation
- breathing
- fatigue
- weight
- gravity
- surface interaction
- species-specific posture
- natural asymmetry
- individual variation
- environmental response

Photorealism alone is insufficient.

BIOLOGICAL PLAUSIBILITY IS REQUIRED.
