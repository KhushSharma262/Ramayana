# BIOLOGICAL / BEHAVIORAL QA

Ask:

- Does the animal look physically real?
- Is anatomy plausible?
- Is posture plausible?
- Is the behavior motivated?
- Is the response species-appropriate?
- Is the animal behaving like an animal rather than a human performer?
- Does training alter the response appropriately without erasing instinct?
- Does the environment support the behavior?
- Is the reaction proportional to the trigger?
