# VALIDATION GATE

Minimum validation domains:

1. Source/provenance
2. Species/taxonomy
3. Biology
4. Behavior
5. Instinct
6. Movement
7. Ecology
8. Domestic/training state
9. Interaction
10. Visual identity
11. Continuity
12. Historical/canonical context
13. Technical quality
14. Scene/shot requirements

Any critical conflict = BLOCKED.
