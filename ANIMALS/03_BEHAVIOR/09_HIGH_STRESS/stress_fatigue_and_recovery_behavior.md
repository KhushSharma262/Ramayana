# STRESS / FATIGUE / RECOVERY BEHAVIOR

Stress is an internal state with observable behavioral consequences.

## POSSIBLE SIGNS

increased vigilance
avoidance
restlessness
altered movement
vocalization
reduced feeding
repetitive behavior
defensive behavior
escape attempts
freezing

Species-specific validation required.

## FATIGUE

Fatigue may alter:

speed
movement precision
attention
rest frequency
response time
willingness to continue
recovery time

## RECOVERY

Recovery should be gradual.

Track:

event
peak state
duration
cool-down
rest
return to baseline

## AI REALISM

Do not create endless high-energy behavior.

Animals need:

rest
breathing recovery
repositioning
water
feeding where appropriate
sleep where appropriate

## HEALTH

Pain, illness and injury belong to BIOLOGY.

Behavior records how those biological states manifest behaviorally.
