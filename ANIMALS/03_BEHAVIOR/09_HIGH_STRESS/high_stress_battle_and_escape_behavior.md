# HIGH-STRESS / BATTLE / ESCAPE BEHAVIOR

This file governs extreme scenes.

## TRIGGERS

weapons
loud impacts
fire
smoke
blood
injury
crowds
running humans
horses
elephants
chariots
falling bodies
sudden movement
restricted escape
predator attack

## RESPONSE

Do not assume every animal panics.

Response depends on:

species
training
habituation
individual
distance
escape route
injury
social group
noise
previous experience

## GROUP RESPONSE

Group reactions should not automatically be perfectly synchronized.

Possible sequence:

one animal detects threat
→ orientation
→ nearby animals respond
→ group movement develops
→ some animals hesitate
→ others flee
→ group reorganizes

## BATTLE REALISM

Animals may:

avoid danger
startle
change direction
refuse movement
attempt escape
become difficult to control
fatigue
injure themselves
recover

## FORBIDDEN

No supernatural bravery.

No human-style military discipline unless training and species behavior
support the action.

No automatic charging merely because the scene is a battle.

No repeated identical panic animation.
