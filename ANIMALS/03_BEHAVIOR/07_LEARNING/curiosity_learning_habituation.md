# CURIOSITY / LEARNING / HABITUATION

## CURIOSITY

Curiosity should be represented through observable exploratory behavior:

orientation
approach
inspection
sniffing
touching
listening
watching
withdrawal
re-approach

Not every animal investigates every novelty.

## LEARNING

Relevant mechanisms may include:

habituation
conditioning
social learning
spatial learning
experience-based avoidance
cue recognition

Species-specific evidence required.

## HABITUATION

Repeated harmless exposure may reduce response.

But habituation is:

species-specific
individual-specific
stimulus-specific
context-dependent

## NOVELTY

Response to a novel:

person
animal
object
sound
smell
environment
movement

must depend on species and individual experience.

## CONTINUITY

A previously habituated animal should not automatically react as if the
stimulus is completely new in every shot.

## CAUTION

Do not use "curiosity" as a generic explanation for unexplained animal
behavior. Define the observable behavior and context.
