# BACKGROUND POPULATION BEHAVIOR

Background animals must look alive without becoming individually scripted.

## POPULATION VARIATION

vary:

age
sex where visually determinable
posture
orientation
activity
distance
movement speed
attention
rest
feeding
social position

## ASYNCHRONY

Do not make all animals:

turn together
walk together
look together
eat together
stop together
fly together
vocalize together

unless the species naturally coordinates that behavior.

## ACTIVITY DISTRIBUTION

A realistic population may contain:

resting individuals
feeding individuals
moving individuals
watching individuals
grooming individuals
interacting individuals
sleeping individuals
juveniles
adults

depending on species, time and environment.

## CINEMATIC USE

Background behavior should support the environment rather than compete
with principal action.

## AI ARTIFACT CONTROL

Avoid:

duplicate animals
identical poses
identical motion cycles
identical spacing
impossible group density
floating animals
teleportation
species mixing without ecological justification

# BEHAVIOR RESEARCH AND EVIDENCE

Behavioral claims must be evidence-backed.

## SOURCE PRIORITY

1. Primary scientific research
2. Authoritative zoological institutions
3. Major reference works
4. Reliable secondary literature
5. Secondary sources where primary evidence is unavailable
6. Directorial reconstruction

AI-generated text, AI images and AI videos are not biological evidence.

## CLAIM CHAIN

SOURCE
→ EVIDENCE
→ INTERPRETATION
→ CLAIM
→ DECISION
→ APPROVAL
→ PRODUCTION

## EACH IMPORTANT CLAIM SHOULD RECORD

claim:
species:
context:
source:
evidence:
interpretation:
scope:
geography:
age:
sex:
season:
uncertainty:
confidence:

## SCOPE

Do not generalize:

individual
→ species

population
→ entire species

one sex
→ both sexes

juvenile
→ adult

one season
→ all seasons

one geographic population
→ all populations

without evidence.

## INDIA

Prefer India-specific evidence where relevant.

Global evidence may be used where appropriate but must retain its actual
geographic scope.

## UNKNOWN

If evidence is insufficient:

RESEARCH_REQUIRED

Do not fabricate a plausible answer.
