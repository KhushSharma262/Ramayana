# BEHAVIOR CONFLICTS AND UNCERTAINTY

Conflicts must remain visible.

## CONFLICT TYPES

source vs source
species population vs global species
wild vs domestic
juvenile vs adult
male vs female
season vs season
individual vs species baseline
historical source vs modern biology
canon description vs zoological interpretation

## DO NOT

average incompatible behavioral claims merely to remove conflict.
