# FEAR / DEFENSE / PREDATOR / PREY BEHAVIOR

## THREAT CHAIN

DETECTION
→ ORIENTATION
→ ASSESSMENT
→ VIGILANCE
→ RESPONSE
→ ESCALATION OR ESCAPE
→ RECOVERY

## POSSIBLE RESPONSES

freeze
orient
hide
withdraw
avoid
flee
climb
seek_water
group_together
alarm_signal
defensive_display
defensive_attack
counterattack

Species-specific evidence determines which responses apply.

## PREDATOR

Predator behavior can include:

search
detect
orient
stalk
approach
pause
assess
pursue
attack
capture
consume

Do not depict predators as continuously attacking.

## PREY

Prey behavior can include:

vigilance
group scanning
alarm
freeze
escape
direction change
cover seeking
group movement
defensive behavior

## ESCAPE

Escape depends on:

terrain
distance
predator position
group structure
animal fitness
injury
experience
available refuge

## CORNERED ANIMAL

An animal unable to escape may shift from avoidance to defense.

This is species-specific.

## RECOVERY

After threat removal:

vigilance may remain elevated
attention may continue scanning
movement may remain cautious
normal behavior gradually resumes

Do not instantly reset the state.
