## HUMAN ANTHROPOMORPHISM CONTROL

Do not interpret animal behavior as human intention unless evidence supports it.

A dog approaching a person does not automatically mean affection.

A horse resisting does not automatically mean disobedience.

An elephant approaching a person does not automatically mean friendship.

Interpret behavior through species biology and context.
