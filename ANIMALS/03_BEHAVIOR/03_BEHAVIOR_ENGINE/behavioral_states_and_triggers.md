# BEHAVIORAL STATES AND TRIGGERS

Behavioral states are not human emotions.

They represent biologically meaningful internal conditions that influence
observable behavior.

## IMPORTANT

One stimulus does not guarantee one response.

Response depends on:

species
individual
age
sex
health
experience
distance
escape availability
social context
previous habituation
current energy
current stress

## CONTINUITY

A strong state should persist across adjacent shots unless there is a
credible reason for recovery.

Do not reset an animal to neutral after every cut.
