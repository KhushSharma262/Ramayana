## ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS

HEAT
→ altered activity, shade-seeking, water use or other species-specific
responses where supported.

COLD
→ species-specific thermoregulatory behavior.

RAIN
→ shelter, movement, feeding or activity changes where biologically supported.

WIND
→ sensory and movement effects where relevant.

DUST
→ respiratory/sensory/environmental response where relevant.

SMOKE
→ avoidance and respiratory effects where relevant.

FIRE
→ threat detection, avoidance and escape.

FLOOD
→ altered movement, refuge-seeking and escape where relevant.

DROUGHT
→ resource changes and altered movement/foraging.

NOISE
→ startle, vigilance, avoidance or habituation depending on species.

LIGHT
→ altered activity only where biologically relevant.

## ENVIRONMENT OWNERSHIP

ENVIRONMENT defines the physical conditions.

BEHAVIOR defines how the animal responds.

Neither system should duplicate the other.
