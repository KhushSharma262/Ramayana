# REPRODUCTION / PARENTING / OFFSPRING BEHAVIOR

Reproductive behavior is biologically relevant even though mating scenes
will not be shown.

## COVERAGE

courtship
mate selection
seasonality
territorial behavior
nest/den preparation
pair bonding where applicable
parent-offspring recognition
maternal behavior
paternal behavior where applicable
offspring dependence
feeding offspring
protection
weaning
separation

## PARENT–OFFSPRING

Record:

recognition
contact
following
feeding
protection
alarm response
separation response
reunion
teaching where supported

## AGE

Behavior must match life stage.

Do not give juveniles adult behavioral capabilities without evidence.

## PRODUCTION RULE

Reproductive biology may influence:

season
social structure
territoriality
parenting
group composition

Mating itself does not need to be depicted.
