# AUDIO OWNERSHIP AND BOUNDARIES

## Biology

Owns physical and physiological ability to produce sound.

## Behavior

Owns motivation, behavioral state, communication purpose, fear, aggression, courtship, distress, alarm, social signaling, rest, feeding and other behavioral causes.

## Movement

Owns physical movement and mechanical sound consequences.

## Ecology

Owns acoustic environment, habitat soundscape, species presence, environmental sources, seasonality and ecological relationships.

## Domestic/Trained

Owns domestication, training, handler relationships, work context, commands, equipment and human-animal acoustic context.

## Interaction

Owns interaction event identity, participants, event type, outcome and relationship consequences.

## Continuity

Owns persistence and state continuity across time and scenes.

## Audio

Owns audible representation, recording/reference selection, spatialization, synchronization, mixing and approved sound implementation.

## AI Production

Owns the generation technology and implementation pipeline.

AI cannot redefine upstream truth.
