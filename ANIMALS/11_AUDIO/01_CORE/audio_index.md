# 11_AUDIO

## Purpose

The Audio System defines how animal sounds, movement sounds, environmental sound, interaction sound, human-animal sound, spatial acoustics, and cinematic audio representation are researched, approved, generated, implemented, validated, and preserved across the Ramayana production.

## Master Rule

THE ANIMAL SOUNDS LIKE THE ANIMAL — NOT LIKE A MOVIE VERSION OF THE ANIMAL.

## Core Principle

Every audible element must have a biological, behavioral, physical, ecological, interactional, human-contextual, narrative, or explicitly approved cinematic reason for existing.

Audio must never add animal vocalization merely because the audience expects it.

## Authority

Audio represents approved truth from upstream systems.

Audio does not redefine:

- Biology
- Behavior
- Movement
- Ecology
- Domestic/Trained state
- Interaction
- Visual Identity
- Continuity

AI-generated audio is implementation output, not evidence and not source of truth.

## Naturalism Priority

Priority order:

1. Biological plausibility
2. Behavioral appropriateness
3. Physical/acoustic plausibility
4. Ecological soundscape plausibility
5. Historical/canonical appropriateness
6. Continuity
7. Cinematic composition
8. Dramatic emphasis

Cinematic emphasis may never violate higher-priority truth.

## Silence

Silence is a valid and sometimes required audio state.

An animal does not need to vocalize simply because it is visible.
