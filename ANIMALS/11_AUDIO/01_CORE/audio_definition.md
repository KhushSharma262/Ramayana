# AUDIO DEFINITION

Audio is the controlled representation of audible reality and approved cinematic sound.

It includes:

- animal vocalizations
- breathing and physiological sounds where audible
- feeding sounds
- locomotion sounds
- wingbeats
- hoof/paw/claw contact
- body contact with surfaces
- environmental sound
- water
- vegetation
- insects
- distant animals
- human activity
- interaction sounds
- domestic/work sounds
- spatial acoustics
- recording representation
- sound design
- mixing
- synchronization
- AI audio generation
- validation
- continuity

Audio does not own the biological reason an animal makes a sound. It implements approved upstream states.
