# AI ANIMAL SOUND REALISM

AI-generated animal audio must be checked for:

- species authenticity
- vocalization authenticity
- behavioral appropriateness
- anatomical plausibility
- age/sex plausibility
- natural variation
- realistic duration
- realistic rhythm
- realistic intensity
- absence of human-like vocal patterns
- absence of cinematic exaggeration
- absence of repeated synthetic loops

A convincing AI sound is still rejected if it is biologically wrong.
