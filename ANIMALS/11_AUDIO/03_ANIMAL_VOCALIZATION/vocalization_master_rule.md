# VOCALIZATION MASTER RULE

An animal vocalization must be justified by the animal's approved biological and behavioral state.

Do not use generic cinematic substitutes.

Do not assume:

- visible animal = vocalization
- predator = roar/growl
- horse = neigh
- cow = moo
- dog = bark
- bird = constant calling
- monkey = constant chatter
- snake = constant hiss

The correct output may be:

- vocalization
- non-vocal sound
- distant sound
- muffled sound
- partial sound
- intermittent sound
- silence

Natural absence of vocalization is valid.
