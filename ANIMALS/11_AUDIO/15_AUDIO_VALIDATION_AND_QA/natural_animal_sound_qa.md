# NATURAL ANIMAL SOUND QA

Reject if:

- species is wrong
- vocalization is unsupported
- sound occurs without behavioral cause
- sound is stereotyped for cinematic convenience
- animal is unnaturally vocal
- silence should have been used
- sound repeats unnaturally
- age/sex is inconsistent
- intensity is implausible
- spatial position is impossible
- sound contradicts visible action
- AI artifacts alter biological identity
- soundscape contains impossible species or sources
