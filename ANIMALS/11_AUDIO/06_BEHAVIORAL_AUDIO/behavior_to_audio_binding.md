# BEHAVIOR TO AUDIO BINDING

Behavior determines whether an animal should produce an audible signal.

Examples:

CALM REST:
Usually low activity; vocalization only when justified.

ALARM:
May produce species-appropriate alarm signaling when supported.

FEAR:
May produce species-appropriate distress or escape-associated sound; may also remain silent.

AGGRESSION:
Sound depends on species and interaction context.

COURTSHIP:
Species-specific reproductive sounds only when applicable.

PARENT-OFFSPRING:
Contact sounds must match species and life stage.

FEEDING:
Feeding sounds may occur without vocalization.

SLEEP/REST:
Do not insert unnecessary sounds.

No behavior state may be translated into a generic cinematic sound automatically.
