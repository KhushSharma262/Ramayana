# RECORDING AND REFERENCE POLICY

Reference hierarchy:

1. Verified real-world animal recordings
2. High-quality scientific/acoustic recordings
3. Reliable documentary/natural-history recordings
4. Expert-validated archival material
5. Controlled recreated recordings
6. AI-generated reference material

AI-generated audio is never evidence of real animal behavior.

References must identify:

- species
- context
- location where known
- recording conditions where known
- source
- date where available
- uncertainty
