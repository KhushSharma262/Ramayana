# MOVEMENT AUDIO

Movement audio represents physically plausible consequences of movement.

Examples:

- hoof impacts
- paw steps
- claw contact
- wingbeats
- body movement
- fur/feather movement where audible
- branch contact
- ground disturbance
- water displacement
- breathing associated with exertion

Movement audio must correspond to the actual movement state.

Impossible movement cannot be made plausible through sound design.
