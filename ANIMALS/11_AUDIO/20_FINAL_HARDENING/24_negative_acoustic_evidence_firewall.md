# NEGATIVE ACOUSTIC EVIDENCE FIREWALL

UNKNOWN, ABSENT and SILENT are different states.

UNKNOWN:
Evidence is insufficient.

ABSENT:
The source is established as not present.

SILENT:
The source exists but is not currently producing an audible sound.

Never convert:

UNKNOWN → ABSENT
UNKNOWN → SILENT
ABSENT → SILENT

without appropriate evidence/state transition.

Do not infer absence merely because a sound was not recorded.
