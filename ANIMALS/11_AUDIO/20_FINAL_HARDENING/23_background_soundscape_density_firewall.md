# BACKGROUND SOUNDSCAPE DENSITY FIREWALL

Background audio must reflect ecological plausibility.

Control:

- species abundance
- population density
- habitat
- season
- time
- weather
- breeding activity
- human disturbance
- resource availability

A forest is not automatically filled with continuous animal calls.

A village is not automatically filled with random livestock.

A river is not automatically filled with arbitrary water sounds.

Soundscape density must be justified by world state.
