# TEMPORAL AUDIO SYNCHRONIZATION FIREWALL

Audio timing must correspond to:

- visible movement
- vocal onset
- vocal offset
- footfall
- wingbeat
- contact
- environmental event
- interaction event

Reject:

- impossible pre-sounds
- delayed causality without explanation
- footstep mismatch
- wingbeat mismatch
- vocalization from a motionless incompatible source
- audio continuing after the causal event without justification

Temporal alignment must remain auditable.
