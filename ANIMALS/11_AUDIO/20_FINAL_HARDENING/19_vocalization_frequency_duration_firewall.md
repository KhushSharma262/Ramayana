# VOCALIZATION FREQUENCY AND DURATION FIREWALL

A biologically valid sound can still be wrong because it occurs:

- too often
- too continuously
- for too long
- at impossible intervals
- with unnatural repetition
- with identical waveform reuse

Validate:

frequency
duration
interval
rhythm
repetition
context
population density
individual state

Repeated identical calls must not create artificial looping behavior.

Cinematic repetition requires explicit approval and must remain biologically plausible.
