# AUDIO SOURCE IDENTITY FIREWALL

Every animal sound must have a resolved source identity.

Required identity dimensions where applicable:

- species
- taxonomic resolution
- individual or population
- sex
- age/life stage
- physiological state
- behavioral state
- scene presence
- acoustic source position

A sound must never be assigned to an animal merely because the animal is visually nearby.

Broad unresolved categories remain unresolved.

If species identity is insufficient for a species-specific sound decision:
BLOCK.
