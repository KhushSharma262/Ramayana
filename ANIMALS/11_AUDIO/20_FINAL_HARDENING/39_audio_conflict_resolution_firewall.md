# AUDIO CONFLICT RESOLUTION FIREWALL

Audio cannot resolve upstream factual conflicts.

Authority order:

Biology → physical possibility
Behavior → behavioral reason
Movement → physical movement
Ecology → environmental source
Domestic/Trained → human-animal context
Interaction → event context
Continuity → temporal persistence
Audio → audible representation

If two upstream systems conflict:
BLOCK AND RETURN TO THE RESPONSIBLE AUTHORITY.

Audio may never choose the convenient answer.
