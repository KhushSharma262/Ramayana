# CAMERA MOVEMENT

Camera movement must preserve:

- physical plausibility
- subject readability
- continuity
- spatial orientation
- action timing

Camera movement may be:

STATIC
PAN
TILT
DOLLY
TRACK
CRANE
HANDHELD
STEADICAM
GIMBAL
AERIAL
OTHER_APPROVED

Impossible camera movement requires explicit cinematic state and must not be mistaken for natural world behavior.
