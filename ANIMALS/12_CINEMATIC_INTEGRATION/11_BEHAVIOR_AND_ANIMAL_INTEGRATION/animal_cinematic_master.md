# ANIMAL CINEMATIC MASTER

THE ANIMAL REMAINS AN ANIMAL.

Cinematic treatment must preserve:

- natural instincts
- species-specific behavior
- individual variation
- fear responses
- curiosity
- social behavior
- feeding behavior
- rest
- territorial behavior
- predator/prey behavior
- domestic behavior
- stress and recovery

Animals are not miniature human actors.

Anthropomorphic acting is prohibited unless explicitly canonical/supernatural and separately controlled.
