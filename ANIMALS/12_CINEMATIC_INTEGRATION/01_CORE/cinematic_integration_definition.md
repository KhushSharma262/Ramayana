# CINEMATIC INTEGRATION DEFINITION

Cinematic Integration determines how approved world states are combined into a coherent cinematic representation.

It controls integration rather than factual ownership.

It answers:

- What is the shot?
- What must be visible?
- What may remain offscreen?
- What is the camera observing?
- What is the audience meant to understand?
- What happens before and after the shot?
- How do image, movement, environment and sound coexist?
- How is cinematic emphasis achieved without falsifying the world?

It does not invent upstream truth.
