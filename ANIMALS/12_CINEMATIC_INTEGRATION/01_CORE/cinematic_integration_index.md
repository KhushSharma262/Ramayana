# 12_CINEMATIC_INTEGRATION

## Purpose

Cinematic Integration is the layer that converts the approved animal/world state into a coherent cinematic scene, shot, sequence and final audiovisual experience.

It integrates:

- Biology
- Behavior
- Movement
- Ecology
- Domestic/Trained
- Interaction
- Visual Identity
- Continuity
- Audio
- Cinematography
- Lighting
- Composition
- Editing
- Color
- AI Production

## Master Rule

CINEMA SHAPES HOW THE APPROVED WORLD IS EXPERIENCED.
IT DOES NOT CHANGE WHAT THE WORLD IS.

## Authority

Cinematic Integration is an integration and orchestration layer.

It does not become a competing source of truth.

## Priority

1. Canonical/source truth
2. Approved domain truth
3. Continuity
4. Physical and biological plausibility
5. Scene causality
6. Cinematic intention
7. Camera/composition
8. Lighting
9. Audio
10. Editing
11. Color treatment
12. AI implementation

Lower layers cannot override higher layers.
