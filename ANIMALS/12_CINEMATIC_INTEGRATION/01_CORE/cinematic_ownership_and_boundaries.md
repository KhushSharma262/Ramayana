# CINEMATIC OWNERSHIP AND BOUNDARIES

## Biology

Owns physical and physiological truth.

## Behavior

Owns motivation, behavioral state and response.

## Movement

Owns biomechanics and physical movement execution.

## Ecology

Owns habitat, environment, resources, ecological relationships and environmental state.

## Domestic/Trained

Owns domestication, training, handler, work, equipment and human-animal context.

## Interaction

Owns interaction events and relationship consequences.

## Visual Identity

Owns canonical visual representation.

## Continuity

Owns persistence, temporal state and reconciliation.

## Audio

Owns audible implementation.

## Cinematic Integration

Owns:

- shot construction
- cinematic emphasis
- camera relationship to subject
- scene-to-shot integration
- visual/auditory integration
- blocking integration
- editorial integration
- cinematic hierarchy
- shot-level implementation constraints

It cannot redefine upstream truth.

## AI Production

Owns generation implementation only.
