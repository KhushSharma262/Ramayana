# CINEMATIC ASSET LINEAGE

Every cinematic asset must retain:

cinematic_record_id
scene_id
shot_id
domain_snapshot_ids
continuity_snapshot_id
camera_state
lens_state
lighting_state
composition_state
audio_state
prompt_id
model_id
generation_id
asset_id
derived_asset_id
validation_state
approval_state
version

Replacement creates new lineage.
Previous versions remain auditable.
