# CINEMATIC PROMPT COMPILATION

Prompt compilation must be deterministic and traceable.

Prompt layers:

1. Canonical/source constraints
2. Domain truth
3. Continuity
4. Scene requirements
5. Shot requirements
6. Cinematic intention
7. Camera/lens
8. Lighting
9. Composition
10. Movement
11. Audio
12. AI implementation parameters

Lower layers cannot contradict higher layers.
