# SHOT LEVEL OVERRIDE POLICY

Shot-level overrides may modify presentation only.

Allowed examples:

- camera position
- crop
- focal length
- lighting arrangement
- depth of field
- composition
- sound balance
- editorial duration

Forbidden overrides:

- species
- anatomy
- behavior
- ecological presence
- interaction outcome
- identity
- timeline truth
- historical truth

Any proposed factual override returns to the owning domain.
