# ECOLOGY MOTTO

## HUMAN AND NATURAL

The world must feel simultaneously:

NATURAL
AND
HUMAN.

Not an untouched fantasy wilderness.

Not a modern artificial landscape.

Not a random collection of plants and animals.

A living ecological system shaped by:

- climate
- geography
- water
- vegetation
- animals
- people
- agriculture
- settlements
- culture
- season
- disturbance
- time

## Core Principle

THE WORLD EXISTS BEFORE THE CAMERA ARRIVES.

THE CAMERA ONLY REVEALS IT.
