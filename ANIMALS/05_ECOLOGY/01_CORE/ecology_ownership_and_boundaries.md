# ECOLOGY OWNERSHIP AND BOUNDARIES

## BIOLOGY

Owns organism structure and physiological truth.

## BEHAVIOR

Owns what an animal does behaviorally.

## MOVEMENT

Owns how the animal physically moves.

## ECOLOGY

Owns:
- where organisms occur
- why they occur there
- habitat
- resources
- environmental relationships
- population distribution
- environmental change

## DOMESTIC_TRAINED

Owns:
- domestication
- training
- working roles
- human conditioning

## HISTORICAL_RECONSTRUCTION

Owns historical claims about:
- land use
- settlement
- agriculture
- infrastructure
- historical environment

Ecology uses approved historical information but does not invent it.

## AI_PRODUCTION

Only implements approved ecological truth.

AI-generated scenery is not ecological evidence.
