# ECOLOGICAL SCALE

Ecology operates across linked scales.

GLOBAL
→ SOUTH ASIA
→ INDIA
→ REGION
→ LANDSCAPE
→ HABITAT
→ MICROHABITAT
→ SCENE

## Rule

A scene must remain compatible with its larger ecological context.

Example:

India
→ Gangetic Plain
→ floodplain
→ riparian habitat
→ riverbank
→ scene

A scene cannot silently contradict the ecological conditions established at the larger scale.

## Production Rule

Unknown scale relationships remain BLOCKED until resolved.
