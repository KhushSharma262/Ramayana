# ECOLOGY DEFINITION

## Definition

Ecology is the study and production representation of relationships among organisms, environment, resources, climate, water, vegetation, land use and human activity.

For this project, Ecology defines why a particular living environment looks and functions the way it does.

## Ecology Owns

- habitat
- landscape
- vegetation communities
- water systems
- climate
- seasonality
- ecological resources
- species distribution
- abundance logic
- population relationships
- ecological interactions
- human land-use effects
- disturbance
- environmental change

## Ecology Does Not Own

Detailed:
- animal anatomy
- animal behavior
- animal biomechanics
- animation implementation
- camera composition
- AI generation settings

## Principle

The environment is a continuous ecological system, not a collection of background objects.
