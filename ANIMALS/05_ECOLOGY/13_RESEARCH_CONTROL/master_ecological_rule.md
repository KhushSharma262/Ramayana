# MASTER ECOLOGICAL RULE

EVERY ECOLOGICAL ELEMENT VISIBLE IN A SCENE MUST HAVE A REASON FOR BEING THERE.

## Required Reason Types

At least one applicable reason must be established:

- geography
- climate
- water
- soil
- habitat
- vegetation
- resource
- season
- species ecology
- population ecology
- ecological interaction
- human land use
- historical evidence
- canonical evidence
- explicitly approved production composition

## But

CINEMATICALLY_COMPOSED is NOT evidence of natural frequency.

SUPERNATURAL is NOT ordinary ecology.

MODERN is NOT automatically HISTORICAL.

UNKNOWN is NOT ABSENT.

ABSENT requires justification.

## Master Causal Audit

CLIMATE
→ WATER
→ SOIL / GEOLOGY
→ VEGETATION
→ RESOURCES
→ SPECIES
→ POPULATION
→ HUMAN ACTIVITY
→ LOCAL ECOLOGICAL STATE
→ SCENE

This is an audit framework, not an absolute one-way ecological law.

## Final Rule

If the ecological reason cannot be established or explicitly marked as an approved uncertainty:

BLOCKED.

Visual richness is never sufficient justification.
