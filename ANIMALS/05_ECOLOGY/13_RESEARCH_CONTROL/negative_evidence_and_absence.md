# ECOLOGICAL NEGATIVE EVIDENCE

Ecological records must represent both presence and absence-related evidence.

## Evidence States

PRESENT
ABSENT
UNKNOWN
CONDITIONALLY_PRESENT
HISTORICALLY_PRESENT
SEASONALLY_PRESENT

## Important

ABSENT is a claim.

UNKNOWN is lack of sufficient evidence.

They are not interchangeable.

## Negative Evidence May Include

- geographic range exclusion
- habitat incompatibility
- historical absence evidence
- ecological exclusion
- documented survey absence
- temporal mismatch
- environmental limitation

## Rule

A species should not be marked ABSENT merely because evidence was not found.
