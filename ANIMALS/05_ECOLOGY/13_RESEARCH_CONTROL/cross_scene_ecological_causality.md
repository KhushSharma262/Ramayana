# CROSS-SCENE ECOLOGICAL CAUSALITY

## Purpose

Ecological events can create consequences that persist into later scenes.

## Example

FIRE
→ vegetation loss
→ animal displacement
→ smoke
→ altered human activity
→ later recovery

FLOOD
→ water rise
→ habitat change
→ animal redistribution
→ damaged agriculture
→ later drying

DROUGHT
→ water reduction
→ vegetation stress
→ resource competition
→ altered animal distribution

## Rule

A consequential ecological event must be represented in later continuity until resolved or naturally decayed.
