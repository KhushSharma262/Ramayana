# ECOLOGICAL VERSION HISTORY AND ROLLBACK

## Rule

Approved ecological records are versioned.

Do not silently overwrite approved data.

## Version

v1.0
v1.1
v1.2
etc.

## Change Record

## Rollback

Rollback means selecting an earlier approved version.

It does not mean deleting history.

## Production

Production references an explicit version/snapshot.

## Hard Rule

No invisible ecological mutation.
