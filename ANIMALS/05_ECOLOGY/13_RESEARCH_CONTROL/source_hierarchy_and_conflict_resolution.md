# ECOLOGICAL SOURCE HIERARCHY AND CONFLICT RESOLUTION

## Purpose

Prevent contradictory evidence from silently producing contradictory approved claims.

## Source Classes

TIER_1:
Primary scientific evidence
Authoritative government / institutional data
Original archaeological / historical evidence
Primary canonical text

TIER_2:
Peer-reviewed synthesis
Authoritative specialist references
Major scientific databases

TIER_3:
Reputable secondary synthesis

TIER_4:
General secondary material

TIER_5:
Unverified / unclear provenance

## Important

Higher tier does not automatically win.

Applicability, date, geographic scope and methodological quality must also be evaluated.

## Conflict Evaluation

When sources conflict, evaluate:

1. source quality
2. source independence
3. geographic applicability
4. temporal applicability
5. methodological quality
6. directness
7. sample / evidence quality
8. relevance to the exact claim
9. publication/retrieval date
10. known limitations

## Resolution States

RESOLVED
PROVISIONALLY_RESOLVED
UNRESOLVED
CONTESTED
BLOCKED

## Hard Rule

Two conflicting claims cannot both be marked APPROVED for the same scope unless they represent genuinely different conditions.
