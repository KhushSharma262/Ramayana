# ECOLOGICAL ABUNDANCE VS SCENE DENSITY

These are separate concepts.

## ECOLOGICAL ABUNDANCE

How common a population is within its applicable ecological area.

Possible states:

VERY_RARE
RARE
UNCOMMON
OCCASIONAL
COMMON
ABUNDANT
LOCALLY_CONCENTRATED

## SCENE DENSITY

How many individuals are actually visible or implied in a specific scene.

Possible states:

NONE_VISIBLE
SINGLE
FEW
SMALL_GROUP
LARGE_GROUP
HIGH_CONCENTRATION

## Rule

ABUNDANT does NOT mean:

"many animals must be visible in every shot."

## Scene Density Depends On

- habitat patch
- available resources
- season
- time
- water
- disturbance
- social structure
- camera framing
- visibility
- local concentration

## Cinematic Composition

Camera selection may reveal a concentration without changing the underlying population abundance.

## Hard Rule

Scene density cannot be used as evidence of ecological abundance.
