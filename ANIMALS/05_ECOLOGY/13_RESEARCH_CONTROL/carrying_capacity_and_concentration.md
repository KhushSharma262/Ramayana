# ECOLOGICAL CARRYING CAPACITY AND CONCENTRATION

## Purpose

Prevent unrealistic concentrations of organisms and human activity.

## Relevant Variables

- habitat area
- food availability
- water
- shelter
- breeding sites
- season
- predation
- competition
- disease
- human disturbance
- social structure

## Rule

Population concentration must remain compatible with available resources.

## Local Concentration

High local concentration is allowed when supported by:

- water source
- food patch
- breeding site
- shelter
- migration stop
- seasonal resource
- social behavior
- human feeding/management where applicable

## Hard Failure

Do not place unusually high densities of unrelated species together merely because the scene looks rich.

## Important

Carrying capacity is not necessarily an exact numerical population model.

It is a plausibility constraint.
