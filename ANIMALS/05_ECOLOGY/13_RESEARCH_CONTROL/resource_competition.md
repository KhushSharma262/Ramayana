# ECOLOGICAL RESOURCE COMPETITION

## Purpose

Prevent unlimited simultaneous resource assumptions.

## Resources

- water
- food
- shelter
- nesting sites
- breeding sites
- grazing
- fruit
- seeds
- prey
- carrion
- cultivated resources

## Required Relationship

SPECIES
→ RESOURCE
→ DEMAND
→ AVAILABILITY
→ COMPETITION
→ DISTRIBUTION

## Rule

If multiple species depend on a limiting resource, their coexistence must remain ecologically plausible.

## Possible States

NO_LIMITATION
LOW_LIMITATION
MODERATE_LIMITATION
HIGH_LIMITATION
SEASONALLY_LIMITED
LOCALLY_CONCENTRATED

## Hard Rule

Do not grant every species unlimited access to the same limited resource merely to populate a scene.
