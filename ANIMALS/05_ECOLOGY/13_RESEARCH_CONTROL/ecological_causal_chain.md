## Important

This is not a mandatory one-directional biological law.

It is a production audit chain.

Any scene element should be traceable to one or more relevant upstream causes.

## Example

If a scene contains many deer:

Ask:

1. Is the species geographically plausible?
2. Is the habitat appropriate?
3. Is food available?
4. Is water available?
5. Is the season appropriate?
6. Is the population state compatible?
7. Is human disturbance compatible?
8. Is the local concentration plausible?

If the chain cannot be justified:

BLOCKED.

## Rule

Visual convenience is not an ecological cause.
