# ECOLOGICAL GEOGRAPHIC PRECISION

## Principle

Use the smallest geographic scope necessary to support the claim.

## Example

INDIA
is too broad when evidence only supports:

NORTHERN INDIA

Likewise:

NORTHERN INDIA
may be too broad when evidence supports:

GANGETIC FLOODPLAIN

and:

GANGETIC FLOODPLAIN
may be too broad when evidence supports:

SPECIFIC RIPARIAN HABITAT

Claim precision must not exceed evidence precision.
