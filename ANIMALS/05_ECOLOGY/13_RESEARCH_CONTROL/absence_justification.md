# ABSENCE JUSTIFICATION STANDARD

An ABSENT claim must identify why the organism or ecological feature is absent.

## Valid Bases

GEOGRAPHIC_EXCLUSION
HABITAT_EXCLUSION
CLIMATE_EXCLUSION
TEMPORAL_EXCLUSION
RESOURCE_EXCLUSION
HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE
SURVEY_EVIDENCE
DOCUMENTED_LOCAL_ABSENCE
ECOLOGICAL_INCOMPATIBILITY

## Required

absence_status:
absence_scope:
absence_period:
absence_reason:
evidence:
source:
confidence:
approval_state:

## Invalid

"No evidence found."

That means UNKNOWN unless stronger evidence exists.

## Rule

ABSENT requires justification.
UNKNOWN requires no invented justification.
