# ECOLOGICAL SOURCE INDEPENDENCE

## Purpose

Repeated claims are not automatically independent evidence.

## Source Relationship

Every source must be classified as:

PRIMARY
SECONDARY
TERTIARY
DERIVED
REPEAT_OF_EXISTING_SOURCE
UNKNOWN

## Independence

Two sources count as independent only when their evidence does not simply derive from the same original claim.

## Example

Source A:
original field study.

Source B:
textbook summarizing Source A.

These are NOT independent confirmations.

## Rule

Multiple copies of the same unsupported claim do not increase confidence.

## Hard Rule

Evidence strength cannot be inflated by source duplication.
