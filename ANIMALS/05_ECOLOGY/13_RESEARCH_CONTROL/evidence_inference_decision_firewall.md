## Three Primary Layers

EVIDENCE
→ INFERENCE
→ PRODUCTION DECISION

## EVIDENCE

What the source actually supports.

## INFERENCE

Reasoned interpretation derived from evidence.

## PRODUCTION DECISION

What the production system chooses to represent.

## Rule

Each layer must retain its identity permanently.
