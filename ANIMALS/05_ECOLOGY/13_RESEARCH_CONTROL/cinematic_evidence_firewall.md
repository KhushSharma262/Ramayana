# CINEMATIC ECOLOGY EVIDENCE FIREWALL

## Purpose

Prevent cinematic composition from becoming ecological evidence.

## Three States

NATURAL
CINEMATICALLY_COMPOSED
SUPERNATURAL

## NATURAL

Represents an ecological arrangement supported by evidence.

## CINEMATICALLY_COMPOSED

A production arrangement selected for:

- framing
- storytelling
- visibility
- visual clarity
- dramatic composition

It may be plausible without being representative of normal ecological frequency.

## SUPERNATURAL

Requires separate canonical/supernatural authorization.

## Hard Rule

A cinematic arrangement must NEVER be copied backward into:

- species distribution
- abundance
- habitat preference
- population density
- ecological interaction
- historical evidence

## Required Label

scene_ecology_status:

NATURAL
CINEMATICALLY_COMPOSED
SUPERNATURAL

## Evidence Rule

PRODUCTION OUTPUT ≠ ECOLOGICAL EVIDENCE.
