# UNKNOWN IS NOT ABSENT

## UNKNOWN

Means:

"There is insufficient evidence to determine the state."

## ABSENT

Means:

"There is sufficient evidence or ecological reasoning to establish non-occurrence within the declared scope."

## Example

If no source confirms that a species occurs in a specific habitat:

INCORRECT:
ABSENT

CORRECT:
UNKNOWN

If authoritative evidence establishes that the species' range excludes the area:

ABSENT

## Production

UNKNOWN may remain BLOCKED when the scene requires certainty.

Do not convert UNKNOWN into PRESENT merely to populate a scene.
Do not convert UNKNOWN into ABSENT merely to simplify the database.
