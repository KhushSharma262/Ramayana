# HISTORICAL HUMAN LANDSCAPE CONTROL

## Purpose

Human ecological features must be historically applicable.

## Controlled Features

- settlements
- roads
- trails
- agriculture
- orchards
- irrigation
- wells
- ponds
- grazing
- livestock
- forestry
- resource extraction
- hunting
- fishing
- managed vegetation
- fire
- waste systems

## Required Historical Fields

feature:
historical_period:
geographic_scope:
evidence:
source:
source_reference:
confidence:
uncertainty:
approval_state:

## Hard Rule

Modern existence does not establish ancient existence.

## Example

A modern road cannot become an ancient road because the road is visually convenient.

## Production

Historical human landscape must be:

EVIDENCED
or
EXPLICITLY MARKED AS RECONSTRUCTION

Unsupported historical features are BLOCKED.
