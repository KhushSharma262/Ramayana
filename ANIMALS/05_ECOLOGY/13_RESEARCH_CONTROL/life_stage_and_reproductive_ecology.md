# ECOLOGICAL LIFE-STAGE AND REPRODUCTIVE STATE

## Purpose

Species-level presence does not automatically establish every life stage or demographic state.

## Optional Dimensions

life_stage:
sex:
reproductive_state:
breeding_state:
parental_state:
juvenile_state:
nesting_state:

## Rule

Use these dimensions only where ecologically relevant.

## Examples

Species present
does not mean:

juveniles present
breeding adults present
nesting active
pregnant individuals present

unless supported by:
- season
- population ecology
- reproductive cycle
- habitat
- evidence

## Hard Rule

Do not infer demographic composition merely from species presence.
