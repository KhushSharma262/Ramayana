# CANONICAL LITERARY ECOLOGY INTERPRETATION

## Purpose

Prevent literary language from being silently converted into ecological measurements.

## Required

textual_statement:
source:
passage:
literal_meaning:
literary_function:
ecological_implication:
interpretation_type:
confidence:

## Example

"Forest filled with animals"

does not automatically establish:

- exact population density
- exact species count
- simultaneous presence of every possible animal

## Possible Interpretation Types

DIRECT_ECOLOGICAL_STATEMENT
LITERARY_DESCRIPTION
POETIC_EXAGGERATION
SYMBOLIC_DESCRIPTION
THEOLOGICAL_DESCRIPTION
AMBIGUOUS

## Rule

Canonical richness of description is not automatically quantitative abundance evidence.
