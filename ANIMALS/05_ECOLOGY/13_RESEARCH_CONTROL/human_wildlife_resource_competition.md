# HUMAN WILDLIFE RESOURCE COMPETITION

## Purpose

Human ecology and wildlife ecology share physical resources.

Where applicable, account for:

- water
- grazing land
- crops
- forest resources
- fishing areas
- settlement expansion
- livestock
- hunting
- protected areas
- disturbance

## Rule

Human activity can:

INCREASE
DECREASE
REDIRECT
FRAGMENT
CONCENTRATE
or
REMOVE

ecological resources.

## Example

A village near water may attract:

humans
+
livestock
+
some commensal wildlife

while simultaneously reducing habitat suitability for sensitive species.

## Hard Rule

Human presence and wildlife presence must be jointly plausible where they compete for the same resources.
