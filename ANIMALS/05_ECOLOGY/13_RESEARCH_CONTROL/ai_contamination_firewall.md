# ECOLOGICAL AI CONTAMINATION FIREWALL

## Authority

ECOLOGICAL TRUTH
→ DIRECTING
→ AI_PRODUCTION

## AI Output

AI-generated:
- image
- video
- background
- vegetation arrangement
- animal placement
- weather
- water appearance

is production output.

It is NOT ecological evidence.

## Forbidden Feedback

AI_OUTPUT
→ ECOLOGICAL_DATABASE

unless independently researched and re-established through approved evidence.

## Rule

An AI-generated image cannot prove that:

- a plant belongs in a habitat
- an animal occurs in a region
- a population has a given density
- a landscape looked a certain way historically
- an ecological relationship exists

## Production Gate

AI production receives approved ecological constraints.

It cannot redefine them.
