# ECOLOGY RESEARCH AND EVIDENCE

Required chain:

SOURCE
→ EVIDENCE
→ INTERPRETATION
→ CLAIM
→ DECISION
→ APPROVAL
→ VERSION
→ SNAPSHOT

## Evidence Priority

Prefer:

1. authoritative Indian ecological sources
2. peer-reviewed research
3. authoritative South Asian sources
4. Asian ecological literature
5. global scientific literature
6. reputable secondary sources

AI-generated images/videos/prompts are never ecological evidence.

## Geographic Applicability

Every claim must specify where it applies.

## Temporal Applicability

Every claim must specify whether it applies to:

MODERN
HISTORICAL
CANONICAL
INFERRED
PRODUCTION

where relevant.
