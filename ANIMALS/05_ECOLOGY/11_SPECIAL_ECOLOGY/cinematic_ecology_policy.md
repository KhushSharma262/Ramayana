# CINEMATIC ECOLOGY POLICY

Three states:

NATURAL
CINEMATICALLY_COMPOSED
SUPERNATURAL

Cinematic composition may determine what is shown in a shot.

It may not falsely redefine ecological relationships.

Example:

A shot may deliberately compose:
- water
- predator
- prey
- vegetation

for visual clarity.

That does not establish that those organisms naturally occupy that exact spatial arrangement continuously.

Cinematic selection is a production decision, not ecological evidence.
