# ECOLOGY / MOVEMENT BOUNDARY

ECOLOGY OWNS:

- terrain
- soil
- vegetation
- water
- weather
- environmental conditions
- habitat

MOVEMENT OWNS:

- hoof contact
- traction
- sinking
- slipping
- swimming mechanics
- climbing mechanics
- physical obstacle negotiation
- physical response to terrain

Example:

ECOLOGY:
mud exists.

MOVEMENT:
hoof sinks and traction decreases.

Neither system should duplicate the other's authority.
