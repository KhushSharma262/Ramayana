# NATURAL VARIATION AND IMPERFECTION

Natural environments are not perfectly arranged.

Include plausible variation in:

- plant spacing
- vegetation density
- soil texture
- water edges
- animal distribution
- damaged vegetation
- fallen branches
- tracks
- scat
- nests
- burrows
- seasonal dryness
- human disturbance

Avoid:
- repeated assets
- identical spacing
- perfectly distributed animals
- artificial symmetry
- uniform vegetation
- synchronized ecological events
