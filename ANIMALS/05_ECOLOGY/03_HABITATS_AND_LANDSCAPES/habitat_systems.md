# HABITAT SYSTEMS

## Purpose

Define habitats as functioning ecological systems.

Examples may include:

- tropical deciduous forest
- dry forest
- moist forest
- grassland
- scrub
- riverine habitat
- floodplain
- wetland
- agricultural landscape
- pastoral landscape
- settlement edge
- mountain habitat
- coastal habitat

## Rule

Habitat is not equivalent to scenery.

A habitat must explain:
- vegetation
- water
- animals
- resources
- climate
- seasonal behavior
- human influence where present
