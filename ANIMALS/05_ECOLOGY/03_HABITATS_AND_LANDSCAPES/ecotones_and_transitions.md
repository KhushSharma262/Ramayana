# ECOTONES AND TRANSITIONS

Natural environments transition gradually.

Examples:

FOREST
→ FOREST EDGE
→ SCRUB
→ GRASSLAND

RIVER
→ RIPARIAN VEGETATION
→ FLOODPLAIN
→ AGRICULTURAL LAND
→ SETTLEMENT

Do not use arbitrary hard boundaries unless geography or human infrastructure supports them.

Ecological transitions should influence:
- vegetation density
- species occurrence
- water
- soil
- human activity
- animal density
