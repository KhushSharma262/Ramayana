# RIVER FLOODPLAIN AND WETLAND SYSTEMS

Model relationships among:

RIVER
→ BANK
→ RIPARIAN VEGETATION
→ FLOODPLAIN
→ WETLAND
→ AGRICULTURE / SETTLEMENT

Include:
- seasonal water level
- flooding
- sediment
- erosion
- deposition
- aquatic life
- terrestrial wildlife
- human use

Do not treat a river as a decorative blue strip.
