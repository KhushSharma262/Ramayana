# POPULATION DENSITY AND ABUNDANCE

Exact population counts are not always required.

The system instead establishes plausible relative abundance:

VERY_RARE
RARE
OCCASIONAL
COMMON
ABUNDANT
LOCALLY_CONCENTRATED

Abundance must have ecological reasoning.

Examples:

HIGH_RESOURCE
→ higher plausible density

LOW_WATER
→ concentration near remaining water

HIGH_DISTURBANCE
→ sensitive species decline

HUMAN_SETTLEMENT
→ some species increase
→ others decrease
