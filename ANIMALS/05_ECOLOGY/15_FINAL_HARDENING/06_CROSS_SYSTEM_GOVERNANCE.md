# CROSS-SYSTEM ECOLOGY GOVERNANCE

## PURPOSE

Prevent Ecology, Biology, Behavior, Movement, Geography, Climate, Human Systems, Directing, and AI Production from silently overwriting one another.

## OWNERSHIP

BIOLOGY OWNS:
- anatomy
- morphology
- physiological limits
- biological structure

BEHAVIOR OWNS:
- behavioral states
- behavioral triggers
- social behavior
- behavioral intent/patterns

MOVEMENT OWNS:
- locomotion
- biomechanics
- physical movement execution
- physical interaction with terrain

ECOLOGY OWNS:
- habitat
- landscape
- ecological communities
- water systems
- vegetation communities
- climate/ecological seasonality
- species distribution
- population ecology
- resources
- ecological interactions
- human ecology
- ecological disturbance

GEOGRAPHY OWNS:
- geographic identity
- geographic reference
- spatial representation

HUMAN SYSTEMS OWN:
- human activity
- settlement systems
- social/economic human use

DIRECTING OWNS:
- shot selection
- framing
- composition
- dramatic emphasis

AI PRODUCTION OWNS:
- generation implementation
- asset generation
- technical rendering

## CROSS-SYSTEM RULE

One system may request a change to another system.

It may not silently overwrite another system's approved truth.

## CONFLICT STATES

- COMPATIBLE
- CONDITIONALLY_COMPATIBLE
- CONFLICTED
- UNKNOWN
- BLOCKED

## CHANGE PROPAGATION

When an upstream approved fact changes:

IDENTIFY DEPENDENTS
→ INVALIDATE AFFECTED CLAIMS
→ IDENTIFY AFFECTED SCENES
→ IDENTIFY AFFECTED ASSETS
→ IDENTIFY AFFECTED PROMPTS
→ REVALIDATE
→ RESNAPSHOT

## OWNERSHIP CONFLICT

If two systems claim ownership of the same field:

FIELD_OWNERSHIP_REVIEW_REQUIRED

No silent resolution.

## ECOLOGY SNAPSHOT

Production uses an immutable ecology snapshot.

Live research changes do not automatically alter production.

## ROLLBACK

Every approved production state must be recoverable to the previous approved snapshot.
