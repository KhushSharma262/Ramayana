# SPATIAL TEMPORAL DEMOGRAPHIC ECOLOGICAL MODEL

## PURPOSE

Prevent ecological facts from leaking between place, time, habitat, season, population, sex, age, or reproductive state.

## REQUIRED SPATIAL SCALE

GLOBAL
→ SOUTH_ASIA
→ INDIA
→ REGION
→ LANDSCAPE
→ HABITAT
→ MICROHABITAT
→ SCENE
→ SHOT

A claim cannot automatically propagate downward.

Regional presence does not prove scene presence.

## SPATIAL CONNECTIVITY

Where relevant, record:

- habitat corridor
- river corridor
- forest corridor
- seasonal movement route
- fragmented habitat
- barrier
- human obstruction
- migration route
- dispersal route

Connectivity must be biologically plausible.

## TEMPORAL DIMENSIONS

Every production-relevant ecological state may require:

- era
- date range
- season
- time of day
- weather
- recent weather
- water state
- vegetation state
- disturbance state
- resource state

## TEMPORAL DURATION

Temporary ecological states require:

start:
end:
duration:
recovery_state:

Examples:

flood
fire
drought
storm
land clearing
harvest
grazing pulse

An event cannot persist indefinitely unless evidence supports persistence.

## DEMOGRAPHY

Where relevant:

- sex
- age class
- juvenile
- subadult
- adult
- elderly
- reproductive state
- pregnancy
- lactation
- breeding condition
- family group
- solitary
- social group

## DEMOGRAPHIC CONSISTENCY

Population composition must not be arbitrary.

Example:

breeding season + juveniles + adults + resource availability

must form a plausible ecological combination.

## SEASON × TIME × WEATHER

These are separate dimensions.

Do not assume:

season = weather
time_of_day = season
rainy season = continuously raining

## HISTORICAL CONTROL

Modern environmental conditions cannot automatically populate historical/canonical scenes.

## PRODUCTION RULE

If spatial, temporal, or demographic conditions conflict:

CONFLICTED → REVIEW_REQUIRED

If unresolved:

BLOCKED.
