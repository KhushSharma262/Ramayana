# CANONICAL HISTORICAL AND SUPERNATURAL ECOLOGY CONTROL

## PURPOSE

Prevent canonical literature, historical reconstruction, scientific evidence, and supernatural material from being conflated.

## RULE

"Forest filled with animals"

does not automatically become:

"high animal population density."

## HISTORICAL RECONSTRUCTION

Historical ecology requires its own evidence.

Modern ecology cannot silently become ancient ecology.

## PALEOECOLOGY

When evidence is indirect:

evidence_precision must constrain claim_precision.

## SUPERNATURAL

Supernatural ecological effects must remain explicitly marked.

Allowed:

ORDINARY_ECOLOGY
CANONICAL_EXCEPTION
SUPERNATURAL_ECOLOGY
PRODUCTION_EXCEPTION

## FIREWALL

SUPERNATURAL_EVENT
must NOT silently alter
ORDINARY_ECOLOGICAL_DATABASE.

Any persistent ordinary ecological consequence requires separate justification.

## CANONICAL → ECOLOGY BRIDGE

Required chain:

CANONICAL_PASSAGE
→ TEXTUAL_MEANING
→ ECOLOGICAL_INTERPRETATION
→ CONFIDENCE
→ ECOLOGICAL_CLAIM
→ APPROVAL

No direct:

CANONICAL_TEXT → FACTUAL_ECOLOGICAL_MEASUREMENT

## TRADITION CONFLICT

If Valmiki Ramayana, Ramcharitmanas, regional traditions, Puranic material, or other sources differ:

PRESERVE EACH TRADITION

Do not merge contradictory traditions into one silent "fact."

## PRODUCTION RULE

The selected tradition/version must be explicitly recorded for each canonical production decision.
