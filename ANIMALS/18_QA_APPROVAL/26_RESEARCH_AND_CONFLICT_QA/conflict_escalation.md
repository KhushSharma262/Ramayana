# CONFLICT ESCALATION

If conflicting evidence is discovered:

1. record the conflict
2. preserve both sources
3. identify affected claims
4. identify affected snapshots
5. determine whether production output is affected
6. block affected scope if material
7. escalate to appropriate authority
8. record approved resolution
9. invalidate affected downstream approvals
10. rerun QA

QA does not arbitrarily choose a preferred source.
