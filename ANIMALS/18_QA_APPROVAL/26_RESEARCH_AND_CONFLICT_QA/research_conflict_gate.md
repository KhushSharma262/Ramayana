# RESEARCH / CONFLICT QA

QA must respect the central uncertainty states:

UNKNOWN
EVIDENCE_GAP
CONFLICT
AMBIGUITY
UNCERTAINTY
VARIABILITY
BLOCKED
RESOLVED
SUPERSEDED

QA cannot convert:
UNKNOWN -> ABSENT
CONFLICT -> RESOLVED
AMBIGUITY -> CERTAINTY
VARIABILITY -> ERROR

Where unresolved uncertainty materially affects the output,
approval is BLOCKED.
