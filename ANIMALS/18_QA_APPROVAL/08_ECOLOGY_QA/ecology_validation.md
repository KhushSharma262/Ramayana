# ECOLOGY QA

Verify:
- habitat
- terrain
- vegetation
- water
- climate
- resources
- seasonality
- abundance
- species-habitat compatibility
- human modification
- ecological interactions
- ecological causal justification

Every visible ecological element must have a valid reason for being present.

No arbitrary vegetation, animal population, water body, terrain, weather,
resource, or ecological interaction may be inserted solely for visual attractiveness.

Visual attractiveness is never sufficient ecological justification.

QA verifies ecological validity against the approved Ecology snapshot.
QA does not invent ecological truth during review.
