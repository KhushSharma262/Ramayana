# FINAL ZERO-LOOPHOLE AUDIT

The QA system must prevent:
- unauthorized truth creation
- approval without evidence
- approval from stale snapshots
- partial package approval
- identity drift
- continuity drift
- AI source-of-truth contamination
- cinematic override
- silent conflict resolution
- rejection reuse
- lineage loss
- cross-system contradiction
- self-validation
- scope leakage

No known architecture-level loophole may remain before freeze.
