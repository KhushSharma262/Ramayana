# FINAL OUTPUT GATE

Final output may be approved only when:

1. required upstream snapshots are approved
2. production handoff is valid
3. scene is valid
4. shot is valid
5. asset is valid
6. cross-system QA passes
7. continuity passes
8. provenance passes
9. no critical or major unresolved defect remains
10. all mandatory QA records exist
11. approval scope is explicit
12. final lineage is complete

No shortcut bypasses the gate.
