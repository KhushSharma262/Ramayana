# DEFECT CLASSIFICATION

CRITICAL:
Makes final approval impossible.

MAJOR:
Materially violates approved world-state or production requirement.

MINOR:
Non-blocking defect that does not alter approved truth.

UNKNOWN:
Observed condition cannot yet be classified.

CONFLICT:
Two credible approved inputs disagree.

EVIDENCE_GAP:
Required evidence is missing.

STALE:
Input is no longer current.

PROVENANCE_FAILURE:
Lineage cannot be established.

Classification must not hide uncertainty.
