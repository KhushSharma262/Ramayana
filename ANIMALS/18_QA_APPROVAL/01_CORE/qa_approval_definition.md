# QA APPROVAL DEFINITION

QA Approval is the controlled determination that a production output
satisfies all applicable approved requirements.

QA is an independent verification and acceptance layer.

QA evaluates:
1. identity
2. source/snapshot validity
3. authority boundaries
4. biological plausibility
5. behavioral plausibility
6. movement plausibility
7. ecological plausibility
8. domestic/training correctness
9. interaction correctness
10. visual identity
11. continuity
12. audio
13. stress/battle requirements
14. costume/equipment
15. cinematic compliance
16. AI production compliance
17. scene/shot requirements
18. asset integrity
19. cross-system consistency
20. provenance and lineage

A missing mandatory requirement is not assumed satisfied.

Unknown, conflict, ambiguity, evidence gap, stale state,
invalid snapshot, or missing provenance may cause BLOCKED
or REJECTED status depending on impact.
