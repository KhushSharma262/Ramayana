# REAPPEARANCE POLICY

After death, reappearance is BLOCKED unless supported by an approved
canonical, supernatural, mistaken-identity, or continuity mechanism.

A visually similar AI asset is insufficient.

Reappearance must specify:

reappearance_id
original_entity_id
mechanism
evidence
new_or_returned_identity
timeline
approval
snapshot

Without this record, the entity remains closed.
