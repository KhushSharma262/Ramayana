# INDIVIDUALIZATION THRESHOLD

A background animal becomes an individual continuity entity when it is
persistent, identifiable, narratively relevant, interactionally relevant,
or otherwise required to maintain continuity.

Once individualized:

- it receives an immutable entity_id
- its state becomes persistent
- visual identity may receive a visual_identity_id
- interactions receive interaction references
- replacement rules apply

Individualization cannot be used merely to avoid population-level logic.
