# CONTINUITY COMPLETENESS FIREWALL

For every production-relevant persistent entity, continuity must resolve:

IDENTITY
TIMELINE
BRANCH
LIFE STATE
TEMPORAL POSITION
REQUIRED DOMAIN STATES
ACTIVE RELATIONSHIPS
ACTIVE INTERACTIONS
EQUIPMENT STATE
TRAINING STATE WHEN APPLICABLE
VISUAL STATE
DEPENDENCY STATUS

Fields may be:

KNOWN
UNKNOWN
NOT_APPLICABLE

They may NOT be silently omitted when required.

UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN.

Required unresolved state = BLOCKED.
