# AI PRODUCTION HANDOFF

AI Production receives:

entity_id
continuity_snapshot_id
scene_id
event_context
visual_identity_snapshot_id
required domain states
prompt_id
production constraints

AI Production may generate representation.

AI Production may NOT:

- create canonical truth
- change identity
- change timeline
- change training
- change relationships
- change biology
- change ecology
- change interaction history
- change visual identity

Asset disagreement = asset rejection.
