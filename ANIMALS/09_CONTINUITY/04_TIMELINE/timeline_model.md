# TIMELINE MODEL

Continuity separates:

CHRONOLOGICAL TIME
from
NARRATIVE PRESENTATION ORDER.

Every temporal context requires:

timeline_id
temporal_context
absolute_or_relative_position
date_or_period_when_known
time_precision
preceding_position
following_position
confidence
approval

Narrative order may differ from chronological order.

Flashbacks do not become present state.

Dreams and visions do not automatically mutate physical continuity.

Uncertain historical/canonical time remains explicitly uncertain.
