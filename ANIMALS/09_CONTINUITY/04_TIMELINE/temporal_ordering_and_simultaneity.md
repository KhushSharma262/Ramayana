# TEMPORAL ORDERING AND SIMULTANEITY

Events must be classified as:

BEFORE
AFTER
SIMULTANEOUS
OVERLAPPING
UNKNOWN_ORDER

Artificial ordering is forbidden when evidence establishes genuine
simultaneity or when ordering cannot be established.

If Event B depends causally on Event A, B cannot occur before A.

If two events are simultaneous, they share the appropriate temporal
position and are not arbitrarily serialized merely for convenience.

Temporal ambiguity propagates unless independently resolved.
