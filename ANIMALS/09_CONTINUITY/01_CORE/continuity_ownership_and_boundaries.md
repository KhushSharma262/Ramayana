# CONTINUITY OWNERSHIP AND BOUNDARIES

## Continuity owns

- persistence
- temporal ordering
- snapshot composition
- scene state inheritance
- event/state transition tracking
- cross-system reconciliation
- identity persistence
- replacement tracking
- rollback
- branch/version tracking
- contradiction detection
- continuity audit
- production continuity gating

## Continuity does not own

BIOLOGY:
taxonomy, anatomy, morphology, physiology, health, biological limits

BEHAVIOR:
motivation, behavioral state, emotional/behavioral response, communication

MOVEMENT:
physical execution, biomechanics, gait, balance, load mechanics

ECOLOGY:
habitat, resources, population ecology, environmental constraints

DOMESTIC/TRAINED:
domestication, training, work role, handler, equipment assignment,
husbandry, historical animal use

INTERACTION:
interaction identity, participants, event type, outcome, interaction
relationship consequences

VISUAL IDENTITY:
canonical appearance, morphology as visually represented, appearance
states, forms, markings, visual continuity

AI PRODUCTION:
generation, rendering, asset construction

Continuity references these authorities. It never silently replaces them.
