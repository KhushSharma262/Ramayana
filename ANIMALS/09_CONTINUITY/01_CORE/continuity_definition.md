# CONTINUITY DEFINITION

Continuity is the controlled persistence of approved entity and world
states across scenes, timelines, events, assets, and production outputs.

Continuity answers:

- What existed before this scene?
- What state did it have?
- What changed?
- Why did it change?
- When did it change?
- Which event caused the change?
- Which authoritative domain approved the changed state?
- Which snapshot contains the approved state?
- Which scene consumed that snapshot?
- Which asset represented it?
- Does the final shot remain consistent?

Continuity does not invent missing information.

Unknown remains UNKNOWN.

Off-screen does not mean nonexistent.

Absence from a scene does not erase state.

A regenerated asset does not create a new state.

A visual inconsistency does not justify changing canonical state.
