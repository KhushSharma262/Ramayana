# CONTINUITY INDEX

Continuity is the persistence, temporal ordering, state inheritance,
transition, reconciliation, and audit layer of the ANIMALS system.

Continuity does NOT own biological, behavioral, movement, ecological,
domestic/training, interaction, or visual truth.

It preserves approved states from those authoritative systems and
determines how those states persist across time and scenes.

MASTER RULE:

NOTHING CHANGES IN CONTINUITY WITHOUT A TRACEABLE CAUSE,
AND CONTINUITY NEVER OVERRIDES THE SYSTEM THAT OWNS THE TRUTH.
