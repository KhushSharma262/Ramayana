# OFF-SCREEN EXISTENCE FIREWALL

OFF-SCREEN != NONEXISTENT.

An entity remains in continuity unless:

- it died
- it was replaced
- it left the relevant continuity context through an approved event
- the timeline branch explicitly excludes it
- its existence was never established

Absence from a shot does not change state.

ON-SCREEN != NEW ENTITY.

A newly visible entity must either reference an existing identity or
be explicitly created/individualized through an approved process.
