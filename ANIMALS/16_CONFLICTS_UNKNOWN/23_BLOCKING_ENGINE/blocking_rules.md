# BLOCKING ENGINE

BLOCK when uncertainty can materially alter:

- species identity
- anatomy
- behavior
- movement feasibility
- ecological presence
- domestic/training state
- interaction identity
- visual identity
- audio identity
- historical accuracy
- canonical event
- continuity
- safety/animal welfare
- final shot validity

Do not block harmless presentation uncertainty when the underlying truth remains intact.

Blocking must be proportional to dependency impact.
