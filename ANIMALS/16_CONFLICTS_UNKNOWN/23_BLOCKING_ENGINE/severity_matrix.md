# SEVERITY MATRIX

S0 — INFORMATIONAL
No meaningful downstream effect.

S1 — LOW
Minor uncertainty; no truth-critical effect.

S2 — MODERATE
May affect a domain decision.

S3 — HIGH
Can affect scene or asset validity.

S4 — CRITICAL
Can invalidate canonical, biological, ecological, continuity or identity truth.

S5 — PRODUCTION STOP
Cannot safely proceed until resolved or explicitly approved as controlled uncertainty.
