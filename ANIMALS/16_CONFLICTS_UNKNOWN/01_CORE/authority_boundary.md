# AUTHORITY BOUNDARY

16_CONFLICTS_UNKNOWN owns:

- uncertainty classification
- conflict registration
- severity
- dependency impact
- blocking state
- resolution workflow
- resolution provenance
- exception records
- uncertainty snapshots

Other systems own their domain truth.

A conflict involving anatomy is resolved by Biology.

A conflict involving behavior is resolved by Behavior.

A conflict involving ecological presence is resolved by Ecology.

A conflict involving historical costume is resolved by Costume Track / Historical Research.

A conflict involving persistence is resolved by Continuity.

16_CONFLICTS_UNKNOWN may BLOCK a downstream decision when uncertainty is material.

It may not arbitrarily decide the underlying truth.
