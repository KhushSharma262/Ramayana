# UNCERTAINTY VOCABULARY

UNKNOWN:
Required information is currently unavailable.

EVIDENCE_GAP:
The claim cannot currently be supported because required evidence is missing.

CONFLICT:
Two or more relevant claims/evidence items materially disagree.

AMBIGUITY:
A source or entity permits multiple interpretations.

UNCERTAINTY:
Evidence exists but confidence remains limited.

VARIABILITY:
Multiple outcomes are naturally possible within the real system.

UNRESOLVED:
The issue remains open.

RESOLVED:
An explicit approved resolution exists.

BLOCKED:
The uncertainty prevents safe downstream use.

SUPERSEDED:
An older record has been replaced by a newer approved state.
