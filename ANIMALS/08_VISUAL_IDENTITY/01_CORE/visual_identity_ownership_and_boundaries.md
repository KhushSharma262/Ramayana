# OWNERSHIP AND BOUNDARIES

BIOLOGY owns:
taxonomy, anatomy, morphology, physiology, health, biological limits.

BEHAVIOR owns:
behavioral state, emotion/response, motivation and expression context.

MOVEMENT owns:
physical execution, posture, gait and biomechanics.

ECOLOGY owns:
habitat, environmental context and ecological plausibility.

DOMESTIC/TRAINED owns:
training, domestication, equipment use, husbandry and human-animal use.

INTERACTION owns:
interaction events and relationship consequences.

VISUAL IDENTITY owns:
approved visual representation of all upstream truth.

AI PRODUCTION owns:
generation and rendering implementation only.

No downstream visual system may silently override upstream truth.
