# FACIAL AND BODY MORPHOLOGY

Persistent visual identity may include:

- facial proportions
- cranial and jaw structure
- eye placement
- nose structure
- mouth and lip structure
- ear shape
- neck proportions
- shoulder and torso proportions
- limb proportions
- hand/foot structure
- body mass distribution
- species-specific visible morphology

These must remain compatible with Biology.

Aesthetic optimization cannot override biological or canonical constraints.
