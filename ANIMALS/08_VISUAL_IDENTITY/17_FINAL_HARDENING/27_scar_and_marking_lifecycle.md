# SCAR AND MARKING LIFECYCLE

Permanent visual marks have lifecycle states:

ABSENT
→ EVENT
→ FRESH
→ HEALING
→ MATURE
→ STABLE

Each state has:

cause
date/time
location
appearance
source
approval
snapshot

A scar cannot appear before its cause.

A mature scar cannot instantly disappear without a validated event.

Ritual/cultural markings have their own lifecycle and must not be confused with biological scars.
