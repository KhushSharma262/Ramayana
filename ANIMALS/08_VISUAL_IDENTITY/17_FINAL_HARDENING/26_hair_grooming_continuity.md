# HAIR AND GROOMING CONTINUITY

Hair and grooming are stateful visual properties.

Track:

length
density
style
parting
braid
tie
shave
beard
moustache
oil
washing
dust
wetness
damage
growth

Material changes require an event.

Examples:

CUT
SHAVE
GROW
BRAID
UNTIE
WASH
OIL
DAMAGE
REGROW

AI generation cannot randomly alter a persistent hairstyle and call it natural variation.
