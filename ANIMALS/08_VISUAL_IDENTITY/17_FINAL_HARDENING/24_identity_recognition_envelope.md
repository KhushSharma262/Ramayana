# IDENTITY RECOGNITION ENVELOPE

Every persistent identity has an approved variation envelope.

VARIATION_ALLOWED:
minor natural asymmetry
hair displacement
temporary expression
lighting change
environmental surface effects
natural pose-dependent appearance

VARIATION_LIMITED:
weight/body-condition change
temporary grooming
temporary swelling
temporary dirt
temporary damage

VARIATION_FORBIDDEN:
species
face-defining structure
identity-defining proportions
permanent markings
canonical age state
canonical form
sex-defining traits where identity-critical

Identity recognition is evaluated across multiple reference views where possible.

A generation may be technically similar yet still fail identity continuity.

Recognition failure = REJECT.
