# CAMERA AND RENDERING DISTORTION FIREWALL

Camera and rendering can alter apparent proportions.

Potential causes:

wide-angle distortion
telephoto compression
perspective
camera height
camera distance
lens characteristics
depth of field
lighting
shadow
motion blur
occlusion

These are presentation effects.

They cannot redefine:

face structure
body proportions
species morphology
age
identity

Validation must distinguish:

IDENTITY CHANGE
from
CAMERA-INDUCED APPEARANCE CHANGE.

A correct identity may look different across lenses without becoming a different identity.
