# VISUAL CONFLICT RESOLUTION

Conflicting visual evidence must never be resolved by aesthetic preference.

Resolution order:

1. Direct authoritative canonical statement where applicable
2. Direct biological evidence where applicable
3. Direct historical/archaeological evidence where applicable
4. Strong corroborated evidence
5. Context-specific scholarly interpretation
6. Traditional interpretation
7. Controlled production inference

The hierarchy is claim-dependent.

Conflicts must record:

conflict_id
claim_id
sources
contradiction
temporal scope
geographic scope
authority owner
resolution
reason
approval
version

Possible states:

RESOLVED
CONDITIONALLY_RESOLVED
CONTESTED
UNRESOLVED
BLOCKED

UNRESOLVED visual conflicts cannot silently become production truth.

Popularity, visual attractiveness, previous cinema, AI consensus or frequency of depiction do not determine truth.
