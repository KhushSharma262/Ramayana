# ASSET TRAIT AUDIT

Every important generated asset receives a trait-level audit.

Minimum audit fields:

asset_id
entity_id
visual_identity_id
snapshot_id
scene_id
prompt_id
model_id
generation_version
identity_match
morphology_match
age_match
surface_match
marking_match
hair_match
clothing_match
ornament_match
equipment_match
form_match
temporary_state_match
camera_effect_checked
ai_bias_checked
compatibility_checked
continuity_checked
decision
reviewer
timestamp

Each critical trait is:

PASS
FAIL
NOT_APPLICABLE
UNKNOWN

UNKNOWN critical trait = REJECT until resolved.

Asset-level audit is separate from source-level approval.
