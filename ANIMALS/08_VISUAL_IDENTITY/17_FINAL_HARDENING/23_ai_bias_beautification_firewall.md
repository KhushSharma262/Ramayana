# AI BIAS AND BEAUTIFICATION FIREWALL

AI models frequently introduce unrequested visual assumptions.

The following are NOT permitted unless explicitly supported:

beautification
facial symmetry enhancement
skin smoothing
skin-lightening
age reduction
body idealization
muscle enhancement
slimming
eye enlargement
jawline alteration
nose normalization
hair-density enhancement
perfect teeth
perfect skin
fashion modernization
ethnic/phenotypic homogenization
species homogenization

The model must follow the approved identity.

A visually attractive generation is not evidence of correctness.

Where a generated asset exhibits model bias:

ASSET = REJECTED

The canonical identity is unchanged.
