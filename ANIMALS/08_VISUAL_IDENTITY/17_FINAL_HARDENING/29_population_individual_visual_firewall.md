# POPULATION / INDIVIDUAL VISUAL FIREWALL

Population rules define distributions and variation.

Individual identity defines a specific entity.

Population traits must not be copied identically to every individual.

Individual traits must not be generalized to the population.

Population-level generation may sample from approved variation envelopes.

Persistent individuals require individual snapshots.

This prevents:

clone populations
accidental identity creation
individual-to-species contamination
species-to-individual overconstraint
