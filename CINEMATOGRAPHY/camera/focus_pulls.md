# FOCUS PULLS

## SYSTEM ROLE

Defines deliberate shot-level transitions between focus planes.

---

## REQUIREMENTS

A focus pull must have:

- starting target
- ending target
- reason
- timing
- transition duration
- physically achievable focus movement

---

## HUMAN EXECUTION

A focus pull may be:

- precise
- gradual
- anticipatory
- slightly responsive
- intentionally imperfect

Do not add visible mistakes unless narratively justified.

---

## MOVEMENT COORDINATION

Focus pulls must account for:

- camera movement
- subject movement
- lens breathing
- changing subject distance
- framing changes

---

## ATTENTION

A focus pull should correspond to a meaningful shift in visual attention.

Do not pull focus merely to demonstrate shallow depth of field.

---

## FINAL RULE

A focus pull is a physical change in focal plane, not a post-processing blur transition.
