# DOLLY RULES

## SYSTEM ROLE

Operational rules for physical dolly shots.

---

## DOLLY DEFINITION

A dolly physically translates the camera.

Therefore it may change:

- perspective
- parallax
- spatial relationships
- relative subject/background scale

It does not inherently change focal length.

---

## PATH

Possible paths:

- forward
- backward
- lateral
- straight
- curved
- controlled arc

The selected path must exist physically.

---

## START / STOP

Model:

- preparation
- acceleration
- travel
- deceleration
- settling

Avoid instantaneous starts/stops.

---

## TRACK SPACE

Consider:

- track length
- terrain
- obstacles
- operator access
- subject path
- camera clearance

---

## FOCUS

Camera translation may alter focus distance.

Focus must therefore be coordinated with the dolly path.

---

## FRAMING

Framing may require controlled head movement or camera rotation.

Do not change lens/focal length unless explicitly selected.

---

## FINAL RULE

A dolly creates real camera translation and therefore real perspective/parallax consequences.
