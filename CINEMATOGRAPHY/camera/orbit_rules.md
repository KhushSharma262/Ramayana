# ORBIT RULES

## SYSTEM ROLE

Defines physical camera translation around a reference point.

---

## ORBIT CENTER

Explicitly define:

- character
- object
- group
- environmental point

---

## ORBIT RADIUS

Radius must remain physically coherent.

If radius changes, the camera must physically move inward or outward.

---

## CONSEQUENCES

An orbit changes:

- camera position
- perspective
- parallax
- subject angle
- background relationship

These are real spatial consequences.

---

## OPERATION

Respect:

- obstacles
- terrain
- camera support
- operator access
- speed
- acceleration
- camera mass
- focus
- framing

---

## FULL ORBITS

Do not default to 360-degree movement.

A complete orbit requires narrative and physical justification.

---

## FINAL RULE

An orbit is physical translation around a reference point, never digital background rotation.
