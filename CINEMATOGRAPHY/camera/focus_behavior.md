# FOCUS BEHAVIOR

## SYSTEM ROLE

Shot-level focus orchestration.

Physical focus mechanics and lens behavior remain in their authoritative systems.

---

## FOCUS TARGET

Possible target:

- eyes
- face
- body
- object
- foreground
- background
- environmental plane
- moving subject

---

## FOCUS DECISION

Focus changes require a cause:

- subject priority change
- blocking change
- camera movement
- subject movement
- narrative attention shift
- deliberate operator action

---

## HUMAN OPERATION

A real operator may:

- anticipate movement
- begin focus adjustment early
- correct focus
- briefly lag behind movement
- recover
- perform deliberate focus pulls

These behaviors must be physically plausible.

Do not create random mistakes.

---

## MOVEMENT INTERACTION

Focus may need to respond to:

- dolly
- tracking
- crane
- orbit
- handheld
- subject movement

---

## TEMPORAL CONSISTENCY

Focus changes must be continuous.

No:

- random sharpness changes
- frame-to-frame focus jumps
- artificial blur transitions
- impossible focal-plane behavior

---

## FINAL RULE

Focus must behave like a real optical focusing system operated by a real person or physically plausible control system.
