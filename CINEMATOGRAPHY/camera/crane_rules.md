# CRANE RULES

## SYSTEM ROLE

Operational rules for crane/jib shots.

Detailed crane physics remain in the root physical crane system.

---

## CRANE REQUIRES

- physically available crane/jib
- valid payload
- valid operating space
- achievable boom path
- plausible acceleration
- plausible deceleration
- operator control

---

## MOVEMENT

Possible movement:

- rise
- descend
- arc
- translate
- pan
- tilt
- combined motion

Every axis must correspond to physical crane behavior.

---

## INERTIA

Heavy camera/crane systems cannot:

- change direction instantly
- stop without deceleration
- accelerate infinitely

Use physical acceleration and settling.

---

## SUBJECT RELATIONSHIP

Maintain the intended:

- camera distance
- camera height
- framing
- focus relationship

while respecting the actual crane path.

---

## OBSTACLE RULE

The crane must not pass through:

- architecture
- terrain
- people
- trees
- props
- other physical objects

---

## FINAL RULE

A crane shot must look like a real camera mounted on a real crane or jib.
