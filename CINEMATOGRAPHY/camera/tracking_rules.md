# TRACKING RULES

## SYSTEM ROLE

Defines physical tracking of moving subjects.

---

## TRACKING PURPOSE

Possible goals:

- maintain framing
- maintain distance
- follow
- lead
- move beside
- approach
- retreat
- reveal environment

---

## HUMAN OPERATION

A real operator may:

- anticipate movement
- react to speed changes
- make framing corrections
- reposition
- adjust focus
- temporarily lose perfect framing
- recover

Use only when physically justified.

---

## SPEED

Camera speed must remain compatible with:

- subject speed
- support system
- terrain
- available path
- operator ability
- intended relationship

---

## PARALLAX

Tracking creates genuine depth-dependent parallax.

Foreground and background must not move as a single flat layer.

---

## FOCUS

Focus must respond to changing subject distance where necessary.

---

## PATH

The camera cannot pass through physical obstacles.

---

## FINAL RULE

Tracking must appear to be a real camera physically following a real subject.
