# TRIPOD RULES

## SYSTEM ROLE

Defines physical tripod-supported operation.

---

## LOCKED SHOTS

A locked tripod shot keeps:

- camera position
- camera height
- orientation

stable unless a deliberate operator action occurs.

Do not add random movement.

---

## TRIPOD HEAD

Pan/tilt behavior must account for:

- operator input
- head resistance
- acceleration
- deceleration
- inertia
- settling

---

## REFRAMING

Reframing should be deliberate.

Small corrections may occur when an operator is actively following action.

Do not manufacture constant micro-jitter.

---

## ENVIRONMENT

Minor physical disturbance may occur from:

- floor vibration
- wind
- operator contact
- environmental activity

Only when physically justified.

---

## SUPPORT CONTINUITY

Do not change from tripod to another support system mid-shot without a physical transition.

---

## FINAL RULE

A tripod shot must look like a real camera mounted on a real tripod and operated by a real cinematographer.
