# HANDHELD RULES

## SYSTEM ROLE

Defines physically plausible handheld camera operation.

Detailed human-camera physics remain in the root human-camera system.

---

## HANDHELD IS NOT SHAKE

Handheld behavior emerges from:

- human posture
- grip
- camera mass
- lens mass
- body movement
- footsteps
- breathing
- balance
- subject tracking
- operator correction
- inertia

---

## STABILITY LEVELS

Handheld can be:

- restrained
- controlled
- responsive
- active
- intentionally unstable

Choose according to shot purpose.

---

## HUMAN LIMITS

Movement must remain anatomically plausible.

Avoid:

- mechanical jitter
- random high-frequency vibration
- weightless floating
- impossible rotation
- independent axis noise

---

## TRACKING

When following a subject, the operator attempts to maintain:

- intended framing
- subject priority
- usable focus
- movement direction

Small corrections may occur.

---

## FOOTSTEPS

Footstep response depends on:

- walking/running speed
- operator posture
- camera mass
- stabilization
- terrain
- lens
- support

---

## FINAL RULE

Handheld footage must look like a human physically carrying and operating a real camera.
