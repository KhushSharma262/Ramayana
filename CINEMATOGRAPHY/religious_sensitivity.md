# RELIGIOUS SENSITIVITY

The Ramayana project depicts sacred figures, events, locations and traditions.

Cinematography must therefore combine visual realism with respectful representation.

## REPRESENTATION

Sacred figures must not be reduced to generic fantasy archetypes.

Visual decisions should preserve:

- dignity
- cultural context
- appropriate symbolism
- character-specific identity
- narrative seriousness
- consistency with the project's researched source material

## DIVINE REPRESENTATION

Divinity does not require artificial visual effects.

A divine presence may be communicated through:

- extraordinary but coherent lighting
- scale
- stillness
- composition
- environmental response
- performance
- spatial relationships
- controlled visual emphasis

All such effects must remain physically integrated.

## HORROR OR WRATH

Frightening divine states should remain dignified and physically believable.

Do not automatically use:

- red lighting
- black smoke
- glowing eyes
- excessive particles
- artificial aura
- exaggerated camera shake

The visual result should emerge from the scene.

## SOURCE DISCIPLINE

Religious or textual claims must be distinguished from cinematographic interpretation.

Research evidence belongs in the research subsystem.

This file defines representation principles, not scriptural facts.
