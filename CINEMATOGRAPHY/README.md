# CINEMATOGRAPHY SYSTEM

This folder is the top-level cinematography architecture for the Ramayana project.

The root layer defines system-wide cinematographic principles, authority boundaries, visual philosophy, continuity philosophy, and cross-system constraints.

Detailed operational authority belongs to dedicated subsystems:

- CAMERA — physical camera, movement, support, operation, stability, positioning and execution
- LENSES — lens physics, focal length, perspective, field of view, depth of field and optical behavior
- framing — image-space organization, subject placement, headroom, leadroom, frame boundaries and framing relationships
- lighting — physical light sources, transport, shadows, material response, exposure and lighting continuity
- grammar — shot-to-shot relationships, editorial grammar, screen direction, eyelines and coverage relationships
- shot_design — individual-shot purpose, construction, information, blocking, duration, reveal, rhythm and validation
- research — evidence and reference material only

No root file may redefine an authoritative subsystem.

The final generated image must behave as if it was captured by a real professional camera, lens, operator, lighting environment and physical set.

PHYSICAL REALISM ALWAYS HAS PRIORITY OVER CINEMATIC EFFECT.
