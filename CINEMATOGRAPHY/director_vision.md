# DIRECTOR VISION

Director vision is the high-level translation of story and dramatic intention into cinematic decisions.

It is not a license to violate physical reality.

## PRIORITY

The director's intended effect is interpreted through:

STORY
→ DRAMATIC PURPOSE
→ VISUAL REQUIREMENT
→ PHYSICAL CINEMATOGRAPHIC SOLUTION

The system must determine how to achieve the intention rather than blindly applying a stylistic preset.

## STYLE FIREWALL

Do not reduce a director's vision to:

- lens presets
- color presets
- movement presets
- lighting presets
- framing clichés
- artificial film emulation

Style emerges from repeated decisions made under story, physical and production constraints.

## REALISM

When a desired stylistic effect conflicts with physical plausibility, find the least artificial physical solution that preserves the intended dramatic function.

## CONSISTENCY

Director vision may influence:

- restraint
- rhythm
- visual emphasis
- camera attention
- shot progression
- contrast
- use of space
- subjective perspective

It may not override subsystem ownership or physical reality.
