# LENS DISTORTION BEHAVIOUR

## PURPOSE

Control geometric distortion produced by the physical optical design of the selected lens.

Lens distortion must be treated as a property of the optical system.

It is NOT a generic cinematic effect.

It is NOT automatically associated with wide lenses.

It is NOT something to exaggerate simply to make footage look cinematic.

The system must determine distortion from:

- lens design
- focal length
- focus distance
- sensor coverage
- subject position
- frame position
- optical construction
- lens generation
- selected lens profile

The resulting geometry must remain physically plausible.

==================================================
NON-NEGOTIABLE PHOTOREALISM
==================================================

The final video must look like professionally captured live-action footage through a real physical lens.

It must NEVER look AI-generated.

Lens distortion must remain:

- physically plausible
- lens-dependent
- spatially coherent
- temporally stable
- restrained
- consistent with the selected optical system

NEVER create:

- arbitrary warping
- rubber-like geometry
- melting architecture
- unstable faces
- changing facial proportions
- random edge stretching
- digital fisheye effects
- artificial barrel distortion
- artificial pincushion distortion
- frame-specific geometry changes
- objects bending differently between frames
- perspective morphing
- warped straight lines without optical justification

When uncertain, use the least noticeable physically plausible distortion.

PHYSICAL REALISM ALWAYS TAKES PRIORITY OVER VISUAL EFFECT.

==================================================
WHAT LENS DISTORTION IS
==================================================

Lens distortion is a deviation between the ideal geometric projection and the actual projection produced by the optical system.

It primarily affects image geometry.

Common forms include:

- barrel distortion
- pincushion distortion
- moustache distortion
- complex asymmetric distortion
- field-dependent geometric distortion

Distortion must be distinguished from:

- perspective
- perspective convergence
- lens compression
- field of view
- anamorphic squeeze
- chromatic aberration

Do not use one phenomenon to simulate another.

==================================================
BARREL DISTORTION
==================================================

Barrel distortion causes image magnification to decrease toward the edges relative to the centre.

Straight lines near the frame edges may appear to bow outward.

It is commonly associated with some:

- wide-angle lenses
- ultra-wide lenses
- compact optical designs
- older lens designs

However:

wide-angle does NOT automatically mean strong barrel distortion.

Modern lenses may be highly corrected.

Apply only the amount justified by the selected lens.

==================================================
PINCUSHION DISTORTION
==================================================

Pincushion distortion causes image magnification to increase toward the edges.

Straight lines near the frame edges may appear to bow inward.

It can occur in:

- telephoto lenses
- complex zoom designs
- specific optical configurations

Do not automatically apply pincushion distortion to all telephoto lenses.

==================================================
MOUSTACHE DISTORTION
==================================================

Moustache distortion is a more complex combination in which distortion changes character across the image.

A line may:

- bend outward
- transition
- bend inward

depending on its position in the frame.

This is a lens-specific characteristic.

Do not use moustache distortion unless the selected lens profile supports it.

==================================================
RADIAL DISTORTION
==================================================

Radial distortion changes with distance from the optical centre.

The system should understand that:

CENTRE:
usually least affected

EDGES:
usually more affected

CORNERS:
may show the strongest geometric deviation

Do not apply identical distortion uniformly across the image.

==================================================
TANGENTIAL / DECENTERING DISTORTION
==================================================

Real lenses may also exhibit asymmetric distortion caused by:

- lens element misalignment
- manufacturing tolerances
- optical decentering
- mechanical imperfections

Character lenses may show subtle asymmetry.

However:

do not introduce random asymmetric warping.

Any asymmetry must remain stable and lens-specific.

==================================================
DISTORTION VS PERSPECTIVE
==================================================

These are fundamentally different.

### PERSPECTIVE

Caused primarily by:

- camera position
- subject distance
- relative depth relationships

### LENS DISTORTION

Caused by:

- optical projection characteristics of the lens

A wide lens placed close to a face may produce strong perspective effects even if the lens has excellent distortion correction.

Do not label perspective exaggeration as lens distortion.

==================================================
FACIAL GEOMETRY
==================================================

Faces are highly sensitive to perspective and distortion.

The system must preserve:

- stable facial proportions
- eye spacing
- nose geometry
- jawline
- forehead
- cheek structure
- mouth proportions

A wide lens may naturally exaggerate near/far facial depth when used close to the subject.

That is perspective.

Do not add additional artificial barrel distortion.

For character close-ups:

prefer restrained distortion unless the selected lens genuinely exhibits visible distortion.

==================================================
ARCHITECTURE
==================================================

Architecture provides strong geometric references.

Maintain:

- stable walls
- straight columns
- stable temple structures
- consistent rooflines
- stable windows
- stable doorways
- coherent horizons

If the selected lens has distortion:

allow straight lines to bend naturally according to the lens profile.

Do not overcorrect them.

Do not allow buildings to visibly warp between frames.

==================================================
WIDE-ANGLE LENSES
==================================================

Wide and ultra-wide lenses can produce strong edge geometry depending on design.

Use them when the story requires:

- environmental scale
- spatial immersion
- close foreground relationships
- expansive architecture
- large landscapes
- dynamic proximity

But do not automatically exaggerate distortion.

A modern 24mm lens may look highly rectilinear.

A specialized ultra-wide lens may show more pronounced distortion.

The lens profile determines the result.

==================================================
RECTILINEAR LENSES
==================================================

Rectilinear lenses are designed to preserve straight-line geometry more effectively across the frame.

For such lenses:

- architectural lines remain relatively straight
- edge geometry remains controlled
- distortion is restrained

Do not impose fisheye-like curvature.

==================================================
FISHEYE LENSES
==================================================

Fisheye lenses intentionally produce strong geometric distortion.

If explicitly selected:

allow:

- pronounced edge curvature
- extreme field of view
- strong peripheral stretching
- distinctive spatial rendering

However:

fisheye behaviour must remain consistent with the selected lens.

Do not accidentally apply fisheye distortion to normal wide-angle lenses.

==================================================
TELEPHOTO LENSES
==================================================

Telephoto lenses generally have narrower FOV and may exhibit different distortion characteristics.

Do not assume that a telephoto lens automatically produces pincushion distortion.

Determine the actual lens profile.

Perspective compression remains separate from distortion.

==================================================
ZOOM LENSES
==================================================

Zoom lenses may change distortion characteristics across their focal-length range.

If a zoom is used:

distortion may evolve as focal length changes.

This evolution must be:

- continuous
- lens-specific
- optically coherent

Do not interpolate arbitrary distortion between focal lengths.

Use the established lens profile.

==================================================
FOCUS DISTANCE
==================================================

Some lenses exhibit focus-dependent distortion changes.

If supported by the lens profile:

distortion may subtly change as focus distance changes.

However:

do not introduce geometry changes merely because the lens is focusing.

Focus breathing and distortion are separate behaviours.

==================================================
LENS DISTORTION AND FOCUS BREATHING
==================================================

During a focus pull:

focus breathing may alter apparent framing.

Lens distortion may remain largely stable.

If the selected lens has focus-dependent distortion:

allow only the physically expected change.

Do not turn breathing into geometry warping.

==================================================
ANAMORPHIC DISTORTION
==================================================

Anamorphic lenses may introduce distinctive geometric behaviour.

Potential characteristics include:

- horizontal compression
- edge stretching
- close-focus geometric changes
- subject shape variations
- characteristic spatial rendering

However:

anamorphic squeeze is NOT the same thing as conventional radial distortion.

Treat separately:

ANAMORPHIC SQUEEZE
vs
LENS DISTORTION.

Do not create excessive distortion simply because the lens is anamorphic.

==================================================
ANAMORPHIC MUMPS
==================================================

Some anamorphic designs may produce characteristic close-focus facial or body distortion, historically referred to as "mumps."

If the selected anamorphic lens profile supports this:

apply it only when:

- focus distance
- subject distance
- focal length
- lens design

make it physically plausible.

Do not automatically distort faces with anamorphic lenses.

Modern anamorphic systems may strongly reduce this behaviour.

==================================================
CROP FACTOR
==================================================

Crop factor changes which portion of the lens image circle reaches the sensor.

Therefore:

a smaller sensor may use a less distorted central portion of the same lens image.

Do not treat crop factor as an independent distortion effect.

The physical lens distortion remains a property of the lens.

The sensor determines which portion of that distortion is captured.

==================================================
SENSOR COVERAGE
==================================================

The selected lens must properly cover the sensor.

If the lens image circle is insufficient:

natural optical consequences may include:

- vignetting
- edge falloff
- mechanical coverage limitations

Do not invent geometric distortion to compensate.

==================================================
FRAME POSITION
==================================================

Distortion is usually more noticeable toward the image periphery.

Therefore:

a subject near the centre may appear relatively unaffected.

The same subject near the edge may experience greater geometric distortion depending on the lens.

This must happen naturally.

Do not distort subjects simply because they are near the edge unless the lens profile supports it.

==================================================
CAMERA MOVEMENT
==================================================

During:

- pan
- tilt
- dolly
- tracking
- crane
- orbit
- handheld movement

distortion must remain attached to the optical image.

Do not allow distortion to:

- flicker
- change randomly
- follow objects independently
- remain fixed to individual subjects

The optical distortion field moves with the camera's imaging system.

==================================================
SUBJECT MOVEMENT
==================================================

When a subject moves through the frame:

its distortion should change naturally as its position within the lens's distortion field changes.

For example:

centre
→ moderate edge position

may produce a gradual increase in geometric distortion.

The transition must be smooth.

Do not suddenly warp the subject when it reaches a particular screen coordinate.

==================================================
EDGE BEHAVIOUR
==================================================

The system should understand that lens distortion is generally weakest near the optical centre and more pronounced toward the edges.

However:

the exact pattern is lens-specific.

Do not use a universal edge-warp formula for all lenses.

==================================================
TEMPORAL CONSISTENCY
==================================================

Lens distortion must remain temporally stable.

NEVER allow:

- architecture to bend differently between frames
- faces to stretch and contract
- straight lines to oscillate
- edge distortion to flicker
- objects to change shape randomly
- geometry to morph
- distortion to pulse
- lens character to change without cause

Every change must have a physical explanation.

==================================================
LENS CONTINUITY
==================================================

Once a lens is selected:

maintain its distortion profile throughout the shot.

If the same lens continues across multiple shots:

preserve its general optical character.

A lens change may legitimately change distortion.

The system must recognize the change explicitly.

Do not randomly switch between:

- barrel
- pincushion
- rectilinear
- fisheye

behaviour.

==================================================
DISTORTION CORRECTION
==================================================

If the selected camera workflow includes optical distortion correction:

apply correction consistently.

Do not simultaneously:

- preserve strong native distortion
- apply full digital correction
- then add artificial distortion

unless the workflow explicitly requires this.

The final image should represent the intended optical pipeline.

==================================================
NATURAL CORRECTION
==================================================

Modern cinema lenses may be designed to minimize distortion.

Therefore:

"real lens" does NOT mean "visible distortion."

The physically correct result may be almost distortion-free.

Do not introduce distortion simply to make footage appear more photographic.

==================================================
VINTAGE / CHARACTER LENSES
==================================================

Vintage lenses may show:

- barrel distortion
- pincushion distortion
- complex distortion
- asymmetry
- edge character

Allow these only according to the selected profile.

Do not combine every vintage characteristic simultaneously.

Vintage does not automatically mean:

- heavy distortion
- strong softness
- heavy flare
- strong CA
- extreme vignetting

Each optical characteristic must be evaluated independently.

==================================================
DISTORTION AND FLARE
==================================================

Flare is separate from geometric distortion.

A lens can have:

- strong distortion and low flare
- low distortion and strong flare
- both
- neither

Do not increase distortion because flare is present.

==================================================
DISTORTION AND CHROMATIC ABERRATION
==================================================

Chromatic aberration concerns wavelength-dependent image displacement.

Geometric distortion concerns spatial mapping.

They may coexist but must remain independent.

Do not create coloured edges merely because geometric distortion is strong.

==================================================
DISTORTION AND COMPRESSION
==================================================

Lens compression is primarily related to camera position and focal length.

It is not equivalent to lens distortion.

A telephoto lens can have:

- strong spatial compression
- very low geometric distortion

Do not confuse the two.

==================================================
DISTORTION AND FIELD OF VIEW
==================================================

A lens can have:

- wide FOV with low distortion
- wide FOV with strong distortion
- narrow FOV with low distortion
- narrow FOV with moderate distortion

FOV does not determine distortion magnitude.

The optical design determines it.

==================================================
STORY USE
==================================================

Lens distortion should normally remain subordinate to story.

Use noticeable distortion only when it contributes meaningfully to:

- spatial immersion
- subjective perspective
- disorientation
- extreme proximity
- architectural scale
- unusual visual perspective
- intentionally distinctive lens character

Do not use distortion simply to make an image look more cinematic.

==================================================
CHARACTER CLOSE-UPS
==================================================

For important character close-ups:

default toward restrained geometric distortion.

Prioritize:

- identity preservation
- natural facial geometry
- stable proportions
- realistic anatomy

If the selected lens genuinely has distortion:

allow the natural optical result without exaggeration.

==================================================
ENVIRONMENTAL WIDE SHOTS
==================================================

For environmental shots:

distortion may be more noticeable near the frame edges.

Maintain:

- stable horizon
- stable architecture
- coherent foreground scale
- realistic spatial relationships

Do not bend the environment purely for visual drama.

==================================================
DEFAULT RAMAYANA PROFILE
==================================================

Unless the shot specifies otherwise:

DISTORTION:
natural / lens-dependent

DEFAULT:
restrained

CENTRE:
minimal distortion

EDGES:
lens-dependent

CORNERS:
lens-dependent

FACES:
protected from unnecessary distortion

ARCHITECTURE:
geometrically stable

WIDE LENSES:
no automatic exaggeration

FISHEYE:
disabled unless explicitly selected

ANAMORPHIC DISTORTION:
separate from squeeze

DIGITAL WARPING:
none

ARTIFICIAL GEOMETRIC DISTORTION:
none

TEMPORAL CONSISTENCY:
extremely high

OPTICAL REALISM:
extremely high

AI_ARTIFACT_TOLERANCE:
none

==================================================
FINAL DISTORTION CHECK
==================================================

Before generating the shot, evaluate:

1. What lens is selected?
2. What is its distortion profile?
3. What is the focal length?
4. What sensor format is being used?
5. How much of the lens image circle is captured?
6. Where are important subjects within the frame?
7. Is the shot architecture-sensitive?
8. Is the shot face-sensitive?
9. Is the lens rectilinear, fisheye, spherical, or anamorphic?
10. Is focus distance relevant to the lens's distortion?
11. Is camera movement changing subject position within the distortion field?
12. Is a zoom changing distortion characteristics?
13. Is digital correction part of the workflow?
14. Is the resulting geometry stable frame-to-frame?
15. Is every visible distortion physically explainable?

If distortion cannot be physically justified:

reduce it.

==================================================
DECISION HIERARCHY
==================================================

Determine lens distortion in this order:

1. STORY PURPOSE
2. SHOT PURPOSE
3. CAMERA SYSTEM
4. SENSOR FORMAT
5. LENS TYPE
6. LENS OPTICAL DESIGN
7. FOCAL LENGTH
8. LENS DISTORTION PROFILE
9. CAMERA POSITION
10. SUBJECT POSITION IN FRAME
11. FOCUS DISTANCE
12. APERTURE
13. ZOOM STATE
14. ANAMORPHIC / SPHERICAL STATE
15. SENSOR COVERAGE
16. RESULTING GEOMETRIC DISTORTION
17. TEMPORAL CONSISTENCY
18. FINAL OPTICAL REALISM

Never begin with:

"Add barrel distortion."

Instead determine:

WHAT LENS IS BEING USED?
→ WHAT IS ITS ACTUAL DISTORTION PROFILE?
→ WHAT SENSOR IS CAPTURING IT?
→ WHERE ARE THE SUBJECTS WITHIN THE IMAGE?
→ WHAT DOES THE OPTICAL SYSTEM PHYSICALLY DO TO THOSE POSITIONS?
→ DOES THE RESULT REMAIN STABLE THROUGHOUT THE SHOT?

Lens distortion must be a consequence of real optical design.

It must never be an artificial visual effect.

The final video must preserve stable geometry, realistic faces, physically plausible optical behaviour, and consistent lens character.

It must never look AI-generated.
