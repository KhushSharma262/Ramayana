### LENS COMPRESSION BEHAVIOUR

Treat perceived spatial compression as a consequence of perspective and scene geometry, not as an optical effect independently created by focal length.

The final video must look like professionally photographed live-action footage captured through a real physical camera and lens.

It must NOT look AI-generated, digitally manipulated, artificially stylized, or visually enhanced.

==================================================
CORE PRINCIPLE
==================================================

Do not describe or generate "lens compression" as though a lens physically compresses space.

Perspective is determined primarily by CAMERA POSITION relative to the subject and the three-dimensional scene geometry.

Focal length primarily determines angle of view and framing.

When framing is kept similar while focal length changes, the camera generally must move relative to the subject. That change in camera position changes the perspective relationship between the subject and background.

Therefore:

CAMERA POSITION
+
SCENE GEOMETRY
+
SUBJECT DISTANCE
+
BACKGROUND DISTANCE
+
FRAMING
=
PERCEIVED PERSPECTIVE RELATIONSHIP

Focal length determines the required field of view and framing for a given camera position and sensor format. It does not independently create spatial compression.

Do not incorrectly imply that:
- a longer focal length inherently compresses physical space
- a shorter focal length inherently expands physical space
- changing focal length from the same camera position changes the underlying perspective
- "compression" is a digital or optical post-processing effect

Same camera position + different focal length:
- perspective relationships remain physically determined by the same camera position
- field of view and framing change
- background magnification within the frame changes as a consequence of the changed field of view

Similar framing + different focal length:
- camera position generally changes
- perspective relationships therefore change
- the resulting change may be perceived as stronger or weaker spatial compression

Never simulate spatial compression using:
- digital image warping
- artificial background scaling
- depth-map manipulation
- screen-space distortion
- arbitrary subject enlargement
- artificial perspective transformation

All perceived compression must emerge from the physical camera position, lens, sensor, framing, and scene geometry.

==================================================
PHYSICAL DEPENDENCIES
==================================================

Perceived spatial relationships must remain consistent with:
- camera position
- camera orientation
- sensor format and active sensor area
- focal length
- field of view
- subject distance
- background distance
- relative subject/background geometry
- framing
- perspective
- atmospheric conditions
- camera movement

Do not alter scene geometry to obtain a desired compression effect unless the physical scene itself requires that geometry.

==================================================
PHOTOREALISM — NON-NEGOTIABLE
==================================================

The final video must look indistinguishable from professionally captured live-action footage.

Maintain:
- physically plausible perspective
- realistic spatial relationships
- realistic facial proportions
- realistic body proportions
- realistic object dimensions
- physically consistent reflections
- realistic atmospheric depth
- realistic focus behaviour
- realistic motion
- realistic motion blur
- consistent lens behaviour
- consistent lighting
- stable geometry
- stable textures
- stable faces
- stable hair
- stable clothing
- stable accessories
- stable environmental details

Do NOT introduce AI-generated visual characteristics such as:
- plastic or wax-like skin
- unnaturally smooth faces
- artificial eyes
- unstable facial geometry
- changing facial proportions
- texture crawling
- flickering details
- morphing objects
- unstable hair
- unstable clothing
- duplicated details
- inconsistent jewelry or accessories
- physically impossible reflections
- inconsistent shadows

==================================================
NATURALISM RULE
==================================================

Perceived spatial compression must remain a natural consequence of the physical camera configuration and scene geometry.

Never add visible "compression effects" as an independent visual layer.

If the physical camera configuration does not produce a noticeable change in spatial relationships:
    spatial compression = NONE.

If the configuration produces a subtle change:
    preserve it naturally.

If the configuration produces a strong perspective relationship:
    reproduce the physical relationship without exaggeration.

Physical camera geometry always has priority over cinematic terminology or stylistic intent.
