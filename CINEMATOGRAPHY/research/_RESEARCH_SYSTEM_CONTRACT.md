# CINEMATOGRAPHY RESEARCH SYSTEM CONTRACT

## PURPOSE

The RESEARCH subsystem stores, organizes, evaluates and transfers cinematographic knowledge.

It exists to answer:

- What has been observed?
- What has been documented?
- What cinematographic principles can be extracted?
- What techniques have historically been used?
- What physical camera practices support those techniques?
- Which observations are reliable?
- Which conclusions are uncertain?
- Which findings are appropriate for this project?

Research informs the cinematography system.

Research does NOT directly control shot generation.

==================================================
ABSOLUTE AUTHORITY RULE
==================================================

AUTHORITATIVE CINEMATOGRAPHY SYSTEMS
>
RESEARCH
>
REFERENCE MATERIAL.

Research may inform an authoritative subsystem.

Research may NEVER override:

- physical reality
- camera physics
- lens physics
- lighting physics
- spatial continuity
- temporal continuity
- framing rules
- grammar rules
- shot-design rules.

==================================================
NO DIRECT EXECUTION
==================================================

Research must never issue instructions such as:

"Use a 50mm lens."

"Use anamorphic."

"Use handheld."

"Use golden lighting."

unless the statement is explicitly framed as:

- historical observation
- documented production choice
- comparative reference
- research hypothesis.

The authoritative subsystem must independently determine whether that technique is appropriate.

==================================================
REFERENCE ≠ RULE
==================================================

A reference showing a cinematographic technique does not establish that the technique is universally correct.

Example:

A film may use:

- shallow depth of field
- strong backlight
- handheld movement
- anamorphic optics
- unusual color
- long lenses.

These are observations.

They are not default instructions.

==================================================
PHYSICAL REALITY PRIORITY
==================================================

If research conflicts with physical reality:

PHYSICAL REALITY WINS.

If a film appears to contain physically impossible imagery:

do not treat the imagery as evidence of physical cinematography without verification.

==================================================
PROJECT REALISM PRIORITY
==================================================

The project's central objective is:

REAL HUMAN APPEARANCE
+
REAL ENVIRONMENT
+
REAL CAMERA BEHAVIOR
+
REAL LIGHTING
+
REAL OPTICAL BEHAVIOR.

Research must support this objective.

==================================================
SOURCE EVIDENCE
==================================================

Every significant research claim should be classified as:

DOCUMENTED
OBSERVED
MEASURED
INFERRED
INTERPRETED
STYLISTIC
UNCERTAIN.

Never silently convert:

INFERENCE → FACT.

==================================================
CONFLICTING SOURCES
==================================================

When sources disagree:

DO NOT silently choose one.

Record:

- source A
- source B
- claim difference
- evidence strength
- unresolved status.

==================================================
NO HALLUCINATED METADATA
==================================================

Never invent:

- camera model
- lens model
- focal length
- aperture
- filtration
- lighting equipment
- sensor format
- frame rate
- shutter angle
- exposure settings
- production method.

If undocumented:

mark UNKNOWN.

==================================================
STYLE FIREWALL
==================================================

Research may study:

- directors
- cinematographers
- films
- visual movements
- historical cinematography
- visual language.

It must NOT create a direct imitation system.

Do not reduce a filmmaker to:

"their look."

==================================================
NO DIRECTOR PRESETS
==================================================

Never create rules such as:

"Director X = anamorphic + low light + blue."

Such simplifications are research failures.

Study:

- why
- where
- when
- under what production conditions
- with what physical equipment
- for what narrative purpose
- with what limitations.

==================================================
AI RESEARCH FIREWALL
==================================================

AI-generated images, frames or videos may be used as conceptual references.

They are NOT reliable evidence of real cinematographic physics.

Do not infer real-world:

- lens behavior
- lighting behavior
- camera movement
- exposure
- depth of field
- material response

from AI imagery alone.

==================================================
FINAL RULE
==================================================

Research describes evidence.

Systems make decisions.

Physical reality validates both.
