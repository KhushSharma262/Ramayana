# CAMERA REFERENCE

## PURPOSE

Research real camera operation and capture systems.

==================================================
RESEARCH DOMAINS

Study:

- camera body architecture
- sensor formats
- sensor readout
- rolling shutter
- frame rates
- shutter
- exposure
- dynamic range
- recording formats
- stabilization
- support systems
- operator limitations
- camera weight
- camera ergonomics
- practical operating constraints.

==================================================
CAMERA REFERENCE RULE

A camera model is evidence of one production configuration.

It is not automatically a project default.

==================================================
PHYSICAL VALIDATION

Do not infer:

camera body
→
specific visual look

without considering:

- lens
- lighting
- exposure
- color pipeline
- filtration
- movement
- post-production.

==================================================
FINAL RULE

Camera research describes capture possibilities.

The CAMERA subsystem chooses the physical configuration.
