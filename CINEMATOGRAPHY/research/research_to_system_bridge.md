# RESEARCH TO SYSTEM BRIDGE

## PURPOSE

Define how research findings may influence authoritative cinematography systems.

==================================================
TRANSFER PIPELINE

RESEARCH
↓
EVIDENCE VALIDATION
↓
PHYSICAL INTERPRETATION
↓
PROJECT RELEVANCE
↓
AUTHORITATIVE SYSTEM REVIEW
↓
SYSTEM RULE
↓
SHOT APPLICATION.

==================================================
RESEARCH CANNOT SKIP STEPS

Never:

RESEARCH
↓
PROMPT

A research finding must pass through the appropriate subsystem.

==================================================
EXAMPLES

CAMERA RESEARCH
→ CAMERA subsystem.

LENS RESEARCH
→ LENSES subsystem.

LIGHTING RESEARCH
→ LIGHTING subsystem.

FRAMING RESEARCH
→ FRAMING subsystem.

EDITORIAL RESEARCH
→ GRAMMAR subsystem.

SHOT-CONSTRUCTION RESEARCH
→ SHOT_DESIGN subsystem.

==================================================
PHYSICAL VALIDATION

Before transfer ask:

- Is it physically possible?
- Is it documented?
- Is it reproducible?
- Is it appropriate to this scene?
- Does it conflict with existing authority?
- Does it introduce AI artifacts?
- Does it improve realism?

==================================================
REFERENCE ADOPTION

A reference should be adopted only when:

REFERENCE
+
EVIDENCE
+
PHYSICAL VALIDITY
+
PROJECT RELEVANCE

are all acceptable.

==================================================
REFERENCE REJECTION

Reject a reference when it requires:

- impossible optics
- impossible lighting
- impossible camera movement
- digital manipulation masquerading as physical photography
- unsupported assumptions
- style imitation without narrative justification.

==================================================
FINAL RULE

Research provides candidates.

The production system selects.
