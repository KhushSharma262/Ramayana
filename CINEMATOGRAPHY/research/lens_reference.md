CRITICAL FIREWALL

Never infer:

"cinematic"

→

"anamorphic."

Never infer:

"shallow depth of field"

→

"specific lens."

Never infer:

"background compression"

→

"telephoto."

Physical alternatives must remain open until evidence supports one.
