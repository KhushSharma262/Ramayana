# CINEMATOGRAPHY RESEARCH

## PURPOSE

Central research knowledge base for cinematography.

This file records general cinematographic principles and research findings.

==================================================
FOUNDATIONAL DOMAINS

Research may cover:

- camera systems
- lenses
- exposure
- focus
- depth of field
- perspective
- camera movement
- composition
- framing
- lighting
- color
- atmosphere
- film stock / digital capture
- production design interaction
- environmental realism
- visual continuity
- editorial grammar
- historical cinematography
- documentary observation
- visual storytelling.

==================================================
PHYSICAL CINEMATOGRAPHY

Study the relationship between:

CAMERA
+
LENS
+
SUBJECT
+
LIGHT
+
ENVIRONMENT
+
MOVEMENT
+
EXPOSURE.

Never study these as isolated visual effects.

==================================================
CAMERA

Research:

- sensor formats
- shutter
- frame rate
- exposure
- dynamic range
- rolling shutter
- stabilization
- camera support
- operator limitations.

==================================================
LENS

Research:

- focal length
- perspective
- distortion
- depth of field
- bokeh
- flare
- ghosting
- chromatic aberration
- breathing
- anamorphic behavior
- spherical behavior.

==================================================
LIGHTING

Research:

- natural sources
- practicals
- motivated lighting
- source size
- falloff
- bounce
- negative fill
- atmospheric scattering
- material response
- skin response.

==================================================
MOVEMENT

Research:

- tripod
- handheld
- dolly
- tracking
- crane
- orbit
- pan
- tilt
- stabilization
- operator behavior.

==================================================
VISUAL STORYTELLING

Research:

- shot purpose
- visual emphasis
- information revelation
- spatial continuity
- temporal continuity
- subjective viewpoint
- visual rhythm
- restraint.

==================================================
PROJECT FILTER

Every research finding should ultimately be evaluated against:

REALISM
+
HUMAN APPEARANCE
+
PHYSICAL PLAUSIBILITY
+
TEMPORAL CONSISTENCY
+
SPATIAL CONSISTENCY.

==================================================
FINAL RULE

The goal is not to reproduce famous cinematography.

The goal is to understand why real cinematography works.
