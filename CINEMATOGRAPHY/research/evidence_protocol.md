# EVIDENCE PROTOCOL

## PURPOSE

Prevent unsupported cinematographic conclusions.

==================================================
OBSERVATION

Record what is actually visible or documented.

Example:

"Subject has a bright edge on the camera-right side."

Do not immediately claim:

"Dedicated rim light was used."

==================================================
CAUSE

Determine possible physical causes:

- sun
- practical
- bounce
- window
- artificial fixture
- reflection
- atmospheric scattering
- optical effect.

==================================================
VALIDATION

Ask whether each proposed cause explains:

- direction
- intensity
- falloff
- shadow
- reflection
- neighboring objects.

==================================================
CONFIDENCE

Record:

CONFIRMED
LIKELY
POSSIBLE
UNKNOWN.

==================================================
NEGATIVE EVIDENCE

Absence can matter.

If no source is visible:

do not assume a source did not exist.

It may have been:

- off-camera
- hidden
- reflected
- modified on set.

Record uncertainty.

==================================================
AI FRAME ANALYSIS

AI-generated imagery may reveal a visual idea.

It cannot establish physical production facts.

==================================================
FINAL RULE

Never convert visual appearance into technical fact without sufficient evidence.
