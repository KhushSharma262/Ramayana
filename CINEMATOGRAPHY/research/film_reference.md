NO STYLE COPYING

A film reference is an analytical case study.

It must not become:

"make the Ramayana look like Film X."

Instead extract transferable principles such as:

- restraint
- spatial clarity
- motivated movement
- naturalistic lighting
- selective optical character
- controlled visual escalation.
