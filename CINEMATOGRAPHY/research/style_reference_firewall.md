# STYLE REFERENCE FIREWALL

## PURPOSE

Prevent cinematographic research from turning into imitation presets.

==================================================
NO DIRECTOR IMITATION

Do not encode:

DIRECTOR
→
CAMERA STYLE.

Do not encode:

CINEMATOGRAPHER
→
LENS STYLE.

Do not encode:

FILM
→
COLOR PRESET.

==================================================
STYLE DECOMPOSITION

If a reference is described as "beautiful", "dark", "epic", "intimate" or "cinematic":

decompose it into observable components.

Example:

"dark"

may involve:

- lower exposure
- reduced fill
- controlled practicals
- deeper environmental shadows
- lower background luminance
- atmospheric conditions.

"beautiful"

may involve:

- source direction
- color harmony
- composition
- material response
- contrast control
- exposure.

==================================================
REFERENCE EXTRACTION

Extract:

PHYSICAL TECHNIQUE
+
NARRATIVE PURPOSE
+
ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITION
+
LIMITATION
+
RESULT.

==================================================
NO UNIVERSALIZATION

A technique observed in one film must not become a universal rule.

==================================================
FINAL RULE

Research style to understand causality.

Do not research style to copy appearance.
