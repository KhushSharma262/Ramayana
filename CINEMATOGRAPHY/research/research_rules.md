# RESEARCH RULES

## RESEARCH PURPOSE

Research should increase understanding rather than create presets.

Every research item should answer:

WHAT?
WHY?
WHERE?
WHEN?
HOW?
UNDER WHAT PHYSICAL CONDITIONS?
HOW CERTAIN?

==================================================
RESEARCH RECORD

Each important finding should conceptually contain:

SOURCE
SUBJECT
OBSERVATION
EVIDENCE_TYPE
CONFIDENCE
PHYSICAL_BASIS
CINEMATOGRAPHIC_PRINCIPLE
PROJECT_RELEVANCE
LIMITATIONS
SYSTEM_DESTINATION.

==================================================
EVIDENCE TYPES

DOCUMENTED:
Explicitly supported by reliable production documentation.

MEASURED:
Derived from measurable information.

OBSERVED:
Clearly visible in reliable footage or photography.

INFERRED:
Reasonable conclusion from evidence.

INTERPRETED:
Analytical interpretation.

STYLISTIC:
Describes perceived visual character.

UNCERTAIN:
Insufficient evidence.

==================================================
CONFIDENCE

HIGH:
Multiple strong sources or direct documentation.

MEDIUM:
Strong observation with limited documentation.

LOW:
Plausible but weakly supported.

UNKNOWN:
Cannot be established.

==================================================
RESEARCH VS FACT

A visual observation may be correct while the physical cause remains unknown.

Example:

OBSERVATION:
"Background appears compressed."

DO NOT automatically record:

"Telephoto lens was used."

Possible causes must remain separate until evidence supports one.

==================================================
ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

When several physical explanations exist:

record them.

Do not force one explanation simply because it produces the desired cinematic result.

==================================================
PROJECT TRANSFER

A research finding may transfer into the production system only when:

1. evidence is sufficient
2. physical behavior is understood
3. project context supports it
4. no authoritative subsystem is contradicted
5. it survives realism validation.

==================================================
NO RESEARCH BLOAT

Do not store duplicate rules across research files.

Research records should link to principles rather than restating entire systems.

==================================================
FINAL RULE

Research increases knowledge.

It does not increase visual effects.
