==================================================
PHYSICAL FIREWALL

Never infer emotional meaning directly from movement.

"Handheld = tension"

is not a universal rule.

Research must determine:

why the movement was chosen
+
how it was physically executed.

==================================================
AI FIREWALL

Do not treat impossible AI camera movement as evidence of real cinematography.

Reject:

- teleporting camera
- impossible acceleration
- floating camera
- zero-mass camera movement
- impossible parallax.

==================================================
FINAL RULE

Study how real cameras move before using movement as visual language.
