# RITUAL SHOTS

## PURPOSE

Design ceremonial, religious, cultural, sacred, or procedural actions where sequence, gesture, objects, participants, spatial relationships, repetition, and meaning matter.

## CORE DESIGN

A ritual shot must determine:

- what ritual is occurring
- who is participating
- what physical action is occurring
- what object or objects matter
- what sequence/order matters
- what the audience must understand
- what must remain unknown
- what changes during the shot
- what state is inherited by the next shot

## RITUAL STRUCTURE

Where applicable:

PREPARATION
→ PARTICIPATION
→ OBJECT
→ GESTURE
→ ACTION
→ REPETITION
→ CONSEQUENCE
→ COMPLETION

Not every ritual requires every stage.

## PHYSICAL PERFORMANCE

Gestures must appear physically performed by real people.

Preserve:

- posture
- hand position
- object contact
- body orientation
- eye direction
- movement timing
- breathing
- clothing interaction
- natural human coordination

Do not make participants move mechanically for visual symmetry.

## OBJECT CONTINUITY

Track:

- object identity
- ownership
- position
- orientation
- condition
- interaction
- resulting state

Objects must not:

- duplicate
- disappear
- change shape
- teleport
- change material
- change position without physical cause

## SEQUENCE

If ritual order carries meaning, preserve the correct sequence.

Do not rearrange actions merely because a different order produces a more attractive image.

## CAMERA

Shot design may specify:

- which action must remain readable
- which relationship must remain visible
- when a detail must be isolated
- when geography must be established
- when observation should remain uninterrupted

CAMERA determines physical implementation.

## FRAMING

FRAMING determines exact image-space organization.

Shot design determines only what information must be visible and prioritized.

## LENS

LENSES determines:

- focal length
- perspective
- depth of field
- optical behavior
- macro behavior
- anamorphic behavior

Shot design must not prescribe these as stylistic presets.

## LIGHTING

Ritual lighting must remain physically motivated.

Fire, lamps, sunlight, moonlight, reflected light, smoke, shadows, skin, fabric, metal, stone, wood, and architecture must respond as real physical materials and light would.

Divine or sacred significance does not justify artificial glow.

## ENVIRONMENT

The ritual environment must remain physically coherent:

- architecture
- floor
- walls
- furniture
- vessels
- fabric
- vegetation
- fire
- smoke
- water
- atmospheric conditions

## INFORMATION

If the ritual communicates narrative information, explicitly define:

INFORMATION_REVEALED
INFORMATION_WITHHELD
AUDIENCE_KNOWLEDGE_BEFORE
AUDIENCE_KNOWLEDGE_AFTER

## SYMBOLISM

Symbolic meaning may exist, but the physical event must remain credible.

Do not manufacture symbolism using arbitrary:

- glow
- slow motion
- artificial camera shake
- floating particles
- impossible light
- synthetic atmosphere
- exaggerated lens effects

## CONTINUITY

Preserve:

- participant identity
- costume
- ornaments
- objects
- hand positions
- object condition
- spatial geography
- lighting state
- environmental state
- temporal state

unless a deliberate change occurs.

## AI-VIDEO REALISM FIREWALL

Reject:

- morphing faces
- unstable hands
- changing jewelry
- changing ritual objects
- duplicated objects
- disappearing objects
- impossible contact
- unstable fire
- unstable smoke
- inconsistent shadows
- impossible reflections
- geometry drift
- texture crawling
- artificial motion blur
- impossible camera movement

## FINAL RULE

The ritual must look like a real physical event photographed by a professional cinematography team.

Meaning may be extraordinary.

Physical behavior must remain credible.

# SHOT DESIGN ZERO-LOOPHOLE HARDENING

## AUTHORITY

This file does not override:

CAMERA
LENSES
FRAMING
LIGHTING
GRAMMAR
CONTINUITY
GEOGRAPHY

## UNKNOWN STATE

Unknown information remains UNKNOWN.

Never invent ritual order, blocking, object placement, timing, camera movement, or lighting motivation when the source does not establish it.

## MINIMUM COMPLEXITY

Use the simplest physically credible construction that communicates the required ritual information.

## FINAL VALIDATION

The shot is valid only when:

PURPOSE
+
RITUAL ACTION
+
PERFORMANCE
+
OBJECT CONTINUITY
+
SPACE
+
TIME
+
INFORMATION
+
PHYSICAL REALISM
+
CONTINUITY

are mutually compatible.
