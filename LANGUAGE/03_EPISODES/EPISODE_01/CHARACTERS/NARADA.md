# NARADA — EPISODE 01 DIALOGUE PROFILE

## 1. DIALOGUE IDENTITY

Narada's dialogue is:
- intelligent
- observant
- mobile
- concise when delivering information
- capable of strategic questioning
- spiritually informed
- socially perceptive
- occasionally provocative

Narada should not sound like a generic narrator.

## 2. BEHAVIORAL CORE

Narada:
- observes relationships
- notices what others overlook
- carries information between people
- asks questions that expose assumptions
- can deliberately provoke thought
- may reveal truth gradually
- understands consequences
- can speak respectfully while still unsettling the listener

Traditional Ramcharitmanas material presents Narada as a figure whose counsel
can significantly alter the course of others' lives, while Shiva Purana material
also presents him explaining Parvati's identity and destiny to Himavan. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="2"}

## 3. VOCABULARY FINGERPRINT

Preferred semantic fields:
- truth
- consequence
- destiny
- dharma
- knowledge
- relationship
- divine purpose
- cause
- effect
- illusion
- devotion
- future
- event
- understanding
- question
- insight

Narada frequently moves from:
observation → implication.

## 4. SENTENCE FINGERPRINT

Default:
- concise
- controlled
- intelligent
- pointed

When revealing information:
fact → context → implication

When provoking:
question → pause → implication

When teaching:
explanation without unnecessary ornament

## 5. QUESTION STYLE

Narada's questions are often strategically useful.

He may ask a question when:
- he already understands the answer
- the listener needs to discover the answer
- a contradiction must be exposed
- the listener is emotionally avoiding something

This is a major part of his dialogue fingerprint.

## 6. PROVOCATION

Narada may provoke without being rude.

His provocation should feel:
- deliberate
- intelligent
- spiritually purposeful
- occasionally mischievous

Do not turn him into a prankster.

## 7. HUMOR / IRONY

Possible, but controlled.

His humor should emerge from:
- knowing more than the listener
- observing contradictions
- timing
- subtle irony

Never modern stand-up comedy.

## 8. EMOTIONAL RESTRAINT

Narada generally remains more controlled than Lakshmana or Maina.

He may understand emotional turmoil without becoming consumed by it.

## 9. INFORMATION BEHAVIOR

Narada should not dump exposition.

He can:
- reveal one fact
- allow a question
- answer
- introduce another implication

This makes information feel conversational.

## 10. WHAT NARADA DOES NOT SOUND LIKE

Do NOT make him:
- an omniscient narrator in every conversation
- a clown
- constantly sarcastic
- excessively mysterious
- cryptic for no reason
- modern
- slang-heavy
- emotionally flat

## 11. CONTRAST

Narada ≠ Brahma.

Brahma:
states and establishes.

Narada:
observes, questions, connects and reveals.

Narada ≠ Kāmadeva.

Narada:
insight + consequence

Kāmadeva:
desire + attraction + persuasion

## 12. CANON / ADAPTATION STATUS

Narada's role as sage, messenger, adviser and catalyst is strongly grounded
in traditional narrative material.

Exact vocabulary patterns are production characterization unless directly
supported by source text.

## 13. AI GENERATION RULE

Before writing Narada, ask:

"What does Narada know that the listener does not?"

If the answer is "nothing," his characteristic information/insight behavior
may need to be reduced.
