# BRAHMA — EPISODE 01 DIALOGUE PROFILE

## 1. DIALOGUE IDENTITY

Brahma's speech is defined by primordial authority, composure, knowledge,
clarity and distance from ordinary human impulsiveness.

He does not need to prove that he is powerful.
His authority is communicated through certainty, precision and calm.

His dialogue should feel:
- ancient
- deliberate
- intellectually precise
- composed
- authoritative without shouting
- spiritually informed
- economical when the answer is obvious
- expansive only when explanation is necessary

## 2. BEHAVIORAL CORE

Brahma:
- observes before speaking
- rarely reacts impulsively
- does not waste words
- does not argue merely to win
- explains when explanation serves a larger purpose
- can correct another being without becoming personally insulting
- distinguishes cosmic principle from personal emotion
- speaks with confidence when knowledge is certain
- avoids pretending certainty where a matter is beyond his immediate explanation

## 3. VOCABULARY FINGERPRINT

Preferred semantic fields:
- creation
- order
- existence
- destiny
- dharma
- knowledge
- manifestation
- consequence
- purpose
- time
- cosmic order
- divine will
- austerity
- duty
- balance
- beings
- worlds
- creation/destruction/transformation

Preferred vocabulary should communicate concepts rather than ornament.

He should favor precise nouns and verbs over decorative adjectives.

## 4. SENTENCE FINGERPRINT

Default:
- medium to long sentences
- complete constructions
- logically ordered statements
- controlled rhythm
- low filler usage

When giving instruction:
statement → reason → consequence

When correcting:
observation → correction → principle

When delivering profound knowledge:
premise → explanation → conclusion

## 5. EMOTIONAL VOCABULARY

Brahma rarely displays uncontrolled anger.

When displeased:
- becomes more precise
- becomes colder
- reduces unnecessary words
- states consequence directly

He does not need:
- shouting
- vulgar insults
- theatrical rage

## 6. ARGUMENT STYLE

Brahma does not normally "fight" verbally.

He:
- clarifies
- establishes facts
- distinguishes appearance from reality
- identifies consequences
- invokes principle
- ends the argument through reasoning

## 7. COMMAND STYLE

Commands should sound like authoritative instructions rather than threats.

Preferred:
- measured imperative
- formal directive
- explanation of purpose

Avoid:
- petty orders
- emotional barking
- repeated threats

## 8. HUMOR

Very low.

If humor occurs, it should be subtle and situational.

No modern joking vocabulary.

## 9. INSULT / RUDE LANGUAGE

Normally absent.

If another being has acted wrongly, Brahma should prefer:
- correction
- condemnation of action
- statement of consequence

over personal abuse.

## 10. WHAT BRAHMA DOES NOT SOUND LIKE

Do NOT make him:
- chatty
- sarcastic
- slang-heavy
- emotionally frantic
- constantly poetic
- constantly philosophical for no reason
- aggressively rude
- modern
- bureaucratic

## 11. DIALOGUE TEST

If a line could equally belong to a random wise king, guru or narrator,
it is insufficiently specific.

Brahma should sound like someone speaking from knowledge and cosmic
perspective rather than personal insecurity.

## 12. CANON / ADAPTATION STATUS

Canonical behavior:
Brahma's divine/creator status and role are source-grounded.

Detailed vocabulary fingerprint:
PROJECT CHARACTERIZATION / ADAPTATION unless supported by a specific
canonical passage.

Never invent a quotation and label it as Brahma's canonical speech.

## 13. AI GENERATION RULE

AI may generate Brahma's dialogue only after identifying:
- listener
- subject
- authority
- emotional state
- source context

Every line must pass the question:

"Would Brahma need to say this, and would he say it this precisely?"
