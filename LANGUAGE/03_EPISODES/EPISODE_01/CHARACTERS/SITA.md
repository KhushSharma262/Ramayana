# SITA — EPISODE 01 DIALOGUE PROFILE

## 1. CORE DIALOGUE IDENTITY

Sita's dialogue is shaped by:
- dignity
- devotion
- intelligence
- emotional strength
- compassion
- moral clarity
- sensitivity
- self-respect
- courage

She must never be reduced to a passive or decorative character.

---

## 2. BEHAVIORAL ENGINE

Sita:
- listens carefully
- understands emotional nuance
- can express affection
- can question
- can disagree
- can endure hardship
- can make morally serious decisions
- can speak firmly without becoming crude

Her gentleness and strength coexist.

---

## 3. VOCABULARY FINGERPRINT

Primary semantic fields:
- dharma
- Rama
- family
- duty
- affection
- truth
- compassion
- dignity
- courage
- loyalty
- responsibility
- suffering
- welfare
- resolve
- righteousness

Her vocabulary should often emphasize:
relationship + principle.

She should not sound like Lakshmana's vocabulary simply transferred to a
female character.

---

## 4. SENTENCE FINGERPRINT

Normal:
- clear
- graceful
- measured

Affectionate:
- warmer
- personal
- attentive

Questioning:
- thoughtful
- precise
- respectful

Firm:
- direct
- morally clear
- controlled

Distressed:
- emotionally exposed
- simpler
- occasional repetition

---

## 5. MORAL ASSERTIVENESS

Sita may challenge another person's position.

Her disagreement should not automatically be:
softened until meaningless.

When she believes something is morally necessary, she can become extremely
clear.

Her strength should appear through conviction rather than vulgar aggression.

---

## 6. RELATIONSHIP WITH RAMA

Sita's speech toward Rama should contain:
- respect
- affection
- trust
- emotional intimacy
- moral honesty

She should not speak to Rama like:
- a subordinate employee
- a modern girlfriend
- a worshipper in every ordinary domestic interaction

The relationship must retain both intimacy and dignity.

---

## 7. RELATIONSHIP WITH OTHERS

With elders:
- respectful

With women/family:
- warm
- attentive
- compassionate

With servants/subordinates:
- dignified
- humane

With hostile characters:
- controlled
- firm
- morally clear

---

## 8. CONFLICT STYLE

Sita generally argues through:
- principle
- relationship
- moral consequence
- emotional truth

She should not need to shout to be strong.

---

## 9. ANGER

Sita's anger should feel:
- controlled
- morally grounded
- personal when dignity is violated

Avoid making her anger identical to Lakshmana.

Lakshmana:
action + defense

Sita:
conviction + dignity + moral clarity

---

## 10. AFFECTION

Affection may appear through:
- concern
- personal address
- reassurance
- shared memory
- respect
- sacrifice
- quiet understanding

Do not overuse explicit declarations of love.

---

## 11. HUMOR

Low to moderate depending on context.

If present:
- subtle
- warm
- relational

Never modern comedy.

---

## 12. WHAT SITA MUST NOT SOUND LIKE

Do not make her:
- passive
- constantly submissive
- endlessly poetic
- constantly crying
- modern-casual
- sarcastic without reason
- vulgar
- identical to Parvati
- identical to Maina

---

## 13. DISTINCTIVE CONTRAST

Sita:
DIGNITY + DEVOTION + MORAL CLARITY + INNER STRENGTH

Parvati:
DEVOTION + INQUIRY + SPIRITUAL DISCOVERY

Sati:
DEVOTION + INNER CONFLICT + EMOTIONAL RUPTURE

Maina:
MATERNAL PROTECTION + EMOTIONAL CONCERN

---

## 14. CANON / ADAPTATION

Sita's core characterization is grounded in Ramayana traditions.

Exact recurring vocabulary is production characterization unless supported
by direct source dialogue.

Never invent a canonical quotation.

---

## 15. FINAL TEST

Before writing Sita, ask:

"What does she believe is right?"

Then ask:

"How can she say it with dignity without becoming artificially passive?"

The answer should determine the line.

## CHARACTER-SPECIFIC HARD OVERRIDES

Sita's dialogue must combine:

DIGNITY
+
AFFECTION
+
MORAL CLARITY
+
INNER STRENGTH.

### SITA WORD PRIORITY

HIGH:
dharma, Rama, duty, truth, dignity, compassion, responsibility,
welfare, resolve, affection

MEDIUM:
suffering, courage, family, sacrifice

LOW:
status competition, petty insult, gratuitous dominance

### SITA ASSERTION RULE

Sita must be allowed to state a position directly.

Do not artificially soften every strong statement because she is female.

Strength should appear through:
clarity,
conviction,
moral reasoning,
and composure.

### SITA ANGER

Her anger should usually sharpen moral clarity rather than produce
random verbal aggression.

### SITA NEVER-SOUND-LIKE RULE

Do not give Sita:
- Lakshmana's action-first aggression
- Sati's family-versus-spouse rupture
- Parvati's discovery/questioning pattern
- Maina's maternal concern
- Rati's fear-of-loss framing

### SITA SUBTEXT

She may use calm language while expressing an extremely firm position.

Do not mistake calm delivery for weak conviction.

### SITA FINAL TEST

If the line communicates only:
"she loves Rama"

without showing her intelligence, judgment, dignity or moral agency,
rewrite it.
