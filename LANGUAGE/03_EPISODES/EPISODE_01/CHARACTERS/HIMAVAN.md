# HIMAVAN — EPISODE 01 DIALOGUE PROFILE

## 1. DIALOGUE IDENTITY

Himavan's dialogue is:
- dignified
- paternal
- hospitable
- thoughtful
- emotionally warm
- respectful
- measured

His authority is quiet rather than aggressive.

## 2. BEHAVIORAL CORE

Himavan:
- considers before deciding
- values family
- respects sages
- listens seriously
- asks sincere questions
- expresses concern without immediately becoming hostile
- protects without humiliating others
- can carry doubt while remaining respectful

Traditional material depicts Himavan asking Narada serious questions about
Shiva rather than simply accepting information without thought.

## 3. VOCABULARY FINGERPRINT

Preferred semantic fields:
- daughter
- family
- welfare
- marriage
- duty
- blessing
- household
- guest
- sage
- mountain/home
- protection
- concern
- destiny
- happiness
- suitability
- responsibility

His vocabulary should frequently return to:
"What is best for my family?"

## 4. SENTENCE FINGERPRINT

Default:
- medium length
- complete
- gentle
- explanatory

When concerned:
- questions increase
- sentences become more personal
- concern becomes explicit

When welcoming:
- warm
- respectful
- hospitable

When angry:
- still dignified
- avoids unnecessary vulgarity

## 5. QUESTION STYLE

Himavan asks genuine questions.

His questions should feel like:
- seeking understanding
- testing implications
- protecting family
- reconciling duty with affection

He should not sound interrogative like an interrogator.

## 6. EMOTIONAL LANGUAGE

Primary emotional vocabulary:
- concern
- affection
- wonder
- reverence
- parental anxiety
- gratitude
- relief

He can express fear for Parvati without becoming melodramatic.

## 7. CONFLICT STYLE

Himavan tends toward:
question → listen → consider → respond

rather than:

insult → threaten → dominate.

## 8. AFFECTION

Affection should be:
- protective
- dignified
- paternal
- sincere

Avoid modern parental slang.

## 9. WHAT HIMAVAN DOES NOT SOUND LIKE

Do NOT make him:
- timid
- weak
- constantly sentimental
- excessively poetic
- aggressive
- sarcastic
- casual
- modern

His gentleness is compatible with authority.

## 10. CONTRAST

Himavan ≠ Maina.

Himavan:
measured concern + reasoning + dignity

Maina:
maternal concern + emotional immediacy + protective anxiety

## 11. CANON / ADAPTATION STATUS

His role as Parvati's father and his questioning/concern in traditional
sources are source-supported.

The exact vocabulary fingerprint is a production characterization unless
directly evidenced.

## 12. AI GENERATION RULE

Before writing Himavan, identify:
- what family concern is operating
- what information he has
- what he does not know
- whether he is speaking as father, king, host or devotee

Do not make every line sound like a philosophical speech.
