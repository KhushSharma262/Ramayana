# VISHNU — EPISODE 01 DIALOGUE PROFILE

## 1. CORE DIALOGUE IDENTITY

Vishnu's dialogue is distinct from Rama's.

Vishnu represents:
- cosmic preservation
- divine purpose
- knowledge
- stability
- compassion
- foresight
- divine order
- transcendence

Rama is an embodied narrative character.

Vishnu is the cosmic/divine identity.

Their dialogue must therefore NOT be interchangeable.

---

## 2. BEHAVIORAL ENGINE

Vishnu:
- sees consequences across a larger scale
- speaks with certainty when divine purpose is involved
- does not need to persuade through ego
- can reassure other divine beings
- understands cosmic balance
- may explain divine purpose
- can be compassionate without becoming sentimental

---

## 3. VOCABULARY FINGERPRINT

Primary semantic fields:
- dharma
- cosmic order
- preservation
- divine purpose
- incarnation
- time
- creation
- beings
- protection
- restoration
- balance
- divine will
- consequence
- righteousness
- world/order
- manifestation

Vishnu's vocabulary may become more abstract than Rama's.

---

## 4. SENTENCE FINGERPRINT

Default:
- calm
- complete
- highly controlled
- declarative

Divine purpose:
- concise certainty

Explanation:
- structured
- conceptually precise

Reassurance:
- calm
- compassionate
- confident

Cosmic declaration:
- elevated
- measured
- not theatrical

---

## 5. CERTAINTY

Vishnu may speak with a level of certainty appropriate to divine knowledge.

Avoid unnecessary:
- "perhaps"
- "maybe"
- "I think"
- uncertainty

when the character is speaking about matters he knows with divine certainty.

However, do not make every line omniscient exposition.

---

## 6. COMPASSION

Vishnu's compassion should not sound sentimental.

It is expressed through:
- reassurance
- protection
- understanding
- commitment
- restoration of order

---

## 7. AUTHORITY

Vishnu does not need to repeatedly announce:
"I am God."

His authority should be implicit in:
- certainty
- knowledge
- calm
- purpose
- consequences

---

## 8. HUMOR

Very low.

If used, it should be subtle and context-dependent.

---

## 9. RUDENESS

Normally very low.

Vishnu can condemn destructive action without using vulgar or petty language.

If severe:
- concise
- absolute
- controlled

---

## 10. RELATIONSHIP MODULATION

With other deities:
- composed
- respectful
- authoritative where appropriate

With devotees:
- compassionate
- reassuring

When discussing incarnation:
- purposeful
- certain
- explanatory

When discussing cosmic imbalance:
- serious
- precise

---

## 11. WHAT VISHNU MUST NOT SOUND LIKE

Do not make Vishnu:
- identical to Brahma
- identical to Shiva
- identical to Rama
- permanently philosophical
- theatrical
- boastful
- vague
- modern
- casual

---

## 12. DISTINCTIVE CONTRAST

Vishnu:
COSMIC PURPOSE + PRESERVATION + CERTAINTY

Brahma:
CREATION + KNOWLEDGE + COSMIC ORDER

Shiva:
DETACHMENT + INSIGHT + COMPASSION + TRANSFORMATION

Rama:
EMBODIED DUTY + HUMAN RELATIONSHIP + SELF-CONTROL

This distinction must survive every dialogue scene.

---

## 13. INCARNATION RULE

If Vishnu speaks about Rama's incarnation, do not let the dialogue erase
the distinction between:

Vishnu — divine identity

Rama — embodied manifestation/character

The exact theological framing must follow the selected project source and
adaptation decision.

---

## 14. SILENCE

Vishnu should not explain everything.

Cosmic knowledge should not become an excuse for exposition dumping.

Use:
- concise statements
- implication
- deliberate pauses
- selective explanation

---

## 15. CANON / ADAPTATION

Vishnu's divine role and relationship to preservation/incarnation are
source/tradition grounded.

Exact dialogue vocabulary and sentence patterns are production
characterization unless directly supported by a specific source.

The project must not fabricate quotations from scripture.

---

## 16. FINAL TEST

Ask:

"Is Vishnu speaking from cosmic perspective, or is he speaking like Rama?"

If the latter, rewrite.

Ask:

"Is this cosmic information actually necessary?"

If not, reduce the exposition.

## CHARACTER-SPECIFIC HARD OVERRIDES

Vishnu's dialogue must maintain a clear separation between:

COSMIC IDENTITY
and
EMBODIED CHARACTERIZATION.

### VISHNU WORD PRIORITY

HIGH:
dharma, preservation, divine purpose, incarnation, cosmic order,
protection, restoration, balance, beings, time

MEDIUM:
compassion, destiny, consequence, devotion

LOW:
petty social status, personal insecurity, casual gossip

### VISHNU CERTAINTY RULE

When Vishnu possesses divine knowledge, avoid artificial uncertainty.

However:

DIVINE KNOWLEDGE ≠ CONSTANT EXPOSITION.

He does not need to explain everything he knows.

### VISHNU SPEECH ECONOMY

A cosmic character should not require cosmic-length sentences.

Prefer:
precise,
controlled,
purposeful statements.

### VISHNU NEVER-SOUND-LIKE RULE

Do not give Vishnu:
- Rama's interpersonal domestic vocabulary
- Shiva's detached experiential voice
- Brahma's creation/order emphasis
- Narada's probing style
- Daksha's status language

### VISHNU INCARNATION RULE

When speaking about Rama, preserve the project's approved theological
framework.

Do not casually collapse Vishnu and Rama into interchangeable dialogue
voices.

### VISHNU FINAL TEST

If the line sounds like a king explaining his duty rather than a divine
being speaking from cosmic perspective, reassess it.
