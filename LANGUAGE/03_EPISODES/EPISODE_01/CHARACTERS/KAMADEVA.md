# KAMADEVA — EPISODE 01 DIALOGUE PROFILE

## 1. DIALOGUE IDENTITY

Kāmadeva's speech should be:
- confident
- persuasive
- socially fluid
- playful where context allows
- self-assured
- observant of attraction and desire
- willing to test boundaries

He is not a comedian.

His playfulness comes from confidence and his awareness of desire.

## 2. BEHAVIORAL CORE

Kāmadeva:
- notices attraction
- understands emotional vulnerability
- may speak with confidence about desire
- can be provocative
- may underestimate danger when overconfident
- can become serious when consequences become real

## 3. VOCABULARY FINGERPRINT

Preferred semantic fields:
- desire
- attraction
- beauty
- longing
- glance
- heart
- affection
- charm
- spring
- flowers
- persuasion
- union
- separation
- emotion
- awakening
- attraction
- impulse

His vocabulary should be sensory and relational more often than abstract.

## 4. SENTENCE FINGERPRINT

Default:
- smooth
- moderately concise
- persuasive
- confident

When teasing:
- shorter
- indirect
- suggestive
- lightly provocative

When persuading:
observation → possibility → temptation

When frightened:
confidence should fracture rather than remain artificially perfect.

## 5. PLAYFULNESS

Playfulness must never become modern comedy.

Avoid:
- jokes based on contemporary culture
- slang
- one-liners designed for audience applause
- sexual vulgarity

His wit should arise from:
- confidence
- perception
- provocation
- irony
- desire

## 6. RUDE / PROVOCATIVE LANGUAGE

Kāmadeva can be provocative without being vulgar.

If he challenges someone:
- tease
- implication
- confidence
- rhetorical pressure

not profanity.

## 7. EMOTIONAL SHIFT

His greatest dialogue contrast should occur when confidence fails.

Before danger:
confident / persuasive / playful

During danger:
shorter / uncertain / urgent

After consequence:
serious / reflective / subdued

Do not keep him permanently charming.

## 8. WHAT KAMADEVA DOES NOT SOUND LIKE

Do NOT make him:
- a modern flirt
- a comedian
- vulgar
- sexually explicit
- childish
- constantly poetic
- identical to Narada

## 9. CONTRAST

Kāmadeva persuades through:
DESIRE + POSSIBILITY + CHARM

Narada influences through:
INSIGHT + QUESTION + KNOWLEDGE + CONSEQUENCE

## 10. CANON / ADAPTATION STATUS

The characterization of Kāmadeva as associated with desire and attraction is
source/tradition grounded.

The exact conversational fingerprint is an adaptation tool.

## 11. AI GENERATION RULE

Never let Kāmadeva's dialogue become modern pickup language.

His confidence must belong to the mythic world and remain culturally dignified.
