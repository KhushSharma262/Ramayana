# MAINA — EPISODE 01 DIALOGUE PROFILE

## 1. DIALOGUE IDENTITY

Maina's dialogue is driven by:
- maternal love
- protectiveness
- emotional perception
- concern for Parvati
- fear of suffering
- family attachment
- tenderness

She should feel like a mother with a strong emotional stake in her daughter's
future, not simply "the female character."

## 2. BEHAVIORAL CORE

Maina:
- notices emotional danger
- thinks about her daughter's welfare
- asks questions when uncertain
- may worry before others do
- can become emotionally expressive
- seeks reassurance
- values family security
- can be deeply moved by spiritual truth

## 3. VOCABULARY FINGERPRINT

Preferred semantic fields:
- daughter
- child
- home
- family
- welfare
- marriage
- happiness
- suffering
- protection
- future
- concern
- blessing
- motherly affection
- fear
- reassurance

Her vocabulary should repeatedly return to:
"What will happen to my daughter?"

## 4. SENTENCE FINGERPRINT

Calm:
- warm
- conversational
- measured

Worried:
- more questions
- repeated concern
- shorter clauses
- emotional emphasis

Deeply distressed:
- incomplete constructions may appear
- repetition may increase
- names/kinship terms may become more frequent

Reassured:
- softer
- grateful
- emotionally released

## 5. QUESTION STYLE

Maina asks because she genuinely needs reassurance.

Her questions should expose:
- uncertainty
- maternal fear
- desire for understanding

She should not interrogate like Daksha.

## 6. EMOTIONAL LANGUAGE

Maina is permitted greater emotional visibility than Himavan.

However:
emotional ≠ hysterical.

Use:
- concern
- tenderness
- fear
- pleading
- gratitude
- relief

Avoid melodrama in every line.

## 7. CONFLICT STYLE

Maina's first instinct is often:
concern → question → appeal

rather than:
judgment → command → insult.

When pushed too far, she can become firm.

## 8. AFFECTION

Affection may appear through:
- kinship terms
- reassurance
- protective statements
- concern for comfort
- concern for future
- emotional repetition

No modern pet names.

## 9. WHAT MAINA DOES NOT SOUND LIKE

Do NOT make her:
- permanently crying
- weak
- unintelligent
- submissive
- constantly poetic
- modern-romantic
- sarcastic
- vulgar

## 10. CONTRAST

Maina ≠ Himavan.

Himavan:
reasoned paternal concern

Maina:
emotionally immediate maternal concern

## 11. CANON / ADAPTATION STATUS

Maina's maternal relationship to Parvati and concern within traditional
Parvati narratives are source/tradition grounded.

The detailed dialogue fingerprint is a production characterization.

## 12. AI GENERATION RULE

Before writing Maina, identify the maternal concern underneath the line.

If no concern, affection, protection or family issue exists, reconsider whether
the line sounds like Maina.
