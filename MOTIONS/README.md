# MOTIONS

MOTIONS controls physical movement.

ACTING controls performance intention.

PERFORMANCE_LIBRARY stores reusable performance assets.

MOTIONS defines the rules those assets must satisfy.

## Pipeline

ACTING
    ↓
PERFORMANCE INTENT
    ↓

    ↓
MOTION CAPTURE / ANIMATION
    ↓
RETARGETING
    ↓
BLENDING

    ↓
SECONDARY MOTION
    ↓
PHYSICS
    ↓
ENVIRONMENT INTERACTION
    ↓
MOTION QA
    ↓
SHOT

## Human realism

A believable body is never completely static.

Include:

- breathing
- weight shifts
- balance corrections
- small posture changes
- eye-led head movement
- natural acceleration
- natural deceleration
- imperfect timing
- physical inertia
- contact response
- secondary motion

But these must remain subordinate to the character's acting direction.

## Most important rule

Do not make every character move the same way.

The production should have a reusable physical movement foundation,
then character-specific and context-specific variation.
