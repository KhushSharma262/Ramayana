# MOTION WEIGHT RULES

Movement must respect:

- center of mass
- gravity
- momentum
- inertia
- support area
- ground contact
- body mass
- carried-object mass

Heavy movement does not mean simply moving slowly.

Light movement does not mean simply moving quickly.

Weight must be communicated through:

- acceleration
- deceleration
- force
- body compensation
- foot pressure
- torso response
- momentum
- recovery

Characters carrying objects must physically respond to their weight.
