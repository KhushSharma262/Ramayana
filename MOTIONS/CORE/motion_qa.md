# MOTION QA

## Anatomy

- no impossible joints
- believable joint limits
- natural coordination

## Balance

- believable center of mass
- correct foot support
- believable weight transfer

## Timing

- no robotic repetition
- natural acceleration
- natural deceleration
- believable pauses

## Contact

- feet contact ground
- hands contact objects correctly
- bodies interact correctly
- no floating

## Continuity

- correct start state
- correct end state
- no unexplained motion reset
- correct character movement identity

## Secondary motion

Check:

- hair
- cloth
- jewelry
- props
- weapons

## Environment

Movement must respond appropriately to:

- terrain
- stairs
- mud
- water
- wind
- rain
- slopes

## AI

Check:

- anatomical artifacts
- temporal artifacts
- motion drift
- repeated patterns
- impossible physics
