# AI MOTION RULES

AI-generated movement must be treated as a candidate performance,
not automatically accepted as physical truth.

Check for:

- extra limbs
- broken joints
- foot sliding
- floating
- impossible acceleration
- unnatural symmetry
- repeated gestures
- robotic timing
- frozen body parts
- incorrect hand-object contact
- incorrect weight transfer
- temporal flicker
- movement identity drift

Human motion realism has priority over visual spectacle.

If AI motion contradicts the approved movement system:

REJECT
→ CORRECT
→ REGENERATE / RETARGET
→ REVIEW
